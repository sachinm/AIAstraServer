CARD: `blood` (Blood & vitality)

What this card answers: This person's stamina and recovery through the year.

What it reads: The Sun and Mars (heat and vigour), the Moon (fluids), the lagna lord, the 6th and 8th; the rows on them in each span. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Fitness is its own card. Pace, heat, heaviness, fluids and stamina only; no condition, organ, test result or diagnosis.
