CARD: `fitness` (Fitness & strength)

What this card answers: When to push the body and when to hold, and what kind of effort suits this person.

What it reads: Mars and the 3rd (effort, courage), the lagna lord's strength, the 6th (training against resistance), the 1st; the rows on them in each span. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Rest and sleep belong to the sleep card, stamina over the year to the blood card. Speak of effort, load and recovery only; no injury, condition or diagnosis.
