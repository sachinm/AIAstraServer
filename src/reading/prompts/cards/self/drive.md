CARD: `drive` (Drive & ambition)

What this card answers: What pushes this person, what it is for, and when it gains.

What it reads: The Atmakaraka and Karakamsa, the Amatyakaraka, the 10th and its lord, Mars and the Sun, the 3rd, 9th, 11th and 12th where they steer the aim. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Work itself is the Work board's; keep to the drive and its timing.
