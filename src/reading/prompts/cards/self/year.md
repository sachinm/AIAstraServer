CARD: `year` (This year)

What this card answers: This person's year from one Tithi Pravesha return to the next.

What it reads: The running Tithi Pravesha year (the YEAR TP rows and the year clock's tones), its lagna and lord, its year dasha now and next; every year the spans touch is on the YEARS line. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Never take the calendar birthday as the boundary. When no Tithi Pravesha year covers today, say so in the headline and keep every span to what the clocks agree on.
