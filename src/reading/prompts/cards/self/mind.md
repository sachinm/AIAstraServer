CARD: `mind` (How you think)

What this card answers: How this person feels, thinks, finds peace and judges, and what each span does to it.

What it reads: The Moon (house, sign, nakshatra and its lord, cancellations), Mercury, the 4th and 5th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Focus and sharpness have their own card, the emotional weather its own (mood). Keep to the way of thinking and judging.
