CARD: `purpose` (Purpose & meaning)

What this card answers: What this life is for, and what this year asks of it.

What it reads: The 9th (dharma) and its lord, the Atmakaraka and Karakamsa, Jupiter, the 5th and 12th; the rows on the 9th in each span. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Drive and career are other cards; keep to meaning and calling.
