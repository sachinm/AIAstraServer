CARD: `sharpness` (Sharpness & focus)

What this card answers: When this person's mind is quick and when it is foggy.

What it reads: Mercury (its dignity, combustion, avastha), the 5th (intelligence) and the 3rd (attention), the Moon; the rows on them in each span. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: The way of thinking is the mind card's; keep to quickness, attention and focus over the year.
