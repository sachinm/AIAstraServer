CARD: `chapters` (Chapters)

What this card answers: The long arc of this life, chapter by chapter.

What it reads: The running main period as the arc, the previous one in a line, its sub-periods ahead as the frame shows them, its end and the next lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: The chapter now is the chapter card's; keep to the arc.
