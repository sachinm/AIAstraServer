CARD: `layers` (The five layers)

What this card answers: Body, breath, mind, judgment, joy: which layer needs care now, span by span.

What it reads: The lagna and Sun (body), the Moon (mind), Mercury (judgment), Jupiter and the 9th and 12th (joy and release), the 4th (breath and ease); the rows on them. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: No practice is prescribed here and no medical word is used; name the layer and what it asks.
