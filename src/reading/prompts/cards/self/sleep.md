CARD: `sleep` (Sleep & rest)

What this card answers: When rest runs thin and when it comes back, span by span.

What it reads: The 12th (bed, withdrawal) and its lord, the 4th (inner peace), the Moon; the rows on the 12th and 4th in each span. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Mood has its own card. Speak of rest, evenings, pace and quiet; never a sleep disorder, a medicine or a symptom.
