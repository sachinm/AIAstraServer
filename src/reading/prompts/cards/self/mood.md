CARD: `mood` (Mood & inner weather)

What this card answers: The emotional tone of each stretch of the year.

What it reads: The Moon and its dispositor, the 4th, the 12th, Saturn's passage over the Moon where stored; the rows on the 4th and on the Moon in each span. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Never a mental-health condition, a medicine or a diagnosis. Speak of the weather inside: heavy, mixed, light, and what lifts it.
