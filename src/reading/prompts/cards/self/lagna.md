CARD: `lagna` (Who you are)

What this card answers: This person's nature, the way they meet the world.

What it reads: The lagna, its lord's house and dignity, the Arudha Lagna and Sree Lagna; how others see them against what steers them. Charts: the D1 and D9, the D9 lagna leading for temperament; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1, then its D9; the charts past the natal D1 and D9 only where the shared data carries them. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: How others read them is the presence card's; keep to their own nature and how this year presses on it.
