CARD: `play` (Play & joy)

What this card answers: What entertains and delights this person, and when there is room for it.

What it reads: The 5th (play, creativity) and its lord, Venus, the 11th (friends, gains), the 3rd (hobbies); the rows on them in each span. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Love and children are other boards; keep to play, hobbies and delight.
