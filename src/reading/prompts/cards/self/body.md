CARD: `body` (Body & energy)

What this card answers: What wears this person's body in each span of the year, and what steadies it.

What it reads: The lagna and its lord, the 6th and 8th, the Trimsamsa, and the live rows on them; the Sun and the Moon as vitality. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Sleep, mood and blood have their own cards. No condition, organ, symptom, medicine or diagnosis: pace, rest, heat, heaviness and stamina only.
