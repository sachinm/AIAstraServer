CARD: `presence` (How you come across)

What this card answers: How other people read this person, which they cannot see themselves.

What it reads: The Arudha Lagna against the Moon and the 4th, the 1st house with its occupants and aspects, Venus and the 2nd. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Their inner nature is the lagna card's; keep to the outer impression and its season.
