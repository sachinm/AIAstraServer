CARD: `presence` (How you come across)

What this card answers: How other people read this person, which they cannot see themselves.

What it reads: The Arudha Lagna against the Moon and the 4th, the 1st house with its occupants and aspects, Venus and the 2nd. Charts: the D1 and D9, the D9 lagna leading for temperament; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1, then its D9. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Their inner nature is the lagna card's; keep to the outer impression and its season.
