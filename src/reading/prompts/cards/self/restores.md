CARD: `restores` (What restores you)

What this card answers: What brings this person peace, and where and when to find it.

What it reads: The 4th (peace, home) and the 12th (retreat), the 9th (faith), Jupiter and the Moon; the light rows on them in each span. Charts: the D1 and D9, with the D16 (mental happiness) as this card's own chart; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1, then its D16; the charts past the natal D1 and D9 only where the shared data carries them. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Practices and remedies belong to the Indian-lens cards; name kinds of rest and places of peace.
