CARD: `restores` (What restores you)

What this card answers: What brings this person peace, and where and when to find it.

What it reads: The 4th (peace, home) and the 12th (retreat), the 9th (faith), Jupiter and the Moon; the light rows on them in each span. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

What it leaves to other cards: Practices and remedies belong to the Indian-lens cards; name kinds of rest and places of peace.
