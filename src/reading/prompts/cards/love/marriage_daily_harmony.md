CARD: `marriage_daily_harmony` (Warmth & Day-to-Day Harmony)
Board: Love, Marriage lens.

What this card answers: "Where does natural warmth and day-to-day ease live in your partnership right now, and which coming stretches best nurture closeness?"

What it reads: The 4th and its lord, the 7th and the argala on it, the Moon and Venus, and the D9 4th and 7th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For this person: Married or committed. With young children, protect couple time inside family life; where work is heavy, keep small rituals that survive busy weeks. Small, concrete habits: a shared meal, a walk, an evening without screens. Ladder: best, daily thanks and unhurried time together; practical, put couple time on the calendar; drift, taking each other for granted; floor, no carrying work's tiredness home as coldness.

Confirm: whether home felt noticeably lighter, warmer or easier together in a past span (tag `warm_home`).

What it leaves to other cards: Friction is Distance, Strain & Conflict Cycles'; talking things through is Communication & Unspoken Resentments'.
