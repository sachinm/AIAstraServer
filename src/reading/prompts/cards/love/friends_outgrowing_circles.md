CARD: `friends_outgrowing_circles` (Outgrowing Circles & Friendship Friction)
Board: Love, Friends lens.

What this card answers: "Why are certain friendships feeling distant or strained right now, and is this a rough patch or a natural season of outgrowing an old circle?" When the shift runs, and what can still be mended.

What it reads: Ketu and Saturn on the 11th or the A11, the 6th and 8th lords on the 11th, and the 6th and 8th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. The D11 only if present in the shared data; until then the 11th, the A11 and the D9 and D10 11th stand in for it.

For this person: Use the changes they told you about (a move, a new relationship, new work) to explain why circles shift. Natural completion without bitterness; never a verdict on a named friend. Ladder: best, bless the shared past and let mismatched ties go kindly; practical, raise a real issue once, then spend your time elsewhere; drift, clinging to a group out of fear of change; floor, no gossip or burnt bridges in a heated stretch.

Confirm: whether a long-standing friend or group began quietly fading or felt out of step in a past span (tag `circle_fading`).

What it leaves to other cards: New circles are Finding Your Tribe & New Communities'; one-sided ties are Boundaries & Energy Drains'.
