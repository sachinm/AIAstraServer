Board: Love, Friends lens.

What this card answers: "Across the coming twelve months, which stretches energise you through active social connection, and when do you need quieter time alone to recharge?"

What it reads: The 11th (going out), the 12th (time alone) and the 3rd, the A11 against the Arudha Lagna, the Moon and Mercury, and Saturn's rows over them. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. The D11 only if present in the shared data; until then the 11th, the A11 and the D9 and D10 11th stand in for it.

For this person: Their age band, their work (a people-heavy job needs more recharge) and where they live, if stated. A quiet stretch is a battery to refill, never guilt. Ladder: best, be fully there when out and fully at rest when in; practical, group plans into the high-energy weeks and block recovery days; drift, going out when spent and feeling flat; floor, no vanishing on close friends without a short honest word.

Confirm: whether group plans felt heavier than usual in a past span, making quiet time at home essential (tag `quiet_needed`).

What it leaves to other cards: New circles are Finding Your Tribe & New Communities'; draining people are Boundaries & Energy Drains'.
