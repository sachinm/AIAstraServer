CARD: `marriage_conflict_cycles` (Distance, Strain & Conflict Cycles)
Board: Love, Marriage lens. Sensitive card.

What this card answers: "What passing pressure or recurring friction is straining your bond right now, and on what date does it begin to ease?" Passing weather or a deeper pattern: say which.

What it reads: Mars and Saturn on the 7th and 4th, the nodes on the 1st and 7th, the Gnatikaraka against the Darakaraka, the 6th, 8th and 12th, and the D9 7th and 2nd. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. Charts: the D1 and D9; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1, then its D9. The easing date is a span edge.

For this person: Calm and de-escalating; never plant a fear of breaking up. Honour every declined topic. If they mention fear for their safety, their safety and outside help come first, not couple advice. Ladder: best, name the outside stress so the partner is not the enemy; practical, agreed cool-off rules on heated days; drift, keeping score; floor, no words that cannot be taken back in a hot stretch.

Confirm: whether arguments, cold distance or household stress rose sharply from a past date (tag `strain_spike`).

What it leaves to other cards: Talking is Communication & Unspoken Resentments'; after a separation, Repair & Reconciliation. No separation or divorce verdict.
