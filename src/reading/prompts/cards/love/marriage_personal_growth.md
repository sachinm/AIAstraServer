CARD: `marriage_personal_growth` (Your Role & Growth Inside Marriage)
Board: Love, Marriage lens.

What this card answers: "What personal growth, or balance between independence and devotion, is this partnership asking of you in this cycle?" Whether they over-merge or over-guard, and when the shift comes.

What it reads: The lagna lord against the 7th lord, the Atmakaraka against the Darakaraka, the Arudha Lagna against the Upapada, the 1st and 7th, and the D9 Karakamsa. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For this person: Married or committed. Use their work and focus if stated to name what is theirs to protect. Younger bands: finding who they are inside the bond; older bands: becoming more fully themselves. Ladder: best, grow alongside the partner while keeping your own path; practical, protected time for your own pursuits and friends; drift, losing your voice to keep the peace; floor, no swinging between giving everything and pulling away.

Confirm: whether they felt a strong pull to reclaim their own voice or direction inside the relationship in a past span (tag `own_voice`).

What it leaves to other cards: The self in its own right is the You board's; friendships are the Friends lens's. No separation or divorce verdict.
