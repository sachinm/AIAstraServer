CARD: `friends_core_allies` (The Friends Who Truly Carry You)
Board: Love, Friends lens.

What this card answers: "What kind of people show up as your most loyal, dependable friends, and where in your life now do those steady allies live?" And when they step forward.

What it reads: The 11th and its lord, the 9th, the A11, Jupiter, the Bhratrikaraka, and the D9 11th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. Charts: the D1 and D9, the 11th counted from the lagna and from the Arudha Lagna; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1.

For this person: Keep friend roles apart: a peer confidant, a sibling-like elder, a thinking companion are different people, not one type. Few and steady over many and loose. Ladder: best, show up in their storms without keeping count; practical, put your time into your few most trusted friends; drift, neglecting old friends for exciting new ones; floor, no expecting loyalty from people kept only for convenience.

Confirm: whether one steady friend or sibling-like ally stepped up quietly and practically in a past span (tag `friend_showed`).

What it leaves to other cards: Circles that are fading are Outgrowing Circles & Friendship Friction's; older guides are Mentors, Elders & Supportive Guides'.
