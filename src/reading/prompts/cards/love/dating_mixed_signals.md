CARD: `dating_mixed_signals` (How They Feel & Mixed Signals)
Board: Love, Dating lens.

What this card answers: "Are the mixed signals around this connection temporary hesitation and timing fog, or a genuine mismatch in readiness and intent?" And when the fog clears.

What it reads: Mercury and the Moon, Rahu over either, the 3rd and 12th, the 5th and 7th lords, the A7 against the Arudha Lagna, and the station rows of Mercury and Venus. Charts: the D1 and D9; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1, then its D9; the charts past the natal D1 and D9 only where the shared data carries them. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. The clearing date is a span edge or a station date in the rows.

For this person: Dating or it's complicated. Only this person's chart is read: say what they may notice and do, never what the other person feels or intends. Ladder: best, one calm boundary, then let steady behaviour answer; practical, give the uncertainty a time limit and keep your own life full; drift, over-reading every message; floor, never chase harder when someone pulls away.

Confirm: whether communication or emotional steadiness wobbled in a past span (tag `signals_wobble`).

What it leaves to other cards: Defining the bond is Turning Dating Into Commitment's; letting go is Exes, No-Contact & Reopening Doors'.
