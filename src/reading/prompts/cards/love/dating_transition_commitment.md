CARD: `dating_transition_commitment` (Turning Dating Into Commitment)
Board: Love, Dating lens.

What this card answers: "During which coming dates is your dating life supported to step from casual romance into defined commitment, and what conversation shows whether this bond can hold it?"

What it reads: The 5th and 7th, the link between the 5th lord, the 7th lord and the Upapada, the Darakaraka, the D9 7th and Upapada, and Jupiter and Saturn both on the 7th where the rows carry it. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. Romance active without the 7th, the Upapada or the D9 agreeing is a "may", never a commitment claim. Stay inside the frame.

For this person: Single or dating, and wanting something defined. Never say whether another person will commit. Ladder: best, state your long-term intent calmly and honour their honest capacity; practical, agree on next steps and a shared timeline; drift, staying undefined and hoping; floor, no ultimatum fired in a tense stretch.

Confirm: whether a conversation about defining the relationship or a next step came up in a past span (tag `define_talk`).

What it leaves to other cards: Once committed, the Marriage lens takes over. Mixed signals are How They Feel & Mixed Signals'.
