CARD: `marriage_inlaws_family` (Family, In-Laws & Shared Home)
Board: Love, Marriage lens.

What this card answers: "How are extended family expectations or household boundaries shaping your partnership right now, and when do home pressures settle?"

What it reads: The 4th (the shared home), the 2nd (their own family), the 8th (the partner's family), the 2nd from the Upapada, the A4, Saturn on the 4th, 8th or 2nd, and the D9 4th and 8th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. The D4 only if present in the shared data. Keep the three families apart; do not merge them into one "family".

For this person: Married or committed; children and where they live only if stated. Speak of the couple as one team facing family, never one side against the other. Ladder: best, honour elders and keep the couple's decisions between you; practical, agree roles, money limits and visits together; drift, venting to parents about the partner; floor, no letting family split the two of you into camps.

Confirm: whether a disagreement over parents, in-laws or living arrangements tested their peace in a past span (tag `family_test`).

What it leaves to other cards: Shared money is Shared Money & Responsibilities'; everyday warmth is Warmth & Day-to-Day Harmony's. No separation or divorce verdict.
