CARD: `marriage_communication` (Communication & Unspoken Resentments)
Board: Love, Marriage lens.

What this card answers: "What unspoken tensions or speaking habits are creating distance between you, and which coming dates best support an honest, calm conversation?"

What it reads: The 2nd (speech) and 3rd (reaching out), the 7th, Mercury and the Moon, the D9 2nd, and Mercury's station rows. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. A clear-talk window is a span edge or a station date in the rows.

For this person: Married, committed or separated. Practical and never blaming; only this person's chart is read, so speak of their part of the talk, never what the partner thinks. Ladder: best, truthful kindness, feelings over accusations; practical, a short weekly check-in with turns; drift, swallowing hurt until it leaks out; floor, no sharp words on a heated night.

Confirm: whether a small misunderstanding or badly timed talk grew larger than meant in a past span (tag `talk_spiral`).

What it leaves to other cards: Trust is Trust, Transparency & Hidden Stress'; everyday warmth is Warmth & Day-to-Day Harmony's.
