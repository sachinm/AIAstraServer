CARD: `allies_managing_people` (Managing & Leading People)
Board: Love, Allies lens.

What this card answers: "How does your way of leading land on the people who report to you right now, and how can you delegate cleanly and build steady loyalty?" When leadership flows and when delegation is tested.

What it reads: The 10th, 6th and 5th, the Sun's directional strength, Jupiter and Saturn, the A10 against the A5, and the D10 lagna, 5th, 6th and 10th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For this person: A manager, senior leader or owner with staff, as they told you. How they come across to the people they lead, never what a report thinks. Ladder: best, clarity, protecting the team and growing their skill; practical, clear outcomes and regular check-ins; drift, micromanaging or dodging hard feedback; floor, no ruling by temper or by coldness.

Confirm: whether a delegation change, a hard people decision or a dip in team morale tested how they lead in a past span (tag `lead_test`).

What it leaves to other cards: Upward relations are Bosses, Authority & Mentors at Work's; the leadership role itself is the Work board's. No firing verdict.
