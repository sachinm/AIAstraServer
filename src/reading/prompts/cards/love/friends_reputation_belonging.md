CARD: `friends_reputation_belonging` (Social Reputation & Belonging)
Board: Love, Friends lens.

What this card answers: "How does your presence land in groups and communities right now, and how can you build a deeper, real belonging without performing?" When goodwill rises, and when being seen is awkward.

What it reads: The Arudha Lagna against the A11, the lagna as the inner self, the 10th and 11th and the link between their lords. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. Charts: the D1 and D9, the 11th counted from the lagna and from the Arudha Lagna; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1.

For this person: The gap between how groups see them and how they feel inside, and how to close it honestly. Use their place and focus if stated. Ladder: best, let kindness and reliability shape your name; practical, show up visibly where your real strengths help; drift, editing yourself to fit in and still feeling unseen; floor, no performing for status or answering petty gossip.

Confirm: whether they noticed a shift in how a wider group or community saw or welcomed them in a past span (tag `group_welcome`).

What it leaves to other cards: Standing at work is the Work board's; finding new groups is Finding Your Tribe & New Communities'.
