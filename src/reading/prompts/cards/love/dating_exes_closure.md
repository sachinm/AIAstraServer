CARD: `dating_exes_closure` (Exes, No-Contact & Reopening Doors)
Board: Love, Dating lens. Sensitive card.

What this card answers: "When a past connection resurfaces or weighs on your mind in this window, is your chart supporting a genuine second chapter or final emotional closure?" And when the pull of the past fades.

What it reads: The 8th and 12th, retrograde 5th and 7th lords, Ketu with Venus, the Upapada and the 2nd from it, the D9 7th and 9th, and 8th or 12th lord sub-periods in the timing block. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. A second chapter is a `build` claim only where the D9 and the 7th confirm real change; otherwise speak of closure.

For this person: Gentle and steady. Honour every declined topic and any wish for closure; never push a return, and never claim someone will get in touch. If they mention fear or harm, their safety comes first. Ladder: best, keep the lesson and the door closed unless the pattern truly changed; practical, ask for accountability before any contact; drift, breaking no-contact out of boredom; floor, no on-off cycle that costs years.

Confirm: whether an old relationship thread, memory or message resurfaced in a past span (tag `past_resurfaced`).

What it leaves to other cards: Repeating habits are Breaking Your Dating Loop's; new meetings are When You Meet Someone's.
