CARD: `allies_bosses_authority` (Bosses, Authority & Mentors at Work)
Board: Love, Allies lens.

What this card answers: "How can you work cleanly with senior people right now, and during which coming dates is backing or recognition from them most supported?" Strict high standards against plain micromanagement: say which.

What it reads: The 10th and 9th, the Sun, Saturn and Jupiter, the A10, and the D10 9th and 10th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For this person: Employed. Keep senior figures apart: the direct manager and a supportive senior sponsor are different people. Only this person's chart is read, so never say what a boss thinks or plans. Ladder: best, excellent work without flattery and quiet respect; practical, show your results in the terms your manager cares about; drift, private resentment while hoping for approval; floor, no power struggle with an insecure boss in a tense stretch.

Confirm: whether friction with a manager, or unexpected backing from a senior person, shaped their standing in a past span (tag `boss_turn`).

What it leaves to other cards: Promotions are the Work board's; written terms are Contracts, Disputes & Protection's. No job-loss or firing verdict.
