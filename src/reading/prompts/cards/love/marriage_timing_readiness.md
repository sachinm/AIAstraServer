CARD: `marriage_timing_readiness` (Marriage Timing & Readiness)
Board: Love, Marriage lens.

What this card answers: "During which coming date ranges is formal marriage or lifelong commitment most strongly supported, and what personal readiness clears the way?"

What it reads: The 7th and 9th, the 7th lord, the Upapada, the Darakaraka, Mars on the marriage houses, the D9 7th and 9th, and Jupiter and Saturn both on the 7th where the rows carry it. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. A commitment window needs the D1 and the D9 to agree; otherwise it is a readiness span.

For this person: Committed: vows and alignment. Single and wanting marriage: windows and readiness. Separated: no rush. Never a deadline, and never present Mars on the marriage houses as a defect or a fear. Ladder: best, align values and family ethics first; practical, settle money, papers and moves ahead of the window; drift, fixing on a calendar date over fit; floor, no rushing in to quiet outside pressure.

Confirm: whether marriage, engagement or serious family talks came up in a past span (tag `marriage_talk`).

What it leaves to other cards: Meeting someone is the Dating lens's; life after the wedding is Warmth & Day-to-Day Harmony's and Family, In-Laws & Shared Home's.
