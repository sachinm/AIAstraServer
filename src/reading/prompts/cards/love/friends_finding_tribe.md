CARD: `friends_finding_tribe` (Finding Your Tribe & New Communities)
Board: Love, Friends lens.

What this card answers: "During which coming windows, and through what kinds of spaces or shared interests, are you most supported to meet kindred friends?"

What it reads: The 11th, 9th and 3rd, Rahu in the 11th or on the A11, and Jupiter's rows over the 11th, 3rd and 9th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. Charts: the D1 and D9, the 11th counted from the lagna and from the Arudha Lagna; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1. The kind of space (study, craft, faith, a trade group, abroad) comes from the houses; the window from the spans.

For this person: Use a recent move, their focus and their work if stated to name a real place to start. Ladder: best, groups built on learning, service or shared craft over status; practical, go to one group steadily for a couple of months; drift, one awkward evening and "nobody fits me"; floor, no drama-heavy crowd just to avoid being alone.

Confirm: whether a new group, class or circle brought warm new people into their life in a past span (tag `new_circle`).

What it leaves to other cards: Older guides are Mentors, Elders & Supportive Guides'; how groups see them is Social Reputation & Belonging's.
