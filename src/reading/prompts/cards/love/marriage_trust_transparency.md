CARD: `marriage_trust_transparency` (Trust, Transparency & Hidden Stress)
Board: Love, Marriage lens. Sensitive card.

What this card answers: "Where are unspoken worries about openness or hidden stress testing trust right now, and how can transparency be rebuilt in this cycle?" When uncertainty peaks and when things come into the open.

What it reads: Rahu on the 7th lord or Venus, the 8th and 12th, the Upapada against the A8 and A12, and the D9 7th, 8th and 12th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For this person: The chart shows this person's season of uncertainty and clarity, never another person's acts: never say a partner is hiding, lying or unfaithful. Neither feed an unchecked suspicion nor dismiss what they have seen. Honour every declined topic. Ladder: best, calm direct questions about what you observed; practical, open agreements on money and time; drift, quiet checking and private worry; floor, no accusation without facts, and no ignoring plain dishonesty.

Confirm: whether a hidden worry, an unspoken money matter or a boundary issue surfaced in a past span (tag `hidden_surfaced`).

What it leaves to other cards: Repair is Repair & Reconciliation's; friction is Distance, Strain & Conflict Cycles'. No separation or divorce verdict.
