Board: Love, Allies lens.

What this card answers: "Across the next twelve months, what is the overall rhythm between you and the people you work with, and which stretches bring smooth teamwork and which bring friction?"

What it reads: The 10th, 6th, 7th and 11th, the D10 lagna, 6th, 7th, 10th and 11th, the A10, the Arudha Lagna and the Amatyakaraka. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. The year lord and the YEAR TP D10 only if present in the shared data.

For this person: Employed or running their own business, at the level they told you. An employee hears of colleagues and managers; an owner hears of staff, partners and clients. Ladder: best, steady dignity with everyone and no part in gossip; practical, written agreement with key people before a rough stretch; drift, being caught off guard by a reshuffle; floor, no picking fights in a heated stretch.

Confirm: whether team dynamics or the tone from leadership shifted noticeably in a past span (tag `team_shift`).

What it leaves to other cards: Bosses, peers, clients and contracts each have their own card; the job itself is the Work board's. No job-loss or firing verdict.
