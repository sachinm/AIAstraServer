CARD: `marriage_pulse` (Where Your Partnership Stands)
Board: Love, Marriage lens.

What this card answers: "Across the next twelve months, what is the overall emotional climate of your partnership, and which stretches bring natural closeness and which test your patience?"

What it reads: The 7th and its lord, the 4th, the Upapada, the Darakaraka, Venus and Jupiter, the D9 lagna and 7th, and the YEAR TP rows on the 7th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. The year lord only if present in the shared data.

For this person: Married, committed or separated, as they told you; children only if stated. A heavy stretch is pressure on the pair, never a forecast of an ending. Ladder: best, meet hard stretches as a team against outside stress; practical, put big shared decisions in the steady stretches; drift, reacting mood by mood; floor, never turn a passing strain into a threat to the bond.

Confirm: whether a past stretch felt noticeably steadier, or more demanding, than usual between them (tag `season_shift`).

What it leaves to other cards: Warmth is Warmth & Day-to-Day Harmony's; strain is Distance, Strain & Conflict Cycles'; money and family have their own cards. No separation or divorce verdict.
