CARD: `friends_boundaries_drains` (Boundaries & Energy Drains)
Board: Love, Friends lens.

What this card answers: "Where are boundaries blurring in your friendships right now, and how can you protect your energy calmly without needless conflict?" When giving too much peaks, and when resetting is easier.

What it reads: The 12th, 6th and 11th, the 6th lord in the 11th, the Moon, and the rows of the nodes over the Moon. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. Charts: the D1 and D9, the 11th counted from the lagna and from the Arudha Lagna; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1.

For this person: For the one who always listens and helps. Kind, clear "no" without guilt; never a condition or a diagnosis, and never a verdict on a named friend. Ladder: best, say no kindly without over-explaining or rescuing; practical, set limits on late calls and favours; drift, saying yes resentfully, then pulling away; floor, no months of silent giving that end in a blow-up.

Confirm: whether a one-sided friendship or constant favour-asking left them noticeably worn down in a past span (tag `drained`).

What it leaves to other cards: Circles that are ending are Outgrowing Circles & Friendship Friction's; the year's social rhythm is Your Social Energy This Year's.
