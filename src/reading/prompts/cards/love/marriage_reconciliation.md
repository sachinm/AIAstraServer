CARD: `marriage_reconciliation` (Repair & Reconciliation)
Board: Love, Marriage lens. Sensitive card.

What this card answers: "In this strained or separated chapter, where does your chart show genuine openings for repair, and what must change for healing to hold?" How long the cooling-off runs, and when a repair talk is best supported.

What it reads: The Upapada and the 7th lord, Ketu on the 7th or the Upapada, Jupiter over the Upapada, the 8th and 9th, and the D9 lagna, 7th and 9th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. A repair window needs the Upapada, Jupiter and the 7th to open together; otherwise it is a span for steadying.

For this person: Separated, or in a strained marriage or commitment. Honour every declined topic and any stated wish for closure: a peaceful ending is as dignified as repair. A neutral counsellor or mediator may be named as an option, never legal advice. Ladder: best, own your half and forgive without forgetting; practical, a neutral third person and clear milestones; drift, long limbo that neither repairs nor closes; floor, no return to the same harm out of fear of being alone.

Confirm: whether distance widened suddenly or a strain reached a breaking point in a past span (tag `strain_break`).

What it leaves to other cards: Renewed warmth is Warmth & Day-to-Day Harmony's; dating again is the Dating lens's. No verdict that the bond will end or reunite.
