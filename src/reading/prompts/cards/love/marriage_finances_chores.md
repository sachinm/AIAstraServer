CARD: `marriage_finances_chores` (Shared Money & Responsibilities)
Board: Love, Marriage lens.

What this card answers: "Where are money expectations or day-to-day household duties pulling out of balance, and which coming stretches support money alignment and teamwork?" Lean stretches against stretches of surplus and agreement.

What it reads: The 2nd, 8th, 11th and 6th, the dhana yogas, the A2 and A8, the D2 hora, and the D9 2nd, 8th and 11th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For this person: Married or committed. Use their work status if given (between jobs, building a business) and children if stated. Fairness and shared stewardship, never blame; no investment or tax advice. Ladder: best, treat money and chores as one shared care with no scorekeeping; practical, a calm monthly review of budget and who does what; drift, quiet resentment over uneven effort or spending; floor, no hidden debts or purchases.

Confirm: whether shared expenses, uneven workload or a money decision needed renegotiating in a past span (tag `money_reset`).

What it leaves to other cards: Earnings and pay are the Work board's; family pressure is Family, In-Laws & Shared Home's.
