CARD: `friends_mentors_guides` (Mentors, Elders & Supportive Guides)
Board: Love, Friends lens.

What this card answers: "During which coming windows, and through what kinds of learning or community settings, are wise mentors, elders or trusted advisors drawn into your path?" And how to tell a real guide from a self-serving one.

What it reads: The 9th and its lord, the 11th, Jupiter, the Bhratrikaraka, the A9, and the D9 9th. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given. Charts: the D1 and D9, the 11th counted from the lagna and from the Arudha Lagna; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1; the charts past the natal D1 and D9 only where the shared data carries them.

For this person: Younger bands: finding a guide. Older bands may be guided and guiding at once. Use their focus if stated (craft, faith, life direction). Ladder: best, come to a teacher humble and ready to practise; practical, choose proven elders and bring thought-out questions; drift, collecting advice and using none; floor, never hand your judgement to a charismatic figure.

Confirm: whether an older guide, teacher or wise friend offered a view that shifted their direction in a past span (tag `guide_shift`).

What it leaves to other cards: Bosses and sponsors at work are the Allies lens's; friends of their own age are The Friends Who Truly Carry You's.
