CARD: `craft_creative_blocks` (Creative Blocks & Loss of Motivation)

Board: Work, Hobby lens.

What this card answers: What is constricting this person's creative motivation, and when the spark reopens. The question: what is making self-expression feel heavy now, and from what dated point does the spark reopen?

What it reads: Saturn and Ketu on the 5th or its lord; a combust or weak Venus or Mercury; papakartari on the 5th or A5; the 12th; the D9 5th and the D24; running sub-periods 6th or 8th from the main period; the transits that lift. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Dissolve the shame: a dry spell is a season, with a date it thaws and small low-stakes exercises meanwhile. Working: when the day job bleeds into making time. Retired or 60 and over: gentle and unhurried. Under 25 light. A build span is for reopening stalled drafts with small daily warm-ups; a get-through span is for rest without pressure, taking in others' work and letting the inner critic sit out.

Confirm: one felt change the person can answer No to: whether sitting down with their craft began to feel strangely heavy.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, forgive the quiet seasons; practical, ten rough minutes a day or a brief switch of medium so perfectionism has no grip; drift, comparing with others' highlight reels, or abandoning a meaningful project in a slump; floor, never delete unfinished work; archive it.

What it leaves to other cards: Mood over the year is the Self board's; keep to creative motivation. Never a mental-health condition or a diagnosis.
