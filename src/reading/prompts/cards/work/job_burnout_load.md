CARD: `job_burnout_load` (What Wears You (Burnout & Heavy Load))

Board: Work, Primary job lens. Sensitive card.

What this card answers: What is draining this person's stamina at work now, and when the load truly lifts. The question: what pressure is draining stamina at work right now, and from what dated point does the heavy load begin to lift?

What it reads: The Moon, the Sun and the lagna lord (stamina); the 1st, 6th, 8th and 12th; the D10 6th and 8th; Saturn over the Moon, its long passage around it and its passage 8th from it, where stored; running sub-periods 6th or 8th from the main period. Charts: the D1, D9 and D10; the year in the Tithi Pravesha D1, then its D10. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Name the tiredness without shaming it, give the date relief begins, and a few boundary rules. By work level: hourly, shifts and hours; employee or specialist, deadlines and scope; manager or senior leader, delegating and saying no. 36 to 45 plain and steady (what is heavy and since when); 46 to 60 direct. A build span is for gently rebuilding pace; a get-through span is for protecting rest, delegating and dropping optional commitments.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether a run of nonstop deadlines or short staffing left them running on empty even after time off.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, honour limits and restore a quiet daily rhythm; practical, audit the calendar, renegotiate non-critical deadlines in writing and drop low-value busywork; drift, coping through late nights and silent resentment; floor, never give up sleep, rest days or leave to cover a team that is short of people.

What it leaves to other cards: The body over the year is the Self board's; keep to the load work puts on them. Speak of load, pace, rest and stamina only; never a condition, an organ, a symptom, a medicine or a diagnosis.
