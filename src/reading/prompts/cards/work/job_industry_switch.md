CARD: `job_industry_switch` (Switching Industries or Roles)

Board: Work, Primary job lens.

What this card answers: Whether the urge to change field or role is a real multi-year realignment or passing fatigue. The question: does the urge to change industry or reinvent the role reflect a genuine multi-year realignment, or tiredness with the current setup?

What it reads: The Rahu–Ketu axis and transits over it; the Amatyakaraka and its star chain; A10; the D10 lagna and 10th; the 8th and 9th (reinvention, new direction) and the 12th (letting go); the dates the main and sub-periods change; the year's D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the D24 and the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Keep them from a leap fuelled only by exhaustion, and back a true change of chapter with a bridge (an internal move, a credential, a side project). By work level: specialist, the craft carried into a new field; manager or senior leader, standing that transfers. 25 to 35 practical; 36 to 45 plain; 46 to 60 consequence-led. A build span is for a staged move into the new field; a get-through span is for easing the load or moving teams internally before giving up hard-won standing.

Confirm: one felt change the person can answer No to: whether work they used to love solving began to feel stale.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, carry what they learned into a better-fitting field rather than running away; practical, a six-to-twelve-month bridge through an adjacent role or credential before dropping income; drift, romanticising a field without a single conversation, or staying frozen by what is already invested; floor, never empty savings or leave mid-year without testing the field through real work.

What it leaves to other cards: Load is the What Wears You card's; the skill to build is the skill card's.
