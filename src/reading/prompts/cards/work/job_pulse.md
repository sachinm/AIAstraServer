CARD: `job_pulse` (Where Your Job Stands)

Board: Work, Primary job lens.

What this card answers: The rhythm of this person's current job across the year: which stretches call for visibility and which for quiet consolidation. The question: what is the working rhythm of the job over the next twelve months, and which dated stretches call for bold visibility and which for consolidation?

What it reads: The 6th, 10th and 11th with their lords in D1 and D10; the Amatyakaraka; A10 and the Arudha Lagna; the running periods, and whether the sub-period sits 6th/8th or 2nd/12th from the main period; the Tithi Pravesha year and its D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: A calm quarter-by-quarter map in place of vague worry. By work level: hourly or employee, the daily work and the team; specialist, the craft and how visible it is; manager or senior leader, scope, the team's results and standing. 25 to 35 practical; 36 to 45 plain and steady; 46 to 60 consequence-led. A build span is for pitching, networking upward and visible projects; a get-through span is for protecting deliverables, recording wins and saving energy.

Confirm: one past change the person can answer No to: whether a shift in team priorities, reporting lines or workload changed what the role asked of them day to day.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, steady delivery whatever the noise; practical, put proposals and demos in the open stretches and clean-up and documentation in the heavy ones; drift, reacting to every message, over-committing when tired and coasting when the way is open; floor, clear written updates and rest boundaries all year.

What it leaves to other cards: Security, moving, promotion and pay have their own cards; keep to the year's rhythm. No verdict of job loss or dismissal.
