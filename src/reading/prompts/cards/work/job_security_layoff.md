CARD: `job_security_layoff` (Job Security & Restructuring Risk)

Board: Work, Primary job lens. Sensitive card.

What this card answers: How stable the ground under this person's role is over the year, and which stretches call for quiet readiness. The question: how stable is the organisation beneath the current role over the next twelve months, and which dated stretches call for calm contingency readiness?

What it reads: The D10 8th and 12th against its 6th, 10th and 11th; the 8th lord's link to the Amatyakaraka; the junctions between main and sub-periods; Saturn 8th from the Moon or in a quarter from it, where stored; the bindus of the 10th; the Tithi Pravesha year and its D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Take the dread out while respecting real pressure; turn it into calm, dated preparation (a cash cushion, an updated record of results, warm outside contacts). By work level: hourly or employee, the seat and the team; manager or senior leader, the function and its budget. 36 to 45 plain; 46 to 60 direct. A build span says the seat is durable whatever the headlines; a get-through span gives a readiness plan and the date the pressure eases.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether their company or department went through a reorganisation, leadership change or budget cut that moved roles around them.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, security rooted in transferable skill and reliability rather than one org chart; practical, months of savings, a quarterly record of results and warm outside contacts; drift, ignoring clear signals or living on rumours; floor, never confront leadership in anger or resign without an offer in a heavy stretch.

What it leaves to other cards: Whether to move is the stay-or-change card's. Never say the person will lose their job, be laid off or be dismissed; speak of pressure, readiness and when it eases. No legal or employment advice.
