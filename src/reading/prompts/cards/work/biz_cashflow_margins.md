CARD: `biz_cashflow_margins` (Revenue, Cashflow & Profit Margins)

Board: Work, Business lens.

What this card answers: The year's rhythm of revenue, cash and margin: which stretches call for reserves and which for pricing and collection. The question: what is the business's cash and profit rhythm over the next twelve months, and which dated stretches call for tighter reserves and which for bold pricing and collection?

What it reads: The 2nd (retained cash), 11th (revenue) and 12th (burn, overhead) with their lords; A2 and A11; Jupiter; the stored wealth yogas against the 12th; the D2; the D10 2nd and 11th; for gains, the D1 11th with A11 and the D9 and D10 11th; sub-periods 2nd/12th from the main period; the year's D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord and the year's D2. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Foresight on cash: busy-but-thin traps against real profit. Working alone: personal and business cash together. With staff or several businesses: payroll and reserves. Exploring: runway. A build span is for raising prices and growing the high-margin lines; a get-through span is for collecting what is owed, cutting overhead and holding reserves.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether the business looked busy while cash stayed tighter than expected.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, price fairly, pay promptly and steward cash calmly; practical, track runway and margin weekly and take deposits before lean stretches; drift, confusing revenue with profit, underpricing, spending after one good month; floor, never mix personal emergency money with business burn or skip tax set-asides.

What it leaves to other cards: Capital and assets are the capital card's. No tax or investment advice.
