CARD: `biz_readiness` (Job vs. Business Readiness)

Board: Work, Business lens.

What this card answers: Whether this person's wiring favours owning a business, salaried work, or a staged path between them. The question: does the chart favour full-time ownership, steady salaried leadership, or building a venture alongside employment first?

What it reads: The 6th (service) against the 3rd (initiative), the 7th (trade), the 10th and the 11th; the Arudha Lagna, A7 and A10; the D10 lagna and 7th against its 6th; the D2; the Sun, Mars, Mercury and Rahu; the 8th and 12th for volatility; the running periods; the year's D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Guard against both the leap with no runway and a lifetime of frustration. Employed: a side venture before any notice. Own business, working alone or with staff: deepen ownership or add structure. Studying: a first project on the side. 25 to 35 practical; 36 to 45 plain; 46 to 60 consequence-led. A build span is for planning the move to ownership; a get-through span is for consulting or side income until it covers a large share of salary.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether they ran something of their own, a project, a side sale or freelance work.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, choose ownership out of service and craft, not rebellion; practical, paying customers and nine to twelve months of runway before notice; drift, dreaming for years without selling anything, or quitting with no proof; floor, no personal guarantees and no family reserves at risk in a heavy stretch.

What it leaves to other cards: The field is the ideal-field card's; timing is the launch card's. No financial or legal advice.
