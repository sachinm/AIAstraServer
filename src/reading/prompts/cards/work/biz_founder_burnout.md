What this card answers: How this person keeps their energy through the year so running the business does not quietly wear them down, when it dips and when it returns. The question: how can stamina be protected over the next twelve months so the weight of the business does not drain it, and when does energy come back?

What it reads: The lagna lord, the Sun and the Moon (vitality, steadiness); the 1st, 6th, 8th and 12th; Saturn over the Moon, its long passage around it and its passage 8th from it, where stored; the D10 6th (workload); running sub-periods 6th or 8th from the main period. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: A pace that lasts for years. Working alone: they carry everything, so name what to drop. With staff or several businesses: hand fires to a strong second. 25 to 35 practical; 36 to 45 load and since when; 46 to 60 consolidate and hand over. A build span is for steady rhythms of rest, movement and time off; a get-through span is for protecting rest, delegating and no seven-day weeks.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, protect daily quiet, movement and family time; practical, treat recovery as a business measure, with backup in operations, a strong second and hard evening and weekend off-switches; drift, wearing long hours as a badge until forced to stop; floor, never give up core rest for minor fires in a heavy stretch.
