CARD: `job_leading_teams` (Leading Teams & Authority)

Board: Work, Primary job lens.

What this card answers: How this person's leadership lands on the people who report to them now, and where cleaner delegation would multiply results. The question: how does the leadership style land on direct reports right now, and where can delegation be cleaner?

What it reads: The D10 5th (followers), 6th (staff, service) and 10th (command); the Sun's directional strength; Jupiter (guidance), Saturn (accountability) and the Moon (care); A5 and A10; the running periods; the year's D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Balance warmth with clear accountability, without micromanaging. By work level: manager, a first-line team and one-to-ones; senior leader, leaders of leaders and the shape of the organisation. 36 to 45 plain; 46 to 60 successors and hand-over are fair words. A build span is for taking on more scope, developing rising people and setting high standards; a get-through span is for clear one-to-ones, delegating by checklist and no rescuing.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether letting a team member own an important piece of work paid off.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, lead through clarity, fairness and mentoring; practical, explicit ownership, weekly checkpoints and clear escalation points; drift, micromanaging, or dodging hard conversations until morale slips; floor, never blame reports upward for unclear direction, nor silently absorb every dropped ball.

What it leaves to other cards: Relations with those above are the bosses card's; keep to the team below.
