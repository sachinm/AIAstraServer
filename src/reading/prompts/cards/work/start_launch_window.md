CARD: `start_launch_window` (Your Career Launch Window)

Board: Work, Career start lens.

What this card answers: The next twelve months of starting a working life: which stretches open doors to applications and first traction, and which are for quiet readiness. The question: across the next twelve months, when does launching a career gain traction, and when does readiness pay more than forcing a break?

What it reads: The 5th and 9th lords (study finished, grace) and their link to the 6th (entry into service) and the 10th; the D10 lagna and 10th; A10; the Amatyakaraka; Jupiter and Saturn together over the 10th or its lord; the running periods; the Tithi Pravesha year and its D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the D24 and the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: A calm month-by-month calendar that takes the panic out of the first search. Studying: internships, final projects and the first applications. Working or between jobs: applying and showing early results. Under 25 short and encouraging; 25 to 35 practical. A build span is for outreach and flagship applications; a get-through span is for skills, proof of work and steady pacing.

Confirm: one past change the person can answer No to: whether a sudden shift in their study plan, an internship or a target date made them rethink what to pursue next.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, apply hardest in the open stretches and use the quiet ones to study and find mentors; practical, build proof-of-work projects in quiet stretches so there is something to show when doors open; drift, applying everywhere in slow stretches and tiring of rejection just before the good one; floor, keep money discipline and never burn a bridge with a teacher or early contact.

What it leaves to other cards: Interview timing is the first-role card's; keep to the year's arc and when it opens. Speak of chances and stretches; never promise an offer.
