CARD: `job_skill_building` (What Skill to Build Next)

Board: Work, Primary job lens.

What this card answers: Which skill, credential or capability gives the most leverage if built now, for the cycles ahead. The question: looking at the coming cycles, what skill, credential or capability offers the highest career leverage if built now?

What it reads: Mercury (skill) and Jupiter (learning); the 3rd, 5th and 9th with their lords, and the 10th; the Putrakaraka and the Amatyakaraka; Saraswati yoga where stored; the next main and sub-period lords. Read only if present in the shared data: the D24 and the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Stop collecting courses; tie learning to the next cycle. Employed: the skill for the next step in their field. Between jobs: the skill that makes them hireable for the next role, never a current workplace. 25 to 35 practical; 36 to 45 plain; 46 to 60 depth and what to pass on are fair words. A build span is for a targeted credential or programme; a get-through span is for short daily applied practice rather than heavy tuition.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether a skill they picked up on the side created real momentum in their work.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, learn the foundations with curiosity; practical, one scarce skill tied to the next pay step, used in a real project within ninety days; drift, hoarding unopened courses instead of visible work; floor, no heavy debt for a speculative degree in a lean stretch.

What it leaves to other cards: A change of field is the switching card's; learning for its own sake is the Self board's.
