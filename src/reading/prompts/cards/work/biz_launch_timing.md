CARD: `biz_launch_timing` (Timing Your Launch or Major Pivot)

Board: Work, Business lens.

What this card answers: Which stretches best support incorporating, a public launch or a major pivot, and which are for building quietly. The question: in which dated stretches are incorporating, launching publicly or a major pivot best supported?

What it reads: The 7th, 10th and 11th with their lords, in D1 and D10; A10 and A7; Mercury (contracts) and its stations, and Venus stations; Jupiter and Saturn together on the 10th or 11th; running sub-periods 6th/8th or 2nd/12th from the main period; the Tithi Pravesha year and its D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord and the year's D2. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: A founder's sequence: quiet building, setup and the public launch, each in its own stretch, so no stretch is spent waiting. Own business: a pivot or flagship product. Exploring: setting up and a first launch. 25 to 35 practical; 46 to 60 consequence-led. A build span is for launching, signing and announcing on the span's dates; a get-through span is for a private trial, building out of sight and preparing the paperwork.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether a rushed start under heavy pressure later needed costly rework.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, launch with substance and clear promises; practical, keep the quiet setup apart from the public go-live, and put go-live and signings in open stretches; drift, delaying past open stretches out of perfectionism, or rushing to an arbitrary date; floor, no founding documents or irreversible contracts without proper review.

What it leaves to other cards: Cashflow and capital have their own cards. No legal advice.
