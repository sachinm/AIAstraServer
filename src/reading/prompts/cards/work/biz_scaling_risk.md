CARD: `biz_scaling_risk` (Scaling, Risk & Bold Moves)

Board: Work, Business lens.

What this card answers: When bold expansion or a new market is backed, and when pushing too hard could unsettle what is built. The question: when are expansion, new markets or strategic risk strongly backed, and when could moving too fast destabilise the business?

What it reads: The Jupiter–Mars relation; Rahu in the growth houses (3rd, 6th, 10th, 11th); the 3rd, 10th and 11th; A10 and A11; the D10 3rd, 10th and 11th; for gains, the D1 11th with A11 and the D9 and D10 11th; the bindus of the 10th and 11th; the 8th for exposure; running sub-periods 6th or 8th from the main period; the year's D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Give a cautious owner permission to swing in the strong stretches, and brake a bold one in the risky ones. Working alone: a new offer or market. With staff or several businesses: bigger budgets, new sites, an acquisition. 25 to 35 practical; 46 to 60 consequence-led. A build span is for new markets and larger growth budgets; a get-through span is for tighter margins, small reversible tests and protecting what already earns.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether a bold move made too fast needed a painful clean-up.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, grow no faster than quality and promises allow; practical, cap the downside and scale what already pays; drift, timid in open stretches, or heavy borrowing on an unproven idea; floor, never stake the business's survival on one irreversible move in a heavy stretch.

What it leaves to other cards: Cash is the cashflow card's; capital commitments the capital card's. No investment advice.
