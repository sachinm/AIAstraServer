CARD: `biz_clients_sales` (Clients, Sales & Market Pull)

Board: Work, Business lens.

What this card answers: When demand and new clients pick up, and which positioning earns a buyer's trust. The question: when does demand and client acquisition accelerate over the next twelve months, and what approach builds the deepest buyer trust?

What it reads: The 7th (clients, market) and A7; A10 (reputation); the 3rd (outreach) and the 10th; Mercury and Venus and their stations; Jupiter's transit; the D10 3rd, 7th and 10th; the running periods; the year's D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: A sales rhythm: campaigns, launches and big pitches in the strong stretches. Working alone: their own selling. With staff or several businesses: the team's pipeline. Exploring: the first paying customers. A build span is for more outreach, demonstrations and closing; a get-through span is for looking after existing clients, earning referrals and sharpening the pitch.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether their best client came through a referral or word of mouth.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, sell from conviction and never oversell; practical, aim outreach at the ideal buyer in open stretches, with clear results and steady follow-ups; drift, redesigning the website instead of talking to buyers, or discounting in a panic; floor, never take a bad-fit client out of desperation.

What it leaves to other cards: Partners are the partnerships card's; cash is the cashflow card's.
