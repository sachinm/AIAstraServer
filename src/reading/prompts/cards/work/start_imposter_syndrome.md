CARD: `start_imposter_syndrome` (Overcoming Imposter Syndrome & Early Doubt)

Board: Work, Career start lens. Sensitive card.

What this card answers: What pressure makes this person's ability feel smaller than it is in these first years, and when their footing steadies. The question: what temporary pressure is amplifying self-doubt, and when does inner footing steady?

What it reads: The Moon (its house, its dispositor, and Kemadruma where stored), the Sun (confidence), Mercury (ability), the lagna and its lord, the 4th and 5th; Saturn over the Moon and its long passage around it where stored; running sub-periods 6th or 8th from the main period. Read only if present in the shared data: the D24. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Normalise the beginner's curve: the ability is real even while confidence is tested. Studying: exams, presentations and first critiques. Working: first reviews and new rooms. Under 25 short and kind; 25 to 35 practical. A build span is for visible work, asking questions and trusting preparation; a get-through span is for small wins written down and a gentler inner judge.

Confirm: one felt change the person can answer No to: whether minor critical feedback or a new room set off a wave of self-doubt bigger than the work deserved.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, anchor worth in daily learning, not instant perfection; practical, keep a log of problems solved and praise received, and ask early; drift, overworking in silence or putting off visible work to dodge judgment; floor, never quit, withdraw or turn down a stretch assignment just to escape beginner discomfort.

What it leaves to other cards: Mood over the year is the Self board's; keep to confidence in study and early work. Never a mental-health condition, a diagnosis or a treatment; where the cells are thin, say less.
