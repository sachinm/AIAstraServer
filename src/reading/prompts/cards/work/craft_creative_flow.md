CARD: `craft_creative_flow` (Your Creative Flow This Year)

Board: Work, Hobby lens.

What this card answers: The year's tide of inspiration and creative stamina: which stretches surge and which are quiet incubation. The question: what is the creative rhythm of the next twelve months, and in which dated stretches do inspiration, play and output peak?

What it reads: The 5th (creativity, play), the 3rd (hands, practice) and the 11th (sharing, fruit) with their lords; Venus, Mercury and the Moon; A5; Saraswati yoga where stored; the D9 5th and the D24; the running periods; the Tithi Pravesha year. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Make creative seasons normal: ride the surges, refill the well in the quiet stretches. A stated focus on creativity leads. Working: around the day's work. Studying: alongside study. Retired or 60 and over: unhurried, part of the day's rhythm. Under 25 light. A build span is for bold milestones, new drafts and protected making hours; a get-through span is for incubation: sketching, reading, wandering.

Confirm: one felt change the person can answer No to: whether ideas came unusually easily for a stretch.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, make from wonder and honour the fallow seasons; practical, block making hours in the peak stretches and batch admin into the quiet ones; drift, waiting for lightning, or forcing output until the joy sours; floor, keep rest, and never turn the hobby into self-punishment.

What it leaves to other cards: Which medium suits them is the craft card's; dry spells are the blocks card's.
