CARD: `job_stay_or_leave` (Should You Stay or Change Jobs?)

Board: Work, Primary job lens.

What this card answers: Whether now favours a move out or staying to consolidate, and which stretch makes the cleaner change. The question: is the chart aligned for an outside move now, or does staying to consolidate pay better until a cleaner change opens?

What it reads: The 3rd, 8th and 12th (leaving, starting over) against the 6th, 10th and 11th (a new seat, more pay), in D1 and D10; the D2 for the pay side; the Amatyakaraka and A10; Jupiter and Saturn together on the 10th or 11th; Mercury and Mars stations for the terms; the running periods; the year's D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Keep them from quitting in a passing rough patch, and name the stretch where a move truly lifts standing and pay. Employed: staying against moving. Between jobs: which offer to take and when the search lands, never a current employer. 25 to 35 practical; 36 to 45 plain; 46 to 60 consequence-led. A build span is for interviewing and negotiating an exit; a get-through span is for quietly updating materials and growing the network so the move lands in the stretch the card names.

Confirm: one felt change the person can answer No to: whether a strong restlessness to move on from their work came over them.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, leave cleanly with thanks and good hand-offs; practical, never resign just to escape, but on a signed offer that lands in an open stretch with clear pay upside; drift, talking about leaving for months without interviewing, or taking the first sideways escape; floor, never burn the notice period or miss notice, restraint or vesting dates.

What it leaves to other cards: Security risk is the security card's; a change of field is the switching card's. The choice is the person's; never a verdict that they will be pushed out.
