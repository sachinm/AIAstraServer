CARD: `start_offer_decision` (Choosing Between Two Paths or Offers)

Board: Work, Career start lens.

What this card answers: Which quality, stability, learning speed or upside, suits this person now when weighing two offers or two early paths. The question: when weighing competing offers or two early-career paths, which qualities fit the chart right now?

What it reads: The 6th (service) against the 3rd and 7th (own initiative, trade); the 10th and 11th in D1 and D10; the Amatyakaraka and its dispositor; the Arudha Lagna against the lagna (image against substance); the D2 for what pays; the 8th and 12th for volatility; the running periods and the year's D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: A clear way to compare without choosing for them: which path compounds over the next years and which wears them out early. Studying: two courses, a placement or a first offer. Working: two roles or employers. Under 25 light; 25 to 35 practical. A build span backs the faster-growing, more independent path where resilience holds; a get-through span backs the steadier option with mentoring and a cushion.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether they faced and settled a choice between two courses, internships or offers.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, choose where real skill and honest mentors grow, not prestige; practical, get learning scope, mentoring and pay steps in writing before signing; drift, choosing by peer comparison and second-guessing every hard week; floor, never take an offer with unclear duties or a draining pace just to end the discomfort of deciding.

What it leaves to other cards: Offer timing is the first-role card's. Never decide for the person; set out the trade-off.
