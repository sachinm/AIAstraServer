CARD: `start_first_role_timing` (Landing Your First Role)

Board: Work, Career start lens.

What this card answers: When interviews, callbacks and offers come most easily, and how to pace the search between them. The question: during which stretches are interviews, callbacks and formal offers best supported, and how should the search be paced in between?

What it reads: The 6th (competing and being chosen), the 10th (appointment) and the 11th (first pay) with their lords, in D1 and D10; A6, A10 and A11; the Amatyakaraka; the running sub-periods that touch the 6th, 10th or 11th; Jupiter and Saturn together over the 10th or its lord; the Tithi Pravesha year and its D10 chart. Read only if present in the shared data: the year lord. Read them through this card's ledger cells and matter rows; the frame's tone per span is given.

For the person: Turn luck into a hiring calendar. Studying: campus hiring, internships and graduate schemes. Between jobs: the search itself, never a current workplace. Under 25 short and steady; 25 to 35 practical. A build span is for referrals, outreach and final rounds; a get-through span is for warm referrals over cold portals, practice interviews and sharper applications.

Confirm: one past event the person can answer No to: whether a promising interview or application stalled over timing, a hiring freeze or a delay outside their control.

Sections: the default four. Ladder, when on: best, prepare deeply and lean on trusted referrals in the open stretches; practical, track applications like a funnel and time follow-ups to the open weeks; drift, one-click applying only and crashing after one rejection; floor, keep the daily routine, rest and follow-up manners intact.

What it leaves to other cards: The year's arc is the launch-window card's; choosing between offers is the offers card's. Never promise an offer or a hiring date.
