LENS RECIPE: `self.body` (Self board: body and energy, fitness, sleep, stamina, the five layers)
Cards: `body`, `fitness`, `sleep`, `blood`, `layers`.

Read this before the card brief. It fixes the order of the story for every card in this lens. Use the charts, sign
dasha, year charts and points that this card's ROUTE line names. This lens is about pace and energy. It keeps out every
illness and longevity method: Rath's whole health method is sensitive and waits on the owner's decision
(sanjay-rath/10 §1; sanjay-rath/14 row 64). Facts about energy and rest are read; disease is never named.

SYSTEMS: read, name and count only these, and only where the data marks them as applying: Vimshottari (every level);

PURPOSE QUESTION
How should this person pace their body through this chapter: when to push, when to hold back and rest, and what keeps
them steady?

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4). Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - For this lens, ask whether the maha dasha lord is, joins or aspects the lagna, the lagna lord, the Sun or (for
     `fitness`) Mars. The Sun stands for vitality. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). The paka lagna (the sign the
     lagna lord occupies) is the physical self; the lagna lord's sign lord carries bodily matters. [Student] (02 §3);
     [Rath] (sanjay-rath/07 rules 1.8, 1.9).
   - Narayana D1 maha dasha: the sign, its occupants, its lord, its place from the lagna. [Inferred] from [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/04 §7). It shows the outer pressure on the routine, never a health event.

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 is a heavier stretch, kendra, trikona, 3rd,
     11th a lighter one; the house names the stretch. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9). Word a heavy stretch as load and pace, never as illness.

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.

     read alone. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna, the year lagna lord, the hora lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by
     weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the 1st and its lord and the Sun in the year D1 (the 12th and 4th for `sleep`). No year divisional chart is
     routed for the body: gap (docs/techniques/07 §4.2 routes the year D1 only).
   - Patyayini places the stretch inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how the energy felt, as a confirm question. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.

   - `layers`: the lagna and Sun (body), the 4th (breath and ease), the Moon (mind), Mercury (judgment), Jupiter with
