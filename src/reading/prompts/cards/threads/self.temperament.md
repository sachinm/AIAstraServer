LENS RECIPE: `self.temperament` (Self board: who you are, how you come across, your strengths and sticking points)
Cards: `lagna`, `presence`, `yogas`.

Read this before the card brief. It fixes the order of the story for every card in this lens. Use the charts, sign
dasha, year charts and points that this card's ROUTE line names.

Source tags: [Rath] Sanjay Rath's own text · [Student] his students (P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, Sarajit Poddar) · [Text] a
classical verse · [Inferred] assembled by the research, stated by no one · gap: no source gives this rule. Paths are
research files under docs/deep-research/. Carry the tag into your evidence; never call an [Inferred] step or a gap
Rath's rule.

SYSTEMS: read, name and count only these, and only where the data marks them as applying: Vimshottari (every level);
Narayana at the maha dasha level only (its sub-periods are not verified); Lagna Kendradi (low weight, never decisive);
the one conditional dasha the data marks as applying (it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari); Patyayini (inside the
year); the Tithi Pravesha year chart. Sudasa is not read on this lens. Never read, name or count: Yogini, Chara, Drig,
Trikona, Mandooka, Tara, Naisargika, Mudda, any longevity dasha (Shoola, Sthira, Brahma, Niryana Shoola, Navamsa
dasha, Aayu), or a conditional dasha that does not apply. Never name the software that computed the data.

PURPOSE QUESTION
Who is this person underneath, how do they meet the world, how do others read them, and what does the running chapter
draw out of that nature?

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Vimshottari maha dasha lord, judged from the lagna: the houses it owns, the house it sits in, its dignity, its
     navamsa sign (vargottama is strong; a friendly sign with an enemy or debilitated navamsa is mixed) and its links
     (conjunctions, aspects). [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §1, §4).
   - Then its star lord: the house and condition of the lord of the nakshatra it occupies. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23-nakshatra-natal-reading.md §5).
   - For this lens, ask whether the maha dasha lord is, joins or aspects the lagna, the lagna lord, the AL or the AK.
     Vimshottari times the person's own experience and reaction. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §1). The AK's own Vimshottari
     dasha often brings hardship; a Narayana dasha of the AK's sign, or of a sign it aspects, can bring achievement.
     [Rath] (sanjay-rath/07 §1 "Timing method").
   - Narayana D1 maha dasha (sign dasha): judge the sign itself, its occupants, its lord, and its place from the lagna
     and from the AL. [Inferred] from [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §7; 08 §1 T4). Narayana shows the environment, Vimshottari
     the mind. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §1).

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6th, 8th or 12th strains, a kendra, trikona, 3rd or
     11th works, and the house it falls in from the maha dasha lord names what the stretch is about. [Text]
     Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79 (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6th, 8th or 12th from
     the dasha lord act against it; the 7th from it is adverse. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/02 §9).
   - Then the sub-period lord's own condition from the lagna, as in step 1. [Text] (same file §4).

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - The year chart is cast when the Sun is in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle from the Sun, for the
     birthplace. [Rath, co-credited with Rao] (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). It is never read alone; the birth chart overrides
     it. [Student] Poddar (sanjay-rath/05 §2).
   - Year lagna counted from the birth lagna: a kendra eases, a trikona gains, a dusthana obstructs; then the year
     lagna lord; then the hora lord as the year's focus. [Student] Poddar (sanjay-rath/05 §1.3). The year lord is the
     weekday lord: gap, a house rule decided 2026-11-09; the sources dispute it (sanjay-rath/05 §1.2).
   - Then this lens's houses in the year D1 (the 1st, the year AL), then the year D9. Which houses to read in a year
     chart is a gap; the order D1 then the card's chart is [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 2).
   - Inside the year, Patyayini places the stretch. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how it felt, put as a question the person can confirm; never a prediction. [Inferred]
     (sanjay-rath/05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The Moon is the mind: how the person feels their own nature. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Read the maha and
     sub-period lords' houses counted from the Moon's sign (read them from the signs; nothing is counted for you).
   - Rath gives no temperament rule from the Moon sign or its nakshatra on its own: gap (sanjay-rath/07 §1 "Gaps"). Use
     the Moon only for how the nature is felt, never as a second character sketch.

5. D9 and the card's own chart.
   - Temperament blends the D1 and D9 lagnas; the D9 dominates; planets in the lagna modify both. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/07 rule 1.5). Each lagna sign has a sketch. [Rath] (07 rule 1.4).
   - The lagna lord by house, each result conditional on its strength. [Rath] (07 rule 1.6). The lagna lord's star lord
     carries the mind, its sign lord the body; the paka lagna is the sign the lagna lord occupies. [Rath] (07 rules
     1.8, 1.9).
   - The Sun's and the AK's unobstructed argala on the lagna. [Rath] (07 rules 1.2, 1.3). The AK's lesson by planet.
     [Rath] (07 rule 1.11). Count from the AK to the D9 lagna; that house's results lead. [Rath] (07 rule 1.13).
   - `presence`: the AL is perception and status. [Student]; AL for status also [Rath] (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Lagna
     against AL is reality against appearance, not a contest to settle. [Inferred] (02 §3).
   - `lagna`: the Sree Lagna is the prosperity lagna. [Student] (02 §3).
   - `yogas`: note the yoga, then the planet that cancels it. [Rath] (02 §2.4). A raja yoga must also show in the D5 and
     the D10. [Rath] (02 §8). Yogas in the 2nd or 4th act early, in the lagna late. [Rath] (02 §8). A raja yoga given
     in Rahu dasha does not last. [Rath] (02 §8). A planet linked to the lagna and the HL (wealth) or the GL (power) is
     a yogada and brings luck whatever its functional nature. [Rath] (02 §9; 08 rule 2.8).

6. The other verified systems, and where they overlap.
   - Vimshottari pinpoints; a sign dasha (Narayana) must confirm. [Rath] Crux p.3 (sanjay-rath/04 §7). Narayana and
     Vimshottari never truly conflict; a gap between them is the reader's limit. [Rath] (04 §7).
   - Lagna Kendradi: material fortune, low weight. [Student] (04 §1). The conditional dasha that applies is read only when its condition holds [Rath] (04 §4);
     it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari. [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step 2).
   - Name the dates where two or more of these point to the lagna, its lord, the AL or the AK. No source requires a
     number of agreeing systems; the count is support, not proof. [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 3).

7. The trigger.
   - No Rath transit rule for temperament: gap (sanjay-rath/05 §4).
   - The classical dasha-transit gate only: the sub-period lord transiting its own or exalted sign releases, its
     debilitation or an enemy sign blocks; the Sun entering the sub-period lord's exaltation sign dates the good.
     [Text] Phaladeepika 20.37-38 (dasha-results/dasha-transit-combination.md §1).

8. The verdict.
   - Nature is not timed. The verdict is what this chapter brings out of the nature, from when to when, and what would
     change the reading. Use the one gate: confirmed, leaning, open, contested, not promised. [Inferred] from [Rath]
     (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step 3; sanjay-rath/04 §7).
