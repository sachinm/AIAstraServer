PURPOSE QUESTION
Where does this person's job stand in the running chapter: how secure, how rewarded, how heavy, whether to stay or
move, and when each stretch turns?

Order of judgment [Inferred] from Rath's tenth-house chapter (sanjay-rath/08 §1): the rasi 10th, its lord, karakas
and bindus; argala on the 10th; the paka lagna and AL screen; the 10th from the Moon and the HL; the navamsa layer;
unemployment and ill-fame checks; the AL, A10 and A7; the AmK; the D10 as a full chart; service against business;
timing.

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4). Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - Then the same lord in the D10: the 5th lord brings openings, the 8th marks exit or retirement, the 7th lord
     unsettles service, the 6th lord in the 5th cuts authority, the 12th marks intrigue or loss. [Rath] (08 §1 T1). The
     11th lord's Vimshottari dasha is generally unfavourable for money. [Rath] (08 §2 W3).
   - Sign dasha: the route names Narayana D10, which starts from the sign the D1 10th lord occupies in the D10 [Rath]
     rule (sanjay-rath/04 §2), D10 application [Inferred] (06 rule 4.6). It is not cast yet: read the Narayana D1 maha
     dasha sign and say the timing comes from Narayana D1. Narayana signs tied to the AL or the A10 time titles. [Rath]
     (08 §1 T4).

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 strain, kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th work; the
     house names the stretch; 2/12 from it touches money. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9).
   - The 10th lord's navamsa dispositor, if malefic and hostile, spoils the 10th lord's dasha, above all in its own
     antardasha. [Rath] (08 rule 1.7).

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - Cast as the Sun in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle. [Rath, co-credited] (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). Never
     read alone. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna, the year lagna lord, the hora lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by
     weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the card's houses (10th, 6th, 11th, 2nd, 8th, 12th) in the year D1, then the year D10 (and the year D2 on
     `job_salary_bonuses`, the year D24 on `job_skill_building`). Houses to read: gap; order [Inferred]
     (docs/techniques/07 decision 2).
   - Patyayini places the stretch inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how the job felt, as a confirm question. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The 10th from the Moon (Mars there: work far away). [Rath] (08 rule 1.5). From the Moon, a planet in the 10th is
     mental activity in the profession; from the AL, professional status. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Read the
     running lords' houses from the Moon's sign.

5. D9 and the card's own chart (D10) and the points.
   - D9: planets in the 10th from the D9 lagna or the Karakamsa (malefics fluctuate, benefics steady). [Rath] (08 rule
     1.6). The 10th lord's navamsa sign (fixed a desk, movable on the move). [Rath] (08 rule 1.8).
   - D10 as a full chart: exaltation or debilitation both show success. [Rath] (08 rule 1.19). A raja yoga must also
     show in the D10 (and the D5). [Rath] (08 rule 1.17). Service (D10 6th) against business (D10 7th). [Rath] (08
     rule 1.20). The 10th from the D10 lagna is true conduct, the A10 in the D10 the workplace, the A5 in the D10
     following and awards. [Student] Rao (08 rule 1.22).
   - The A10 rules titles; the AL is the visible career. [Rath] (08 rule 1.10). If the 10th or its lord falls in the
     badhaka sign from the paka lagna or the AL the career is obstructed. [Rath] (08 rule 1.4).
   - `job_security_layoff`: Saturn with the 10th or its lord, debilitated planets in the 10th, malefics in the navamsa
     10th point to a break in work. [Rath] (08 rule 1.9). Say "a stretch to watch" with dates, never "you will be laid
     off".
   - `job_promotion_recognition`: a debilitated planet in an exalted navamsa gives a sudden promotion, short-lived if
     retrograde. [Rath] (08 §1 T6). The GL is power and promotion. [Student] (02 §3).
   - `job_salary_bonuses`, `job_stay_or_leave`: the 2nd and Jupiter, the HL; the 11th from the AL names the sources,
     the 12th from the AL the drains. [Rath] (08 rules 2.1, 2.6, 2.7). The D2, tagged as a disputed chart. [Rath] (06
     rule 1.9).
   - `job_boss_politics`, `job_leading_teams`: the D10 9th is the boss, the 5th subordinates and authority, the 6th
     rivals and service. [Rath] (08 rule 1.20). Keep the manager, the sponsor, the peers and the team apart.
   - `job_remote_abroad`: the 12th decides, then the 7th and 9th; Venus and the 12th lord. [Rath] (10 §4).
   - `job_skill_building`: the 4th, 5th and 9th confirmed in the D24. [Rath] (10 §2). `job_industry_switch`: the AmK in
     cases only. [Rath] cases (08 rule 1.11).
   - `job_burnout_load`: the 6th, 8th and 12th from the lagna and the sub-period's 6/8 relation as load, never illness.

6. The other verified systems, and where they overlap.
   - Vimshottari pinpoints; Narayana must confirm. [Rath] Crux p.3 (sanjay-rath/04 §7). Lagna Kendradi: material
     fortune, low weight. [Student] (04 §1). The conditional dasha that applies is read only when its condition holds [Rath] (04 §4); it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari. [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step 2).
   - Pay cards: Sudasa [Rath] role, calculation [Student] (08 §2 W1, W2); a Sudasa sign holding or aspecting the HL
     brings wealth, the GL power [Student] (08 W2); check Narayana with Sudasa [Student] Rao (04 §7). Never "Sudasa when
     the Moon sign is stronger": withdrawn (04 §8).
   - Name the dates where two or more point to the 10th, the card's D10 house, the A10 or the AL. No required count.
     [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 3).

7. The trigger.
   - Jupiter over the AL brings a win; Saturn over the A10 a loss of title; Saturn aspecting the AL strains the image.
     [Rath] (08 §1 T5).
   - Jupiter and Saturn together on the 10th or 11th is modern practice: gap (dasha-results/dasha-transit-combination.md
     §1). Saturn's passage over the Moon is not Rath's: gap (sanjay-rath/05 "Use in the product").
   - The classical gate: the sub-period lord's transit and the Sun's entry into its exaltation. [Text] Phaladeepika
     20.37-38 (same file).

8. The verdict.
   - The gate: confirmed, leaning, open, contested, not promised. [Inferred] from [Rath] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step
     3). A stay-or-leave verdict is a window and a condition, never an order.
