LENS RECIPE: `self.purpose` (Self board: purpose and meaning, drive and ambition)
Cards: `purpose`, `drive`.

Read this before the card brief. It fixes the order of the story for every card in this lens. Use the charts, sign
dasha, year charts and points that this card's ROUTE line names (D20 for `purpose`, D10 for `drive`).

Source tags: [Rath] Sanjay Rath's own text · [Student] his students (P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, Sarajit Poddar) · [Text] a
classical verse · [Inferred] assembled by the research, stated by no one · gap: no source gives this rule. Paths are
research files under docs/deep-research/. Carry the tag into your evidence; never call an [Inferred] step or a gap
Rath's rule.

SYSTEMS: read, name and count only these, and only where the data marks them as applying: Vimshottari (every level);
Narayana at the maha dasha level only (its sub-periods are not verified); Lagna Kendradi (low weight, never decisive);
the one conditional dasha the data marks as applying (it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari); Patyayini (inside the
year); the Tithi Pravesha year chart. Sudasa is not read on this lens. Never read, name or count: Yogini, Chara, Drig,
Trikona, Mandooka, Tara, Naisargika, Mudda, any longevity dasha (Shoola, Sthira, Brahma, Niryana Shoola, Navamsa
dasha, Aayu), or a conditional dasha that does not apply. Never name the software that computed the data.
Rath's own clock for spiritual matters is Drig dasha, read before a D20 Narayana [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §1); it is not
in the verified set: do not read it and do not name it.

PURPOSE QUESTION
What is this life for, what drives this person towards it, and what does the running chapter ask of that aim?

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4). Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - For this lens, ask whether the maha dasha lord is the AK, the 9th lord, Jupiter, or (for `drive`) the AmK or the
     10th lord. The AK's own Vimshottari dasha often brings hardship, above all a malefic AK; the Narayana dasha of the
     AK's sign, or of a sign it aspects, can bring major achievement. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/07 §1 "Timing method").
   - Narayana D1 maha dasha: the sign, its occupants, its lord, its place from the lagna and the AL. [Inferred] from
     [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §7). A D20 Narayana would start from the sign the D1 8th lord occupies in the D20 (Table 29
     names the 8th as the D20's controlling house). [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §2). It is not cast yet: say the timing
     comes from Narayana D1.

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 strain, kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th work, and
     the house names the stretch. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9). Then its own condition from the lagna. [Text].

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - Cast as the Sun in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle. [Rath, co-credited] (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). Never
     read alone. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna, the year lagna lord, the hora lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by
     weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the 9th (and for `drive` the 10th) in the year D1, then the year D20 (`purpose`) or year D10 (`drive`). Houses
     to read in a year chart: gap; order [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 2).
   - Patyayini places the stretch inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line, as a confirm question. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The Moon carries ambition and how one views one's work. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). The 10th from the Moon
     shows mental activity in the profession. [Student] (02 §3); the 10th from the Moon in the career chapter is
     [Rath] (sanjay-rath/08 rule 1.5). Read the running lords' houses from the Moon's sign.

5. D9 and the card's own chart.
   - The AK and the Karakamsa (the AK's sign in the D9), read as a lagna, the seat of the soul. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/07 rule 1.18). The AK's lesson by planet. [Rath] (07 rule 1.11). A malefic AK suggests higher
     spiritual development, a retrograde AK a deep-rooted desire, a combust AK insight. [Rath] (07 rule 1.12). The count
     from the AK to the D9 lagna: its house leads. [Rath] (07 rule 1.13).
   - The 9th lord is the divine in the chart and the protector. [Rath] (07 rules 2.1, 2.3). Planets in the 9th from the
     AL bring fortune and protection. [Rath] (07 rule 2.2). Planets in the 9th from the D9 lagna. [Rath] (07 rule 2.6).
     The 12th from the Karakamsa is liberation. [Student] (sanjay-rath/02 §3).
   - `purpose`: in the D20 the 9th is the strongest trine; its lords and the Moon shape devotion; the 5th lord links to
     prayer. [Rath] (07 rule 3.12). D20 casting variant and house scheme: gap (07 §3 "Gaps").
   - `drive`: the AmK shapes the profession in Rath's worked charts, with no standalone rule. [Rath] cases
     (sanjay-rath/08 rule 1.11). The D10 decides success and is read like a rasi chart. [Rath] (08 rule 1.16). The
     years of fortune per planet (Saturn 35-36, Jupiter 33-34 ...) as a note when the person's age is within one year.
     [Rath], to be checked in print (07 §2 "Timing method").

6. The other verified systems, and where they overlap.
   - Vimshottari pinpoints; Narayana must confirm. [Rath] Crux p.3 (sanjay-rath/04 §7). Lagna Kendradi low weight.
     [Student] (04 §1). The conditional dasha that applies is read only when its condition holds [Rath] (04 §4); it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari. [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step 2).
   - Name the dates where two or more point to the AK, the 9th lord, the Karakamsa or (for `drive`) the AmK and the
     10th. No required count. [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 3).

7. The trigger.
   - `purpose`: Ketu's transit through the trines to the lagna or the AL marks an inward turn. [Rath] (07 §3 "Timing
     method"). Say "turning inward", never renunciation of family or work.
   - `drive`: Jupiter over the AL brings a win; Saturn over the A10 a loss of title; Saturn aspecting the AL strains the
     image. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/08 §1 T5).
   - Otherwise the classical gate: sub-period lord's transit and the Sun's entry into its exaltation. [Text]
     Phaladeepika 20.37-38 (dasha-results/dasha-transit-combination.md §1).

8. The verdict.
   - The gate: confirmed, leaning, open, contested, not promised. [Inferred] from [Rath] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step
     3). Purpose itself is not timed; the verdict is what this chapter asks of it, with dates.
