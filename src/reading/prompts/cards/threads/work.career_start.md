PURPOSE QUESTION
When does this person's working life open, what first role and direction suit them, and what should they build in the
first years?

Order of judgment [Inferred] from Rath's tenth-house chapter (sanjay-rath/08 §1): the rasi 10th, its lord and karakas;
argala on the 10th; the paka lagna and AL screen; the 10th from the Moon; the navamsa layer; the AL, A10 and A7; the
AmK (cases only); the D10 as a full chart; then timing.

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4). Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - Then the same lord in the D10: is it the D10 5th lord (openings), the 10th lord, or the 6th lord (service)? [Rath]
     (08 §1 T1). Raja yogas formed in the 2nd, 4th, 10th or 8th act early in life, in the 1st, 7th, 3rd or 9th late.
     [Rath] (08 §1 T2).
   - Sign dasha: the route names Narayana D10, which starts from the sign the D1 10th lord occupies in the D10 [Rath]
     rule (sanjay-rath/04 §2), D10 application [Inferred] (06 rule 4.6). It is not cast yet: read the Narayana D1 maha
     dasha sign (occupants, lord, place from the AL and the A10) and say the timing comes from Narayana D1.

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 strain, kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th work; the
     house names the stretch. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9). The 10th lord's navamsa dispositor, if malefic and hostile, spoils the 10th lord's dasha in
     its own antardasha. [Rath] (08 rule 1.7).

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - Cast as the Sun in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle. [Rath, co-credited] (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). Never
     read alone. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna, the year lagna lord, the hora lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by
     weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the 10th, 6th and 11th in the year D1, then the year D10. Houses to read: gap; order [Inferred]
     (docs/techniques/07 decision 2).
   - Patyayini places the stretch inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how the search or study felt, as a confirm question. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The 10th from the Moon: Mars there points to work far away. [Rath] (08 rule 1.5). The Moon carries ambition and
     how one views one's work. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Read the running lords' houses from the Moon's sign.

5. D9 and the card's own chart (D10) and the points.
   - D9: planets in the 10th from the D9 lagna or the Karakamsa (malefics fluctuate, benefics steady). [Rath] (08 rule
     1.6). The 10th lord's navamsa sign: fixed a desk, movable on the move, dual mixed. [Rath] (08 rule 1.8).
   - D10: field by the dominant D10 planet (Sun politics, Moon government or hotels, Mars police or army, Jupiter law
     or teaching, Mercury trade and writing, Venus the arts, Saturn heavy work, Rahu imports, Ketu exports); "dominant"
     has no stated measure. [Rath] (08 rule 1.18). Exaltation or debilitation in the D10 both show success. [Rath] (08
     rule 1.19). Service (D10 6th) against business (D10 7th), the stronger decides. [Rath] (08 rule 1.20).
   - The A10 rules titles; the AL's occupants name the field. [Rath] (08 rule 1.10). The AmK shapes the profession in
     worked charts only. [Rath] cases (08 rule 1.11).
   - `start_launch_window`: the 4th schooling, 5th knowledge, 9th higher study, confirmed in the D24. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/10 §2).
   - `start_offer_decision`: the D2 for what pays, tagged as a disputed chart. [Rath] (06 rule 1.9).
   - `start_relocation_abroad`: the 12th decides, then the 7th and 9th; Venus and the 12th lord strong; the 4th and 8th
     as trines to the 12th. [Rath] printed order (10 §4). Mars in the 10th from the Moon: livelihood abroad. [Rath] (10
     rule 4.4). The D4 for going abroad. [Student] Rao (10 rule 3.9).
   - `start_imposter_syndrome`: the Moon, the Sun and Mercury from the lagna; the mind through the lagna lord's star
     lord. [Rath] (07 rule 1.8). Doubt is described as a stage, never as a condition.

7. The trigger.
