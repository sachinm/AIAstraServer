PURPOSE QUESTION
How does this person think, judge, feel and learn, and what does the running chapter do to their mind: when is it
quick, when heavy, what eases it, and when does learning stick?

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Vimshottari is the dasha of the mind and the person's experience. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §1); it times the state
     of mind counted from the Moon. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/07 §1 "Timing method").
   - Judge the maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links. [Text] BPHS
     47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19 (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4).
   - Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - The mind is read through the lagna lord's nakshatra lord. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/07 rule 1.8). Say whether the maha
     dasha lord is that planet, the Moon, Mercury, or the lord of the 4th or 5th.
   - Narayana D1 maha dasha: the sign, its occupants, its lord, its place from the lagna and the AL; it is the
     environment around the mind. [Inferred] from [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §1, §7).

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6th, 8th, 12th strain; kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th
     work; the house it falls in names the stretch. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9).
   - Then its own condition from the lagna. [Text]. The Moon judges the antardasha in Rath's "tripod". [Student],
     attributed to Rath by Rao (sanjay-rath/04 §3).

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.

     read alone; the birth chart overrides. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna (kendra eases, trikona gains, dusthana obstructs), the year lagna lord, the hora
     lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the 4th and 5th (and the 12th for `mood`, `stuck`, `restores`) in the year D1, then the year chart the route
     names (D24 or D16). Houses to read in a year chart: gap; the order is [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 2).

   - Last year: one line on how it felt, as a confirm question; never a prediction. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
