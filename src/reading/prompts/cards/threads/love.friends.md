LENS RECIPE: `love.friends` (Love board, Friends lens)
Cards: `friends_social_energy`, `friends_core_allies`, `friends_outgrowing_circles`, `friends_finding_tribe`,
`friends_boundaries_drains`, `friends_mentors_guides`, `friends_reputation_belonging`.

Read this before the card brief. It fixes the order of the story for every card in this lens. Use the charts, sign
dasha, year charts and points that this card's ROUTE line names: the 11th from the lagna and from the AL, in the D1
and the D9.

THE TEXTS GIVE NO RULE FOR FRIENDSHIP. Rath is silent on friends; the classical list for the 11th is gains, elder
siblings and the fulfilment of desires, with no friends in it (natal-techniques/02 §4); the 11th from the AL is income
and elder siblings in Rath (sanjay-rath/08 rule 2.6; 09 rule 5.2). Say so in the story where it matters, and do not
fill the gap with invented rules. What you can read is the general dasha machinery below, applied to the 11th from the
lagna and from the AL; tag every friendship meaning "gap".

Never read the A5 for friends, from the AL or anywhere: no source gives it, and the A5 is the mantra pada [Rath]
(sanjay-rath/09 rule 1.8). The verse that tests a pada against the AL (BPHS 29.35) is about amity between padas, not
friendship (jaimini-apparatus/arudha-padas.md). Never read the D11: Rath names no area for it, and its D11 use is
destruction (sanjay-rath/06 rule 1.2; 15 #23).

Source tags: [Rath] Sanjay Rath's own text · [Student] his students (P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, Sarajit Poddar) · [Text] a
classical verse · [Inferred] assembled by the research, stated by no one · gap: no source gives this rule. Paths are
research files under docs/deep-research/. Carry the tag into your evidence; never call an [Inferred] step or a gap
Rath's rule.

SYSTEMS: read, name and count only these, and only where the data marks them as applying: Vimshottari (every level);
Narayana at the maha dasha level only (its sub-periods are not verified); Lagna Kendradi (low weight, never decisive);
the one conditional dasha the data marks as applying (it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari); Patyayini (inside the
year); the Tithi Pravesha year chart. Sudasa is not read on this lens. Never read, name or count: Yogini, Chara, Drig,
Trikona, Mandooka, Tara, Naisargika, Mudda, any longevity dasha (Shoola, Sthira, Brahma, Niryana Shoola, Navamsa
dasha, Aayu), or a conditional dasha that does not apply. Never name the software that computed the data.

PURPOSE QUESTION
Who carries this person, which circles fit them, when do people come in or fall away, and where should they hold a
line?

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4). Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - Ask whether it owns or sits in the 11th, or the 11th from the AL, or joins Jupiter (the 11th's karaka [Text],
     natal-techniques/02 §4). What that means for friendship: gap.
   - Narayana D1 maha dasha: the sign, its occupants, its lord, its place from the lagna and the AL. [Inferred] from
     [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §7).

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 strain, kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th work; the
     house names the stretch. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9).

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - Cast as the Sun in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle. [Rath, co-credited] (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). Never
     read alone. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna, the year lagna lord, the hora lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by
     weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the 11th (and the 12th for `friends_social_energy`, `friends_boundaries_drains`) in the year D1. No year
     divisional chart is routed: gap (docs/techniques/07 §4.2).
   - Patyayini places the stretch inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how the circle felt, as a confirm question. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The Moon is the mind: the wish for company or room. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Read the running lords'
     houses from the Moon's sign. Friendship meaning: gap.

5. D9 (this lens's own chart) and the points.
   - The D9 11th and its lord, for whether the circle holds up. gap: no source for friends in the D9.
   - The AL is how the person is perceived. [Student]; AL for status [Rath] (sanjay-rath/02 §3). The 11th from the AL

     (sanjay-rath/10 rule 5.3). Use it only where the 6th lord is actually there.

6. The other verified systems, and where they overlap.
   - Vimshottari pinpoints; Narayana must confirm. [Rath] Crux p.3 (sanjay-rath/04 §7). Lagna Kendradi low weight.
     [Student] (04 §1). The conditional dasha that applies confirms only. [Rath] (04 §4).
   - Name the dates where two or more point to the 11th, its lord or the 11th from the AL. No required count. [Inferred]

7. The trigger.
   - No source gives a friendship trigger: gap. The classical gate is the only one: the sub-period lord's transit and
     the Sun's entry into its exaltation. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.37-38 (dasha-results/dasha-transit-combination.md §1).

8. The verdict.
   - Leaning at most: "the signs lean toward" a fuller or quieter circle in a stretch. Never "you will meet your friend

     step 3); with no friendship rule behind it, a friends card is never "confirmed".
