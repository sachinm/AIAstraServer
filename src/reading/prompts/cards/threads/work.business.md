PURPOSE QUESTION
Does a business of their own suit this person, in which field, when to launch, grow or hold, and how do money,
partners, clients and team move in the running chapter?

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4). Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - Then the same lord in the D10: the 5th lord brings openings, the 7th lord unsettles service, the 8th marks exit,
     the 12th intrigue, loss or sales. [Rath] (08 §1 T1). A planet linked to the lagna and the HL is the dhana yogada
     and brings luck whatever its functional nature. [Rath] (08 rule 2.8).
   - Sign dasha: the route names Narayana D10, which starts from the sign the D1 10th lord occupies in the D10 [Rath]
     rule (sanjay-rath/04 §2), D10 application [Inferred] (06 rule 4.6). It is not cast yet: read the Narayana D1 maha
     dasha sign (occupants, lord, place from the AL, the A7 and the A10) and say the timing comes from Narayana D1.
     Narayana dasha times changes in life. [Rath] (08 §2 W3).

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 strain, kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th work; the
     house names the stretch; 2/12 from it touches money. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9). Kendra lord's dasha with a trikona lord's sub-period, and the reverse, is good. [Text]
     Phaladeepika 20.42 (same file §4).

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - Cast as the Sun in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle. [Rath, co-credited] (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). Never
     read alone. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna, the year lagna lord, the hora lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by
     weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the 7th, 10th, 11th, 2nd in the year D1, then the year D10 (and the year D2 or D4 where the route names it).
     Houses to read: gap; order [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 2).
   - Patyayini places the stretch inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how the business felt, as a confirm question. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The 10th from the Moon. [Rath] (08 rule 1.5). The Moon carries ambition and how one views one's work. [Student]
     Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Read the running lords' houses from the Moon's sign.

5. D9 and the card's own chart (D10) and the points.
   - D9: planets in the 10th from the D9 lagna or the Karakamsa (malefics fluctuate, benefics steady). [Rath] (08 rule
     1.6). Mercury or Venus in the 10th suit business. [Rath] (08 rule 1.2).
   - D10: service (6th) against business (7th): weigh where the 6th and 7th lords sit from the D10 lagna and its lord;
     the lagna lord in the 9th suits business, in the 5th service. [Rath] (08 rule 1.20). A benefic 8th favours
     business over service. [Rath] (08 §2 W6).
   - `biz_capital_assets`, `biz_cashflow_margins`: the D10 2nd is capital; the 5th, 8th and 11th from it are partners'
     capital, loans and own funds. [Rath] (08 rule 1.21). The 2nd and Jupiter, the HL; the 11th from the AL names the
     sources, the 12th from the AL the drains. [Rath] (08 rules 2.1, 2.6, 2.7). The D2, tagged as a disputed chart.
     [Rath] (06 rule 1.9). The D4 is fixed property and premises. [Rath] (06 rule 1.2).
   - `biz_ideal_industry`: the field by the dominant D10 planet (Sun politics ... Venus the arts, Mercury trade, Rahu
     imports, Ketu exports); "dominant" has no stated measure. [Rath] (08 rule 1.18). The AmK shapes the profession in
     cases only. [Rath] cases (08 rule 1.11).
   - `biz_partnerships_fit`: the 7th lord against the lagna lord, the A7. The DK stands for the spouse [Student]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §6): read it only to keep the spouse apart from a business partner. Keep partner, investor, key
     hire and spouse apart.
   - `biz_hiring_team`: the D10 5th is subordinates and authority, the 6th service. [Rath] (08 rule 1.20). The A5 in
     the D10 is following. [Student] Rao (08 rule 1.22).
   - `biz_scaling_risk`: the 8th from the D10 lagna is business loans and speculation. [Rath] (08 §2 W7).
   - `biz_founder_burnout`: the 1st, 6th, 8th, 12th and the lagna lord, Sun and Moon as energy and load, never illness.

6. The other verified systems, and where they overlap.

7. The trigger.
   - Jupiter over the AL brings a win; Saturn over the A10 a loss of title; Saturn aspecting the AL strains the image.
     [Rath] (08 §1 T5). Rath gives no rule for business start dates: gap (08 §1 "Gaps").
   - Jupiter and Saturn together on the 10th or 11th is modern practice: gap (dasha-results/dasha-transit-combination.md

     Phaladeepika 20.37-38 (same file).
