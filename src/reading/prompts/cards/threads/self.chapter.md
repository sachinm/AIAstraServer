LENS RECIPE: `self.chapter` (Self board: your life right now, this chapter, your life phases, your year)
Cards: `diagnosis`, `chapter`, `chapters`, `year`.

Read this before the card brief. It fixes the order of the story for every card in this lens. Use the charts, sign
dasha, year charts and points that this card's ROUTE line names.

Source tags: [Rath] Sanjay Rath's own text · [Student] his students (P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, Sarajit Poddar) · [Text] a
classical verse · [Inferred] assembled by the research, stated by no one · gap: no source gives this rule. Paths are
research files under docs/deep-research/. Carry the tag into your evidence; never call an [Inferred] step or a gap
Rath's rule.

SYSTEMS: read, name and count only these, and only where the data marks them as applying: Vimshottari (every level);
Narayana at the maha dasha level only (its sub-periods are not verified); Lagna Kendradi (low weight, never decisive);
the one conditional dasha the data marks as applying (it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari); Patyayini (inside the
year); the Tithi Pravesha year chart. Sudasa is not read on this lens. Never read, name or count: Yogini, Chara, Drig,
Trikona, Mandooka, Tara, Naisargika (so no "life stage by Naisargika"), Mudda, any longevity dasha (Shoola, Sthira,
Brahma, Niryana Shoola, Navamsa dasha, Aayu), or a conditional dasha that does not apply. Never name the software that
computed the data.

PURPOSE QUESTION
Where is this person in their life's story: what the running chapter is about, what it asks and gives, when it turns,
and what part this birthday-to-birthday year plays in it?

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: the houses it owns name what the chapter is about; the house
     it sits in, its dignity, navamsa sign and links say how it goes. [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48.2-7; Phaladeepika 20.14,
     20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19 (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4).
   - Its condition at the start of the dasha colours the whole chapter where the data carries the start-date sky.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48.8; Saravali 41.16-18 (same file §1).
   - Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - `chapters`: the previous maha dasha in one line, the running one as the arc, its end date and the next lord.
   - Narayana D1 maha dasha: the sign, its occupants, its lord, its place from the lagna and the AL. [Inferred] from
     [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §7). Narayana is the environment and the definite change; Vimshottari the experience.
     [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §1).

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 strain, kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th work; the
     house names the stretch. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9). Many classical entries split a sub-period into start, middle and end. [Text] (same file §4).
   - `chapter`: this is the card's centre: what the sub-period asks, costs and gives, and its boundary date.
   - `diagnosis`: the lagna lord's state against the running lords; the central tension is where they pull apart.

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - Cast as the Sun in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle, for the birthplace. [Rath, co-credited]
     (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). Never read alone; the birth chart overrides. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna: kendra eases, trikona gains, dusthana or badhaka obstructs. The year lagna lord
     shows success in undertakings; the hora lord sets the year's focus. [Student] Poddar (05 §1.3). The year lord is
     the weekday lord: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09; a year lord that is a functional benefic for the birth lagna and
     well placed gives a good year and wakes its natal yogas. [Student] Poddar (05 §1.2).
   - Then the year D1 houses, then the year D9 for the year lord's dignity. Houses to read: gap; order [Inferred]
     (docs/techniques/07 decision 2). The same planet in the same sign in the birth and year charts gives its full
     results. [Text] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step 4).
   - Patyayini places the stretches inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how it felt, as a confirm question; never a prediction. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the
     product"). `year` makes this year its centre.

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The Moon is the mind: how the chapter is felt. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Read the running lords' houses
     from the Moon's sign; Vimshottari is counted from the Moon. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/07 §1 "Timing method").

5. D9 (this lens's own chart).
   - The running lords in the D9: vargottama, dignity, and the house each occupies from the D9 lagna; a planet gives
     in its dasha what its places across the charts promise. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §8). Debilitated in the D1
     with an exalted navamsa gives a sudden rise. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/08 §1 T6).
   - If the timing block marks the D9 maturity rule (age 36), treat it as the house rule it is: gap, no source in any
     tier (sanjay-rath/06 §6 "Gaps"; docs/techniques/07 decision 1).
   - Years of fortune per planet as a note when the person's age is within one year of a cited planet's year. [Rath],
     to be checked in print (sanjay-rath/07 §2 "Timing method").

6. The other verified systems, and where they overlap.
   - Vimshottari pinpoints; Narayana must confirm. [Rath] Crux p.3 (sanjay-rath/04 §7). Lagna Kendradi low weight.
     [Student] (04 §1). The conditional dasha that applies confirms only. [Rath] (04 §4).
   - Name the dates where the period boundaries of two or more systems fall together. No required count. [Inferred]
     (docs/techniques/07 decision 3).

7. The trigger.
   - No Rath transit rule for a chapter: gap (sanjay-rath/05 §4).
   - The classical gate: the sub-period lord's transit and the Sun's entry into its exaltation. [Text] Phaladeepika

8. The verdict.
   - The gate: confirmed, leaning, open, contested, not promised. [Inferred] from [Rath] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step
