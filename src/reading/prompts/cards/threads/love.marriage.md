Read this before the card brief. It fixes the order of the story for every card in this lens. Use the charts, sign

only and is never read here.

SYSTEMS: read, name and count only these, and only where the data marks them as applying: Vimshottari (every level);
Narayana at the maha dasha level only (its sub-periods are not verified); Sudasa (only on `marriage_finances_chores`,
whose route adds it); Lagna Kendradi (low weight, never decisive); the one conditional dasha the data marks as
applying (it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari); Patyayini (inside the year); the Tithi Pravesha year chart. Never
read, name or count: Yogini, Chara, Drig, Trikona, Mandooka, Tara, Naisargika, Mudda, any longevity dasha (Shoola,
Sthira, Brahma, Niryana Shoola, Navamsa dasha, Aayu), or a conditional dasha that does not apply. Never name the
software that computed the data.

PURPOSE QUESTION
How does this person's marriage or partnership stand, when does it open, strain or mend, and what does the running
chapter ask of them inside it?

Order of judgment [Inferred] from Rath's seventh-house chapter (sanjay-rath/09 §2): the 7th, its lord and Venus; then
the UL and the A7 (the UL and the 7th alone cannot settle a marriage; the A7 must be seen [Rath], sanjay-rath/02 §2.4);
then the D9 and the DK as confirmers; then timing.

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4). Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - For marriage, the Vimshottari maha dasha planet should link to the D9 lagna or its lord. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/09 §2 M1). Venus is the main karaka and the 7th lord behaves like Venus. [Rath] (09 rule 2.1).
   - Sign dasha: the route names Narayana D9. A D9 Narayana starts from the sign the D1 9th lord occupies in the D9
     (Table 29: the 9th controls the D9). [Rath] (sanjay-rath/04 §2); its use for marriage is [Student]
     (docs/techniques/07 §4.2). It is not cast yet: read the Narayana D1 maha dasha sign (its occupants, its lord, its
     place from the 7th, the UL and the A7) and say the marriage timing comes from Narayana D1. [Inferred] (04 "Use in
     the product").

2. Its sub-period.
   - The antardasha lord should relate to the D9 7th lord. [Rath] (09 §2 M1).
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 strain, kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th work; the
     house names the stretch. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9).

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - Cast as the Sun in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle. [Rath, co-credited] (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). Never
     read alone. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna, the year lagna lord, the hora lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by
     weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the 7th, its lord and Venus in the year D1, then the year D9 (and the year D4 on `marriage_inlaws_family`).
     Houses to read: gap; order [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 2).
   - Patyayini places the stretch inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how the partnership felt, as a confirm question. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The Moon is the mind: how the partnership is felt day to day. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Read the running
     lords' houses from the Moon's sign. No Rath marriage rule from the Moon: gap.

5. D9 (this lens's own chart) and the points.
   - The D9 is the marriage chart; the rasi may serve better where the marriage is a living arrangement rather than a
     dharmic union. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §10).
   - The UL is the marriage and the spouse; planets with it show the spouse's family. [Rath] (09 rule 2.3). The 2nd from
     the UL (or from the 7th) is the marriage's sustenance. [Rath] (09 rule 2.4). The UL in the 2nd: wealth through the
     spouse. [Rath] (09 rule 2.5). The 3rd lord's argala on the 7th gives benefit from the spouse. [Rath] (09 rule 2.6).
   - The DK is read in the D9 as a confirmer; no selection rule beyond his examples. [Rath] cases (09 rule 2.9).
   - Spouse significator: Venus for both sexes; Jupiter in a woman's chart as an added view, not a replacement. [Rath]
     against [Student] (sanjay-rath/02 §10, open conflict).
   - `marriage_inlaws_family`: the Sun in the 12th from the UL strains relations with the father-in-law; the Moon in
     the 7th from the UL a hostile mother-in-law. [Rath] (09 rule 2.3). The D4 is home and fixed property. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/06 rule 1.2). Keep the person's family, the partner's family and the shared home apart.
   - `marriage_finances_chores`: the 2nd and Jupiter, the A2 as the source of income, the UL in the 2nd. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/08 rules 2.1, 2.5). The D2 is material wealth, but Rath's two texts differ on it: tag the D2 as a
     disputed chart. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/06 rule 1.9).
   - `marriage_personal_growth`: harmony between the 7th and 12th and their arudhas, above all from the Karakamsa,
     supports growth through the partner. [Rath] (09 rule 2.7).
   - `marriage_timing_readiness`: the spouse's home region from the 7th lord counted from Venus. [Rath] (09 rule 2.8).

6. The other verified systems, and where they overlap.
   - Vimshottari pinpoints; Narayana must confirm. [Rath] Crux p.3 (sanjay-rath/04 §7). Lagna Kendradi low weight.
     [Student] (04 §1). The conditional dasha that applies is read only when its condition holds [Rath] (04 §4); it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari. [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step 2).
   - `marriage_finances_chores`: Sudasa times finance and fortune [Rath] role; its calculation from the Sree Lagna is
     [Student] (sanjay-rath/08 §2 W1, W2). Check Narayana with Sudasa. [Student] Rao (04 §7).
   - Do not claim that Narayana is more accurate than Vimshottari for marriage, or that Sudasa leads when the Moon sign
     is stronger: both claims were withdrawn by the research (sanjay-rath/04 §8).
   - Name the dates where two or more point to the 7th, its lord, Venus, the UL or the D9 7th lord. No required count.
     [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 decision 3).

7. The trigger.
   - Jupiter's transit over the 7th, the 7th lord or the 2nd from the UL is a trigger; so is the Sun's transit. [Rath]
     (09 §2 M3).
   - Jupiter's progression, the Sudarshana year and the Vivaha saham are Rath's too [Rath] (09 M4-M6) but are not
     computed: gap.
   - Otherwise the classical gate: the sub-period lord's transit and the Sun's entry into its exaltation. [Text]
     Phaladeepika 20.37-38 (dasha-results/dasha-transit-combination.md §1).

8. The verdict.
   - The gate: confirmed, leaning, open, contested, not promised. [Inferred] from [Rath] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step
     3). A commitment window needs the D1 and the D9 to agree. Never predict a separation or a divorce; a strained
     stretch is described with its easing date.
