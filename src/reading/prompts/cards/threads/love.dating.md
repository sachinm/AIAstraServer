Read this before the card brief. It fixes the order of the story for every card in this lens. Use the charts, sign
dasha, year charts and points that this card's ROUTE line names. Romance is read from the 5th, Venus, the D9 and the A7

not a romance point [Rath] (sanjay-rath/09 rule 1.8): where a brief says A5, read the A7.

SYSTEMS: read, name and count only these, and only where the data marks them as applying: Vimshottari (every level);
Narayana at the maha dasha level only (its sub-periods are not verified); Lagna Kendradi (low weight, never decisive);
the one conditional dasha the data marks as applying (it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari); Patyayini (inside the
year); the Tithi Pravesha year chart. Sudasa is not read on this lens. Never read, name or count: Yogini, Chara, Drig,
Trikona, Mandooka, Tara, Naisargika, Mudda, any longevity dasha (Shoola, Sthira, Brahma, Niryana Shoola, Navamsa
dasha, Aayu), or a conditional dasha that does not apply. Never name the software that computed the data.

PURPOSE QUESTION
When is love open for this person, what kind of partner do they draw, what slows it, and what does the running chapter
ask of them in romance?

Rath has no separate romance chapter; the order below is [Inferred] from his fifth- and seventh-house chapters
(sanjay-rath/09 §1). There is no timing rule specific to love before marriage: gap (09 §1 "Gaps"). Romance is timed
with the general dasha rules below; say so when it matters.

THE THREAD (tell it in this order, as one continuous story)

1. The maha dasha.
   - Judge the Vimshottari maha dasha lord from the lagna: houses owned, house occupied, dignity, navamsa sign, links.
     [Text] BPHS 47.5-6, 48; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.30; Saravali 41.19
     (dasha-results/dasha-lord-condition-principles.md §4). Then its star lord. gap: commentary, no verse
     (natal-techniques/23 §5).
   - For this lens, ask whether the maha dasha lord owns or sits in the 5th or 7th, joins or aspects Venus, or links to
     the A7 or the UL. The 5th governs love and devotion; Venus is the karaka of love. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/09 rule 1.1).
   - Narayana D1 maha dasha: the sign, its occupants, its lord, and its place from the 5th, the A7 and the UL. A sign
     dasha and its sub-sign both linking to the matter's significators confirm an event. [Rath] Tagore illustration
     (sanjay-rath/04 §7); here only the maha dasha sign is read.

2. Its sub-period.
   - Vimshottari antardasha lord counted from the maha dasha lord: 6/8/12 strain, kendra, trikona, 3rd, 11th work; the
     house names the stretch. [Text] Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS 57.79
     (dasha-results/antardasha-results-per-pair.md §4). Planets 6/8/12 from the dasha lord act against it. [Rath]
     (sanjay-rath/02 §9).
   - In one Rath case, many short relationships came in Venus-Moon, then Mars and Rahu sub-periods, with Saturn's
     transits. [Rath] one case, Chart 83 (sanjay-rath/09 §1 "Timing method"). Use it as an illustration, never a rule.

3. The Tithi Pravesha year.
   - Cast as the Sun in its birth sign and the Moon at its birth angle. [Rath, co-credited] (sanjay-rath/05 §1.1). Never
     read alone. [Student] Poddar (05 §2).
   - Year lagna from the birth lagna, the year lagna lord, the hora lord as focus. [Student] (05 §1.3). Year lord by
     weekday: gap, house rule of 2026-11-09 (05 §1.2).
   - Then the 5th, the 7th and Venus in the year D1, then the year D9. Houses to read: gap; order [Inferred]
     (docs/techniques/07 decision 2).
   - Patyayini places the stretch inside the year. gap: verified by an engine run, no Rath text.
   - Last year: one line on how love felt, as a confirm question. [Inferred] (05 "Use in the product").

4. The same from the Moon.
   - The Moon is the mind: how the person feels in love. [Student] Rao (sanjay-rath/02 §3). Read the running lords'
     houses from the Moon's sign. No Rath romance rule from the Moon: gap.

5. D9 (this lens's own chart) and the points.
   - Love and affection are read from Venus and the D9. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/09 rule 1.2). Read Venus, the 5th and 7th
     lords and the D9 lagna, 5th and 7th.
   - The A7 (dara pada) shows partners and governs desire, fulfilled or renounced. [Rath] (09 rule 1.3). Many short
     relationships: the A7 and Rahu. [Rath] (09 rule 1.5).
   - The 2nd and 7th from the UL, with Rahu or malefics and A7 links, show outside attachments. [Rath] (09 rule 1.4).
     Use this only to describe a pattern the person has confirmed; never accuse.
   - The 5th from the AL is recognition of intelligence, not love. [Student] (09 rule 1.7).
   - `dating_transition_commitment`, `dating_exes_closure`: the UL and the 7th alone cannot settle a marriage; the A7
     must be seen. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/02 §2.4). A commitment needs the D1 and the D9 to agree.

6. The other verified systems, and where they overlap.
   - Vimshottari pinpoints; Narayana must confirm. [Rath] Crux p.3 (sanjay-rath/04 §7). Lagna Kendradi low weight.
     [Student] (04 §1). The conditional dasha that applies is read only when its condition holds [Rath] (04 §4); it confirms, never replaces Vimshottari. [Inferred] (docs/techniques/07 §4.1 step 2).
   - Name the dates where two or more point to the 5th, Venus, the A7 or the UL. No required count. [Inferred]
     (docs/techniques/07 decision 3).

7. The trigger.
   - Saturn's transits marked relationships in the one case. [Rath] one case (09 §1).
   - `dating_transition_commitment`, `dating_meeting_timing`: Jupiter's transit over the 7th, the 7th lord or the 2nd
     from the UL, and the Sun's transit, trigger marriage. [Rath] (sanjay-rath/09 §2 M3). Use it for commitment only.
   - Otherwise the classical gate: the sub-period lord's transit and the Sun's entry into its exaltation. [Text]
     Phaladeepika 20.37-38 (dasha-results/dasha-transit-combination.md §1). Jupiter and Saturn together on the 7th is
     modern practice: gap (same file §1).

8. The verdict.
   - The gate: confirmed, leaning ("may"), open, contested, not promised. [Inferred] from [Rath] (docs/techniques/07
     §4.1 step 3). Never promise a partner; a quiet stretch is not a verdict on the person.
