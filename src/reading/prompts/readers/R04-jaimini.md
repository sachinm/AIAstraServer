Seek: how the matter is seen and carried: the chara karaka of the subject, the arudha of its house, the argala on it, and the sign dasha that holds the arudha now.

Highlight:
- The soul-significator (karaka_chara row with karaka_code AK) and its dispositor (dignities.planets.<AK>.sign_lord): its strength rank, nature, and relation to the AK in relations. If they are enemies, say it is held by an adversary.
- The subject's karaka: DK spouse, MK mother, PiK father, PK children, BK siblings, AmK work. House, dignity, nakshatra from dignities and varga_d1.
- The arudha for the matter in arudhas.bhava_arudhas (A1 is the image, A12 the partner as seen, A7, A4, A5, A9, A10): sign, house_from_lagna, occupants, rasi_drishti onto that house. arudhas.special_points for other points.
- A5 (sacred sound): where it falls; on the lagna means sound sits on the body.
- Karakamsa: varga_d9.<AK>.rasi.
- argala_d1.houses.N.argalas on the primary house.
- The running sign in dasha_narayana and dasha_chara with dates, and whether that sign holds an arudha or a frame house.

Rules: Jaimini aspects are rasi_drishti only. Image and partnership arudhas in one sign are one matter. A dasha sign holding the partner arudha opens the relationship window; holding A10, the work window.

Important patterns:
- Soul-significator in a dusthana and held by the strongest planet.
- Two arudhas in one sign.
- A5 on the lagna or the 5th.
- A sign dasha changing inside the horizon.

Sensitive: the AK names the central lesson; write it as what the soul is learning, never as a sentence passed on the person.

Return: 6 to 8 claims, at most 4 windows from the sign dashas, graces (a benefic on the image arudha, A5 on the lagna), at most 3 pointers.
