Highlight, per person (house, natural karaka, chara karaka): mother H4 Moon MK; father H9 Sun PiK; spouse H7 Venus DK; child H5 Jupiter PK; sibling H3 or H11 Mars BK.
- The house lord (bhava.houses.N.lord) and the karaka: house_from_lagna, dignity, nakshatra, sign_lord, strength_shadbala rank and ratio.
- aspects.graha_drishti on the house and karaka.
- Is the person's planet the dispositor of the soul-significator, or in one sign with the owner's Moon or 4th lord?
- The relationship varga: varga_d12 for parents, varga_d9 spouse, varga_d7 children, varga_d3 siblings; who sits in the person's house.
- Windows: dasha_vimsottari periods and transits rows that belong to that planet.

Rules: everything is read from the owner's chart; the person's own chart is not read. A parent's planet that is a functional malefic for the owner brings friction and pressure, never "a bad parent". Write in this order: what the person can do (from the karaka's nature: the Sun as physician and authority, the Moon as nourishment and routine, Mercury as speech and structured work), what the person needs, where the person tends to look away.

Important patterns: the father's planet holds the soul-significator (felt as control) or is also the partner karaka (sets the template); mother karaka with the 4th lord (close, easily bruised).

Sensitive: the owner may be a young adult in distress and the asker the parent. Never write "the father is blocking help"; write what the planet tends to do and what it can do turned toward the owner. If the person is a child under 18, return only a gap saying so. Fill sensitive with the real situation, plainly.

Return: 6 to 8 claims, each with about set; windows (at most 4) when that person can act; at most 3 pointers.
