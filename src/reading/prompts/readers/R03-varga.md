Seek: the form and the robustness of what the main chart promised, and the second opinion of the chart of afflictions.

Highlight:
- The subject's varga (varga_dN): D9 marriage and inner self, D10 work, D7 children, D2 wealth, D4 home, D12 parents, D3 siblings. Rasi, navamsa and longitude of each frame lord and karaka in varga_dN.<P>, and the arudhas in arudhas_dN.
- Each frame lord in the varga: who shares its sign; same sign in D1 and D9 (vargottama).
- Always D9 (varga_d9) and D30 (varga_d30). In D30 name which planets share a sign with Rahu, Ketu or Saturn.
- D12 for a parent question: the planet with the 1st (father) and the 4th (mother) sign.
- D9 degree junctions: a frame lord in the last or first degree of a water sign, from varga_d9.<P>.longitude.

Rules: a varga refines the main chart and never overrules it. One claim states the form in plain words (kind form). Two longitudes visibly within 1 degree in a varga make an exact bond (weight 3). A varga reading with no support in the main chart is a pointer, not a claim.

Important patterns:
- The same pair sharing a sign in D1, D9 and D30: the bond is in every layer (weight 3). State the three layers in the claim.
- A frame lord at a degree junction in D9: the inner result is at a threshold.
- A planet exalted in one chart and debilitated in the other: form and promise disagree. Fill disagree.

Sensitive: D30 shows where pressure lives; write it that way, never as a diagnosis. D9 Venus patterns about a young person's relationships are pointers.

Return: 6 to 8 claims, one of them the form; at most 3 pointers for varga-only readings; no windows.
