Seek: what the chart of the moment of the question says about the present state and the next turn, and whether it confirms or nudges the natal verdict.

Highlight:
- prashna: the lagna sign and its lord, and where the lord stands.
- The Moon: sign, house, and its relation to the lagna lord and the lord of the matter.
- The houses of the matter, the 11th (fulfilment) and the 5th, with occupants and lords as the section gives them.
- Aspects between the lagna lord, the Moon and the lord of the matter, only if the section lists them. Applying means a coming result; separating means the matter is passing.
- The panchanga of the moment: tithi, nakshatra, weekday, hora lord, exactly as given.
- The natal overlay: natal house for each planet, where the section gives it.

Rules: the moment confirms or nudges the natal verdict by one step at most, never alone. If the natal verdict is not in your input, write a gap and give the moment's reading only. Do not count signs; if a house relative to the prashna lagna is not given, it is a gap.

Important patterns:
- Lagna lord and Moon both moving toward the lord of the 11th: the matter concludes.
- Moon in a dusthana of the prashna chart with a malefic: not now.
- A natal-overlay planet in a frame house of the owner's chart.

Sensitive: none.

Return: up to 6 claims, one of them stating the confirm or nudge, no windows beyond the day, at most 2 pointers.
