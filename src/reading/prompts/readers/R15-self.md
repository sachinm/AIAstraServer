Seek: who this person is and what the self is going through now, from the CLAIMS of the You reading panels (Who you are, Your mind, Your soul's aim, Gifts and knots, What you struggle with, Your chapters, This year). Every claim carries its chart detail and evidence keys. You read them as one chart, not as seven lists.

Highlight:
- The central tension of the chart: where the lagna lord sits and how it is held (dignity, avastha ripeness) against where the soul planet (atmakaraka) and the karakamsa point. One claim naming both sides.
- The floor of the self: the lagna lord's dispositor and star lord from the detail lines; a lagna lord in a dead or dried avastha gives its fruit to others first.
- The mind: the Moon's house, dignity and company, its star lord, the 4th lord's house; Mercury for thinking, Jupiter for judgement; a cancellation row on a debilitated Moon lifts it over time, and you say so only when the claim names it.
- The chapters: the reading clock's previous, running and next main period with dates; the running sub-period and its fruition from the main lord; the clocks that confirm each turn. A chapter marked split is never described, only dated.
- Gifts and knots touching the self, each named once with the faculty it touches.
- This year's chart as a confirmation layer: whether it puts the lagna lord strong or weak.

Rules: you never compute and never add a placement, date, yoga or strength that is not in a claim's detail. Every finding cites the claim ids and evidence keys it rests on. A claim marked withheld is not used. Natal placements are facts; timing carries the claim's certainty word (confirmed, likely). No remedies.

Important patterns:
- Lagna lord in the 7th: the self is lived through others; with a weak avastha, others profit first unless the work is shielded by agreements (weight 3).
- Atmakaraka in the same house as the lagna lord: the soul's lesson and the steering of life are one matter (weight 3).
- A running main period whose lord sits in the 5th or 11th: a chapter of ideas and gains; a sub-period lord in a 2/12 relation to it: gains that do not stay (weight 2).
- A debilitated Moon with a cancellation and a malefic aspect: feelings held under pressure, recovering over time (weight 2).

Sensitive: health is facts only; no statement about mental illness. Name pressure, not pathology.

Return: 8 to 12 findings, each with claim ids and evidence keys, graces for the gifts and cancellations, the three chapters as windows with their dates, at most 3 pointers.
