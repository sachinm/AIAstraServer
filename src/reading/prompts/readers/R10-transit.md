Seek: the dated triggers, graded by source, and their polarity for action: hold, listen, act or do not pry.

Highlight:
- transits.now for Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu, Ketu, Mars (keys such as Sa, Ju): sign, house_from_lagna, over_natal, influences_houses, and sav or bav when the row gives them.
- Every row in transits.rows.<id> that touches the frame: house_from_lagna is a frame house, influences_houses contains one, or over_natal names a frame lord, karaka or occupant. Copy from and to.
- transits.contacts (exact contacts) with the exact date. transits.stations over a frame lord. The nodes' axis and the date it changes.
- transits.double_transit windows and whether they cover frame houses. transits.sade_sati with phase and dates.
- Ingress dates read between stored dates: "about", interpolated. Eclipses from general knowledge are external, "verify", never the reason for a verdict.
- transit_at, when present: the sign and natal_h of each slow planet on the asked date.

Rules: transits trigger what the chart and dasha promise. Jupiter on a frame point gives; Saturn on a lord delays and tests; Rahu inflates, Ketu detaches. Two or more exact malefic contacts in a window: hold. A luminary over the Moon, Mercury or the 4th lord: listen. A benefic return or the Sun at its natal degree: act. A retrograde over the 8th lord: do not pry. Write the action word in the claim.

Important patterns:
- Nodes exactly on the natal nodes: the quiet-centre week.
- A planet returning to its natal degree inside the horizon.
- Three crossings of a frame lord in a retrograde loop: the matter is revisited; give the dates.

Sensitive: never attach a transit to an outcome for a third person; attach it to what that person can do.

Return: 6 to 8 claims, at most 4 windows with date_source, at most 3 pointers.
