Seek: for each frame house, whether the matter is promised, in what form, through whom, and who blocks.

Highlight, per frame house in this order:
- The house: bhava.houses.N.sign, .occupants, and the value in ashtakavarga.sav_by_house.
- Its lord (bhava.houses.N.lord): house_from_lagna, dignity, retrograde, combust in dignities.planets; ratio in strength_shadbala.planets; its bav in ashtakavarga.bav.<P>.values for its sign.
- Aspects on the house and on the lord: find the house in aspects.graha_drishti.<P>.aspected_houses and aspects.rasi_drishti.<P>.aspected_houses; name each aspecting planet and its nature (benefic_malefic.planets.<P>.functional_roles).
- argala_d1.houses.N.argalas: primary, secondary, obstructing, with names.
- The lord's dispositor chain, two hops minimum (dignities.planets.<P>.sign_lord).
- The natural karaka of the matter, read the same way.

Rules: SAV 28 or more is strong, 22 or less weak. Ratio 1.0 or more is strong. BAV 4 or more is good. A lord in 6, 8 or 12 from lagna is a culprit. A chain ending in a kendra or trikona holds, in a dusthana leaks, in the frame itself delivers. Divisional charts are R03's. Do not read aspect strength; it is not given.

Important patterns:
- House lord and karaka both afflicted: the matter is denied, not delayed (weight 3).
- A house held only by Jupiter's aspect: a grace.
- A lord near the Sun that the engine does not mark combust: say it is close to the Sun, not combust by the engine's rule.
- The house the strongest planet rules.
- A 6th, 8th or 12th lord sitting in the frame.

Sensitive: when the frame is health (H1, H6, H8, H12), say what is strong first. When the frame is H7 or H5 for a young adult, never describe partners in moral words.

Return: 6 to 8 claims across the frame houses, culprits as obstructing claims, graces as kind grace, at most 3 pointers, no windows.
