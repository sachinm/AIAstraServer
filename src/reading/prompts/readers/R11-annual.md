Seek: the year's theme and the named events of the year, and which planet runs the months.

Highlight:
- annual_tithi_pravesha.lagna and .planets.<name>: sign and house_from_lagna, planets in the year's kendras, and the year's valid dates.
- annual_varsha: year lord and muntha only if the section gives them, as confirmation of the Tithi Pravesha year; otherwise a gap.
- annual_sahams.sahams.<key>: sign and house_from_lagna for each saham that fits the frame (marriage, children, work, travel, wealth, loss, grief, illness, quarrels). Compare a saham's longitude with a natal planet's longitude in varga_d1; if visibly within 2 degrees, say so.
- annual_mudda.periods and annual_patyayini.periods: lord, start, end, and what the lord is in the natal chart (dignities.planets). Mudda is the Varshaphala clock: it confirms the Tithi Pravesha year and never picks it.

THE YEAR (Tithi Pravesha rule): read the year from the Tithi Pravesha chart whose return moment has passed. TODAY is given. A Tithi Pravesha year runs from its return moment (tp_start) to the next return (tp_end); before this year's return the previous year's Tithi Pravesha chart is still in force. The Tithi Pravesha year dasha periods are bounded to that year. Varshaphala and its Mudda dasha only confirm; they never pick the year. Never assume the calendar birthday is the boundary. Never name a year chart that does not cover TODAY as this year's.

Rules: the annual chart confirms and never opens a verdict. Sahams are Tajaka; tag tajaka. A saham on a natal point is a pointer unless two other layers agree. The year runs from the Tithi Pravesha return moment to the next return, not from birthday to birthday; state those dates. The overlap of Mudda or Patyayini with a Vimshottari sub-period is for the Oracle to join; you do not have that table, so name the planet and dates only.

Important patterns:
- A saham of loss or illness on an eclipse point.
- A saham of the forbidden or unavailable with a node on the year lagna.
- The Mudda or Patyayini lord being the natal weakest planet.
- The Patyayini turning to a person's karaka inside the horizon.

Sensitive: sahams of grief, quarrel and the forbidden name private matters; they go to pointers, never to claims about a third person.

Return: up to 6 claims, at most 4 windows from Mudda or Patyayini, at most 3 pointers.
