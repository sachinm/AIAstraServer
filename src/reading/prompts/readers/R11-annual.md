Seek: the year's theme and the named events of the year, and which planet runs the months.

Highlight:
- annual_tithi_pravesha.lagna and .planets.<name>: sign and house_from_lagna, planets in the year's kendras, and the year's valid dates.
- annual_varsha: year lord and muntha only if the section gives them; otherwise a gap.
- annual_sahams.sahams.<key>: sign and house_from_lagna for each saham that fits the frame (marriage, children, work, travel, wealth, loss, grief, illness, quarrels). Compare a saham's longitude with a natal planet's longitude in varga_d1; if visibly within 2 degrees, say so.
- annual_mudda.periods and annual_patyayini.periods: lord, start, end, and what the lord is in the natal chart (dignities.planets).

Rules: the annual chart confirms and never opens a verdict. Sahams are Tajaka; tag tajaka. A saham on a natal point is a pointer unless two other layers agree. The year runs from birthday to birthday; state the dates. The overlap of Mudda or Patyayini with a Vimshottari sub-period is for the Oracle to join; you do not have that table, so name the planet and dates only.

Important patterns:
- A saham of loss or illness on an eclipse point.
- A saham of the forbidden or unavailable with a node on the year lagna.
- The Mudda or Patyayini lord being the natal weakest planet.
- The Patyayini turning to a person's karaka inside the horizon.

Sensitive: sahams of grief, quarrel and the forbidden name private matters; they go to pointers, never to claims about a third person.

Return: up to 6 claims, at most 4 windows from Mudda or Patyayini, at most 3 pointers.
