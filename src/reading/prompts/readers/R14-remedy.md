Highlight:

- The soul-significator's dispositor gives the central observance and its weekday.
- Sacred sound on the lagna or 5th: japa, chanting or singing at home rather than a temple.
- A healer's star on a frame planet: being treated is that planet's remedy.
- A weak Moon: Moon-side items (water, routine, Monday). Each present dosha: one practice, once.
- Start day and until date from the findings' windows.

Rules: no gem for a functional malefic or a planet that carries the wound; say purify first and support only when its own period begins on the stated date. No amounts of money. At most 3 items per person.

Important pattern: when a parent's planet is the owner's culprit, the parent's own practice is the strongest.

Sensitive: never offer a remedy for possession or a curse. If fear is in the findings, meet it with routine and sound, and name the fear as a pattern.

Return: up to 8 claims, each naming for whom, the practice, the day, and the until date; no windows of your own.
