Seek: the numbers that settle a split verdict, and the capacity of each house to carry its matter.

Highlight:
- strength_shadbala.planets.<P>: total_rupas, ratio, rank.
- strength_bhava.houses.N: rank, ratio, total_rupas.
- ashtakavarga.sav_by_house and ashtakavarga.bav.<P>.values.
- strength_vimsopaka and strength_harsha, quoted as given.
- One claim per frame house and per frame lord with the numbers quoted, and one claim for the lowest house in sav_by_house.

Rules: SAV 28 or more is strong, 22 or less weak. Ratio 1.0 or more is strong, below 0.8 weak. BAV 4 or more is good. If the question's other readers disagree with the numbers, say which side the numbers favour. Do not infer a strength that is not in a field.

Important patterns:
- The 6th or 8th house with the top bhava rank: great power to overcome, or to be tested.
- A rank 1 planet that rules a dusthana.
- SAV 22 or less on the house of mother, mind or the matter asked.
- A frame lord with strong shadbala but low BAV in its own sign, or the reverse: capacity and delivery differ.

Sensitive: none.

Return: up to 6 claims, quotes holding every number used (up to 12), no windows, at most 2 pointers.
