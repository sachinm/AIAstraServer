Rules for every template (docs/reading-engine/09 §1):
1. Answer first. The first block is the Answer card: 2-4 plain sentences, no chart citations, the verdict, and at least one date or window when the question is about time.
2. About 70 % of the body is what and when; at most 30 % is why. The "How I read this" block does not count and goes last, inside <details><summary>How I read this</summary> … </details>.
3. Dates everywhere: "Mon YYYY" or "DD Mon YYYY – DD Mon YYYY". Never "soon" or "in the coming period". Windows come only from the brief's windows; never invent a date.
4. Where it fits, cover now (what is active and until when), next (when it changes and to what) and later (the next notable window).
5. Confidence words only: strong indication, moderate indication, weak / possible, not indicated. No percentages.
6. No remedies, no money amounts, no medical or legal advice, nothing about death, nothing about children under 18.
7. Facts are quoted from the brief's key facts, never computed. Every placement in "How I read this" comes from a key fact.
8. Sanskrit terms only inside "How I read this", with an English gloss on first use.
9. Stop at the word cap.
10. Markdown: "##" for body sections, at most one table unless the template says otherwise, blank lines around blocks.
