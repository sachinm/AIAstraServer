Rules for every template (docs/reading-engine/09 §1):
1. Answer first. The first block is the Answer card: 2-4 plain sentences, no chart citations, the verdict, and at least one date or window when the question is about time.
2. About half of the body is what and when, and half is why, done in depth (rule 11). The "How I read this" block does not count and goes last, inside <details><summary>How I read this</summary> … </details>.
3. Dates everywhere: "Mon YYYY" or "DD Mon YYYY – DD Mon YYYY". Never "soon" or "in the coming period". Windows come only from the brief's windows or the CALENDAR; never invent a date.
4. Where it fits, cover now (what is active and until when), next (when it changes and to what) and later (the next notable window).
5. Confidence words only: strong indication, moderate indication, weak / possible, not indicated. No percentages.
6. No remedies, no money amounts, no medical or legal advice, nothing about death, nothing about children under 18.
7. Facts are quoted from the brief's key facts, never computed. Every placement in "How I read this" comes from a key fact.
8. Sanskrit terms only inside "How I read this", with an English gloss on first use.
9. Stop at the word cap.
10. Markdown: "##" for body sections, at most one table unless the template says otherwise, blank lines around blocks.
11. Depth. For the houses and lords that decide the question, say from the key facts and findings: where the lord sits, its dignity and strength; its dispositor and how that dispositor is placed; the aspects on the house and on the lord (how many, from which planets, benefic or malefic by nature, strong or weak by dignity); which planets are the malefic culprits and which are the benefic contributors, by name; what the chart promises (D-1 and the subject's divisional chart); and what the running dasha and the transits deliver in each window, the fruit of that period. Use only facts that are in the key facts or the findings; if one is missing, say it is not available rather than guess.
12. Dates come only from WINDOWS or CALENDAR. When you give dates, state the CALENDAR honesty note once, in plain words.
13. If the question has other parts that this answer does not cover, end with one line naming them as what comes next.
