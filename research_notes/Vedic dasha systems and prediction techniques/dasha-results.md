## What are the explicit rules for judging a period good/bad and for which life area?

### Takeaway
The texts give explicit conditional rules: functional lordship (BPHS 34), lord's dignity and house at birth and at the
dasha start (BPHS 47.5-6, 48.8; Phaladeepika 20.1, 20.14, 20.30), and the bhukti lord's position from the dasha lord
(Phaladeepika 20.29; BPHS antardasha chapters). The life area comes from the houses owned and occupied and from the
bhukti lord's house counted from the dasha lord.

### Cited Findings
- BPHS ch. 47 (e-text): general results from the planet's nature, distinctive from placement; dreshkana sets start/middle/end, reversed for retrograde planets and always for nodes (47.2-4); favourable if lord in lagna/exalted/own/friend at the start, unfavourable in 6/8/12, debilitated or enemy sign (47.5-6) — [archive.org e-text](https://archive.org/stream/ParasharaHoraSastra/BrihatParasharaHoraSastraVedicAstrologyEbook_djvu.txt)
- BPHS ch. 48: dasha results of lords of houses 1-12; 6/8/12 lords joined to a trikona lord become favourable; kendra-trikona exchanges good; 3/6/11 bad; benefics in maraka houses bad — [enjoylearningsanskrit ch. 48](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-48)
- BPHS 57.79 IAST "dāyeśāt ṣaṣṭha-randhre vā vyaye vā bala-varjite": weak planet 6/8/12 from the period lord = quarrels, grief, loss of position — [enjoylearningsanskrit 57.79](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-57/verse-79)
- BPHS ch. 34: kendra lords' benefic/malefic neutralisation, trikona lords good, 3/6/11 bad, 2/12/8 by association, Sun/Moon free of 8th fault, nodes give results of house and joined lord — [enjoylearningsanskrit ch. 34](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-34)
- BPHS ch. 44: 2nd/7th maraka, Saturn related to maraka chief killer, nodes as marakas — [enjoylearningsanskrit ch. 44](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-44)
- Phaladeepika ch. 20: strong/weak bhava-lord dasha lists (2-20), dignity scale 1/0.75/0.5/0.25/0 (30), bhukti position from dasha lord (29), mutual 6/8/12 bad (22), transit rules (34-38, 59-62), Parashari lordship rules (41-54), arohini/avarohini/madhya/adhama (56) — [wisdomlib Phaladeepika ch. 20](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621592.html)
- Phaladeepika ch. 19: per-planet dasha results; Rahu with benefic not in 6/8/12 fully good (15); Rahu in Virgo/Pisces/Scorpio good (16) — [wisdomlib Phaladeepika ch. 19](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621591.html)
- BPHS ch. 52-60: each antardasha entry has a good branch (kendra/trikona, own/exalt, with lagna/9th/10th lord), a bad branch (6/8/12 from dasha lord, debilitated, malefics), a maraka branch (2nd/7th lord) and usually a remedy — [archive.org e-text](https://archive.org/stream/ParasharaHoraSastra/BrihatParasharaHoraSastraVedicAstrologyEbook_djvu.txt)

### Inferences
- Polarity (good/bad) and area (which house) are separable outputs; the texts compute them from different inputs.
- Phaladeepika 20.30 gives the only numeric weight scale found, usable as an engine score.

### Gaps
- Deeptadi-avastha dasha results: unsourced (Saravali 41-44 do not use the deeptadi names; BPHS ch. 45 ties avasthas to bhavas).
- BPHS ch. 45 avasthas, as read in the e-text, are not tied to dasha results.

## How do Phaladeepika, Uttara Kalamrita, Sarvartha Chintamani and Laghu Parashari refine BPHS?

### Takeaway
Phaladeepika refines BPHS clearly (quantified dignity, bhukti-from-dasha-lord rule, dasha-transit timing, restated
lordship rules). Saravali ch. 41-44 (read) adds a dasha-start chart (Moon and lord at the start), a three-step
strength grading, a navamsa override, and position-from-dasha-lord sub-period rules, though its dashas are Moola
(ayurdaya) dashas. Laghu Parashari's content was confirmed only through its BPHS ch. 34 parallel and a contents list.
Uttara Kalamrita and Sarvartha Chintamani were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Phaladeepika 20.41 explicitly cites Parashara for kendra-lord rules — [wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621592.html)
- Laghu Parashari (O. P. Verma, Rajan 2005) contents: sections on trikona/trishadaya lords, kendra lords, 2nd/12th lords, 8th lord, kendradhipatya dosha, Sun/Moon 8th lords, yogakaraka, Rahu-Ketu, maraka houses/planets, Saturn as maraka, special dasa rules, neutral bhukti lord, Venus-Saturn dasa-bhukti rule — [wisdomlib shop listing](https://www.wisdomlib.org/shop/books/jyotisha/laghu-parasari-jataka-chandrika)
- Saravali 41.6-8 (auspicious/inauspicious dashas, arohini/avarohini), 41.9 and 41.15-18 (Sun ripens, Moon sustains; Moon and dasha lord at the dasha start; lord in lagna or 3/6/10/11 of the start chart = excellent), 41.19 (enemy navamsa = mixed), 41.22-24 (three-step grading), 41.66-75 (house and state results; rising-type timing), 42.1-11 (Moola sub-periods by position from dasha lord: 7th spouse trouble, 8th danger, 4th gains), 43.1-4 (evil combinations, e.g. Mars dasha Saturn bhukti), 44.1-3 (dasha-start antidotes) — [Saravali English translation PDF, translator not named](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/docs2/LakshmiNarayanLenasia.com/Astrology%20(Jyotish)%20collection/Saravali%20(Kalyana%20Varma).pdf)
- Uttara Kalamrita translations exist (V. Subrahmanya Sastri 1939/1951/1967; P.S. Sastri, Ranjan) but no online full text found — [CiNii](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA14369068)
- Sarvartha Chintamani: B. Suryanarain Rao and Bhasin translations exist; no online text found — [Exotic India listing](https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/IDJ760/)
- Brihat Jataka ch. 8: Sanskrit read only (Neely, wisdomlib); its dashas are ayurdaya-based, not Vimshottari — [wisdomlib BJ ch. 8](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brihat-jataka-by-varahamihira-sanskrit-english/d/doc1501543.html); Chidambaram Iyer 1885 translation is public domain at the [Wellcome Collection](https://content.wellcomecollection.org/works/afmgm695) (not read)

### Inferences
- Phaladeepika is the best single refinement source available online in full; use it as the second spine after BPHS.

## What is the classical basis for sub-sub-period (pratyantar) results?

### Takeaway
BPHS gives arithmetic for pratyantar, sukshma and prana (61.1, 62.1, 63.1), fixed per-pair texts, and a single judging
rule at 61.2 (no bad results if the planet is in a trikona, lord of a good house, or in a good house with benefic
varga; "judge all others likewise"). Phaladeepika 21.84 extends bhukti logic to antara and antarantara.

### Cited Findings
- BPHS ch. 61-63 structure and 61.2 rule — [archive.org e-text](https://archive.org/stream/ParasharaHoraSastra/BrihatParasharaHoraSastraVedicAstrologyEbook_djvu.txt)
- Phaladeepika 21.2, 21.83-84 — [wisdomlib Phaladeepika ch. 21](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621593.html)

### Inferences
- Beyond level 3 there is no classical judging rule; engine levels 4-6 are timing aids, not independent verdicts.

### Gaps
- 61.2 wording not checked on the Sanskrit site.

  enjoylearningsanskrit. Sharma (wisdomlib) ≈ +2 in dasha chapters. Ch. 48 vv. 9-13 missing in the e-text (present on
  the Sanskrit site). Antardasha verse numbers ±2.

- Dasha sandhi/chidra: no classical verse found; blog sources disagree on window width.

- BPHS 48.18 on enjoylearningsanskrit is cut off before the 3/6/11 result; the e-text's "planets in 3/6/11 bad, nodes
  there good" is unconfirmed in Sanskrit, and it conflicts with ch. 47's "11th good" (recorded in the condition file).
- Advisor-driven corrections made: removed unsourced school attributions and figures; Phaladeepika 20.33 is about
  life thirds, not dasha thirds; 8th-lord lagna-lord exception is Phaladeepika 20.41 only.
