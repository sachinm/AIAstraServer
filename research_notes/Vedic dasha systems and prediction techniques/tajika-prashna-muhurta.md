# Tajika, Prashna and Muhurta: research notes

## Exact rules and tables with verse references

### Takeaway
Tajika rules are well documented at verse level through Gansten's 2020 edition of Balabhadra's *Hāyanaratna*. It
quotes Nīlakaṇṭha's Saṃjñātantra and Varṣatantra with numbering. *Muhūrta-cintāmaṇi* and *Praśna Mārga* could be
cited only at chapter level in this pass.

### Cited Findings

- Year length 365d 15gh 31pal 30vip (365.25875 d). Nīlakaṇṭha Saṃj. 1.16, Varṣ. 1.3–5. Balabhadra rejects time corrections. Gansten notes the true return varies by up to about 25 min — [HR 1.6](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500887.html)
- Muntha: Samarasiṃha (years ÷ 12, count from the lagna); Saṃj. 1.66 / Varṣ. 2.1; Tājikasāra 126 adds the lagna degree; Muktāvalī 2½° a month — [HR 5.1](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500929.html)

## How the annual chart is integrated with the natal dasha

### Takeaway
The Tajika texts read integrate the year with the natal **chart** (houses, Muntha, same-sign planets) and time it with
Tajika's own periods. None prescribes combining with natal Vimśottarī. That rule is modern practice.

### Cited Findings
- A natal house lord strong in the year and aspecting that house delivers it, and without the aspect it comes "like a dream" (Yādava 12.10). Same planet in the same sign in both charts gives full results (Samarasiṃha) — [HR 6.1](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500951.html)
- Muntha read in both charts. Its results come in its lord's period (Hillāja) — [HR 5.4](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500932.html)
- Period-system choice inside the year (Mudda when the natal Moon-sign lord is strong in the year, etc.). Bṛhajjātaka 8.22 Sun natal vs. year — [HR 7.11](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500991.html)
- The year lagna read as a natal house (Praśnavaiṣṇava 13.73–75). A year lagna 8/12 from the natal lagna is inauspicious (Yantrādhikāra) — [HR 1.7](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500888.html)

### Inferences
- AI Astra's existing precedence (natal promise and dasha lead, annual confirms) is consistent with the texts, though not prescribed by them.

### Gaps
- No classical Tajika passage on natal Vimśottarī integration was found.
