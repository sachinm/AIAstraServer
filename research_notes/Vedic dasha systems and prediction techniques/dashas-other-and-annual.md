## Primary vs secondary timing

### Takeaway

- Parashara makes Vimshottari principal and disclaims the minor dashas.
- Sudarshana is a full chapter for annual, monthly and daily timing.
- Varahamihira's working dashas are the ayur-based ones plus Naisargika and Mula.
- Tajika makes Patyayini and Mudda primary within the annual chart only.

### Cited Findings

- BPHS 46.3, 46.14, 46.11 and 74; Rath's Naisargika page. Sources as above.

### Inferences

- Modern layering: natal → annual → in-year → Sudarshana focus → transit.

### Gaps

- No source directly ranks Sudarshana vs Tajika for annual timing.

## Combining an annual-chart dasha with the natal dasha

### Takeaway

- The natal dasha gives the promise window.
- The annual chart confirms the year.
- The Mudda and Patyayini lords time it within the year.
- Agreement across the layers (BPHS 74.27–28; BJ "both agree → sure") is the textual principle.

### Cited Findings

- BPHS 74.27–28 ([ch. 74](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-74)).
- BJ agreement rule via [Rath](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/dasa/naisargika-dasha/).

### Inferences

- The step order in 00-overview §5 is a synthesis of modern practice, not a single source.

### Gaps

- No primary Tajika verse was read on combining Mudda or Patyayini with the natal dasha.
