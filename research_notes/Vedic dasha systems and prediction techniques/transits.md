# Transits (gochara) and ashtakavarga: research notes

Deliverables: `docs/deep-research/transits/00-overview.md` to `19-retrograde-and-stations.md`.
Numbering used for Phaladeepika (PD) is the siva.sh online edition (Devanagari + English gloss derived from the
V. Subrahmanya Sastri 1950 translation). BPHS chapter numbers are R. Santhanam's (Ranjan, 1984), where Ashtakavarga is
ch. 66; G.C. Sharma's edition puts it at ch. 68 (offset +2).

## Exact tables (gochara good houses, vedha pairs, BAV contributions)

### Takeaway
The gochara good-house and vedha tables were read in Phaladeepika (siva.sh 26:2-8), and the BAV tables in PD 23:3-9.
Two caveats:
- The Moon's BAV from Saturn and from the Lagna were cut off on the page and come from the common table.
- All readings came through a summariser of the siva.sh pages.

BAV totals check to 48/49/39/54/56/52/39 = 337. Variants: the Moon's BAV from Jupiter, plus three engine cells.

### Cited Findings
- PD 26:1: the Moon sign (candra-lagna) is the chief reference for transits — [siva.sh PD 26:1-5](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/26/1-5)
- PD 26:2-5: good houses from Moon: Sun 3,6,10,11; Moon 1,3,6,7,10,11; Mars and Saturn 3,6,11; Rahu/Ketu grouped
  (bhoginau) in v.2; all planets good in the 11th. Sun is obstructed by anyone except Saturn; Moon by anyone except
  Mercury; Saturn not obstructed by the Sun — [siva.sh PD 26:1-5](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/26/1-5)
- Vedha pairs: Sun 11-5, 3-9, 10-4, 6-12; Moon 7-2, 1-5, 6-12, 11-8, 10-4, 3-9; Mars/Saturn 3-12, 11-5, 6-9 — same page.
- PD 26:6-8: Mercury 2-5, 4-3, 6-9, 8-1, 10-8, 11-12 (not obstructed by the Moon); Jupiter 2-12, 5-4, 7-3, 9-10, 11-8 (vedha house must be empty); Venus 1-8, 2-7, 3-1, 4-10, 5-9, 8-5, 9-11, 11-3, 12-6 — [siva.sh PD 26:6-10](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/26/6-10)
- Conflict: the siva.sh English for 26:8 says Venus gives "bad effects" in 1,2,3,4,5,8,9,11,12 when the vedha house is
  occupied; 26:2 says Venus is good everywhere except 6,7,10. Read as: these are Venus's good houses and the verse
  states what happens when they are pierced. Translation wording ambiguous — same page.
- Rahu/Ketu: PD 26:2 groups them (summariser said "with the Sun"); a Jataka Sara Deepa blog gives 3,6,11 (like Saturn) — [siva.sh 26:1-5](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/26/1-5); [Eye of Vedas ch.44](https://theeyeofvedas.blogspot.com/2025/11/chapter-44-gochara-phala-adhyaya.html)
- PD 26:26-29: a 7x7-line nakshatra grid counted from Krittika (saptashalaka type, not the 9x9 Sarvatobhadra); Sun's
  vedha on janma, adhana, karma stars; malefics on 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 10th, 19th, 23rd stars; eclipse, sankranti,
  graha-yuddha on the janma triad (1, 10, 19) — [siva.sh PD 26:26-30](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/26/26-30)
- PD 26:30: a bad transit aspected by a benefic, or a good one aspected by a malefic, becomes fruitless — same page.
- PD 26:41: a planet transiting a sign with many bindus in its own AV gives good even in 6, 8, 12 — [siva.sh PD 26:41-45](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/26/41-45)
- PD 26:42-45 latta: forward Sun 12, Mars 3, Jupiter 6, Saturn 8; rear Venus 5, Mercury 7, Rahu 9, Moon 22 — same page.
- PD 23:3-9: BAV tables for Sun, Moon (partial), Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn — [siva.sh PD 23:1-5](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/23/1-5); [siva.sh PD 23:6-10](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/23/6-10)
- PD 23 Moon BAV from Jupiter given as 1,2,4,7,8,10,11 (asterisked); the common BPHS-derived table has 1,4,7,8,10,11,12. Both are 7 points — [siva.sh PD 23:1-5](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/23/1-5)
- PD 23:10: houses not listed are malefic; dignified planets sustain AV good, debilitated ones do not — [siva.sh PD 23:6-10](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/23/6-10)
- BPHS ch. 66 (enjoylearningsanskrit edition, 72 verses): bindu (karana) = inauspicious, rekha (sthana) = auspicious
  (v.69), the reverse of the Phaladeepika/South Indian bindu = benefic convention. The site's per-verse English for the
  tables was too garbled to tabulate — [enjoylearningsanskrit BPHS 66](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-66)
- Brihat Samhita ch. 104 (Graha-gochara), N. Chidambaram Iyer 1884 translation: effects counted from the natal Moon sign — [wisdomlib BS](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brihat-samhita/d/doc229368.html)

### Inferences
- Phaladeepika is the most usable primary for all gochara and BAV tables; BPHS online texts are not verse-clean.
- The 337 total is internally consistent with the PD lists (checked by hand).

### Gaps
- Muhurta Chintamani gochara and tara/chandra-bala verses were not opened (no clean online text found).
- BPHS 66-72 verse-level tables were not verified online. The Santhanam PDF is on archive.org but was not read.
- Vama-vedha: no primary verse located; only secondary blogs.

## Kakshya timing and SAV thresholds

### Takeaway
PD 23:16-19 gives the kakshya division (8 x 3°45', Saturn first, Lagna last) and the rule that a bindu fructifies when
the planet transits the division of the contributor. PD 23:20 gives the 28-bindu SAV threshold.

### Cited Findings
- PD 23:16: divide the sign into 8 equal parts; the bindu fructifies in the part of the planet that gave it — [siva.sh PD 23:16-20](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/23/16-20)
- PD 23:17-18: prastara grid (9 x 13 lines, 96 cells); row lords Lagna, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn — same page.
- PD 23:19: the first division belongs to Saturn, the second to Jupiter; the last to the Lagna — same page.
- PD 23:20: SAV of a sign > 28 → good transits; < 28 → harm in proportion to the shortfall; exactly 28 not stated — same page.
- Kakshya degree ranges 0-3°45' Saturn ... 26°15'-30° Lagna — [slideshare AV technique](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/the-use-of-asthakavarga-as-a-predictive-technique/25369219)
- PD 24:1-5 (siva.sh numbering; "AV effects per Horasara"): 9th from Sun bindus × Sun's sodhya pinda ÷ 27 → nakshatra;
  Saturn's transit there or its trines harms the father; ÷12 variants for signs — [siva.sh PD 24:1-5](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/24/1-5)

### Inferences
- 337/12 ≈ 28.08 is the arithmetic mean per sign, so 28 is "above or below average" (inference, not a text claim).

### Gaps
- The 0-8 BAV bindu result scale (BPHS "effects of AV" chapter) was not verified at verse level.
- Sastri 1950 edition may number the AV chapters differently from siva.sh (one search snippet put AV at ch. 24).

## Engine output (shared sky + overlay)

### Takeaway
The engine (AIAstra_Radha) already exports kakshya, BAV/SAV, tara, chandra bala, sade sati/dhaiya, double transit,
stations and eclipses. Gochara vedha, vama vedha, murti, latta (export), Sarvatobhadra and pinda-transit points are
not exported.

### Cited Findings (repo, read 2026-11-18)
- `src/jhora/daily_export.py:683` `kakshya_of`, `:703` `run_transit_ashtakavarga` (BAV, SAV, kakshya has_bindu per day/month; no BAV for nodes).
- `daily_export.py:61-70`: TARA_NAMES (Janma..Parama Mitra), good/bad sets, `_CHANDRA_GOOD = {1,3,6,7,10,11}`, `_CHANDRA_BAD = {4,8,12}`, KAKSHYA_LORDS.
- `facts_export.py:66` uses different tara names (Pratyari, Sadhaka, Vadha, Ati-mitra) — naming inconsistency.
- `transits_export.py:32` sade sati phases 12/1/2, plus 4 = "kantaka_dhaiyya", 8 = "ashtama_dhaiyya"; `double_transit`; stations.
- `transit_range_export.py`: ingresses, stations, eclipses (Swiss Ephemeris), sade_sati_spans, double_transit_month.
- `horoscope/chart/ashtakavarga.py:59-160`: trikona, ekadhipatya, sodhya pinda. Rasi multipliers `[7,10,8,4,10,6,7,8,9,5,11,12]` (Virgo = 6), graha `[5,5,8,5,10,7,5]`. `natal_export.py:222-246` exports sodhya pindas.
- `const.py:1003` latta table includes Ketu 9 backward; `charts.py:2225` latta function counts with Abhijit (28 stars).
- No `gochara_vedha`, `vama`, `murti` or `sarvatobhadra` found in the export modules, although `docs/techniques/general/general.transit.md` describes Gochara Vedha and Murti as ready.

- `const.py:229` `ashtaka_varga_dict` differs from PD 23 in three cells:
  - Moon's BAV from the Moon has 9 added: engine 1,3,6,7,9,10,11; PD 1,3,6,7,10,11.
  - Moon's BAV from Mars lacks 9: engine 2,3,5,6,10,11; PD 2,3,5,6,9,10,11.
  - Venus's BAV from Mars has 4 for 5: engine 3,4,6,9,11,12; PD 3,5,6,9,11,12.
  - The totals still sum to 49 and 52, so a checksum misses all three.
  - The engine's Moon-from-Jupiter row (1,2,4,7,8,10,11) matches PD.

- A full re-grep of `src/jhora` on 2026-11-18 (no truncation) for vedha, murti and latta found:
  - only `vedha_porutham`, which belongs to marriage matching;
  - the latta helper in `charts.py`, with its constant in `const.py`.
  
  So no gochara-vedha or murti function exists. `general.transit.md`'s claim that they are ready is not borne out by the code.
- BPHS 69:2-4 (enjoylearningsanskrit edition):
  - rasi multipliers: Taurus and Leo 10, Gemini and Scorpio 8, Aries and Libra 7, Capricorn and Virgo 5 ("mṛga-kanye ca pañcabhiḥ"), the rest by their own number (Cancer 4, Sagittarius 9, Aquarius 11, Pisces 12);
  - graha multipliers: Jupiter 10, Mars 8, Venus 7, Mercury 5, the rest 5.
  
  Source: [BPHS 69](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-69)

### Gaps
- Virgo rasi-gunakara: the engine uses 6 and BPHS 69:3 says 5. This is a confirmed discrepancy and needs a TODO. Check whether pyjhora follows a different published table first.
- Latta counting with Abhijit (28) vs 27 stars: PD 26:42-43 counts in 27 nakshatras (no Abhijit stated).
