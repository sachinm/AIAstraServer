## Key question 1: How does Rath define and calculate each Chara Karaka, and does he use 7 or 8 karakas?

### Takeaway
Rath's fetched article on the Atmakaraka and Karakamsha defines Karakamsha by where the Atmakaraka sits and names Rahu as Atmakaraka in two cases. It does not state the number of karakas, the ordered list, or how the Atmakaraka is selected. The 7-versus-8 question and Putrakaraka remain unresolved on every page checked.

### Cited Findings
- Karakamsha defined: "Kārakāṁśa refers to the navāṁśa (and/or other divisional charts) where the ātmakāraka is placed." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- The same article refers to "other Charakāraka (temporal significators) as well" and gives no count. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- Rahu as Atmakaraka: "Rāhu was the ātmakāraka for [two named individuals]." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- Significator example: "Mercury as AK indicates that the native should control his speech and be truthful at all times." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- Rath's Upadesha book page lists "Correct usage of chara, sthira and naisargiha karkas" as a topic, with no rules. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutra/)
- Karakamsha, natural-zodiac reading: "treat the Kārakāṁśa as Aries and examine the planets in the houses from it." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/karakamsa-and-natural-zodiac/) This is the natural-zodiac method.
- "Planets in the ninth and twelfth houses from kārakāṁśa show the dharma and iṣṭa devatā." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/karakamsa-and-natural-zodiac/)
- The page uses the phrase "reckoned from the Karakamsha (in the Navamsha)" for the navamsa reference. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/karakamsa-and-natural-zodiac/)
- "Count from the 10th house to kārakāṁśa- we get 4 signs." followed by "We do not have any dictum or reason to extend this to other houses of the natural zodiac." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/karakamsa-and-natural-zodiac/) The context of this count is not stated on the page.
- A search excerpt says the guiding deity of a chart is seen from the 12th house from the Atmakaraka in the navamsa, called Karakamsa. Not opened. [Rath's site, search excerpt](https://srath.com/celebrity/politics/shiv-sena-party/)
- "the twelfth house from kārakāṁśa, also called jīvanamuktāṁśa shows release from the cycle of rebirth or mokṣa." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- "bandha or bondage in the cycle of rebirth is examined from the seventh house from kārakāṁśa." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- Rath's Upadesha book page lists "Distinction between karakamsa and swamsa" as a topic. No difference is stated. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutra/)

### Inferences
- Karakamsha frame: the pages count from Karakamsha in two settings, as Aries in the natural zodiac and in the navamsa. The pages do not state how the two relate.
- The Atmakaraka article treats Rahu as a possible Atmakaraka, so Rath's scheme, in those charts, includes Rahu. The page does not say whether Rahu is always counted, so this does not settle 7 versus 8.
- Because the article refers to "other" karakas without listing them, the full scheme must be in a source not yet checked.

### Gaps
- **Number of chara karakas (7 or 8):** not stated on any fetched page.
- **Ordered list and Atmakaraka selection rule (highest degree; treatment of Rahu's degree):** not stated on any fetched page.
- **Putrakaraka:** not stated on any fetched page.
- **Unlinked claim:** a search summary says Rath uses 8 karakas for human charts and 7 for mundane charts, and that he always uses 8 for humans, contrary to Parashara's rule for Rahu. No URL was tied to this, so it is not recorded as a finding. [Unlinked]
- **Unlinked titles:** "The final proof of two carakäraka schemes" (SJC USA, 2003, presenter listed as Pt. Sanjay Rath) appears in search results with no URL. A Scribd document whose URL contains SJC-India-2004 appears in results; provenance unclear. Neither was opened. [Unlinked]
- **Karakamsha:** how Karakamsha is computed from a chart, and how Swamsha differs from it, is not stated on any fetched page.
- **Unfetched secondary link:** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmakaraka appeared in search results and was not opened. The standard rule (highest degree in any sign) is from a search summary, not a fetched page. [Unlinked]

## Key question 2: How does Rath choose the Chara Dasha direction and sub-period rules?

### Takeaway
No Chara Dasha direction or sub-period rule was found in Rath's public text. The pages checked either list "Dasha" as a chapter title, name dasha systems without rules, or cover other dasha systems. This question is open.

### Cited Findings
- Volume I of the Upadesha Sutras course lists chapter 13 "Dasha" and chapter 14 "Charakaraka," with no sub-headings and no rule text. The listing says "19 chapters" but names 21. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-scholar-course-book/)
- The Upadesha Sutras page lists other dasha systems by name, including Narayana (Padakrama), Sthira, Shoola, Brahma, Varnada, Mandooka, Paryaya (Gochara), Kendradi and Sudasa. Chara Dasha is not named, and no rules are given. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutra/)
- **Narayana Dasha (not Chara Dasha):** the page says the daśā starts from the stronger of the lagna or the 7th house, which it calls the "seat of truth." The page also says: "To ascertain the stronger of the two signs, the lagna or the 7th, following rules can be followed." The rules are not listed. Book blurb on a listing page (Sagar Publications, 1998). [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/page/63/)
- The dasha category page 1 returned five titles: Mūla Daśā, Introduction 01, Nārāyaṇa Daśā Preface, Vimshottari Dasha #2, Shula Dasha. None is Chara Dasha. The listing is paginated and known to be incomplete: a search result showed Naisargika Dasha and Dvisaptati Sama Dasa under the same path, and neither appeared in the fetch. Page 2 was not checked. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/category/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/dasa/)
- A guessed URL, srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/dasa/chara-dasha/, returned HTTP 404. This does not show the page is absent.
- **Other dashas, search excerpt only (not Chara):**
  - Mula Dasa: the search excerpt says that when the lagna is an odd sign, the dasas of planets in the panapara houses (2nd, 5th, 8th, 11th) follow the kendra dasas. [Rath's site, search excerpt](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/dasa/mula-dasa/)
  - Naisargika Dasha: the search excerpt gives the sub-period order by house type: kendra houses (1, 4, 7, 10), then panapara (2, 5, 8, 11), then apoklimas (3, 6, 9, 12). [Rath's site, search excerpt](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/dasa/naisargika-dasha/)

### Inferences
- Rath's site covers several other dasha systems, but none of the pages checked gives Chara Dasha rules. If he publishes a Chara Dasha method, it is outside the pages checked.
- The Narayana Dasha start rule and the Mula and Naisargika sub-period rules are for other systems. They should not be carried over to Chara Dasha without a source.

### Gaps
- **Chara Dasha direction, sub-period order and period length:** not found on any fetched page.
- **Unlinked claim:** a search summary says Chara Dasha period length equals the distance from a sign to its lord. [Unlinked]
- **Unlinked claim:** a book listing says commonly taught Chara Dasha calculations do not follow the Upadesha Sutras' injunctions. No author was named, and the claim is not tied to Rath. [Unlinked]
- **Non-Rath book for comparison:** "Predicting Through Jaimini's Chara Dasha" (listing title only; the author attribution comes from a search summary and was not confirmed on the listing). [Secondary](https://exoticindiaart.com/book/details/predicting-through-jaimini-s-chara-dasha-nan800)
- **Dasha category pages 2 and later:** not checked.

## Key question 3: Which Jaimini points does Rath use for children and marriage (Putrakaraka, Upapada, Darakaraka)?

### Takeaway
On fetched pages, Rath's marriage points are A7 (Darapada) and the Upapada, labelled UL (A12). He ties them to Venus and Ketu and gives spouse readings from single charts. No page checked gives a Putrakaraka rule, a Darakaraka rule, or a children reading for A7 or UL.

### Cited Findings
- Labels: "upapada (A12 or UL)" and "dārā-pada (A7)." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- "The significators of these are Venus (marriage) and Ketu (renunciation and mokṣa)." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- "The dārāpada (A7) plays a significant role in sexual desires and their fulfilment or renunciation." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- "Venus in 7th house or A7 and Ketu/Rāhu in 12th house or UL can cause loss of spouse or two marriages" [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- Single-chart readings: "the upapada in the 12th house from āruḍha lagna shows that the native may tend to ignore his spouse," and "Upapada is in Libra with the 7th lord Jupiter promising a very good marriage and spiritual growth due to marriage." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- The Complexity article advises looking for "a connection between 12th house and/or Upapada (UL)" with Sun, Venus and Rahu. No calculation or effects are given. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/complexity-of-jaimini-sutra/)

### Inferences
- Rath's public marriage reading uses A7 and UL, with Venus and Ketu as significators. The counting rules are not given.
- His marriage material is illustrated by individual charts, not stated as general rules.

### Gaps
- **Upapada calculation and exceptions:** not stated. The page names UL as A12 only.
- **A7 calculation and exceptions:** not stated.
- **Putrakaraka:** no rule on any fetched page. A search summary says that in the 7-karaka scheme the Putrakaraka is fifth, and that in one 8-karaka ranking the Moon is the Putrakaraka. Neither is sourced, so neither is recorded as a finding. [Unlinked]
- **Darakaraka:** not mentioned on any fetched page.
- **Children reading for A7 or UL:** not stated on any fetched page.

## Key question 4: Which parts are Rath's own published method, and which are general Jaimini tradition he cites?

### Takeaway
Rath's fetched pages show his own positions on Karakamsha, on A7 and UL as marriage points, and on the Narayana Dasha start rule. The traditions he names explicitly are Parashara (BPHS), for rashi drishti and one arudha reference, and the commentator Somanatha, for the Gaunapada reading. Which of his other positions are his own and which are inherited cannot be determined from the pages checked.

### Cited Findings

#### Rashi drishti
- "Parashara tells us to use the Rashi Drishtis (sign aspects)" early in BPHS, before graha drishtis. The page attributes this to Parashara. [Rath's own text, fetched; authorship within page unclear](https://srath.com/?p=3437). On this page, the "Tradition" and "Course Overview" sections are credited to Andrew Foss, and Rath is listed as author.
- "Rashi drsti can cause permanent damage," contrasted with graha drishti. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/complexity-of-jaimini-sutra/)
- The author argues the sutra is not limited to rashi drishti, because "Parashara taught this even before he taught Graha dristi." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/complexity-of-jaimini-sutra/)
- The course lists chapter 12 "Drishti," without the word rashi. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-scholar-course-book/)

#### Arudha padas
- Chapter 17 of Volume I is "Arudha Pada." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-scholar-course-book/)
- In a discussion of one sutra on eyesight, Rath cites the commentator Somanatha's reading, "Gaunapada means Panchama Arudha (A5)." He gives his own reading: Gauna-Pada as "the first vyaya-pada as taught by Maharsi Parashara in BPHS," linked to the 12th house. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/complexity-of-jaimini-sutra/)
- The article quotes a teaching as "the feet shall go to where we put our money." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/complexity-of-jaimini-sutra/)
- **Arudha calculation method:** not stated on any fetched page.

#### Jaimini yogas
- The Upadesha page lists special ascendants for timing rajyoga, and longevity, as topics. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutra/)
- Chapter 19 of Volume I is "Special Ascendants." [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-scholar-course-book/)
- **No Jaimini yoga definitions or rules were found on any fetched page.**

#### Provenance and attribution
- The Upadesha book is described as "a lucid translation of the upadesa sutras of maharishi jaimini," with references to standard texts where needed. [Rath's own text, fetched](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutra/)
- The page says Jaimini titled his work the "Upadesha" sutras to indicate they explain Parashara's teachings. [Rath's own text, fetched; authorship within page unclear](https://srath.com/?p=3437)
- Positions visible on the fetched pages and labelled as Rath's own text: Karakamsha as Aries in the natural-zodiac reading; the 9th and 12th from Karakamsha for dharma and ishta devata; A7 and UL with Venus and Ketu as significators; the 12th from Karakamsha as jivanamuktamsha; the Narayana Dasha start rule. The pages do not say whether these are inherited from a named tradition.

### Inferences
- Rath attributes some rules to Parashara (rashi drishti, the BPHS arudha reference) and presents the Upadesha Sutras as explaining Parashara. The boundary between his method and inherited material is drawn only in places where he names a source.
- On the pages checked, no statement separates a Rath innovation from a traditional rule for karakas, Chara Dasha, Karakamsha, A7 or UL.

### Gaps
- **Own versus inherited:** not determinable for the Chara Karaka scheme, Chara Dasha, or the Karakamsha and A7/UL rules from the pages checked.
- **Rashi drishti mechanics** (which signs aspect which): not stated on any fetched page.
- **Arudha calculation:** not stated on any fetched page.
- **Yogas:** no rules found.
- **Coverage:** only Volume I of the course listing was checked. Other volumes were not checked, so the absence of a topic here is not proof it is absent from his work.
