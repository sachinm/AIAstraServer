## 1. What order does he follow to judge how much wealth is promised and where it comes from?

### Takeaway
In Crux, Rath starts with the 2nd house as stored wealth (Jupiter as dhana karaka) and income from the 11th counted from Arudha Lagna (AL). He finds losses in the 12th from AL. He then confirms with Hora Lagna, Artha Saham, D-2, D-9, argala and the special lagnas, and times the result with Sudasa, Narayana dasa or Vimsottari. I found no single numbered "wealth checklist" in his public text. The order below is pieced together from Crux chapters V, XIV and XV.

### Cited findings
- 2nd house: accumulated wealth, assets, family, speech and food. Jupiter is its natural karaka. Hora Lagna is treated as the "wealth point". (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. V The Second House, §5.1)
- Planets in the 2nd (Rath's own text: Crux §5.2):
  - Sun: good income with lavish spending.
  - Saturn: hard-won income and spending on unwanted causes.
  - Moon: large wealth, unstable if the Sun or Mars aspects it. A waxing Moon raises income and a waning Moon lowers it.
  - Venus aspect: gives wealth. Jupiter aspect: removes harm.
  - Nodes or Saturn affliction: can wipe wealth out.
  - Strong Jupiter in the 2nd: still a dhana yoga, even though a karaka in its own house is normally weakened.
- 2nd lord by house (Rath's own text: Crux §5.3.1–5.3.2):
  - In the lagna: frugal, earns by own effort.
  - In the 2nd: wealth grows.
  - In the 6th: gains through victory over opponents, and losses through the same enemies.
  - Any link to the 12th turns that source of wealth into a source of loss.
- Arudha checks for the 2nd house (Rath's own text: Crux §5.3.3–5.3.4):
  - Dhanapada (A-2) shows the source of income and family relations. A-2 in a dusthana from AL weakens family ties.
  - Upapada in the 2nd: wealth through the spouse.
  - Varnada Lagna in the 2nd: wealth and status. Varnada in the 12th: questionable activities.
- Supporting checks (Rath's own text: Crux §5.3.5):
  - Read Artha Saham, Hora Lagna, D-2 and D-9 together with Sudasa, Vimsottari or Narayana dasa.
  - Malefics or papargala on the lagna bring loss. Strength and subhargala on the lagna bring profit.
- Sources of wealth by house (Rath's own text: Crux §5.4, table):
  - 2nd: financial institutions and inheritance.
  - 11th: gains.
  - 8th: insurance and dowry.
  - 6th: loans and service.
- Argala on a karaka (Rath's own text: Crux Ch. II, §6):
  - Planets in the 2nd, 4th and 11th from a karaka give direct argala, read as visible wealth: the 2nd is bank balance, the 4th immovable property, the 11th movable property.
  - The 5th and 8th give indirect argala, read as invisible wealth. The 8th is tied to inheritance and hidden wealth.
  - An empty 2nd can mean the person prefers building wealth through property rather than savings.
  - Worked example: J.R.D. Tata, with Jupiter as dhana karaka and its argalas (and what blocks them) deciding whether wealth is kept.
- Income is read from the 11th from AL and losses from the 12th from AL (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. XIV The Eleventh House, §14.3):
  - Each planet's natural signification names a source of income. Sun: government or father. Moon: mother or bureaucrats. Mars: younger siblings or the armed forces.
  - Natural benefics in the 11th from AL show legitimate income. Malefics there show illegitimate income.
  - Malefics in the 6th from AL can show money gained by bad means.
  - Ashtakavarga: bindus in the 11th show income and bindus in the 12th show expenditure. A higher SAV total in the 11th than the 12th means income exceeds expenditure.
- Source of income (Rath's own text: Crux §14.9, Charts 133–135):
  - Sun in the 11th from AL: government income.
  - Venus in the 11th from AL: gain through clothing or service.
  - Ketu in the 11th from AL: export income.
  - Rahu with Mars aspecting or in the 11th from AL: machines or exports.
  - The 10th from navamsa lagna or Karakamsa is also an income source.
- 11th lord's house shows the channel of gain (Rath's own text: Crux §14.8):
  - In or aspecting the lagna: own effort.
  - In the 2nd: family wealth.
  - In the 7th: business or in-laws.
  - In the 9th: learning, travel, father or temples.
  - In the Upapada: marriage into a wealthy family.
  - The house holding A-11 (labha pada) is always favoured by the native.
- Losses (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. XV The Twelfth House, §15.7, §15.8, §15.10):
  - Planets that give gains in the 11th from AL turn into losses if they are also linked to the 12th from AL. People those planets signify act as secret enemies.
  - The 12th has argala on the 11th, so the need to spend pushes the person to earn more.
  - 12th lord in the 2nd: loss of wealth. Planets in the 12th cause losses to the houses they own.
  - A 12th lord in a dusthana with another dusthana lord can give vipareeta raja yoga.
  - Moon in the 12th from AL: loss of raja yoga. (Rath's own text: Crux §15.4)
- Ascendant lord placement (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. IV, §4.4):
  - In the 2nd: wealthy, though children may be delayed.
  - In the 3rd: wealthy.
  - In the 6th: debts and ill health, eased by benefic aspect.
- Poverty example: Chart 28 (abject poverty) shows Kalasarpa on the 2/8 axis with Kemadruma and Rahu placements. Jupiter's placement can cancel it. (Rath's own text: Crux §5.5)
- Secondary (non-Rath forum post, no byline): if the 11th from the lagnapada (AL) is occupied or aspected, the native is "happy and rich". This matches Crux §14.3 and is probably drawn from the Jaimini tradition. https://www.tapatalk.com/groups/vedicastrology/arudha-lagna-arudha-padas-t161.html

### Inferences
- A practical order consistent with Crux:
  1. Promise: 2nd house, 2nd lord, Jupiter, and argala on the lagna and on Jupiter.
  2. Visible income: 11th from AL, its occupants, the 11th lord, A-11, and SAV of the 11th against the 12th.
  3. Drains: 12th from AL, 12th lord, links between the 2nd lord and the 12th.
  4. Confirmation: Hora Lagna, Sri Lagna, Artha Saham, D-2, D-4, D-9, D-10 and D-16.
  5. Timing: Sudasa, then Narayana dasa or Vimsottari.
- He keeps two kinds of wealth apart. "Real" stored wealth comes from the 2nd and Jupiter. Perceived, visible earnings come from the arudha (11th and 12th from AL).

### Gaps
- I did not find a numbered wealth-judgment procedure written by Rath himself. The order above is an inference.
- I could not reach a dedicated srath.com article on wealth or dhana yogas. None appears on the current index.

## 2. How does he use Arudha Lagna, Hora Lagna, Ghatika Lagna, Sri Lagna and Indu Lagna for finance? Which D-2 scheme does he use?

### Takeaway
- **AL** gives the perceived flow of money: income in the 11th from AL and loss in the 12th from AL.
- **Hora Lagna (HL)** is the wealth lagna. A planet linked to both the lagna and HL becomes a wealth-giver.
- **Ghatika Lagna (GL)** stands for power, not money.
- **Sri Lagna (SL)** is the Lakshmi or prosperity lagna and is the starting point of Sudasa.
- **Indu Lagna** is named in passing as a wealth lagna, but I found no rules from Rath.
- **D-2:** Rath questions reading the standard Parashari Sun/Moon hora as a wealth chart. Secondary sources say his tradition uses a "Kashinatha hora" variant for wealth.

### Cited findings
- A planet that aspects, conjoins or rules both the lagna and Hora Lagna becomes a **dhana yogada** (wealth-giver). A planet that influences the lagna, HL and GL together is a **raja yogada**. HL signifies wealth and sustenance; GL signifies power and authority. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. II, §2.2)
- Sri Lagna and Indu Lagna are named as special ascendants for wealth and prosperity, with no further detail at that point. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. I, §7.4)
- Sri Lagna is the lagna plus an addition based on the Moon's progress through its nakshatra. It is used to time wealth in the Carnegie and N.T. Rama Rao examples. (Rath's own text: Crux §5.5)
- HL with the Moon: fame and birth into a notable family. HL linked to the 4th or 4th lord: property. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. VII The Fourth House, Chart 48)
- In the Chandraswami example, HL is used to show what kind of income he had. (Rath's own text: Crux §5.5, Chart 27)
- Sri Lagna calculation (secondary: PVR Lesson 15):
  1. Take the fraction of the Moon's nakshatra already traversed (the same fraction used for the Vimsottari balance).
  2. Apply that fraction to 360 degrees and add the result to the lagna degree.
  - SL is associated with Lakshmi, and the strength of SL and its lord shows the level of prosperity.
  - Per Jaimini, a planet exalted or debilitated in SL makes one affluent.
- Name dispute (secondary forum, unattributed): some argue SL should be called Tara Lagna, while Rath's centre calls it Sri Lagna. https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1484937-sudasa-calc/
- D-2:
  - In Crux, the Hora (D-2) is listed against wealth, but Rath questions that common reading and ties the hora to life and health. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. II, §7)
  - Fortune and immovable property go to the D-4. Movable property and luxuries go to the D-16. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. II, §7)
  - Secondary, author not confirmed (Scribd handout): the parivritti hora shows family support, not wealth. The variant that shows wealth is called "Kashinatha Hora", after Pt. Kashinath Rath, Sanjay Rath's uncle and teacher. https://www.scribd.com/document/470778118/24412760-Hora-D-2-chart-pdf
  - Secondary (Scribd document on the Jagannatha Hora software): the software offers six D-2 variants, Kashinatha Hora among them. https://www.scribd.com/document/514281796/Kupdf-net-Jagannath-Hora
- Artha (money) saham = 2nd house cusp minus 2nd lord plus lagna, reversed for night births. Planets or signs influencing a saham time its event. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. I, §7.3)

### Inferences
- For Rath, HL is the real wealth lagna. A lord or aspect shared by the lagna and HL marks the wealth-giving planet, and the GL axis separates power from money.
- Do not treat the plain Sun/Moon D-2 as Rath's wealth chart. His own text doubts it, and his school's wealth hora is a named variant. D-4 and D-16 carry property and possessions in Crux.

### Gaps
- I found no Rath-bylined public text giving the Kashinatha hora's construction rule, or confirming it as his default.
- Indu Lagna: Rath names it, but I found no rules from him for it.
- I found no public Rath text on how GL differs from HL in dasa timing beyond §2.2.

## 3. Which dasha does he recommend for timing wealth, and what are its rules?

### Takeaway
Crux names the Lagnadi rasi dasa, called **Sudasa** (Sri Lagna kendradi rasi dasa), for timing finance, fortune and marriage. Narayana dasa is used for changes in life and effort, and Vimsottari for sub-timing. Rath gives one condition: prefer Sudasa over Narayana dasa only when the Moon sign is stronger. The detailed calculation rules found publicly are by his student P.V.R. Narasimha Rao.

### Cited findings
- The Lagnadi rasi dasa (Sudasa) is generally used to time finance, fortune and marriage. It is preferred to Narayana dasa only when the Moon sign is stronger. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. I, §4)
- Narayana dasa is better for timing changes in life. The 11th lord's Vimsottari dasa is generally unfavourable. (Rath's own text: Crux §14.8–14.9)
- Case studies tracing wealth through Sudasa tables (Rath's own text: Crux §5.5):
  - J.R.D. Tata and Andrew Carnegie: wealth growth traced through the 2nd lord, argala, Sri Lagna and Sudasa.
  - N.T. Rama Rao: Sudasa with Gajakesari yoga explains his rise and decline.
- Queen Victoria (Rath's own text: Crux §5.5): Mercury in the 12th, normally a poverty sign, gave wealth through Varnada Lagna and its link with the lagna lord. Fortunes rose in Mercury dasa.
- Financial turns can also be timed by the sub-sub-period of the relevant lord in D-16. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. VII, §7.5)
- Sudasa rules (secondary: PVR Lesson 15):
  - **Start sign:** always the SL sign, unlike Narayana dasa, which starts from the stronger of the lagna and the 7th.
  - **Order:** kendras from SL (1, 4, 7, 10), then panapharas (2, 5, 8, 11), then apoklimas (3, 6, 9, 12).
  - **Direction:** forward if SL is in an odd sign, backward if even. Saturn in SL forces forward. Ketu in SL reverses the direction.
  - **Length of a dasa:** count from the dasa sign to its lord, minus one. Count forward for odd-footed signs (Ar, Ta, Ge, Li, Sc, Sg) and backward for the rest. A lord in its own sign gives 12 years. Add 1 year if the lord is exalted and subtract 1 if debilitated.
  - **First dasa:** only the part of the SL sign not yet traversed at birth is left.
  - **Antardasas:** equal length, worked out by treating the dasa sign as SL. PVR says Narayana dasa antardasa rules give poorer results here.
- What a Sudasa sign brings (secondary: PVR Lesson 15):
  - Holds or aspects GL or its 7th: power and authority.
  - Holds or aspects HL or its 7th: wealth and financial prosperity.
  - Holds or aspects a bhava: prosperity of that bhava.
  - Holds or aspects Jupiter: prosperity through children.
  - Holds or aspects Ketu: spiritual prosperity.
  - Holds or aspects the lagna or lagna lord: lasting results.
  - Sudasa shows prosperity and well-being; Narayana dasa shows effort and environment.
- Caution (secondary blog, author not confirmed): relying too heavily on Sudasa may mislead, because it is a "materialistic dasa". https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2007/05/sudasa-calculations.html
- Kendradi graha dasas apply only if at least one of the Sun, Moon or lagna is strong. This was a search-result summary of a Rath book excerpt and I did not see the passage myself, so treat it as unverified.
- Rise and fall through the dasa lord's co-operators (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. II, §3):
  - A Pachak friendly to the dasa lord brings wealth and possessions.
  - A friendly Vedhak brings money loss and travel.
  - Planets in the 6th, 8th and 12th from the dasa lord act as enemies.
- Sarvatobhadra latha effects (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. I, §7.2): Mars latha brings financial losses, Saturn latha poverty and loss of livelihood, Rahu latha loss of livelihood, Mercury latha business losses.

### Inferences
- Model: Sudasa from SL sets the prosperity windows, with HL contact as the money trigger. Narayana dasa gives the effort and change context, and Vimsottari plus transits narrow the timing.
- The "Moon sign stronger" condition in Crux suggests he does not apply Sudasa to every chart.

### Gaps
- Rath's own step-by-step Sudasa rules (as opposed to PVR's) were not found in public text. His *Narayana Dasa* book was located (scienceoflight.net PDF) but not read, and its copyright status is unclear.
- I found no stated Rath rule for Sudasa pratyantardasa or for combining it with transits.

## 4. Debt, sudden gains and losses, speculation, and remedies

### Takeaway
In Crux, debts come mainly from Mars and the 8th (from the dasa planet or dasa sign) and from the 2nd lord with Saturn. The 6th is loans and service. The 8th is inheritance, insurance, hidden wealth and business loans. Speculation shows up only as the 8th from the D-10 lagna. Remedies in Crux are tied to individual cases: fasting on the weekdays of planets in the 12th, Ganesha, Surya and Shanti mantras. The contents of *Vedic Remedies in Astrology* on wealth could not be checked.

### Cited findings
- Debts (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. XI The Eighth House):
  - Mars signifies debts. (§11.1)
  - Debts and strife come when Mars is in the 8th from the Vimsottari dasa planet, or in the 8th from the Narayana dasa sign with no benefics in the 12th. (§11.10)
  - The 2nd lord with Saturn, or the 8th lord, can bring debt in its period. (§11.10)
  - Saturn gives inheritance from the dead. (§11.1)
  - A benefic 8th favours business over service. (§11.10)
  - A bank loan to start a business is linked to the 8th lord. (§11.10)
- 6th house (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. IX, §9.1–9.3, §9.6):
  - It is enemies, service and relatives. Enemies are read from the 6th from AL and from Shatru pada (A-6).
  - Malefics in the 6th help in competition.
  - The chapter includes a theft-and-recovery case.
- 8th as hidden wealth, inheritance and insurance (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. II, §6, and §5.4 table).
- Speculation: in the D-10, the 8th from the dasamsa lagna shows business loans, gambling and inherited business. (Rath's own text: Crux, Ch. II, §7) I found no other Rath rule on speculation.
- 12th as loss, and its remedy: the best remedy for planets in the 12th is fasting on the days they rule. (Rath's own text: Crux §15.7)
- Spendthrift: the lagna lord or the AK's dispositor aspecting the 12th from itself. (Rath's own text: Crux §15.8)
- Case-specific remedies in Crux:
  - A Ganesha mantra in an income and job-change example. (§6, Chart 33)
  - Surya mantra, Shanti mantras, a crystal mala, propitiating the Gnati karaka, and pilgrimage for 6th-house troubles. (Ch. IX)
- *Vedic Remedies in Astrology* (Sagar Publications, listed 2007) is described by its publisher as gathering Vedic remedies and showing how to choose them from the chart. Its wealth-remedy content was not available to read. https://www.exoticindia.com/book/details/vedic-remedies-in-astrology-idj596
- Lakshmi remedies (Sri Sukta, Friday lamps) appear only on commercial blogs not tied to Rath. They are excluded and not attributed to him.

### Inferences
- Within Rath's own framework, Sri Lagna is the Lakshmi lagna. A wealth remedy would plausibly target the SL lord, the HL-linked planet or the planets in the 12th from AL, but I found no Rath text that states this.

### Gaps
- No public Rath text on Lakshmi or Sri remedies for financial hardship was found.
- No Rath rule specific to sudden windfalls (lottery or 8th-house sudden gains) was found.
- Crux pages after §15.14 and the final part of the 11th chapter were not read.
