## 1. What sequence does he follow for mother and father, and how does he use D12?

### Takeaway
In Crux he reads each parent from a house (4th mother, 9th father, with the 10th also affecting the father), a fixed significator (Moon, Sun), the house's arudha (Matru pada A4) and the chara Matrikaraka, then confirms in the D12, where the parent's house is counted from both the D12 lagna and the D12 luminary. Longevity of a parent uses the 1st/8th/10th lords counted from the parent's house, and timing of a parent's death uses a parent-specific Shoola dasa (Matru / Pitri shoola), refined with Narayana dasa, Vimsottari and the Sudarshana chakra.

### Cited Findings
Mother (Rath's own text, Crux ch. VII "The Fourth House", pp. 76-81):
- 4th house significations: mother (Moon), vehicles (Venus), home (Ketu), land (Mars), formal education (Mercury), childhood, happiness. Supporting vargas listed: D4 home, **D12 mother and elders**, D16 vehicles, D2, D10, D24 education, **D40 maternal karma**, **D45 paternal karma** — [Crux-archive], ch. VII §7.1, p.76.
- Planets in the 4th have argala on lagna; Moon there gives mother's blessings; Jupiter protective. **[SENSITIVE]** Saturn with Moon in 4th is given as early loss of mother; Rahu in 4th as risk to mother — [Crux-archive], §7.2, pp.76-77.
- Matru Arudha (A4) read from the Arudha Lagna: 3rd/11th from AL friendly, 6th enmity, 8th a poorer maternal family, 12th losses — [Crux-archive], §7.3, pp.77-78.
- **[SENSITIVE]** Chara Matrikaraka in a dusthana from A4 is read as poor maternal health — [Crux-archive], §7.3, pp.77-78. (So he does use the chara Matrikaraka, alongside the Moon.)
- **[SENSITIVE] Mother's longevity rule:** treat the 4th as the mother's lagna and check the lords of the 1st, 8th and 10th counted from it; one strong = up to about 36 years, two = up to about 72, three = beyond 72. He cites BPHS, Jaimini Upadesa Sutras and Jataka Parijata for it — [Crux-archive], Chart 40, pp.80-81; also stated generally in the Longevity chapter (mother's 1st, 8th, 10th = native's 4th, 11th, 1st), p.26.
- **[SENSITIVE] Timing mother's death:** Matru Shoola dasa (9 years per sign) — in Chart 40 the dasas start from Virgo and the mother died in the first dasa; Chart 43 again uses Matru shoola dasa — [Crux-archive], pp.80-81, 84-85.
- Separation from parents in infancy (child raised by grandparents) timed in a one-year Taurus Narayana dasa — [Crux-archive], Chart 44, pp.85-86.
- Mother-in-law: Venus as fixed karaka for spouse and in-laws; **[SENSITIVE]** mother-in-law's death timed with Dara Shoola dasa — [Crux-archive], Charts 41-42, pp.81-84.

Father (Rath's own text, Crux ch. XII "The Ninth House", pp. 214-222):
- 9th house = father, Sun = main significator; the 10th house also affects the father: a malefic in the 10th harms father and fortune, Saturn there gives big swings, nodes there are hostile to the father — [Crux-archive], §12.1-12.2, p.214.
- **[SENSITIVE]** Rahu afflicting the Sun can mean early loss of father — [Crux-archive], §12.8, p.215.
- Sun and Jupiter in the 9th are exempt from karaka-bhava-nasha; Sun in the 9th supports the father and makes the native dutiful to him — [Crux-archive], §12.8/12.11, pp.215-217.
- 9th lord with lagna lord: native receives the father's teachings; 9th lord in the 3rd: father described as untrustworthy — [Crux-archive], §12.9, p.216. (Also in the 3rd-house chapter: 9th lord in the 3rd suggests a debauched father, p.66-67.)
- Remedies tied to the 9th: yellow sapphire for Rahu, ruby for Saturn in the 9th harming father/guru — [Crux-archive], §12.8, p.215.
- A table of planetary years for the 9th (e.g. Sun 21-22 primary, 48 and 70 secondary) is given for fortune/father events — [Crux-archive], §12.10, p.216.
- Father's saham: Saturn - Sun + Asc (day), reversed at night; mother's saham Moon - Venus + Asc (day) — [Crux-archive], intro p.10.

D12 Dwadasamsa (Rath's own text):
- D12 governs parents, elders and heritage/lineage — [Crux-archive], intro p.20; D12 named among the key vargas used in birth-time rectification, p.21.
- **D12 procedure for relatives:** father = 9th from D12 lagna and 9th from D12 Sun; mother = 4th from D12 lagna and 4th from D12 Moon; paternal grandfather = 5th; grandmother = 12th — [Crux-archive], ch. XII, p.214.
- **[SENSITIVE]** If the lord of the relevant D12 "ascendant" (the relative's house in D12) sits in a dusthana in the rasi chart, that relative may die before or just after the native's birth; conjunction with a strong Jupiter or Mercury saves them. Example given: Sun in the rasi 8th with D12 lagna Leo or Pisces = father dies before birth — [Crux-archive], p.214.
- **[SENSITIVE]** Maraka logic for the father: the 2nd and 7th from the father's house (i.e. the 10th and 3rd from lagna) and planets there/aspecting are death-inflicting; death comes in their dasas — [Crux-archive], p.214.
- **[SENSITIVE]** Worked case (Srila Prabhupada): father read from the 9th of the D12 (Cancer D12 lagna); father's death in 1930 in Jupiter-Sun Vimsottari, linked to Pitri Shoola dasa of Scorpio with the Sun as fixed father-karaka, in the father's 35th year — [Crux-archive], pp.221-222.
- Second-house chapter case: father's death (1894) read from the 9th lord conjoining the 8th lord in the 10th of a Narayana-dasa pravesha (solar-ingress) chart, with Jupiter and Rahu in the 8th from lagna; inheritance yogas taken as a sign of a parent's or relative's death — [Crux-archive], ch. V, Chart 23, pp.52-54. **[SENSITIVE]**

Secondary (not Rath, do not attribute): commercial sites state Sun/Moon in D12 and D12 4th/9th for mother/father, e.g. https://jagannathhora.com/dwadasamsa-chart-d12-parents-ancestry/ and https://astrosight.ai/divisional-charts/dwadasamsa-chart-d12-parents — consistent with Crux but no byline to Rath.

### Inferences
- Implied reading order for a parent: (1) house and its lord from lagna, plus the 10th for father; (2) fixed karaka (Moon/Sun); (3) arudha of the house read from AL (relationship and family fortune); (4) chara karaka placement from that arudha (health); (5) D12, counting the parent's house from both D12 lagna and the D12 luminary; (6) longevity via 1st/8th/10th lords from the parent's house; (7) timing via parent's Shoola dasa, then Narayana / Vimsottari / Sudarshana. This order is assembled from the chapter structure; he does not state it as a numbered list.
- Lineage karma is split: D45 (paternal) and D40 (maternal) for inherited karma, D60 for past-birth karma, D12 for the relatives themselves.

## 2. What does he publish on siblings and D3?

### Takeaway
Crux ch. VI gives a full sibling method: younger siblings from the 3rd, elder from the 11th, counted from both lagna (physical presence) and Arudha Lagna (number), with Mars and the chara Bhratrikaraka; individual siblings are picked out in the D3 by a "frog jump" (manduka gati) through alternate houses; deaths and disputes are timed by Bhratri Shoola dasa and D3/D4.

### Cited Findings
All Rath's own text, Crux ch. VI "The Third House", pp.65-75, unless noted:
- 3rd governs co-borns, courage, mental strength, skills, arms; upachaya; malefics give valour, benefics timidity — p.65.
- Younger siblings = 3rd, elder = 11th, from lagna and from AL. From lagna they show physical presence; from AL, number. Counting from AL is zodiacal for odd signs and reverse for even — §6.2, pp.65-66.
- Number of siblings = count of planets conjoining or aspecting (rasi drishti) the 3rd lord (younger) or 11th lord (elder) — §6.2, p.66.
- **[SENSITIVE]** Strong malefics in the 3rd from lagna harm younger siblings; Mars there generally denies younger brothers (Mars in Cancer an exception giving a strong one); Saturn in 3rd/11th from AL harmful to siblings, and with Rahu indicates a sibling's death; Venus in the 8th suggests a stillborn or premature sibling before the native; Venus joining/aspecting 3rd or 11th removes younger/elder siblings — §6.2, pp.65-66.
- D3 for individual siblings: first younger = 3rd lord from D3 lagna, next = 5th lord, and so on by manduka gati; elder siblings from the 11th lord, then 9th lord, same jumps — p.66. Intro chapter restates: sequence via lords of 5th, 7th, 9th, 11th from D3 lagna; Saturn and Mars indicate brothers, Mercury and Sun sisters; he points to the Jaimini Sutras — intro pp.20-23.
- Bhratrikaraka (chara) used in examples: Venus as Bhratrikaraka in the 3rd shows a strong first younger sibling (Chart 7) — intro p.23.
- Co-born saham: Jupiter - Saturn + Asc — intro p.10.
- Twins case (Chart 34): birth of twin brothers timed in Ketu-Venus-Saturn Vimsottari; lagna derived from A3 — pp.69-70.
- **[SENSITIVE]** Death of co-borns: Bhratri Shoola dasa (9 years per sign) starting from the stronger of 3rd or 9th; Sudarshana chakra gives the year; antardasa rule uses the 3rd, 6th, 8th, 12th from the death-inflicting sign; Chart 36 shows two younger brothers lost in an accident — §6.7.3, pp.72-73.
- Disputes with siblings over property read from D3 together with D4, timed in the Sun dasa in Chart 37, ended by remedies — §6.7.4, pp.73-75.
- 3rd from AL as the pada of the 8th shows manner of death (for the native) — §6.3, p.66. **[SENSITIVE]**
- Lagna lord in the 3rd: brothers helpful — ch. IV, p.35.

### Inferences
- Sibling reading is the most fully specified family method in Crux: house + AL count + karaka + D3 ordinal + dedicated Shoola dasa.

## 3. What does he publish on curses and family karma, and what remedies does he attach?

### Takeaway
His curse framework restates Parashara's (BPHS ch. on loss of progeny) list of curses that deny or harm children, each tied to a planet and house (father = Sun/9th, mother = Moon/4th, brother = Mars/3rd, spouse = Venus/7th, maternal uncle = Mercury/6th, brahmana = Jupiter/9th, serpent = Rahu/5th, preta = unperformed rites), adds a test (a benefic or house afflicted by two or more of Mars, Saturn, Rahu), ranks a primary curse, times it in Mula dasa, and attaches both Parashara's prescribed remedies and planetary remedies. Most of the online detail is secondary (forum repost, Sarbani Rath).

### Cited Findings
- Rath's paper "Vedic Remedies for Curses" (also cited as "Vedic Remedies in Curses"), Parashara Vedic Astrology Conference, Belgrade, Serbia, July 2004; he also presented on curses in California 2002 and covers remedies in *Vedic Remedies in Astrology* (Sagar, New Delhi, 1999) — as cited in footnotes by Sarbani Rath, https://sarbanirath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/timing-curses-and-their-remedies-1/ (**secondary**, byline Sarbani Rath, posted 2025-04-07). The paper's Scribd copy (https://www.scribd.com/document/400749081/curses-Serbia-2004-pdf) returned HTTP 410 and could not be read. A search snippet attributes to that paper that a curse may be suffered or redeemed within one life and that the Rama taraka mantra is the highest remedy for curses (**secondary, snippet only, unverified**).
- Curse table attributed to him (**secondary**, IndiaDivine forum repost by a guest user, 2007-08-11, titled "By Pt. Sanjay Rath - All Shapas (Curses) in Horoscope - 1"; no original publication given), https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1475337-by-pt-sanjay-rath-all-shapas-curses-in-horoscope-1/ :
  - Sarpa shapa: Rahu; 5th, 5th lord, Jupiter. Remedy: worship of a gold Naga image; gifts of cow, land, sesame, gold.
  - Pitri shapa (father): Sun; 9th and 9th lord; combinations stress Sun and 9th lord in dusthanas and malefic affliction. Remedy: shraddha at Gaya with feeding of many brahmanas; kanyadana; gift of a pregnant cow.
  - Matri shapa (mother): Moon; 4th and 4th lord, plus the 5th/5th lord. Remedy: bath at Setubandha, a lakh of Gayatri japa; gifts and milk to brahmanas; circumambulating a pipal tree with name-recitation.
  - Bhratri shapa (brother): Mars; 3rd and 3rd lord (with 5th); often 3rd lord with Mars and Rahu in the 5th. Remedy: Vishnu nama japa, Chandrayana vrata, bath in Kaveri, planting pipal, gifts of cows and land.
  - Matula shapa (maternal uncle): Mercury; 6th. Remedy: install a Vishnu image, dig wells.
  - Brahma shapa: Jupiter; 9th. Remedies include Chandrayana and gifts.
  - Patni shapa (spouse): Venus; 7th. Remedy: give a girl in marriage; gift a Lakshmi-Narayana image, cows, beds, clothes (both spouses).
  - Preta shapa: from unperformed last rites. Remedy: gift a Brahma image after Rudra-snana; cows, silver.
  - Planet-level remedies: Mercury and Venus — Shiva worship (Mercury also Harivamsha listening); Moon and Jupiter — mantra, yantra, medicines; Rahu — kanyadana; Sun — Hari kirtana; Ketu — gift of a cow; Mars and Saturn — Rudra japa.
  - Detection test: a natural benefic or a house aspected by at least two of Mars, Saturn, Rahu indicates a curse; Jupiter's curse usually primary.
  - Timing: Mula dasa (Lagna Kendradi Graha dasa, Vimshottari derivative from Saravali), starting from the strongest of lagna, Moon or Sun; the most malefic planet in the curse delivers it.
- Sarbani Rath (**secondary**, same URL as above) adds: Atmakaraka involvement makes the curse operative; 8th house/lord involvement points to a past-birth origin, checked in D60; lagna lord or Atmakaraka involvement means the native bears it; remedying the primary curse removes subordinate ones; capacity to perform remedies judged from the 9th house and the D60 of the 9th lord.
- Crux on inherited karma (Rath's own text): paternal-line karma in the 9th of D45 (Akshavedamsa), maternal-line karma in D40 (Khavedamsa) and its 4th; past-birth karma in D60, given the highest Vimshopaka weight — [Crux-archive], ch. XII, p.215. The ninth-house chapter itself does not discuss curses (as far as read).
- Crux remedies in family contexts (Rath's own text): Ganesha worship advised in a career case (ch. VI Chart 33, pp.67-68); sibling property dispute ended after remedies (Chart 37, pp.73-75); gemstones for Rahu/Saturn in the 9th (p.215).
- Badhaka-sign rule for father's curse (Sun in the badhaka sign in rasi or navamsa) appears only in a non-Rath Medium post (https://medium.com/thoughts-on-jyotish/the-curses-from-the-father-ancestors-29a398ed817), which returned HTTP 403; byline not verified. **Do not attribute.**

### Inferences
- The family-curse list is classical (BPHS) material that Rath teaches and extends with a detection test, a primary-curse rule and Mula dasa timing; the extensions are documented mainly through his students' and forum reposts, not his own byline online.
- All curses in this list are framed in the source as causes of trouble with progeny (5th house), so "pitri / matri shapa" in his usage is primarily a children-denial diagnosis, not a general parent reading.

## 4. What fills the listed gaps (seventh-house chapter, Darakaraka, Putrakaraka, kutumba)?

### Takeaway
The Crux seventh-house chapter (pp.147-185) makes Venus the main marriage karaka, uses the 7th lord, Upapada, Darapada and navamsa, and prefers Narayana dasa over Vimshottari for marriage timing. The chara Darakaraka appears only in worked examples (read in the navamsa), not as a headline rule. He does name the chara Putrakaraka, but only in an example; children rules otherwise use the 5th, Jupiter and D7. The 2nd house is read for family through the 2nd lord and the A2 (dhana pada) from AL.

### Cited Findings
Seventh house (Rath's own text, [Crux-archive], ch. X "The Seventh House", pp.147-178 read; chapter runs to p.185):
- 7th governs marriage, sexual relations, foreign residence/travel, partnerships; also a maraka house — pp.147-148.
- Venus is the main significator of marriage and spouse; the 7th lord behaves like Venus; Jupiter is spouse significator for a woman (intro p.23) — pp.147-148.
- Per-planet effects in the 7th given (e.g. Jupiter learned and fortunate marriage; Venus cultured spouse; Saturn older spouse). **[SENSITIVE]** Mars there: short-lived spouse, friction; Rahu: bad marriage, widowhood or disease — pp.148-151.
- 7th lord in the 2nd or 3rd: difficult spouse; in the 3rd, danger to children — pp.148-149. **[SENSITIVE]**
- Darapada (A7) = business and sexual partners; Upapada = marriage and spouse; planets in UL show spouse's family; Sun in 12th from UL strains relations with father-in-law; Moon in 7th from UL a hostile mother-in-law — pp.149-151.
- Spouse's home region from the 7th lord counted from Venus: fixed sign same region, movable distant, dual nearby — pp.150-151.
- Timing: Vimshottari dasa planet should link to navamsa lagna/lord, antardasa to navamsa 7th lord; Vimshottari alone shortlists too many periods; Narayana dasa called more accurate (Charts 71-79); Jupiter transit over 7th, 7th lord or 2nd from UL; Sun transit as trigger; Sudarshana chakra with Venus as focal point; Vivaha saham — pp.151-163.
- Number of marriages: second from 8th from 7th and 8th from UL; third from the 9th — pp.167-169 (consistent with Chart 7 note, intro p.24).
- **[SENSITIVE]** 3rd from UL (Libra in one case) is the place of spouse's death; marrying in such a dasa risky unless Jupiter strong; debilitated Venus in the 6th flagged as a death-giving placement for spouse — pp.150-154, 162-169.
- Marriage denial / renunciation cases (Charts 76-77) timed with Drig dasa; UL non-functional with Jupiter in 6th from AL — pp.162-169.
- Mahabharata rule cited: marriage confirmed by one solar year of union — ch. VII, Chart 43, pp.84-85.

Darakaraka (Rath's own text):
- Chara Darakaraka Mercury in the 12th from the 7th in the navamsa used to identify the first wife (Chart 7) — [Crux-archive], intro p.24.
- On srath.com, the Darakaraka appears only in passing (Sun as chara Darakaraka in lagna, Chart 6); the essay's rules rest on Venus, the 7th, 12th, A7 and UL; Venus in 6th/12th from AL suggests loss or absence of spouse; Ketu in 7th with Venus in 12th or UL may show two spouses — https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/ (Rath's own text, byline Sanjay Rath; page date not reliable).
- His 7 vs 8 chara-karaka position (8 karakas for human charts, including Rahu) — his paper "The final proof of two chara karaka schemes", SJC USA conference 2003, as reported by search summary (**secondary**, not read directly).

Putrakaraka (Rath's own text):
- Named once in the parts read: chara Putrakaraka Mercury in the 6th is read as a sign of renunciation (Chart 77) — [Crux-archive], ch. X, approx. p.166. Children rules elsewhere use the 5th lord in D7 for the first child, 7th lord for the second, 8th from 5th in D7 for children from a second wife; saham Jupiter - Moon + Asc — intro pp.10, 24. Not named as a primary children rule in the sections read.

Second house / kutumba (Rath's own text, [Crux-archive], ch. V "The Second House", pp.48-58):
- 2nd covers wealth, family, eyes, face, speech, food; Jupiter natural karaka; 2nd lord likened to Vishnu the sustainer — p.48.
- 2nd lord in the 1st makes the 4th (family/home) hostile; **[SENSITIVE]** 2nd lord in the 2nd may harm offspring — p.49.
- A2 (dhana pada) from AL: in a dusthana, no firm family bond; in kendra or trikona, family members get along; in the 11th the native gains from family; in the 3rd the family gains from the native — p.49.
- UL in the 2nd: marriage into a wealthy family — pp.48-49. 2nd is 8th from 7th so 2nd lord in 2nd raises second-marriage chance — p.48.
- Family migration timed in Sudasa/Vimshottari (Carnegie case) — p.56. Dasas recommended for this house: Sudasa, Vimshottari, Narayana — p.49.

### Inferences
- For the product: spouse reading in Crux is Venus- and Upapada-led, with the chara Darakaraka as a navamsa confirmer, not a primary signal.
- Shoola dasas are his recurring family-death timer: Matru (mother), Pitri (father), Bhratri (siblings), Dara (spouse/in-laws), each 9 years per sign, started from the relevant house. **[SENSITIVE]** — product use would conflict with the "no general prediction / confirm first" rules; record only.
