Scope: how Sanjay Rath reads career. Covers the 10th house and its lord, the D10 (Dasamsa), Amatyakaraka, Arudha Lagna (AL) and the Rajya pada (A10), Karakamsa, raja yogas, and timing rules for jobs, promotion, job loss and business. Notes are paraphrased and written neutrally, not in his voice.

## 1. What sequence does he follow to judge profession type, and service versus business?

### Takeaway
Rath starts from the Rasi 10th house and 10th lord, with argala on and from the 10th. He then cross-checks the 10th from the Moon and from Hora Lagna, the 10th lord's navamsa dispositor and the 10th from Karakamsa, the Arudha Lagna and Rajya pada (A10), and the Amatyakaraka. The D10 decides the details. In the D10, the artha trikona (2, 6, 10) gives the profession type, and the 6th against the 7th decides service versus business. The 9th and 5th, and where the lagna lord sits, show whether the person prefers to be independent or subordinate.

### Cited Findings
**Rasi 10th house (Rath's own text, COVA-13 preview)**
- The 10th covers prosperity, karma or work, career, fame, status and power. Its significators are Jupiter (prosperity, fame), Saturn (karma), Mercury (career), the Moon (fame) and the Sun (status, power). It ranks second in importance only to the lagna. Low ashtakavarga bindus in the 10th weaken the whole chart. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **Argala:** the 11th, 1st, 2nd and 8th have argala on the 10th. The lagna's sukhargala on the 10th decides job satisfaction: a lagna planet friendly to the 10th-house planet gives satisfaction, an inimical one gives dissatisfaction. The 11th's dhanargala means incentives drive performance. The 8th's labhargala decides how a career ends. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **Planets in the 10th:**
  - Planets here are generally good. The Sun and Mars with directional strength give dynamism.
  - Saturn gives rapid rise and fall. Crux 1998 repeats this as "ups and downs in career graph" in Charts 124 and 126.
  - Ketu gives a routine job.
  - Mercury or Venus suit business. Mercury or Ketu give a fickle mind.
  — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **10th lord by house:**
  - In the 2nd, or with the 2nd lord, it shows a family business. Affliction disrupts that business.
  - In the 3rd it shows travel or short contracts.
  - In the 5th it shows research, occult work or speculation.
  - In the 1st or 2nd (simhasana yoga) its results come after middle age.
  — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **Paka Lagna:** the Paka Lagna is the sign of the lagna lord. If the 10th or its lord falls in the badhaka sign from the Paka Lagna or the AL, the career is obstructed. If it falls in a dusthana from them, the career is destroyed. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **Natural strength by lordship:** the ascending order of influence is 2nd, 12th, 5th, 9th, 3rd, 6th, 11th, 8th, 1st, 4th, 7th, 10th. This makes the 10th lord the most influential planet in the chart. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **10th from the Moon and from Hora Lagna (following Kalyana Varma):** after the 10th from the lagna, check the 10th from the Moon and the 10th from Hora Lagna. Table 13.3, taken from Saravali, lists the results of planets in the 10th from the Moon. Mars influencing the 10th from the Moon points to livelihood in a distant place. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house). Crux 1998 adds that malefics in the 10th from the Moon can make a remover of evils: a doctor, priest or astrologer. Crux 1998 Chart 107 (female, 27 Jul 1964, Bombay) illustrates this: Mars as 10th lord from the Moon in the 9th, and she moved north for a government job in July 1991. — [Crux 1998, §13.6, pp. 433–434](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)
- **Nakshatra table:** Table 13.1, from Dhruva Nadi, lists professions for each of the 27 nakshatras. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)

**Navamsa and Karakamsa layer (Rath's own text, Crux 1998, §13.7–13.8, pp. 434–435)**
- Planets in the 10th from navamsa lagna or from Karakamsa decide wealth. Malefics there bring fluctuation and losses; benefics bring stable fortune. — [Crux 1998](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)
- **10th lord's navamsa dispositor:** this planet can make or mar the career. If it is malefic and strongly inimical to the 10th lord, the 10th lord's dasa turns unfavourable, above all in the dispositor's own sub-period. — [Crux 1998](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)
- **10th lord's navamsa sign:** a fixed sign suggests a desk job and stability, a movable sign moving around for one's fortune, a dual sign mixed results. — [Crux 1998](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)
- **Source of wealth:** read from the planet that is lord of or placed in the 10th (citing Saravali). The Sun means the father, the Moon the mother, Mars enemies, Mercury friends, Jupiter brothers, Venus women, Saturn servants. — [Crux 1998](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)
- **Skill:** skill and intelligence are seen in the 5th from navamsa lagna or Karakamsa, and the profession is shaped by them. The D10 and the D5 are examined for power and authority. — [Crux scan, ch. V (Fifth House), §8.1](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Unemployment and ill fame (§13.8):**
  - Saturn placed in or associated with the 10th or the 10th lord indicates unemployment, underemployment or job loss. Jupiter indicates virtuous action.
  - Debilitated planets in the 10th, or malefics in the 10th of the navamsa, also point to unemployment.
  - Ill fame comes from Mars and Saturn (or other malefics) with the 10th or 10th lord, from papakartari on the 10th, or from the 10th lord in a malefic navamsa.
  — [Crux 1998](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)

**Service against business (Rath's own text)**
- **In the D10:**
  - The 6th rules service and the 7th rules business. A stronger 6th means service; a stronger 7th means independent business.
  - The 6th lord promotes service. Service may be unstable during the 7th lord's period.
  - Debilitated malefics in the 7th push the person toward service.
  - The preview has a typo that reads "sixth house rules business"; the following sentences make clear the intended word is service.
  — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **Maraka framing:** the 7th (business) is the maraka of the 6th (service). The 8th (11th from the 10th) is retirement, and also gambling, business loans and inherited business. The 4th is the premises. The 9th is the boss. — [Crux scan, ch. II §7, p. 33–34](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Lagna lord's position:** the lagna lord in the 9th makes the person independent, so business suits them. The lagna lord in the 5th (the house of subordinates) makes them prefer service. — [Crux scan, ch. II §7](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf); same rule for the 9th in [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **Boss and subordinates:**
  - The boss is seen from the 9th. A malefic with papargala on the 9th causes trouble with a boss or former boss in its period; a benefic with shubhargala gives a helpful boss.
  - Subordinates are seen from the 5th.
  - The 1st, 6th and 10th are the employment houses. The 3rd, 8th and 12th from them cause problems: the 3rd short leave, the 12th long leave, the 8th retirement.
  — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **Starting a business:** the 2nd (8th from the 7th) is the finance needed to start. The 5th, 8th and 11th from the 2nd show partners' capital, bank loans and own funds. A well-placed 8th brings large enterprise loans; an afflicted 8th brings persistent business debt. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house)
- **Leaving service for business:** in the D10, malefics in the 6th make service difficult. Benefics or strong planets tied to the 7th or 8th tend to pull the person out of service into business. — [Crux 1998, §13.8, p. 435](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)
- **Worked application, T.N. Seshan (Chart 112):** the D10 6th and 7th were both empty, so the lords decided. The 6th lord Mercury was in the 5th (authority) with the Moon. The 7th lord Venus was in the badhaka sign from the lagna. From the lagna lord, Mercury was in a trine and Venus in a dusthana. The conclusion was government service. — [Crux 1998, pp. 446–449](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)
- **Tie-break, Chart 124 (male, 25 Oct 1957, Calcutta):** the 7th was stronger than the 6th, but Saturn sitting in the 7th was read as service, not business. Rath also says to treat the D10 6th as a lagna for service, with planets in the 2nd and 7th from it working against it. — [Crux 1998, pp. 481–484](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)

### Inferences
- Reading order reconstructed from the chapter and its examples. Rath does not print it as a numbered list:
  1. Rasi 10th and 10th lord: strength, argala, Paka Lagna and AL checks.
  2. 10th from the Moon and from Hora Lagna.
  3. Navamsa: the 10th lord's dispositor and navamsa sign, and the 10th and 5th from navamsa lagna or Karakamsa.
  4. AL, A10 and A7 for visible career, titles and business.
  5. AmK.
  6. D10 artha trikona for the type of work, and the 6th against the 7th for service or business.
  7. Raja yogas, timed with dasa and transit.
- In service against business, he weighs where the D10 6th and 7th lords sit relative to the D10 lagna and its lord, not only the house strengths. Seshan and Chart 124 are cases where a simple strength count would have given the wrong answer.

His Jaimini Upadesa Sutras translation would hold this but was not available publicly. Secondary sites describe the 10th from Karakamsa as the career house, but they are not Rath.
- No standalone Rath article on the Amatyakaraka in career was found. Its role above is inferred from the worked charts.

## 2. How does he read the D10 specifically?

### Takeaway
The D10, which Rath calls karmamsa and svarga chakra, is analysed independently like a Rasi chart, with the focus on its 10th house. Profession comes from the artha trikona (2, 6, 10). The 7th is the pada of the 10th and also business. The 8th is retirement and inherited business. The 9th is the boss and independence; the 5th is authority and subordinates, and its lord brings new jobs. The 12th is loss, sales, hidden enemies and hospitals. A D10 planet's dominance maps to a field. Exalted or debilitated planets in the D10 can give outstanding success. He also runs Narayana dasa inside divisional charts. His own text for the D10 deity (dikpala) scheme was not found.

### Cited Findings
- **Names and focus:** the D10 is also called karmamsa and svarga chakra ("pathway to heaven"), and the focus is its 10th house. The artha trikonas (2, 6, 10) normally decide the profession. — [Crux scan, ch. II §7, p. 33](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf) (Rath's own text)
- **Method:** analyse the D10 separately, as with the Rasi. The artha trikona directly governs how one works, and the 7th, as the pada of the 10th, is studied too. Raja yoga must be confirmed in the D5 and the D10. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house) (Rath's own text)
- **Dominant planet to field:**

  | Dominant planet | Field |
  |---|---|
  | Sun | politics or public service |
  | Moon | government service or hotels |
  | Mars | police, intelligence, army |
  | Jupiter | law, judiciary, priesthood, astrology |
  | Mercury | trade, writing, publishing |
  | Venus | entertainment, arts |
  | Saturn | heavy or menial work |
  | Rahu | imports |
  | Ketu | exports |

  — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house) (Rath's own text)
- **Exalted and debilitated planets:**
  - Planets exalted or debilitated in the D10 show success. In Muhammad Ali's D10, the 10th lord Sun was debilitated in the 12th and aspected by Mars, which gave boxing.
  - For Seshan, debilitated Saturn as D10 10th lord in the D10 lagna gave brilliant results, power and independence. Rath adds that Saturn in the D10 lagna makes a person power-hungry and compares Hitler.
  — [Crux 1998, pp. 442, 446–447](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt) (Rath's own text)
- **House meanings used in the D10 worked charts (Rath's own text, Crux 1998, pp. 435–490):**
  - **5th lord:** authority and the start of a new job. The D10 5th lord "will always give" new job openings in its period (Chart 124).
  - **6th lord in the 5th:** damages authority (Seshan, Mercury antardasa from 1994).
  - **7th lord:** the visible opposition (Seshan, two co-commissioners appointed).
  - **8th:** retirement or resignation. Seshan had Ketu in the D10 8th. Chart 126 had Jupiter in the D10 8th; he left government service in Jupiter dasa (1984), and the business failed because Jupiter was 8th from the D10 7th lord.
  - **9th:** independent work or contracts (Ali, Seshan, Chart 109), and loyalty to mentors (Joshi).
  - **10th:** the party or organisation. For Joshi, Pisces in the D10 10th stood for the BJP lotus.
  - **12th:** intrigue by crafty enemies or jealous colleagues (Chart 123, Rahu antardasa, job change 1996–97; Joshi, Rahu dasa 1979–97), obstruction of service (Chart 109), sales (Chart 124), and hospitals (Nightingale).
  - Saturn aspecting the D10 lagna or its lord gives hard work and envious colleagues (Chart 123).

  — [Crux 1998](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt)
- **Narayana dasa in divisional charts:** for a divisional chart, start the Narayana dasa from the sign occupied, in that division, by the Rasi lord of the relevant house, then treat the division as a separate chart. Rath demonstrates this on the D4, including job-linked moves in 1969 and 1991. — [Crux scan, ch. VI (Fourth House), pp. 155–157](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf) (Rath's own text). His book *Narayana Dasa* is described as covering this varga Narayana dasa method with an illustration for each division. — [secondary: astroamerica listing of Narayana Dasa](https://astroamerica.com/text/narayanadasa.html)
- **Calculation:** odd signs are counted from the sign itself and even signs from the 9th from it. A search snippet attributed this to a Sanjay Rath author page on a parasarahora.in subdomain, citing Parashara, Satyacharya and Saravali. That page could not be fetched because DNS failed, so treat this as unverified. — [secondary snippet; page unreachable](https://pjc2a.parasarahora.in/author/srath/page/9/)
- **D10 deities (dikpalas), secondary only:**
  - Parashara's scheme gives the ten parts of an odd sign to Indra, Agni, Yama, Rakshasa (Nirriti), Varuna, Vayu, Kubera, Isana, Brahma and Ananta, in reverse for even signs. — [secondary: Ernst Wilhelm, Dasamsa Varga PDF](https://astrology-videos.com/CourseMaterials/DasamsaVarga.pdf); [secondary: jagannathhora.com D10](https://jagannathhora.com/dasamsa-chart-d10-career-profession/)
  - A Scribd file titled "sr-d10-deities-phyllis-sueann" reads the deity of the 10th lord's D10 position for career direction: Indra as leaders, Agni as fire or energy work, Yama as judging. No author is named, so it cannot be attributed to Rath.
  — [Scribd, uploader only](https://www.scribd.com/document/591075913/sr-d10-deities-phyllis-sueann)

### Inferences
- In practice, Rath uses the D10 as a full chart with house-by-house meanings: 5th new job and authority, 6th service, 7th business and visible rivals, 8th exit, 9th boss and independence, 12th loss and intrigue. He then times it with Vimshottari antardasas of the D10 house lords.
- "Exaltation or debilitation in D10 equals success" runs against ordinary dignity reasoning. A system copying his method would need to encode it on purpose.

### Gaps
- His own text on the D10 deity scheme was not found in public sources, nor in the requested "Varga Chakra" dasamsa paper. *Varga Chakra* (2002 SJC Hyderabad conference volume, Sagar) is edited by Rath, and the dasamsa chapter's author is not confirmed. — [srath.com Varga Chakra page](https://srath.com/?p=58). Any claim that he reads career "direction" from D10 dikpalas is unverified here.
- Table 13.2 of COVA-13 (D10 detail) is referenced but not reproduced in the preview.

## 3. Which dasha and transit rules does he publish for career events?

### Takeaway
Timing goes through house signification first: the 5th is joining, the 8th is retirement or exit, the 7th lord unsettles service, and the 6th lord in the 5th cuts authority. Raja yogas are timed by the houses they occupy (early, middle or late life). Vimshottari antardasas of D10 house lords are applied on top. Narayana (padakrama) dasa signs linked to the AL or A10 time titles. Saturn and Jupiter transits over the AL and A10 time losses and victories. Neechabhanga and Vipareeta raja yogas carry their own conditions for promotion and fall.

### Cited Findings
- **Joining a new job:** judged from the 5th house, the 8th from the 10th, which shows the start and life of an action. — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house) (Rath's own text)
- **Retirement or end of career:** the 8th (11th from the 10th, labhargala on the 10th). — [COVA-13 preview](https://sagittariuspublications.com/book-preview/cova-chapter-xiii-the-tenth-house) (Rath's own text)

### Inferences
- His timing is layered. Promise comes from Rasi, D10, AL and A10. The period is the Vimshottari dasa or antardasa of the D10 house lord matching the event (5th to join, 8th or 12th to exit, 6th lord against 7th lord to switch). Narayana dasa signs tied to the AL or A10 give the title or year. Saturn and Jupiter over the AL and A10 confirm the event or spoil it.
- The 1998 book gives no single rule like "promotion occurs when X". Promotion is handled as raja yoga timing.

- The examples are mostly famous people with fame or title outcomes, plus a handful of ordinary employees (Charts 123, 124, 126). The ordinary-employee charts carry the clearest job-change and job-loss timing logic.
- Several example timings are forecasts written in 1997–98 (Rai Mars dasa 2000, Kavita Jupiter 2000, Chart 126 until 2000). These are predictions in the text, not confirmed outcomes.
