## Q1. In his published case studies and lessons, what is the usual sequence of a D1 analysis?

### Takeaway
Rath's book gives a stated preparation checklist (cast D1, D9, D3 and other vargas, arudhas, special lagnas, karakas) and a ten-point house-judgement checklist. His worked charts do not follow a fixed numbered sequence. In practice they open with lagna and lagna lord in D1 and the navamsa lagna, then yogas, then the Sun/Atmakaraka argala on lagna, then the Arudha Lagna for status, and only then dasa (often Narayana dasa) for timing. His srath.com celebrity articles reorder freely by topic.

### Cited Findings
- **Preparation step before any reading.** To study a horoscope, D1, D9, D3 and other relevant divisional charts, plus arudha, Yamada and upagraha charts, should be cast accurately. Special lagnas (especially Hora and Ghatika lagna) should be marked, and the Karta, Kevala and Yogada planets identified. The chara karakas go in a table. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. II "Concepts & Predictive Principles", sect. 7 "Houses and Divisional Charts", p.30](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Ten factors that bear on any house.** Paraphrased in his order: (i) the house, its sign and planets conjoined or aspecting; (ii) the house lord, its strength and the sign it occupies; (iii) the dispositor of that lord; (iv) the navamsa lord of that dispositor; (v) the arudha and Yamada of the house; (vi) the fixed, temporary and natural karakas of the house; (vii) the house counted from Arudha Lagna and Yamada lagna; (viii) good and bad yogas in the house or involving its lord; (ix) argala and virodhargala on it; (x) the relevant saham, plus a detailed check of the relevant divisional chart. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. II, sect. 7, pp.30-31](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- He backs the varga step with a Saravali citation. His paraphrase: divisional charts hold the whole knowledge of the chart's future, and astrology cannot proceed without them. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. II, sect. 7, p.31](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Worked chart (Chart 11, India's first Prime Minister), order as written:**
  1. D1 lagna sign and navamsa lagna sign, each mapped to temperament;
  2. lagna lord placed in lagna, its sign dispositor and its nakshatra lord (Vimshottari);
  3. the nakshatra lord's house and the yoga it forms (a Mahapurusha yoga);
  4. a negative yoga (Kalamrita) and the planet that cancels it;
  5. lagna lord as Atmakaraka and its navamsa;
  6. house position used to place the yoga's timing in later life;
  7. the Sun's unobstructed argala on lagna;
  8. Varnada lagna as confirmation;
  9. Arudha Lagna and its lord's house from AL, to judge status and enemies;
  10. papakartari on lagna;
  11. a Narayana dasa table that ties periods to events.
  **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV "The Ascendant", Chart 11, pp.61-63](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- The book is organised house by house. After an Introduction on dasa systems, a chapter on concepts and principles, and longevity, it gives one chapter per house from the Ascendant to the Twelfth House. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Contents](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **srath.com article on a former US President.** The author is Sanjay Rath. The sequence is topical, not a checklist:
  1. birth-time rectification, justified through the Atmakaraka and navamsa lagna;
  2. Upapada and its 2nd house, with Darapada;
  3. Saturn's transit over the Upapada;
  4. D10 and Vimshottari, with a vipareeta rajayoga;
  5. life events tied to D9, D12, D3, D24 and D7;
  6. rajayogas;
  7. Arudha Lagna and its 3rd house for a sibling;
  8. dasa timing of career events;
  9. Saturn and Jupiter transits over Paka Lagna and Rajyapada for forecasts.
  Panchanga is not discussed. **Rath's own text** (fetched page; summarised by the fetch tool). [srath.com, Bill Clinton](https://srath.com/celebrity/usa/bill-clinton/)

### Inferences
- The stated method is a checklist that is the same for every house, rather than a linear chart-reading script. A lagna-first pass (lagna, lagna lord, dispositor, nakshatra lord, yogas, AK, AL) appears in the Ascendant chapter because that chapter is about the first house. The other house chapters apply the same ten-point list to their own house.
- Birth-time checking appears early in at least one web case: the rectification in the Clinton article is done before interpretation.

### Gaps
- No published Rath text found states a numbered "first do X, then Y" script for a whole-chart reading.
- Parasara Jyotisa Course and Jaimini Scholar Program lesson pages were not opened this session, so any lesson-level sequence there is unchecked.
- Lecture videos and transcripts were not checked.

## Q2. Which factors does he say must be examined before any prediction?

### Takeaway
In his text the non-negotiables are:
- three ascendants examined independently (lagna, Arudha Lagna, Yamada lagna) and their strength;
- the natural soul karaka Sun and the chara Atmakaraka, with their argala on lagna and their position from AL;
- the relevant divisional chart;
- a correct choice of dasa and of its starting point.

Birth panchanga is not named as a first step in the text read.

### Cited Findings
- The lagna, Arudha lagna and Yamada lagna should be examined independently. Their strength, the karaka Sun and the chara Atmakaraka are called "vital to the chart". **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV, sect. 4.1, p.54](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Sun on lagna:** unobstructed argala on lagna, or obstruction of papargala, gives status and fame. If the lagna lord conjoins or aspects the Sun (rasi drishti included), good moral character follows. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV, sect. 4.1, p.54](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Atmakaraka on lagna:** unobstructed argala on lagna gives good health and a blessed life. An ill-placed or obstructed AK mars the horoscope. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV, sect. 4.1, p.54](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Sun and AK from AL:** these are read from the Arudha Lagna for status and position, and from Varnada lagna for standing in one's community and for health and death matters. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV, sect. 4.1, p.54](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Lagna lord by house:** a table of results for the lagna lord in each house, conditional on lagna-lord strength. Example: with the lord in the 7th, an ascetic or wandering life follows unless the lagnesh is strong. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV, sect. 4.4, pp.59-60](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Special lagnas as yoga-givers:** Hora lagna signifies wealth and Ghatika lagna power. A planet linked to both lagna and Hora lagna becomes a dhana yogada. A planet linked to both lagna and Ghatika lagna becomes a rajya yogada. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. II, sect. 2.2, p.19](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Dasa riders:** dasa is called the most potent predictive tool. Overlooking the "riders" of Vimshottari (for example Tara dasa) is said to cause wrong predictions and remedies that miss. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. I "Introduction", p.1](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Dasa starting point:** a correct choice of starting point for Vimshottari is called the key to timing. Citing Jataka Parijata XVIII.33-35, he says to start from the strongest of four stars: lagna star, Moon star, name-initial star, or Moon star at the query. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. I, sect. 6, p.5](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Before pronouncing on children:** examine the D7. Confirm physical capacity first, and refer to medical attention if the lagna padas are afflicted. **Rath's own text.** Recorded in earlier project notes from the same book. [Crux, sect. 8.5.2 and 8.5.5, pp.172-174](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Before forming a view on marriage:** the Upapada and 7th house alone cannot settle whether marriage occurs, and the A7 must also be examined. **Rath's own text.** [srath.com, Darapada and Upapada](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)

### Inferences
- The Sun (natural atmakaraka) and the chara AK are paired throughout. Each is judged by its argala on lagna and again from AL. This dual-karaka check is one of the most distinctive early steps in his D1 method.
- The Moon enters mainly through dasa. He treats Vimshottari as reading "the mind" (see Q4). In the text read, the Moon is not given a separate "Moon chart first" step.

### Gaps
- **Birth panchanga** (tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga, karana) as a pre-reading step: not found in the text read. Panchanga elements appear elsewhere in his work, for example tithi in the Tithi Pravesha annual chart, but not as a stated first step for D1.
- No explicit Rath statement was found that ranks lagna, Moon and Sun as three equal pillars. General sites use that framing (secondary, not Rath).

## Q3. Does his public material state whether he uses 7 or 8 chara karakas, and how the Atmakaraka is chosen?

### Takeaway
Yes. *Crux* tells the reader to tabulate the Atmakaraka and other chara karakas in the "eight planet chara karaka scheme". It says Rahu's longitude is counted from the end of the sign. A worked chart labels eight karakas, with Rahu as Amatyakaraka. Critics argue that this departs from Parasara, who brings Rahu in only when two planets tie.

### Cited Findings
- The book says the Atma and other chara karakas should be tabulated in the eight-planet scheme. Rahu's longitude is taken from the end of the sign: "considering the longitude of Rahu from the end". **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. II, sect. 7, p.30](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Chart 11 karaka table.** It labels eight karakas: AK, AmK, BK, MK, PiK (written PK), PuK (written PUK), GK and DK. In this chart the Moon is AK and Rahu is AmK. Rahu's printed degree is about 12°44' and its rank is consistent with reverse counting. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV, Chart 11, p.61](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Rahu as AK.** A srath.com article names Rahu as the Atmakaraka for two named individuals. **Rath's own text.** Recorded in earlier project notes. [srath.com, Atmakaraka and Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **AK used for remedy direction.** The same article says Mercury as AK points to controlling speech and staying truthful. **Rath's own text.** [srath.com, Atmakaraka and Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **Seven-karaka scheme for mundane charts (unverified).** A search summary of an independent critique states Rath's position as eight karakas for humans and other beings and seven for mundane charts, used always for humans and not only when degrees tie. The underlying document could not be opened (Scribd truncated). **Critique.** Search-result level only. [Scribd, "Parasara's Teachings On Chara Karakas: An Independent Interpretation"](https://www.scribd.com/doc/56348155/c-karaka)
- **Parasara's tie rule.** A verse commentary on BPHS reads Parasara as including Rahu (eight karakas) when two planets have equal degrees. **Secondary.** Search snippet. [enjoylearningsanskrit.com, BPHS ch.32 v.2](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-32/verse-2)
- **Conference paper (unconfirmed).** A Sri Jagannath Center 2003 conference presentation by Rath titled "The final proof of two carakaraka schemes" appears in search results. It was not opened, so its content and exact title are unconfirmed. **Rath's own text (unverified).** [Scribd SJC-India-2004 listing](https://es.scribd.com/doc/21215811/SJC-India-2004)

### Inferences
- **Selection rule.** The AK is the planet with the highest longitude within its sign. Rahu competes with its degree measured backward from the end of the sign. This follows from the *Crux* instruction and the Chart 11 table, but the "highest degree" wording itself was not found verbatim.

### Gaps
- The exact human-versus-mundane split (8 vs 7) was not confirmed in Rath's own text this session.
- The tie-breaking rule, when two planets have the same degree in his scheme, was not found.

## Q4. What recurring traits mark his teaching and consultation voice?

### Takeaway
His voice is that of a lineage-holder and translator of the classics:
- He anchors claims in named texts (BPHS, Jaimini, Saravali, Jataka Parijata) and in family tradition.
- He corrects "misconceptions" directly.
- He opens with invocations and closes with blessings.
- His case predictions are mostly firm and declarative, sometimes listed as having come true. Conditions are written into rules ("unless the lagnesh is strong").
- Remedies (mantra first, then gems and muhurta) are framed through karma and the astrologer's own faith and purity, with explicit warnings against black magic and careless mantra prescription.
- His site's disclaimer adds modern legal caution.

### Cited Findings: lineage and stated values
- **Family and village.** From a traditional astrologer family of Bira Balabhadrapur Sasan village, Puri, Odisha, tracing to Sri Achyuta Dasa (Achyutananda). **Rath's own text** (about page). [srath.com/series/about](https://srath.com/series/about)
- **Teachers and early career.** Taught by his uncle Pt. Kasinath Rath; grandfather Pt. Jagannath Rath wrote on Jyotisha; trained as a mechanical engineer and left government service for Jyotisha. **Rath's own text** (about page). [srath.com/series/about](https://srath.com/series/about)
- **Holistic, not a personal system.** He presents a holistic approach rooted in the seers and does not promote a system of his own. He builds on BPHS, Jaimini's Upadesa Sutras, Brihat Jataka and Saravali, with the Gita, Upanishads and Puranas. Sri Jagannath Center was founded to pass on the parampara along with mantra, meditation and dharma teaching. **Rath's own text** (about page). [srath.com/series/about](https://srath.com/series/about)
- **Jaimini is not a separate school.** He calls on the community to stop presenting minor differences of view as different systems. He cites B.V. Raman's holistic stance. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Preface, "Is Jaimini different from Parasara?"](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Seers over modern theory.** He says trouble comes when modern astrologers push their own theories instead of understanding the seers. He corrects a specific "misconception" (education is 4th house, with the 5th as argala on it). **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Preface, "Misconceptions"](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Open to correction.** He admits explanations may be inadequate in places and invites constructive criticism. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Preface](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Family-taught methods.** He says certain methods are taught only within Jyotish families. An example is the 5th house in timing death, which needs a teacher to apply. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Preface](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Bounded experimentation.** In another preface he says imperfect findings should not stop learned astrologers from experimenting within the bounds of Vedic astrology. **Secondary** (search summary of the *Collected Papers* preface; not opened). [srath.com/?p=112](https://srath.com/?p=112)

### Cited Findings: devotional framing and scripture
- **Blessings.** The *Crux* acknowledgements invoke Sri Jagannath and Lord Shiva's blessings on the publisher and his family. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Acknowledgement](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Invocation.** The Clinton article opens with a Sanskrit invocation to Brihaspati. **Rath's own text.** [srath.com, Bill Clinton](https://srath.com/celebrity/usa/bill-clinton/)
- **Deities in house results.** Deities are named inside house results. With the lagna lord in the 9th, the native is blessed by Jupiter (Maha-Vishnu); in the 10th, by parents and Ganesha; in the 11th, by Hara. Yogas are credited to "the blessings of the Sun God". **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV, sect. 4.4 and Chart 11, pp.59-62](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Epic stories as precedent.** He sets Saturn in the 4th as the cause of Rama's exile beside a modern politician's early rajayoga from Jupiter in the 2nd/4th. He cites Jaimini that 2nd/4th-house rajayogas fructify early. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. II, p.19](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Verse then paraphrase.** Sanskrit verses (Saravali, Jataka Parijata) are printed and then explained in English. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. I sect. 6; Ch. II sect. 7](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)

### Cited Findings: how predictions and timing are phrased
- **Two dasas, two questions.** Vimshottari and other nakshatra dasas time the state of the mind, measured from the Moon. Narayana dasa is needed for what the environment brings, which he calls the key to jyotish. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Preface, "Narayana Dasa"](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Promise to timing.** House position gates when a promised yoga acts. A rajayoga in lagna functions only in later life, and the Sun in the 5th marks a hard middle life. Yogas in the 2nd/4th fructify early. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. II p.19; Ch. IV Chart 11, pp.62-63](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Dasa-lord modifiers.** Planets in the 6th, 8th and 12th from the dasa lord act inimically. Planets in the 7th from it always give adverse results. Badhaka houses are defined by sign type. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. II, pp.22-23](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Firm forecasts in a case.** In one actor's case, the current Narayana dasa (part of a Sarpa yoga) is said to have "proved to be disastrous", and he states that "the next dasa will be better". Remedies for sarpa dosha are advised in the same paragraph. **Rath's own text.** [Crux, Ch. IV, p.75](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)
- **Clinton article phrasing.** It is mostly declarative ("shows", "will", "will surely") and includes a box of past predictions marked true. Hedging is rare. **Rath's own text** (tone characterised by the fetch tool's summary, so treat as secondary-level description). [srath.com, Bill Clinton](https://srath.com/celebrity/usa/bill-clinton/)
- **Disclaimer on srath.com.** Astrology is not a substitute for licensed professional advice. Interpretations can differ, and remedies are given in good faith without guarantee. Users should disclose their emotional and physical state so the astrologer can judge whether to advise. **Rath's own text** (site disclaimer; from earlier project notes). [srath.com disclaimer](https://srath.com/misc/media/disclaimer/)

### Cited Findings: remedies
- ***Vedic Remedies in Astrology*: method.** It sorts classical remedies by guna and centres on mantra shastra, with worked mantra selection, a mantra compendium and gem tables. It also covers dasa-bhukti remedies and Gandanta births. **Secondary** (publisher blurb on retailer page). [Exotic India listing](https://www.exoticindia.com/book/details/vedic-remedies-in-astrology-idj596)
- ***Vedic Remedies in Astrology*: the author's own sections.** The excerpt on the listing covers the following. **Rath's own text** (excerpt shown on the retailer page; not verified against the book). [Exotic India listing](https://www.exoticindia.com/book/details/vedic-remedies-in-astrology-idj596)
  - **Black magic:** astrologers are warned against black magic.
  - **Karma diagnosis:** the natal chart shows past-life karma. A prasna chart helps judge whether a trouble is past or present karma. Trouble seen only in the prasna points to present karma; seen in both, to past karma.
  - **Muhurta:** muhurta is itself a remedy.
  - **Faith:** the real contest is between the size of the problem and the astrologer's faith. Astrologers should live a pure, simple life.
  - **Vishnu:** Vishnu is presented as the presiding deity of Jyotisha.
- ***Crux*: mantra caution.** He warns that many astrologers prescribe mantras too freely, and that a mismatched propitiation can harm, for example in fifth-house (children) matters. He gives rules for choosing. **Rath's own text** (from earlier project notes). [Crux, sect. 8.4, pp.169-170](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Crux-of-Vedic-Astrology-Timing-of-Events1.pdf)

### Cited Findings: critique and secondary reception
- **K.N. Rao (forum repost).** A forum repost of a 2003 message attributed to K.N. Rao says most charts in *Crux* were wrong. No charts are shown and the claims are unverified. **Critique.** [IndiaDivine thread](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1216566-knrao-about-sanjay-rath/)
- **Former client (2004 forum).** A former client says two 2004 predictions failed. There is no transcript or chart. **Critique.** [IndiaDivine thread](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1183996-sanjay-raths-failure/)
- **Supporter blog (2005).** A supporter's blog credits several political forecasts and counts one miss, a 2004 US election call. **Secondary.** Search snippet only. [jyotish-blog 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/04/predictive-success-etc.html)
- **Eight-karaka critique.** See the Q3 critique entry. **Critique.**
- No independent, systematic test of his predictions was found. (Earlier project notes reached the same conclusion.)

### Inferences
- **Tone.** The register is authoritative and corrective toward peers, devotional in its framing, and humble toward the classics. "Conditional" appears mostly as conditions written into rules (strength of the lagnesh, obstruction of argala) rather than as probability language. Case forecasts are stated firmly.
- **Remedies.** Remedies are tied to diagnosis (karma source, AK, deity from the chart) rather than given as generic advice. The astrologer's own discipline is part of the remedy.
- **Scope of style evidence.** Style evidence here comes from books and web articles. How he speaks in a live consultation is not documented in the sources read.
