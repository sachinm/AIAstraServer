# Chara Dasha as taught by Sanjay Rath and his school (SJC)

Labels used: **[Rath's own text]**, **[secondary (student of Rath)]**, **[other school]**, **[tertiary]** (unsourced web guide, low weight).
All quotations are under ten words. Everything else is paraphrase.

Main primary source found this time: Sanjay Rath, *Narayana Dasa* (Sagar Publications, New Delhi), full text publicly hosted at
[scienceoflight.net PDF](https://scienceoflight.net/wp-content/uploads/extra/Narayana%20Dasa%20by%20Sanjay%20Rath.pdf). Byline on the
title page: "Sanjay Rath, Sagar Publications". Section names below are from that PDF (Preface; Chapter II "Period of Dasa";
the following chapter "Order of Narayana Dasa"). Page numbers are not given because the PDF text extraction does not map cleanly to
printed pages; where a running header page number was visible it is noted as "running header p. N".

Key finding up front: **Rath does not teach Chara Dasa as a separate system with its own rules. In his own book he treats
"Chara dasa" as the movable-ascendant form of Narayana Dasa**, sharing the same period, starting-sign, direction and antardasa
rules. The only rule that is specific to "Chara" in his text is the sequence: plain regular sign-to-sign succession, with no jumps.

---

## 1. Rath's stated rules for start, direction, period length and sub-periods

### Takeaway
Rath's own *Narayana Dasa* gives a full rule set, and he labels the movable-lagna form of it "Chara dasa". Start = stronger of
lagna and 7th; direction = footedness of the 9th from the start sign (BPHS 46.167); years = count to lord by footedness, minus one,
+1 exalted / -1 debilitated, capped at 12; Scorpio/Aquarius by BPHS 46.158-163; second cycle = 12 minus first; 12 equal antardasas
starting from the sign holding the lord of the stronger of dasa sign / its 7th, direction by odd/even of the dasa sign; Saturn and
Ketu exceptions.

### Cited Findings

**Where "Chara dasa" sits in Rath's scheme**
- Narayana dasa has three types, "Chara etc", meant for a movable, fixed or dual ascendant (deities Brahma, Shiva, Vishnu). **[Rath's own text]** — *Narayana Dasa*, Preface (Trimurti passage), [PDF](https://scienceoflight.net/wp-content/uploads/extra/Narayana%20Dasa%20by%20Sanjay%20Rath.pdf)
- Under Jaimini Sutra 2.4.8 ("Prathame prak pratyaktwam"): if the ascendant is movable the dasa sequence is regular; Rath's note: "These are also called Chara dasa". **[Rath's own text]** — *Narayana Dasa*, "Order of Narayana Dasa", Dasa Sequence rule (8), same PDF
- Contrast types: fixed ascendant -> next dasa is the sign in the 6th, and so on (JS 2.4.9, read through katapayadi "Ravi" = 6); dual ascendant -> dasa of the kendra followed by its trines (5th, 9th), then next kendra and its trines (JS 2.4.10). **[Rath's own text]** — same section, rules (9)-(10)
- His book on Jaimini lists, in its table of contents, an entry "Chara Dasa, ketu is a benefic" (Chapter II area, near Navamsa Dasa and Sthira Dasa), and separately "Narayana Dasa (alias Padakrama)" in Chapter II Quarter 4 (pp. 204-234) and "Dasa Periods of Signs" in Chapter I Quarter 1 (pp. 1-29). Preface warns that "Chara dasa" differs from "chara Paryaya dasa". **[Rath's own text, via publisher listing only]** — *Jaimini Maharishi's Upadesa Sutras* (Sagar, 1997), [Exotic India listing](https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/jaimini-maharishi-s-upadesa-sutras-idj587). The content of those sections was not available.

**Starting sign**
- The opening dasa is always the stronger of the 1st and 7th houses (JS 2.4.7, "Satya" principle); almost all rasi dasas use this. **[Rath's own text]** — *Narayana Dasa*, "Order of Narayana Dasa", opening rule
- He rejects variants that start from lagna for men and the 7th for women, or stronger of 1st/7th for men and 4th/10th for women: "not within the rule". **[Rath's own text]** — same section
- Strength between lagna and 7th uses his "sources of strength" (Chapter I, eight rules; rules 1 and 5 not used for Narayana dasa) and the second source (JS 2.4.1): conjunction/aspect of Jupiter, Mercury or the sign lord, using rasi drishti only. Tie rules include higher-longitude lord, odd/even placement of lord, and finally the sign giving more dasa years. **[Rath's own text]** — *Narayana Dasa*, Chapter I strength rules and "Order" rule (7)

**Direction of the dasa sequence**
- Order is zodiacal or reverse according to the sign in the 9th house (Dharma bhava) from the start: 9th is vimsapada (odd-footed) -> direct; samapada (even-footed) -> reverse (BPHS 46.167). **[Rath's own text]** — *Narayana Dasa*, "Order", rule (11)
- Footedness groups (BPHS 46.156): Aries-Taurus-Gemini and Libra-Scorpio-Sagittarius are odd-footed; Cancer-Leo-Virgo and Capricorn-Aquarius-Pisces are even-footed. He warns this is not the same as odd/even sign. **[Rath's own text]** — Chapter II, Table 7
- Exception, Saturn (JS 2.4.33 "Sanou chetyeke"): if Saturn is in the starting sign, the order is zodiacal and regular; the 9th-house rule is set aside, and "Rule (3) for Chara Dasa" (regular succession) applies to every ascendant type, so no 6th-sign or trine jumps. **[Rath's own text]** — "Order", Exceptions rule (14)
- Exception, Ketu (JS 1.1.8 "Vipareetam Ketou"): Ketu in the dasa sign reverses the direction otherwise indicated; for the dasa sequence it reverses (not ignores) the 9th-house rule and does not change the jump pattern of fixed/dual types. **[Rath's own text]** — "Order", rule (15)
- He gives a precomputed order table (Table 10, "General Order of Narayana Dasa") for each start sign; e.g. Aries runs 1-2-3...12, Cancer runs 4-3-2-1-12... **[Rath's own text]** — "Order" chapter

**Period length of each sign**
- Years = number of signs from the dasa sign to the sign holding its lord, counted zodiacally for odd-footed and in reverse for even-footed signs, then reduced by one (BPHS 46.155, whose verse itself uses the word "Charadasam"; JS 1.1.28). Worked example: Leo (even-footed) with Sun in Sagittarius counts in reverse. **[Rath's own text]** — Chapter II "Dasa Period", rule (2)
- Footnote 32: some astrologers do not subtract one; Rath argues the subtraction is right because a lord in its own sign then gives 13-1 = 12, which is the stated maximum. **[Rath's own text]** — Chapter II, footnote 32
- Exceptions (JS 1.1.27 "Na kwachit"): lord exalted -> +1 year; debilitated -> -1 year; for this purpose a planet anywhere in its exaltation sign counts as exalted (e.g. Sun anywhere in Aries adds a year to Leo). **[Rath's own text]** — Chapter II, Exceptions (3)(a)
- Maximum period is 12 years: Mercury in Virgo gives 12, not 13. **[Rath's own text]** — Chapter II, Exceptions (3)(b)
- Exaltation table for "Phalita dasa" (Table 8): Rahu exalted Gemini / debilitated Sagittarius; Ketu exalted Sagittarius / debilitated Gemini. Parasara's node signs (Rahu Taurus, Ketu Scorpio, per his Table 9) are kept for Ayur dasas, not for phalita dasas like Narayana. Own signs: Rahu Aquarius, Ketu Scorpio. **[Rath's own text]** — Chapter II, Tables 8-9 and notes
- Dual lordship, Scorpio (Mars/Ketu) and Aquarius (Saturn/Rahu), BPHS 46.158-163: (a) both lords in the sign -> 12 years; (b) both together in another sign -> count to that sign; (c) one in the sign, the other elsewhere -> count to the one elsewhere (example: Mars in Scorpio, Ketu in Taurus -> 6 years); (d) both elsewhere -> lord in the stronger sign by his sources of strength; (e) equal strength -> take the lord giving more years. **[Rath's own text]** — Chapter II, "Dual Lordship" (4)(a)-(e)
- Second cycle: after all 12 signs, the 13th dasa is again the starting sign, and each second-cycle period = 12 minus the first-cycle period (worked tables show a 12-year sign getting 0). **[Rath's own text]** — Chapter II, "Second Cycle of Dasa" (5) and illustration tables

**Sub-periods (antardasa)**
- Each dasa splits into 12 equal antardasas of the 12 signs (JS 1.1.34). **[Rath's own text]** — "Order", Antardasa rule (12)
- Direction of antardasas follows whether the dasa sign is odd or even (plain odd/even, not footedness), and the 9th house is not used for antardasa direction; JS 2.4.31-32: dasa sign odd or its lord in an odd sign -> zodiacal; even -> reverse. **[Rath's own text]** — rules (12)-(13) and notes
- Starting antardasa, resolved by BPHS 50.30-31 as Rath reads it: find the stronger of the dasa sign and its 7th; the sign holding the lord of that stronger sign gives the first antardasa; antardasas are always regular (no jumps), zodiacal or reverse by odd/even dasa sign. He cites JS 2.4.34 as support for the 7th-house option and says this method is for all phalita dasas. **[Rath's own text]** — rule (13) notes 1-2; also restated in the "Order" opening paragraph (two-step start)
- Saturn in the dasa sign: antardasas counted from the lord are always zodiacal. Ketu in the dasa/antardasa sign reverses the direction. **[Rath's own text]** — Exceptions (14) note 2, (15)

**Reading results of a sign dasa (stated for Narayana dasa, which in his scheme includes Chara)**
- The dasa sign and the "Paka rasi" (sign holding the dasa sign's lord) are both read; one step is to count from the dasa rasi as many signs as it is from lagna. For health, the dasa sign is checked from the Sun, the Atmakaraka and lagna. **[Rath's own text]** — *Narayana Dasa*, Judgement chapter (around running header p. 78)
- Benefics in 2nd/12th or 5th/9th from the dasa or paka rasi give Subhakartari / Saubhagya yoga; malefics there give Papakartari / Bandhana. Badhaka places (11th for movable, 9th fixed, 7th dual) are checked from the dasa sign. **[Rath's own text]** — same chapter, items 14-15
- Chara karakas in kendras to the dasa rasi are strong, in panaparas weaker, apoklimas weakest; a karaka aspecting the dasa rasi makes its significations felt. **[Rath's own text]** — same chapter, item 56
- Narayana dasa shows the impact of the environment on the native, while Vimsottari shows the person's response. **[Rath's own text]** — Preface
- Divisional-chart Narayana dasa: each varga has a controlling house; e.g. strong 10th lord in D-10 promises career height. **[Rath's own text]** — Judgement chapter, varga section (Table 29)

**Supporting / secondary**
- Rath's interpretation of Jaimini is used for all three chara dasa variants in Jagannatha Hora; a K.N. Rao variant was added later. **[secondary (student of Rath)]** — P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, mailing-list post reproduced at [jyotish-blog, Feb 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/02/) ("Chara dasa: Lords of Aq and Sc")
- Ketu is taken as debilitated in Gemini for phalita dasas and in Taurus for ayur dasas; preferring a normal lord over a debilitated one for Scorpio/Aquarius is his group's own inference, not in Parasara. **[secondary (student of Rath)]** — PVR, [jyotish-blog 2005-02-28](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/02/chara-dasa-lords-of-aq-and-sc-re.html)
- The Narayana dasa strength rules are taken from Rath's book; the blogger reordered them after talking with Rath. Odd-footed list matches Rath. **[secondary (student of Rath)]** — "Varahamihira" blog (Sarajit Poddar, inferred from reader comment, byline is a pen name), [Narayana Dasa I: Basics](http://varahamihira.blogspot.com/2004/11/narayana-dasa-i-basics.html)
- A critic says Rath reads JS 2.4.13 so that antardasas start from the sign of the dasa sign's lord; the critic holds they start from the dasa sign. This matches Rath's own rule above. **[other school]** — "Shanmukha", [Jaimini Sutramritam, Nov 2009](http://sutramritam.blogspot.com/2009/11/antardasa-nayanam.html)

### Inferences
- In Rath's system, a "Chara dasa" computed for a movable-sign lagna (or for any lagna when Saturn is in the starting sign) is the same calculation as his Narayana dasa. Anyone implementing "Rath's Chara Dasa" should use the Narayana dasa rules above with regular succession.
- His period rules (minus one, +/-1, cap at 12, dual-lord ladder, 12-minus second cycle) are shared by all his phalita rasi dasas; the variable parts are the start sign and the sequence.

### Gaps
- The actual text of the Upadesa Sutras sections "Dasa Periods of Signs" and "Chara Dasa, ketu is a benefic" was not available; whether Rath there defines a distinct Chara Dasa (for example one that always starts from lagna) is unverified.
- No sohamsa.com or srath.com lesson specifically on Chara Dasa was found. srath.com/?p=804 (Vimsottari Dasa) does not cover it.
- Exact printed page numbers for *Narayana Dasa* not established.

## 2. Does Rath say when Chara Dasa should be used instead of Narayana Dasa?

### Takeaway
No such rule was found. In his book the choice is not "Chara versus Narayana": Chara is the form Narayana dasa takes for a movable
ascendant, and the regular Chara order also applies to any ascendant when Saturn occupies the starting sign.

### Cited Findings
- Three types of Narayana dasa, "Chara etc", are each meant for a movable, fixed or dual ascendant. **[Rath's own text]** — *Narayana Dasa*, Preface, [PDF](https://scienceoflight.net/wp-content/uploads/extra/Narayana%20Dasa%20by%20Sanjay%20Rath.pdf)
- Saturn in the starting sign makes the Chara (regular) order apply for all ascendants. **[Rath's own text]** — "Order", rule (14)
- Preface to his Jaimini translation: do not fixate on names; "Chara dasa" differs from "chara Paryaya dasa". **[Rath's own text, via listing]** — [Exotic India listing](https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/jaimini-maharishi-s-upadesa-sutras-idj587)
- PVR calls Narayana dasa a generalization of chara dasa, and says he would choose Jaimini's Narayana (Padakrama) dasa over both K.N. Rao's and Parasara's chara dasa. He describes chara dasa as the progression of the lagna. **[secondary (student of Rath)]** — [jyotish-blog Feb 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/02/chara-dasa-lords-of-aq-and-sc-re.html); [jyotish-blog Oct 2005, "Chara Dasha example"](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/10/chara-dasha-example.html)
- PVR in 2014 proposed his own "Parasara's Chara Dasa", which departs from both Rath and K.N. Rao: computed per divisional chart; seed = strongest of the signs of lagna, Moon and Sun by their lords' strength; direction by footedness of the 9th from the seed; years by count-to-lord minus one with **no** exaltation/debilitation adjustment; co-lord ladder (both in sign 12; one in sign take the other; then more conjunctions, movable > fixed > dual, more years); antardasas left out. He cites Lomasha for starting from the stronger of lagna and Sun. This is PVR's own research, not Rath's teaching. **[secondary (former student, own innovation)]** — P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, "Unlocking the Power of Parasara's Chara Dasa", 14 April 2014, [vedicastrologer.org PDF](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_chara_dasa.pdf)

### Inferences
- If a single "SJC Chara dasa" is needed, the most defensible reading of Rath's own text is: Narayana dasa rules with the regular sequence. Later SJC/PVR "Chara" variants (PVR 2014) should be labelled as such and not attributed to Rath.
- Note that PVR's 2014 removal of the +/-1 adjustment reverses what he and Rath's book used earlier.

### Gaps
- No Rath statement found naming situations (subject, chart type, varga) where Chara dasa is preferred over Narayana dasa.
- SJC currently teaches Chara Dasa under "Advanced Dasa Systems" in its Jaimini programme (per a search summary of [conf.srijagannath.org](https://conf.srijagannath.org/structure-and-themes/)); that page was not opened and the course content is not public.

## 3. Where Rath's rules differ from K.N. Rao's (and Iranganti Rangacharya's)

### Takeaway
The best-sourced difference is the exaltation/debilitation adjustment: Rath adds/subtracts a year, K.N. Rao explicitly does not.
Other differences (Scorpio/Aquarius lord selection, Ketu's signs, antardasa start, start sign and the fixed/dual jumps) are
reported by Rath's students or follow from comparing Rath's text with K.N. Rao's examples. Rangacharya's version is almost
undocumented in public sources.

### Cited Findings
- K.N. Rao: no extra year for an exalted lord and no deduction for a debilitated one, because a period could exceed 12 years and a Sagittarius period could fall to zero; periods are whole years, maximum 12. **[other school]** — K.N. Rao, "Jaimini Chara Dasha - My Approach Part 1", [Saptarishis Astrology](https://saptarishisastrology.com/?p=2656)
- K.N. Rao's worked example (Mesha lagna) runs Mesha, Vrisha, Mithuna... in plain order and then begins a second cycle with Mesha. **[other school]** — same article
- K.N. Rao judges with chara karakas and their aspects on houses counted from mahadasha, antardasha and pratyantara signs, karakamsha, upapada, several vargas, and cross-checks with Vimsottari and Sthira dasa. **[other school]** — same article
- Rath, by contrast, adds/subtracts one year for exalted/debilitated lords and caps at 12. **[Rath's own text]** — *Narayana Dasa*, Chapter II
- PVR: K.N. Rao ignores Parasara's exaltation exception; K.N. Rao's Scorpio/Aquarius lord rules are incomplete even against BPHS; Rath's method adds further Jaimini sutras. In one chart, Scorpio = 7 years via Ketu (K.N. Rao) vs 6 (Parasara, Ketu debilitated) vs 4 via Mars. **[secondary (student of Rath)]** — [jyotish-blog 2005-02-28](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/02/chara-dasa-lords-of-aq-and-sc-re.html); [jyotish-blog Feb 2005 archive](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/02/)
- PVR on a marriage/divorce example: K.N. Rao's method gives Gemini-Aquarius for divorce; the Parasara/SJC method (with the exaltation exception) gives Gemini-Capricorn, which PVR argues fits better. **[secondary (student of Rath)]** — [jyotish-blog Oct 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/10/chara-dasha-example.html)
- A web guide says K.N. Rao starts from the lagna, sets direction by the 9th house, subtracts one, gives 12 for own sign, makes no exaltation adjustment, and runs antardasas from the sign after the dasa sign with the dasa sign last. **[tertiary]** — "Sandesh", [Supastro guide](https://supastro.com/guides/jaimini-chara-dasha-step-by-step) (cites K.N. Rao's book only through "course notes"; not verified against the book)
- Iranganti Rangacharya's chara dasa is offered in Jagannatha Hora 7.0 and, per PVR's post, is close to Narayana dasa. **[secondary (student of Rath)]** — [jyotish-blog Feb 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/02/)
- Separate view: U.K. Jha holds that the Jaimini sutras contain neither the +/-1 rule nor the 12-for-own-sign rule. **[other school]** — [Saptarishis article by U.K. Jha](https://saptarishisshop.com/what-is-the-correct-procedure-for-computing-jaiminis-chara-dasa-by-uk-jha/) (only the introduction was read; the claim comes from a search summary)

| Point | Rath (own text) | K.N. Rao |
|---|---|---|
| Start sign | Stronger of lagna and 7th | Lagna (tertiary source; his example starts at lagna) |

| Direction | Footedness of 9th from start (BPHS 46.167); Ketu reverses; Saturn forces zodiacal | 9th house (tertiary) |
| Years | Count to lord by footedness, minus 1 | Same basic count (tertiary), max 12 (own) |
| Exalted/debilitated lord | +1 / -1, cap 12 | No adjustment (own) |
| Node exaltation for this dasa | Rahu Gemini, Ketu Sagittarius | Not found |
| Scorpio/Aquarius | BPHS 46.158-163 ladder plus his strength rules | Reported incomplete by PVR |
| Second cycle | 12 minus first-cycle years | Second cycle shown; formula not stated in the article |
| Antardasa | 12 equal; start from sign of lord of stronger of dasa sign/7th; odd/even direction | Next sign first, dasa sign last (tertiary, unverified) |

### Inferences
- Implementations labelled "K.N. Rao" and "SJC/Rath" will diverge whenever a sign lord is exalted or debilitated, whenever a node is in its phalita exaltation/debilitation sign, for Scorpio/Aquarius choices, and for all antardasa sequences.
- For a fixed or dual lagna, Rath's sequence (Narayana) is entirely different from K.N. Rao's regular sequence.

### Gaps
- K.N. Rao's own book *Predicting through Jaimini's Chara Dasha* could not be read (pdfcoffee returned 403); his start-sign, direction and antardasa rules are only from a tertiary guide.
- Iranganti Rangacharya's rules: no primary or detailed secondary source found; only PVR's one-line remark.
- No Rath text was found that names K.N. Rao or Rangacharya directly; *Narayana Dasa* contains no mention of either.
