# Sanjay Rath and his school: which divisional-chart (varga) variants are used, and for what

Attribution labels used throughout:
- **Rath's own text**: article or chapter under Sanjay Rath's byline (srath.com or a book he wrote).
- **secondary (student of Rath)**: P.V.R. Narasimha Rao (who calls Rath his guru, VAIA dedication p. iii), Visti Larsen, Sarajit Poddar, Freedom Cole, the "Sanjay P" SJC blog, and similar.
- **software default**: Jagannatha Hora (JHora) software feature list or options.
- **unattributed**: forum, Scribd upload or third-party site with no traceable SJC byline.

Abbreviations: VAIA = P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach* (Sagar Publications), ch. 6 "Divisional Charts", sections 6.2.1-6.5, printed pp. 43-52. The copy read was a PDF hosted at [lakshminarayanlenasia.com](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/docs2/LakshmiNarayanLenasia.com/Astrology%20(Jyotish)%20collection/Vedic%20Astrology%20an%20Integrated%20Approach%20by%20P.V.R.%20Narasimha%20Rao.pdf).

## 1. For each varga, which variant does Rath use by default, and when does he switch?

### Takeaway
Rath's own public articles show only Parasara's schemes. He uses Parasara's drekkana in his worked example and Parasara's five-planet trimsamsa in his D-30 article, and he describes the hora as a solar/lunar split by sign ownership. He names other drekkana variants (Jagannatha, Somanatha, Parivritti, Parivritti-traya) but nothing found under his byline says when he switches to them. Rules for switching come only from students and unattributed handouts.

### Cited Findings
- Rath's principles article works only one varga calculation, the Parasara drekkana (1st, 5th and 9th signs), on a chart of 7 Aug 1963. It does not discuss the Jagannatha, Somanatha or parivritti drekkanas. Label: Rath's own text. — [Principles of Divisional Charts](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/principles-of-divisional-charts/)
- The same article gives a reading area for each varga: D-2 wealth, D-3 siblings, D-4 immovable property, D-7 children, D-9 spouse, D-10 work, D-12 parents, D-16 happiness and vehicles, D-20 spirituality, D-24 learning, D-27 strengths and weaknesses, D-30 evils, D-40 matrilineal karma, D-45 patrilineal karma, D-60 past-birth karma. It gives mapping rules only for D-2 (ownership halves) and D-3. Label: Rath's own text. — [Principles of Divisional Charts](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/principles-of-divisional-charts/)
- In his D-30 article Rath says there are two methods and discusses only Parasara's. He uses D-30 for shadow self and karma, for health and longevity (1st, 8th, 10th; 6th and 8th for disease), for badhaka obstacles, and times it with Paryaya dasa. Label: Rath's own text. — [Trimsamsa D-30 Chart](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/varga/trimsamsa-d-30-chart/)
- In a reader Q&A on srath.com, Rath names the Parasari, Jagannatha, Somanatha, Parivritti-traya and Parivritti drekkanas. He calls the Parivritti drekkana well known and accepted. He discusses the rishi lords of drekkanas (Narada, Agastya, Durvasa) by guna and their use in Narayana dasa and Drig dasa. He gives no construction rule for the Jagannatha drekkana. Label: Rath's own text. (The fetch tool reported a date that cannot be right, so the post date is unverified.) — [Drekkana reckoning and Rishis](https://srath.com/misc/vastu/drekkana-reckoning-and-rishis/)
- *Varga Chakra* (SJC 2002 Hyderabad varga workshop, edited by Rath) has only two chapters under Rath's byline: "Hora Chakra" and "Shastyamsa". ### Inferences
- In what is publicly traceable, Rath's default is Parasara's scheme for D-3 and D-30, and probably for the others. His D-2 description (solar half Leo to Capricorn, lunar half Cancer back to Aquarius, planets placed by nearness) reads like an ownership-based hora, not the plain Sun/Moon 15° hora. That may be the idea behind the Kashinatha hora, but the article does not say so.
- Use by question, as the school reports it (students or unattributed, not Rath): Kashinatha hora for wealth; Parivritti-dvaya hora for family support; Jagannatha drekkana for the 22nd-drekkana killer point and for past-life karma with D-60; Somanatha drekkana for sexual drive; Parivritti-traya drekkana for drives and initiatives, in prasna.

### Gaps
- No Rath-bylined text was found that says when to switch from the Parasara drekkana to the Jagannatha, Somanatha or Parivritti-traya drekkana.
- No Rath-bylined text was found on D-4, D-7 or D-10 variants. His articles give only their uses. The "9th-from-sign start for even signs" D-10 rule is in PVR's book, not in Rath's text.
- Rath says D-30 has two methods but names only Parasara's. The second method is untraced.
- The contents of the "Hora Chakra" and "Shastyamsa" chapters (Rath's own text in *Varga Chakra*) could not be read. These are the most likely sources for his hora and D-60 choices.
- D-27, D-40 and D-45 variants and Rath's choice among them are untraced.

## 2. What is the construction rule of each named variant?

### Takeaway
Rules were found for the Jagannatha and Parivritti-traya drekkanas (PVR, secondary) and for PVR's own reversed D-24 (secondary). No public source read this session gave the rule for the Kashinatha hora, the Parivritti-dvaya hora, the Jagannatha hora or the Somanatha drekkana.

### Cited Findings
- **Parasara drekkana:** the three 10° thirds go to the 1st, 5th and 9th sign. This is the most common reading and what most software computes. — Rath's own text: [Principles](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/principles-of-divisional-charts/); secondary: [PVR 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/03/interpreting-parasaras-dreshkana.html)
- **Jagannatha drekkana:** PVR reads Parasara's "charaadishu" (movable and so on) as applying to the sign order. Movable signs take 1st, 5th, 9th; fixed signs 9th, 1st, 5th; dual signs 5th, 9th, 1st. This gives one 12-sign order repeated three times. PVR links it to Narayana dasa's pattern for dual signs. Label: secondary. — [PVR 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/03/interpreting-parasaras-dreshkana.html)
- **Parivritti-traya drekkana:** PVR reads "meshaadeh" as "starting from Aries". The 36 drekkanas run Ar to Pi three times, and the rishi lords are given afterwards by the movable-sign rule. Label: secondary. — [PVR 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/03/interpreting-parasaras-dreshkana.html)
- **Somanatha drekkana:** PVR names it, says it does not seem to come from Parasara, and gives no rule. Label: secondary. — [PVR 2005](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/03/interpreting-parasaras-dreshkana.html)
- **Parasara hora:** 15° halves; odd signs Sun then Moon, even signs Moon then Sun. PVR calls this correct but incomplete. Label: secondary. — VAIA §6.2.2 p. 44
- **Rath's hora by ownership:** a solar half (Leo to Capricorn) and a lunar half (Cancer back to Aquarius); the Sun owns Leo, the Moon owns Cancer, and other planets are placed by nearness. Label: Rath's own text. — [Principles](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/principles-of-divisional-charts/)
- **D-24 (PVR's proposal, 2012):** odd signs run Le to Cn twice. Even signs run backwards Cn, Ge, Ta ... Le twice, not the conventional Cn to Ge forwards. Longitudes in reversed divisions are counted from the end of the sign. He shows this with charts of Ashutosh Mukherjee, Vivekananda, Einstein, Gates, Obama, Aurobindo, Denver and JFK. He also suggests other Parasara vargas may be wrongly computed for even signs. Label: secondary (PVR's own research, not Rath's). — [Correct Siddhamsa](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/para_d24.pdf); [Two Novel Transit Principles](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_transits.pdf)
- **JHora "recommended by author" option (v7.65+):** this sets PVR's corrected divisional calculations. Label: software default (PVR's settings, not Rath's). — [Two Novel Transit Principles, p. 1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_transits.pdf)
- Visti Larsen has a page titled "Kashinatha Hora (D-2)" (3 Jan 2016). The fetched text covers only the origin of the word hora and a linked 2004 PDF. No construction rule was visible. Label: secondary (student). — [srigaruda.com](https://srigaruda.com/kashinatha-hora-d-2/)

### Inferences
- Because the Jagannatha drekkana repeats the movable-fixed-dual order, it produces a cyclic pattern similar to the Narayana-dasa order. That fits PVR's link to Narayana dasa and Rath's discussion of rishi lords in Narayana and Drig dasa.

### Gaps
- Kashinatha hora rule: not found. Visti Larsen's linked 2004 PDF and the Scribd class handout are the most likely sources. Neither could be read.
- Parivritti-dvaya hora rule: not found beyond the word "bicyclical".
- Jagannatha hora and the other three JHora hora options: not found.
- Somanatha drekkana rule: not found in any public text read.
- The second D-4 variant and the other two D-30 variants in JHora: rules not found.

## 3. Which statements carry his byline and which are his students'?

### Takeaway
Only three srath.com pages traced to Rath's byline touch varga variants: "Principles of Divisional Charts", "Trimsamsa D-30 Chart" and "Drekkana reckoning and Rishis". The detailed construction rules for the variants all come from P.V.R. Narasimha Rao (student). The use-by-question claims (Somanatha for libido, Parivritti-traya for drives, Kashinatha for wealth, Parivritti hora for family support) come from unattributed or weakly attributed sources.
