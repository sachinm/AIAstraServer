# Indu Lagna in Sanjay Rath's school (wealth)

Research date: 2026-11-17. Public web sources only. Labels: **Rath's own text**, **secondary (student of Rath)**, **classical text**, **unattributed** (third-party, no link to Rath's school).

Bottom line: no public calculation or reading rule for Indu Lagna could be found under Sanjay Rath's byline. The rule traces to Uttara Kalamrita, Chapter IV. The best-known textbook from his circle, by P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, does not cover Indu Lagna at all and treats Hora Lagna (money) and Sree Lagna (prosperity, through Sudasa) as the wealth references.

## Does Rath give a calculation or reading rule for Indu Lagna anywhere under his own byline?

### Takeaway
No. A search of srath.com for "indu lagna" returned no page that mentions it, and no web search tied any Indu Lagna rule, ranking or timing method to Rath. The only known mention is the bare naming in *Crux of Vedic Astrology* reported by earlier research. That was not re-checked here because the book's full text is not legitimately public. "He does not publish this" is the finding for the open web.

### Cited Findings
- **Rath's own text (site search):** srath.com's own search for "indu lagna" returns 81 pages of loosely matched results. None of the first-page titles or snippets (China mundane chart, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa, Narayana Dasa extracts, Rajendra Prasad and others) mention Indu Lagna or "indu". The only lagna named in a snippet is the arudha lagna. — [srath.com search](https://srath.com/?s=indu+lagna)
- **Rath's own text (book metadata):** *Crux of Vedic Astrology – Timing of Events* (Sagar Publications) has a Second House chapter starting at p. 79. The public listing shows the contents but not the text, so its wealth or Indu material could not be checked. — [issuu listing](https://issuu.com/abhimeranaam/docs/crux-of-vedic-astrology-timing-of-e); [eLibrary of Yoga record](https://elibraryofyoga.com/items/a43e2306-0d29-41f1-abd3-48ef3de345a4/full)
- **Unattributed:** Web searches combining "Indu Lagna" with Rath, Sri Jagannath Center, SoHamsa or the Jaimini Scholars program found no linking source. Search results for SJC and SoHamsa covered only the organisations. — [srath.com/?p=41](https://srath.com/?p=41); [srath.com Jaimini Scholar program](https://srath.com/category/learn/jaimini-scholar-program)

### Inferences
- Rath's public teaching on wealth special lagnas appears to run through Hora Lagna, Sree Lagna (Sudasa) and the Arudha system rather than Indu Lagna. This is inferred from his circle's textbook below and from the absence of any Rath material on Indu Lagna. It is not a statement Rath makes.
- Any variant calculation, reading of planets in or aspecting Indu Lagna, or dasha timing "as Rath teaches" would be invented. None is published.

### Gaps
- *Crux of Vedic Astrology* (second and eleventh house chapters) and *Vedic Remedies in Astrology* could not be read. No legitimate public full text exists, so the exact wording of his mention, and any passing rule, is unverified.
- The SJC mailing-list archives (old Yahoo groups such as varahamihira and sohamsa) are not indexed in search. Rath posts there could not be checked.
- No source was found in which Rath ranks Indu Lagna against Hora Lagna, Sri Lagna or Arudha Lagna for money.

## What do his students publish about it?

### Takeaway
P.V.R. Narasimha Rao is a long-time SJC teacher whose textbook is the standard reference of the school. The textbook does not mention Indu Lagna. It assigns money to Hora Lagna and prosperity to Sree Lagna, the latter read through Sudasa. No article on Indu Lagna by another SJC-linked author (Visti Larsen, Sarajit Poddar, Freedom Cole and others) turned up. Jagannatha Hora reportedly computes Indu Lagna, but only a third-party calculator site says so.

### Cited Findings
- **Secondary (student of Rath):** In P.V.R. Narasimha Rao's *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach* (free PDF on his site; first published 2000, file dated 18 Mar 2010), a full-text search finds no "Indu Lagna". The only hit for "Indu" is "Induvara Yoga" (§29.2.2, a muhurta topic). — [vedic_astro_textbook.pdf](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Secondary (student of Rath):** In the same book, Ch. 5 "Special Lagnas" covers Bhava, Hora and Ghati Lagna and Sree Lagna, and says the special lagnas are "defined by Parasara". §5.6 says hora lagna shows money and ghati lagna shows power. It adds that Hora Lagna shows the self from the angle of money, wealth and prosperity, and is key when timing periods for a businessman. — [same PDF, Ch. 5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Secondary (student of Rath):** In the same book, §5.7 calls Sree Lagna important for prosperity ("Sree" meaning wealth or Lakshmi), with its use in Sudasa. Sudasa is a Kendradi rasi dasa started from Sree Lagna, called the Lakshmi sthana. §7.3.8 calls Hora Lagna the reference for analysing one's wealth. §7.3.4 lists Arudha Lagna as a separate reference, and elsewhere the book ties Arudha Lagna to status. — [same PDF, §5.7, §7.3.4, §7.3.8, Sudasa chapter](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Unattributed (about the software of a student of Rath):** PanchangBodh says Jagannatha Hora outputs Indu Lagna, showing the Moon's degree with the Indu sign. It also says Indu Lagna comes from Uttara Kalamrita, not from Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra. No primary Jagannatha Hora documentation was found to confirm this. — [PanchangBodh comparison](https://panchangbodh.com/special-lagna-calculators-compared); [PanchangBodh Indu calculator](https://panchangbodh.com/indu-lagna-calculator)

### Inferences
- In this school, money is first read from Hora Lagna, and prosperity periods from Sree Lagna through Sudasa. Indu Lagna is not part of the core curriculum. If the software computes it, that is a convenience feature, not a taught technique.
- Because PVR frames the special lagnas as Parasara's, and Indu Lagna is not Parasari, its absence from the school's teaching is consistent.

### Gaps
- No SJC-affiliated article, lesson or case study on Indu Lagna was found.
- The Jagannatha Hora help file was not checked directly.

## What is the classical source of the rule and what does it say?

### Takeaway
The rule is in Kalidasa's *Uttara Kalamrita*, Chapter IV, at p. 97 of P.S. Sastri's translation. Kala values in weekday order are 30, 16, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 1. Add the kalas of the 9th lord from the lagna and the 9th lord from the Moon, divide by 12, and count the remainder from the Moon's sign; that sign is the Indu Lagna. Results: a benefic there with no malefic gives very great wealth; a malefic gives modest wealth; an exalted malefic again gives very great wealth. Later dasha, kendra and 3/6/8/12 rules circulate in modern writing and come from other texts or from no text.

### Cited Findings
- **Classical text:** Uttara Kalamrita (Kalidasa), English translation with notes by Prof. P.S. Sastri, published by Ranjan Publications, preface by K.N. Rao. The editor dates the author to the late 17th or early 18th century. — [archive.org full text](https://archive.org/stream/uttara-kalamritam./Uttara%20kalamritam._djvu.txt); dating discussed at [Wikipedia: Uttara Kalamrita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttara_Kalamrita)
- **Classical text:** Ch. IV (p. 97 in Sastri) gives the kalas of the seven planets from the Sun as 30, 16, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 1, then the rule above. The term "Indu lagna" is used. Result as rendered: a benefic without malefic makes one a multi-millionaire; a malefic gives wealth "in thousands"; an exalted malefic again gives multi-millionaire status. The OCR does not legibly show the verse number. — [archive.org text](https://archive.org/stream/uttara-kalamritam./Uttara%20kalamritam._djvu.txt)
- **Classical text (next verse, Sastri):** Two or more strong lords of houses 2, 5, 9 and 11 linked by conjunction, aspect or exchange give lakhs of wealth. A 6th, 8th or 12th lord joining them brings loss, debt and enemies. These effects come in the major periods of the 2/5/9/11 lords. This is a separate dhana rule, not Indu Lagna. — [archive.org text](https://archive.org/stream/uttara-kalamritam./Uttara%20kalamritam._djvu.txt)
- **Classical text (translator's note):** Sastri notes that some astrologers use Indu Lagna to gauge financial assets. His Chart 81 worked example: 9th lord Mercury from both lagna and Moon, 8+8=16, remainder 4, Indu in Aries holding Jupiter; the native held a very high post in an Indian state. — [archive.org text](https://archive.org/stream/uttara-kalamritam./Uttara%20kalamritam._djvu.txt)
- **Unattributed (secondary citation of the verse):** Dr. S. Shantha, in a 2021 magazine reprint, cites Uttara Kalamrita Ch. 4 Sl. 27 for the method and says Jataka Siromani also gives it. She attributes the result rules on exalted, benefic, malefic and empty Indu Lagna to "Grandhandhram". She also says Parashara calls it "Visesha Chandra Yoga", a claim not verified in BPHS here. — [powerofastro.com](https://powerofastro.com/2021/09/30/indu-lagna-and-dhana-prapthi/)
- **Unattributed (modern reading and timing rules, no classical verse given):** The same article lists rules not found in the Uttara Kalamrita verse. Benefics in or aspecting Indu Lagna give great wealth in their dasa. Planets in 3/6/8/12 from it destroy wealth in their dasa. Planets in kendras or trikonas from it give wealth in dasa or antardasa. Jupiter's transit of a kendra from it brings gains. The 2nd and 11th from it show the level of prosperity. Debilitated planets should not occupy or aspect it. — [powerofastro.com](https://powerofastro.com/2021/09/30/indu-lagna-and-dhana-prapthi/)
- **Unattributed:** K.S. Charak, *Elements of Vedic Astrology* (1998), p. 309, as cited by Wikipedia: several planets in Indu Lagna or in kendras from it are said to ensure riches. — [Wikipedia: Dhana yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhana_yoga)
- **Unattributed (variant):** Several calculator sites agree with the remainder method, with a zero remainder counted as 12. One aggregator describes a differing "count 7 from the Moon" step, which conflicts with the verse. — [PanchangBodh](https://panchangbodh.com/indu-lagna-calculator); [vaya.so tool](https://vaya.so/tools/indu-lagna)

### Inferences
- The classical core is narrow: the calculation, plus a three-way result by occupant (benefic, malefic, exalted malefic). Dasha timing, kendra/dusthana-from-IL rules and transit rules are later or regional additions. Any software or reading should label them as such.
- None of these rules, classical or modern, can be presented as Rath's method.

### Gaps
- The sloka number (27 per Shantha) could not be confirmed in the Sastri OCR, and numbering may differ between editions.
- The Sanskrit wording, and whether the original says "koti" (crores) or similar for "multi-millionaire", was not checked.
- The Jataka Siromani, "Grandhandhram" and BPHS "Visesha Chandra Yoga" attributions are second-hand and unverified.
