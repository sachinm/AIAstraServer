# D10 (Dasamsa) deity and direction scheme in Sanjay Rath's school

Research date: 2026-11-17. Labels used: **BPHS**, **Rath's own text**, **secondary (student of Rath)**, **secondary (not Rath school)**, **unattributed**. All wording below is paraphrase.

Bottom line: the deity list, the odd/even order and the dikpala identity are **plain BPHS** (ch. 6, vv. 13-14). No public text with Rath's own byline that lists the ten dasamsa deities or gives each one a profession meaning was found in this pass. Every deity-to-profession table found is secondary, and most are unattributed or outside Rath's school. The one bylined teaching item (a London 2004 Rath lecture on D10, posted by Visti Larsen) is public only as a header page; its body is not online.

## What does Rath himself write about dasamsa deities, and where?

### Takeaway
No retrievable text in Rath's own words sets out the ten dasamsa deities. Two Rath-bylined items on the dasamsa exist (a London 2004 lecture, and a 2015 "Dasamsa" seminar listed in a student library). Their public pages carry no body text, so their content on deities could not be checked.

### Cited Findings
- **Rath's own lecture, content not reachable:** srigaruda.com hosts a post titled "01. Dasamsa D10 – Pandit Sanjay Rath", posted by Visti Larsen on 17 Dec 2018. The photo caption reads "London 2004". The only text on the page is a short biography of Rath; nothing on deities, order or houses is shown. — [srigaruda.com, Visti Larsen post](https://srigaruda.com/01-dasa%E1%B9%81sa-d10-pandit-sanjay-rath/)

### Inferences
- The lead in the brief ("the dasamsa paper in Varga Chakra") looks wrong. That volume has no dasamsa paper, though the general essays by Sarkar or Narasimha Rao may touch on the D10 in passing.
- The most likely primary sources are the London 2004 recording and the 2015 seminar. Both appear to sit behind course or recording access (Larsen's recordings are sold through Rama Academy).

### Gaps
- No public text in Rath's own words gives the ten deities, a direction-to-quality table, or a rule for reading a planet's dasamsa deity. This is recorded as a gap, not a negative finding. The text may exist in the recordings or in print: *Crux of Vedic Astrology* (Sagar), *Jyotish Digest* issues, or SJC course volumes.
- The body of the srigaruda D10 post and the Sapta Matrika 2015 seminar could not be read.

## What meaning does his school give each deity for profession?

### Takeaway
No student of Rath with a byline (Larsen, Poddar, Narasimha Rao, Mahalingam) was found publishing a deity-by-deity profession table. The public tables come from (a) an unattributed blog and (b) Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan class notes outside Rath's school, which cite Rath's *Crux* among other books. The two broadly agree on the direction and planet pairings. They disagree on the zenith and nadir and on some job lists.

### Cited Findings
- **Narasimha Rao (student of Rath), textbook:** *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach* §6.2.10 gives only the D10 calculation: 3° parts, counted from the sign for odd signs and from the 9th for even signs. It lists the other names Dasamaamsa, Karmamsa and Swargamsa. It gives **no deities**. Elsewhere (§9.3–9.4) it reads the 10th from the D10 lagna as one's true conduct and work, the 10th from AL in the D10 as status and how one's conduct is perceived, A10 in the D10 as the workplace, and A5 in the D10 as following, power and awards. — secondary (student of Rath), [vedicastrologer.org PDF, free 2010 re-release](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Bylined notes outside Rath's school (Prof. Anthony Writer, Jyotisha Bharati, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan Mumbai, SlideShare):** the bibliography includes BPHS, Saravali, Raman, Rath's *Crux of Vedic Astrology* and Narasimha Rao's textbook. The notes do not say which table entry comes from which book. Odd-sign spans run 0–3° Indra up to 27–30° Ananta, reversed for even signs. Paraphrased pairings: — secondary (not Rath school), [SlideShare "VARGA D/10 or DASAMA"](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/v-44038347/44038347)
  - Indra – East – Sun – government or royal service, authority
  - Agni – SE – Venus – fire and energy fields (oil, gas, renewables)
  - Yama – South – Mars – law, judiciary, administration; engineering, defence, construction
  - Rakshasa/Nirriti – SW – Rahu – work dealing with law-breakers; hidden or unconventional work
  - Varuna – West – Saturn – water fields (shipping, fisheries), medicine; foreign travel
  - Vayu – NW – Moon – writing, scholarship, teaching, philosophy
  - Kubera – North – Mercury – finance, tax, accounting
  - Isana – NE – Jupiter – priesthood, counselling, teaching, banking
  - Brahma – listed as **downward**, tied to the 4th house – research, investigation
  - Ananta – listed as **upward**, tied to the 10th house – spiritual growth; fame when the 10th is strong
- **Unattributed blog (Astrology & Ayurveda):** gives the same planet and direction pairs, with Brahma at zenith (paired with Ketu) and Ananta at nadir (no planet). Profession glosses: Yama for judges, lawyers and police; Kubera for banking and accounts; Isana for teachers and advisors; Varuna for medicine, security and data; Agni for executives and administration. It cites no sources and names no author. — unattributed, [astrologyayurveda.com](https://astrologyayurveda.com/blog/10-ruling-deities-of-the-dasaṁsa-d10)
- **Commercial sites:** they repeat the scheme. One Jagannath Hora table puts Ananta 9th and Brahma 10th, against BPHS order. The same site warns that a planet in the Kubera part does not guarantee a finance career. — unattributed, [jagannathhora.com](https://jagannathhora.com/dasamsa-chart-d10-career-profession/); see also [vedicrajastrology.com](https://www.vedicrajastrology.com/2022/07/how-to-read-d10-dashamsha-chart-using-dashamsha-deities-in-astrology/)
- **Unbylined Scribd file "D101":** the uploader handle is a1n2u3s4h5a6, with no author named, and the body was cut off. It is unusable as a bylined source. — unattributed, [scribd.com/doc/263210253](https://www.scribd.com/doc/263210253/D101)
- **Visti Larsen and Sarajit Poddar:** searches found no public dasamsa-deity article by either. Larsen's recorded lessons (2005 onward) are sold through Rama Academy. — [rama-academy.teachable.com](https://rama-academy.teachable.com/p/srigaruda-recordings)

### Inferences
- The planet-direction pairs (Sun E, Venus SE, Mars S, Rahu SW, Saturn W, Moon NW, Mercury N, Jupiter NE) match the usual dik-graha scheme. Their pairing with the dasamsa deities looks like a teaching overlay, not something stated in BPHS 6.13–14. Not verified against a Rath text.
- The profession glosses read like the myth of each dikpala made literal: Kubera = wealth, Yama = judgement, Varuna = waters. No source ties them to Rath.

### Gaps
- No bylined student of Rath was found giving per-deity profession meanings. Hari Mahalingam: nothing found.
- No source was found, from Rath or anyone else, for a procedure that reads "the deity of planet X's dasamsa" alongside the D10 houses. The only house-side method traced to Rath's school is the Narasimha Rao reading of the 10th, AL, A10 and A5 in the D10, and it uses no deities.
- The Brahma and Ananta direction conflict (see below) is not settled by any Rath-school text.

## Which parts are plain BPHS and which are his school's reading?

### Takeaway
BPHS gives three things: the calculation (odd signs counted from the sign, even signs from the 9th), the ten deities in order from the east, and the reversal for even signs. Everything else is later teaching overlay with no Rath byline found: planets per deity, profession meanings, and how to combine deities with the D10 houses.

### Cited Findings
- **BPHS ch. 6 v. 13:** for odd signs the dasamsa counts from the sign itself, for even signs from the 9th from it. The deities, starting from the east, are Indra, Agni, Yama and Rakshasa (Nirriti). — BPHS, [enjoylearningsanskrit.com ch. 6](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-6)
- **BPHS ch. 6 v. 14:** Varuna, Maruta (Vayu), Kubera, Isana, Padmaja (Brahma) and Ananta follow. The order runs forward in odd signs and backward in even signs. The Sanskrit is quoted on the verse page. The site's word notes gloss Brahma as the upward direction and Ananta as the downward direction. The site names no translator or edition. — BPHS, [enjoylearningsanskrit.com ch. 6 v. 14](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-6/verse-14)
- **No profession meanings in BPHS:** the verse page carries no career reading of the deities. — [same](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-6/verse-14)
- **Zenith and nadir conflict:** the BPHS gloss and the Astrology & Ayurveda blog put Brahma above and Ananta below. The Anthony Writer notes reverse them (Brahma down with the 4th house, Ananta up with the 10th house). — [enjoylearningsanskrit](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-6/verse-14) vs [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/v-44038347/44038347)
- **Verse numbering:** the enjoylearningsanskrit numbering (6.13–14) was not checked against the Santhanam or G.C. Sharma translations. The Santhanam PDF is at [archive.org](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf) but was not read.

### Inferences
- In practice, the "even signs reversed" rule means even-sign parts run Ananta → Indra. The even-sign D10 sign itself is still counted from the 9th. These are two separate rules, and some web tables mix them up.

### Gaps
- No Rath text ties planets to deities or deities to professions.
- The Santhanam and Sharma verse numbers are unconfirmed.
- The original printing of the "Every Saint…" essay (journal and issue) is unconfirmed.
