# Sanjay Rath: profession from the Karakamsa and Swamsa

Scope note. This pass looked for Rath's own rules for reading profession from the Karakamsa and Swamsa, and kept the Jaimini sutra text apart from his commentary. Bottom line: **no publicly available text found in this pass gives Rath's commentary on planets in the Karakamsa for profession, his definition of Swamsa, a rule for the 10th from Karakamsa, or a rule for the Amatyakaraka.** His book *Jaimini Maharishi's Upadesa Sutras* (Sagar Publications, 1997) is the primary source. Its publisher and catalogue listings confirm that it covers these topics, but no legitimate excerpt of the relevant section was found online. What follows records what can be traced to Rath, what comes from a student with a byline, what comes from a different translation of the sutras, and what is still a gap.

Labels used: "Rath's own text"; "Jaimini sutra as he translates it" (nothing found in this pass qualifies); "secondary (student of Rath)"; "unattributed to Rath" (other translators and authors).

## What profession indications does his Jaimini sutra commentary give for each planet in the Karakamsa?

### Takeaway
Rath's commentary on the planet-by-planet sutras could not be found in any public source. The sutra content itself is available only in a different, older translation by B. Suryanarain Rao (edited by B.V. Raman), which is not Rath's. It must not be cited as "Jaimini sutra as he translates it", because Rath's numbering and readings may differ.

### Cited Findings

**Rath's own text**
- Rath's srath.com page for the book names him as author and gives the 1997 Sagar Publications edition. Its blurb lists the "distinction between karakamsa and swamsa" as a covered topic, illustrated with twins born two minutes apart. The page gives no sutra text and no profession rules. — [srath.com, "Jaimini Maharishi's Upadesa Sutra"](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutra/)
- Catalogue listings confirm that the book is Rath's translation with commentary and that it covers chara, sthira and naisargika karakas and karakamsa versus svamsa. No chapter-level content on profession is shown. — [astrokarak.com listing](https://www.astrokarak.com/jyotish-books/book/upadesa-sutras-of-maharishi-jaimini); [Exotic India listing](https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/jaimini-maharishi-s-upadesa-sutras-idj587); [Bagchee listing](https://www.bagchee.com/books/BB108191/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutras)
- Rath's article "Atmakaraka & Karakamsa" gives each planet's lesson as Atmakaraka: for example, humility for the Sun, control of speech for Mercury, and respect for the guru for Jupiter. It also gives the meaning of the Atmakaraka's house counted from the navamsa lagna. It does **not** give results for planets placed in or aspecting the Karakamsa, and it says nothing about profession. — [srath.com, "Atmakaraka & Karakamsa"](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)

**Unattributed to Rath: the B. Suryanarain Rao translation, edited by B.V. Raman (archive.org OCR text)**
These are the classical sutras in another scholar's English. Sutra numbers are as printed in that edition and were read through OCR that is garbled in places. Treat the numbers as approximate and do not convert them into Rath-style 1.2.x citations.
- **Where the sutras sit.** The heading "Adhyaya 1, Pada 2" opens with Su. 1, which says to fix the Atmakaraka and then judge the results of its navamsa position. The planet-in-Karakamsa sutras follow in the same pada. This places the topic in chapter 1, quarter 2, as the brief expects. — [archive.org, Jaiminisutras English Translation](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.142198/2015.142198.Jaiminisutras-English-Translation_djvu.txt)
- **Occupations for a planet with the Atmakaraka in the navamsa (Su. 14–22, translator's notes, paraphrased).** Caution: the request that produced this list named the expected occupations, so the list may echo that request. The Sun sutra (Su. 14) was confirmed independently.
  - Sun: public service or royal work.
  - Moon with Venus: wealth and comfort, and earning through teaching or learning.
  - Mars: weapons, fire, or the preparation of medicines.
  - Mercury: trade, weaving or clothing, arts, and crafts.
  - Jupiter: Vedic ritual and Vedanta, and a religious life.
  - Venus: a government official, with sexual vigour into old age.
  - Saturn: becoming famous in one's own line of work.
  - Rahu: weapons, theft, a doctor who deals in poisons, or metal work.
  - Ketu: dealing in elephants, or theft.

  — [archive.org, same text](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.142198/2015.142198.Jaiminisutras-English-Translation_djvu.txt)
- **Later in the same translation (numbers shaky; the tool flagged at least one misprint).**
  - The Sun and Venus aspecting the Karakamsa give work for rulers as a trusted confidant.
  - Malefics in both the 3rd and the 6th from the Karakamsa make a farmer; Jupiter in the 9th from it makes a great agriculturist.
  - Planets joining the Karakamsa or its 5th give learning:
    - Moon with Jupiter (on the Karakamsa or its 6th): a writer.
    - Venus: a poet or speaker.
    - Jupiter: a scholar of grammar, the Vedas and the Vedangas.
    - Mercury: a Mimamsaka.
    - Mars: a logician.
    - Moon: Sankhya, poetry, drama and music.
    - Sun: Vedanta and music.
    - Ketu: mathematics.
  - Jupiter's aspect or conjunction makes that learning well grounded.
  - Rahu in the 4th from the Karakamsa gives a mechanic in metals; the Sun there, a living by the sword; Mars there, work with maces and staffs.
  - Venus aspecting the Karakamsa together with the Moon gives an alchemist; Mercury doing the same gives a medical man.

  — [archive.org, same text](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.142198/2015.142198.Jaiminisutras-English-Translation_djvu.txt)
- In this translation every one of these sutras is framed as a planet joining the Atmakaraka **in the navamsa**, and the word "Swamsa" does not appear in the visible text. — [archive.org, same text](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.142198/2015.142198.Jaiminisutras-English-Translation_djvu.txt)

**Unattributed to Rath: other authors**
- Gary Gomes (Kepler College library; byline checked) attributes Karakamsa results to BPHS without verse numbers. Examples: Venus in the Karakamsa gives initiation into a religious order, and benefics there give kingship. He gives no profession rule tied to the Karakamsa. He mentions Rath's book only in passing and attributes no rule to Rath. — [Kepler College library, Gomes](https://library.keplercollege.org/?p=250)

### Inferences
- The classical sutras clearly assign occupations and learning to planets that join or aspect the Karakamsa and its 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th and 9th. Rath's translation very likely carries the same sutras, but how he reads them cannot be stated from public material: whether by aspect, by chart (rasi or navamsa), or with profession examples.
- Any product rule built on these sutras should cite the Suryanarain Rao/Raman translation (or another translation actually seen), not Rath.

### Gaps
- Rath's translation and commentary for chapter 1, quarter 2: sutra wording, numbering, and the examples he uses. No legitimate public excerpt was found.
- Unofficial Scribd copies of Rath material were tried and returned only page furniture: "SJC India 2004" containing his paper "The final proof of two carakaraka schemes", and "Karakamsa". The relevant text was not seen.
- Unverified leads not fetched in this pass, with no byline check: dailymirror.lk "Karakamsa – the key to open one's inner self" (3 parts); jagannathhora.com Amatyakaraka posts; tempora.ltd; astromedha.in.

## How does he define Swamsa versus Karakamsa, and which chart does he read each in?

### Takeaway
The only definition traceable to Rath himself is broad: the Karakamsa is the sign the Atmakaraka occupies in the navamsa "or another divisional chart", treated as a lagna. His book is advertised as distinguishing Karakamsa from Swamsa, but his definition of Swamsa, and which chart he reads each in, were not found. The common teaching that the Karakamsa is read with rasi-chart planets and the Swamsa with navamsa planets could not be traced to Rath.

### Cited Findings
- **Rath's own text.**
  - Rath derives the word from *karaka* (the Atmakaraka and the other chara karakas) plus *amsa* (divisional chart).
  - He defines the Karakamsa as the navamsa, or another divisional chart, in which the Atmakaraka falls. That sign becomes the Karakamsa lagna, described as the seat of the soul and the first house for one's deepest desires.
  - The page does not say whether the sign is carried into the rasi chart, and it does not mention Swamsa.

  — [srath.com, "Atmakaraka & Karakamsa"](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **Rath's own text (book blurb).** The book distinguishes karakamsa from swamsa and shows the difference in the charts of twins born minutes apart. The definition itself is not given. — [srath.com book page](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutra/)
- **Secondary (student of Rath).** P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*, names Rath as his guru in the front matter. In section 7.3.6 (Karakamsa Lagna) he defines the Karakamsa as the sign the Atmakaraka occupies in the navamsa and says the navamsa chart can be analysed from it. His example is the 12th from the Karakamsa for moksha. He does not use the term Swamsa there. — [vedicastrologer.org, PVR Narasimha Rao textbook PDF](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Unattributed to Rath.** Gomes attributes the practice of moving the navamsa sign into the natal chart and calling it the Karakamsa to K.N. Rao. He calls the Atmakaraka's navamsa position itself the "Swamsa chart". — [Kepler College library, Gomes](https://library.keplercollege.org/?p=250)
- **Unattributed to Rath (third party).** The blogger Shanmukha reports that Rath, in his Jaimini commentary, rejects the alternative Krishna Mishra navamsa reckoning. Shanmukha also argues that the Atmakaraka should be considered in the rasi chart as well. This is second-hand and Shanmukha is not a Rath student. — [sutramritam.blogspot.com, Dec 2008 posts](https://sutramritam.blogspot.com/2008)
- **Unattributed to Rath (search snippets only, pages not fetched).** Some web sources say Karakamsa readings use D1 planets and Swamsa readings use D9 planets, with the same lagna sign for both. Others say the Swamsa is a case where the Karakamsa and the navamsa lagna coincide or are linked. These are conflicting and unsourced.

### Inferences
- Rath's phrase "or another divisional chart" suggests that he lets the Karakamsa be taken in vargas other than D9: for example, the Atmakaraka's sign in D10 for career. This is an inference from his wording, not a stated rule.
- The D1-versus-D9 split between Karakamsa and Swamsa is widespread in the Jaimini community, but this pass found no Rath text stating it.

### Gaps
- Rath's actual definition of Swamsa, and his rule on which chart each is read in. These are probably in his commentary on the opening sutras of 1.2 in the book, which was not accessible.
- jaiminisutra.com and sohamsa.com (his course sites) were not reached with readable content in this pass.

## What does he say about the 10th from Karakamsa and the Amatyakaraka for career, and how does he combine this with the D10?

### Takeaway
No Rath text was found that states a rule for the 10th from the Karakamsa, the Amatyakaraka as career significator, or how to combine either with the D10. The sutras on the 10th from the Karakamsa exist in the older translation. The Amatyakaraka career rules found come from PVR Narasimha Rao (a student) and concern Raja Sambandha yogas, not the Karakamsa.

### Cited Findings
- **Unattributed to Rath (Suryanarain Rao/Raman translation; numbers approximate).**
  - Benefics (Jupiter or Venus, per the notes) aspecting the 10th from the Karakamsa give steadiness and determination.
  - The Sun aspecting the 10th gives success through cattle.
  - Mercury aspecting the 10th gives results like Saturn's, which the same section links to asceticism or service.

  — [archive.org, Jaiminisutras English Translation](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.142198/2015.142198.Jaiminisutras-English-Translation_djvu.txt)
- **Unattributed to Rath (same translation).** The only Amatyakaraka sutra seen in this section concerns the 6th from the Amatyakaraka joining an evil Karakamsa, read as devotion to evil spirits. It is not a career rule. — [archive.org, same text](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.142198/2015.142198.Jaiminisutras-English-Translation_djvu.txt)
- **Secondary (student of Rath).** In section 11.8 (Raaja Sambandha Yogas), PVR Narasimha Rao gives Amatyakaraka rules for becoming a minister or an associate of rulers:
  - The 10th lord joined or aspected by the Amatyakaraka or its dispositor.
  - The Atmakaraka and Amatyakaraka conjoined: very intelligent, a minister.
  - The Amatyakaraka strong in its own or exaltation sign: a minister.
  - The Amatyakaraka in a trine from the lagna: a famous minister.
  - The Amatyakaraka in a quadrant or trine from the Atmakaraka: an associate liked by the ruler.

  None of these uses the 10th from the Karakamsa. — [vedicastrologer.org, PVR textbook](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Secondary (student of Rath).** In the same book, PVR describes the chara karaka of a minister as people who give advice. He treats the D10 as the chart of career and achievements, used for questions such as promotion together with the ghati lagna. In D10, the 10th from the lagna shows the true nature of one's work and the 10th from the arudha lagna shows status. — [vedicastrologer.org, PVR textbook](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Unattributed to Rath.** An anonymous Scribd document ("Tenth", uploader only, no byline) says the navamsa shows the skills that lead to employment. Search snippets attribute to it a further line that the dasamsa shows success; this was not seen in the fetched text. — [Scribd, "Tenth"](https://www.scribd.com/doc/154520018/Tenth)
- **Unattributed to Rath.** Gomes calls the Amatyakaraka the career indicator but attributes this to no one. — [Kepler College library, Gomes](https://library.keplercollege.org/?p=250)

### Inferences
- A workable layered method (skills from the Karakamsa in D9, career success from D10, the Amatyakaraka for counsel and office) is consistent with what Rath's student PVR writes. That layering is still an assembly by this note; no Rath source states it as one rule.
- Rath's "or another divisional chart" definition would allow a Karakamsa in D10, but no example of him doing so was found.

### Gaps
- Rath's rule for the 10th from the Karakamsa: not found.
- Rath's rule linking the Amatyakaraka to career, or to the Karakamsa: not found. This confirms the earlier finding of no stated rule.
- Rath's method for combining the Karakamsa with the D10: not found.
- *Crux of Vedic Astrology* (tenth-house and profession chapters) was not seen in a legitimate public copy; issuu and Scribd uploads are unofficial and were not used.
- No published byline material from Visti Larsen or Sarajit Poddar on Karakamsa and profession was found in this pass. Larsen is described as Rath's student and as a Jaimini teacher, but no text of his on this topic was retrieved.
- The body of Rath's conference paper on two chara karaka schemes (SJC 2004, which has a "Karakamsa" section) was not readable via Scribd.
