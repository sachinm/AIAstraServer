# Indian frameworks of the self and the classical Jyotish factors that read them (for a "Self" dashboard)

Research date: 2026-11-09. Tool budget limited this pass to about 17 calls. Many sources reached are secondary
(Scribd uploads, astrology sites); they are labelled. Items marked **[UNVERIFIED - training knowledge]** were not
confirmed by a source fetched in this session and must be checked against a translation (Santhanam/Sharma BPHS,

## 1. Indian frameworks of the person: what each covers and how a modern user relates to it

### Takeaway
Panchakosha (five sheaths) gives the cleanest native "layers of self" scaffold (body, breath/energy, mind, intellect,
bliss), and Ayurvedic prakriti/dosha gives the most familiar "constitution" vocabulary; both are interpretive frames
with weak clinical evidence, so cards should present them as traditional lenses, not diagnoses.

### Cited Findings
- Panchakosha: the koshas "cover" the Self, listed gross to fine as annamaya (food/body), pranamaya (breath/vital
  energy), manomaya (mind), vijnanamaya (intellect/discernment) and anandamaya (bliss); source text is Taittiriya
  Upanishad 2.1-5; commonly pictured as onion layers — [Wikipedia: Kosha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosha)
- A 2023 review (Journal of Ayurveda & Holistic Medicine) frames the koshas as "five layers of awareness" through which
  experience is filtered and links health to dosha balance — [JAHM 2023 via wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/uploads/journals/jahm/volume-11-issue-2-2023/663-547-1404.pdf)
- Government AYUSH portal (Vikaspedia) ties pranamaya health to the five pranas: balance of the five pranas is health,
  imbalance results in disease — [Vikaspedia: Pancha kosha](https://www.vikaspedia.in/health/ayush/yoga-1/pancha-kosha)
- NCERT uses "Panchakosha Vikas" as a framework for child development (physical, mental, behavioural) — shows the frame
  is mainstream in Indian education policy, not fringe — [NCERT e-journal](https://ejournals.ncert.gov.in/index.php/tpt/article/download/4998/4756/9216)
- Prakriti (Ayurvedic constitution, vata/pitta/kapha): a 2015 Scientific Reports genome-wide study screened 3,416
  people, used 262 high-dominance individuals, and PCA of SNPs grouped them into Vata/Pitta/Kapha "irrespective of
  their ancestry" — [PMC4625161](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4625161/)
- CYP2C19 poor-metabolizer genotype was highest in Kapha prakriti (31%) vs Vata (12%) and Pitta (9%) in a study of
  132 healthy people — [PMC search result, see PMC3135904 / PMC4012357 list](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3135904)
- Reviews call the prakriti-genomics evidence "preliminary" and say further studies are warranted; studies are small,
  often single-centre, and partly authored by proponents — [PMC3149388](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3149388);
  [Springer 2020 review](https://springerlink.bibliotecabuap.elogim.com/article/10.1007/s40495-020-00244-3)
- Seven chakras: other Vedic/Hindu/Tantric traditions count five, eight, nine, twelve or more chakras; one author notes
  Upanishads speak of six chakras plus the sahasrara seat (self-published, low weight) —
  [Barbara Pijan: Chakra planets](https://barbarapijan.com/bpa/Graha/Chakra_planets.htm);
  [ebookpoint excerpt](https://ebookpoint.pl/pobierz-fragment/discovering-youniverse-achala-sylwia-mihajlovic,s_002z/pdf)

Framework summary for card design (definitions are standard; sources for each beyond the above were **not** fetched
this session — [UNVERIFIED - training knowledge]):
| Framework | What it covers | Modern user relation |
|---|---|---|
| Panchakosha | Body / energy / mind / intellect / bliss layers | Familiar from yoga classes; good "layers of you" layout |
| Tri-guna (sattva, rajas, tamas) | Quality of mind and action (Bhagavad Gita ch. 14, Samkhya) | Used as "mental state / clarity vs restlessness vs inertia" |
| Prakriti / tridosha | Inborn body-mind constitution | Widely known; diet, sleep, temperament quizzes on every wellness app |
| Sapta dhatu | Seven tissues: rasa, rakta (blood), mamsa, meda, asthi, majja, shukra | Less known by name; "blood", "bone", "strength" are relatable |
| Ojas / tejas / prana | Immunity-resilience / radiance-metabolic fire / life breath | Ojas = "immunity/glow" in popular Ayurveda marketing |
| Chakras (7) | Subtle-body centres | Very popular with urban/diaspora users, but tradition and planet links are modern (see §2) |
| Purusharthas | Dharma, artha, kama, moksha — four aims of life | Natural map to Duty/Work/Love/Inner peace boards |
| Ashramas | Life stages (brahmacharya, grihastha, vanaprastha, sannyasa) | Maps to age/life-stage context |
| Sadhana, dinacharya, ritucharya | Practice; daily routine; seasonal routine | Habits/routine card; seasonal guidance |
| Vrata, upaya | Vows/fasts; remedies (mantra, daan/charity, fasting on a planet's weekday, gemstones) | Most-demanded and most-monetised item (see §4); legally sensitive (see §5) |
| Ishta devata / kula devata | Personal chosen deity / family-lineage deity | High emotional resonance; Ishta derivable from chart (§2) |

### Inferences
- Panchakosha + purusharthas give two native axes: "layers of you" (inside) and "aims of life" (outside). The You /
  Love / Work boards already map onto dharma/kama/artha; moksha maps to inner peace / 12th house.
- Prakriti is the most "personal-feeling" card but needs a questionnaire, not only a chart; the chart can at best hint.
- Keep "constitution" language descriptive ("tends to run warm / restless"), never medical.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed NCBI review of panchakosha found; the evidence base is interpretive.
- No source fetched on how many Indian users know/use gunas, koshas, chakras (no survey found).

## 2. Classical Jyotish factors for body, mind, character, image, vitality, sleep, deity

### Takeaway
BPHS gives an explicit planet-to-dhatu list (3.31) and planet-to-dosha temperaments; the Kalapurusha and drekkana body
maps are classical; planet-to-chakra mappings have no classical Jyotish source found and are modern syntheses with
conflicting versions. Character/deity come from Jaimini (atmakaraka, karakamsha, 12th from karakamsha).

### Cited Findings
- BPHS 3.31: Sun and the following planets "in respective order" lord bone, blood, marrow, skin, fat, semen (virya) and
  sinew → Sun-bone, Moon-blood (rakta), Mars-marrow, Mercury-skin, Jupiter-fat, Venus-semen, Saturn-sinew/muscle —
  [enjoylearningsanskrit: BPHS 3.31](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-3/verse-31)
- Blood is sometimes linked to Mars in other schemes, but BPHS assigns it to the Moon — flag for the "rakta" card —
  [bharatiyajyotish blog](https://bharatiyajyotishmantrasaadhana.blogspot.com/2025/09/the-seven-dhatus-and-their-planetary.html?m=1)
- Planet-to-dosha mappings found online (Pitta: Sun, Mars; Kapha: Moon, Jupiter, Venus; Vata: Saturn, Rahu) come from
  secondary sites; the search could not tie them to BPHS ch. 3 verse numbers —
  [dekhopanchang: Ayurveda-Jyotish](https://dekhopanchang.com/en/learn/ayurveda-jyotish)
  - [UNVERIFIED - training knowledge] BPHS ch. 3 planet descriptions do state temperaments: Sun pitta; Moon vata+kapha;
    Mars pitta; Mercury mixed (all three); Jupiter kapha; Venus vata+kapha; Saturn vata. Rahu/Ketu dosha links are
    later/secondary. Check Santhanam ch. 3 vv. ~23-33.
- Chakra-planet: no classical Jyotish text found that sets out a mapping; popular versions disagree (one puts
  Muladhara under Saturn and Sahasrara under Neptune; some add Uranus/Pluto; an older esoteric text runs Saturn,
  Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Sun from Muladhara up); 17th-c. European mystic Gichtel placed the seven
  planets on the body — origin is Western-esoteric + modern synthesis —
  [Karmastro](https://karmastro-site.pages.dev/en/blog/chakras-astrology-7-planets-energy-centers-correspondence);
  [Mysticryst](https://mysticryst.com/blogs/the-mystic-journal/the-7-chakras-7-classical-planets-your-energy-body-as-cosmic-antenna);
  [Wikisource esoteric writings](https://kwx.liehendr.com/content/wikisource_en_all_maxi_2022-09/A/Page%3AA_Collection_of_Esoteric_Writings.djvu/268)
- Ishta devata (Jaimini): atmakaraka = planet with highest degree; its navamsa sign = karakamsha; the 12th sign from
  karakamsha in navamsa shows the deity (planet there, else the sign lord); tie-breaks vary by source (one gives
  Ketu > Jupiter > Venus > Moon > Sun > Mars > Mercury > Saturn > Rahu, not attributed to a classical text);
  sutra range cited as Upadesa Sutras 1.2.68-82 or 1.2.68-85 (sources disagree) —
  [Medium: Jaimini Ishta devata](https://medium.com/jaimini-astrology-know-your-ishta-devta-spirituali/find-the-ishta-devta-with-jaimini-astrology-be5b4dd51967);
  [vedicmarga calculator](https://vedicmarga.com/ishta-devata-calculator/);
  [sarastrology](https://sarastrology.blogspot.com/2018/02/how-to-determine-ista-devata.html)
- Some Jaimini scholars dispute whether "karakamsha" means the AK's navamsa sign or its rasi sign (the "navamsa
  karakamsa not" debate) — [sutramritam blog](https://sutramritam.blogspot.com/2008/12/krishna-mishra-navamsa-karakamsa-not.html)

Factor map (standard textbook assignments; **[UNVERIFIED - training knowledge]** unless cited above):
| Self area | Classical factors |
|---|---|
| Body, constitution, appearance | Lagna, lagna lord, planets in/aspecting lagna; Sun (vitality, bones); Moon (fluids, blood per BPHS) |
| Body-part map | Kalapurusha: Aries head, Taurus face, Gemini neck/arms, Cancer chest, Leo belly/heart region, Virgo waist/intestines, Libra lower abdomen/pelvis, Scorpio genitals, Sagittarius thighs, Capricorn knees, Aquarius calves, Pisces feet (BPHS ch. 4 / Brihat Jataka 1.4). Drekkana (D3) body parts: 1st decanate = head region, 2nd = trunk, 3rd = lower body, mapped house-by-house (Brihat Jataka ch. 5 / Saravali) |
| Disease / health stress | 6th (illness), 8th (chronic, longevity), 12th (hospital, loss, sleep); their lords; dhatu/dosha of afflicting planet |
| Mind | Moon (manas), 4th house (peace, home, heart), 5th (buddhi, intelligence), Mercury (reasoning, speech) |
| Character / soul | Atmakaraka (Jaimini), karakamsha, navamsa lagna; Sun as natural atma karaka |
| How others see you | Arudha lagna (AL/pada lagna) = image, perceived status (Jaimini; Rath, PVR "Integrated Approach") |
| Vitality, longevity | Lagna/lagna lord strength, 8th, Saturn (ayushkaraka); classical longevity methods (Pindayu etc.). Longevity prediction should be excluded from product as a matter of ethics |
| Sleep | 12th house (shayana sukha, bed pleasures), Moon |
| Ishta devata | 12th from karakamsha in D9 (Jaimini, cited above) |
| Dharma devata / kula devata | Often 9th from karakamsha / other Rath-school rules — unverified |

### Inferences
- Safe, sourced card: "Your dhatus by planet" (BPHS 3.31) — can show which tissue-strength the chart emphasises,
  framed as tradition.
- Chakra cards should either be dropped or explicitly labelled "modern association", since diaspora users may see
  them as authentic Jyotish when they are not.
- Ishta devata is a strong, culturally native, chart-derived card; the engine must state the tie-break rule it used.

### Gaps
- BPHS dosha verse numbers, Kalapurusha verse, drekkana body-part tables, arudha-lagna interpretation and Rath/PVR
  statements on AL were not fetched this session.
- Phaladeepika, Jataka Parijata, Saravali, Prasna Marga health chapters were not opened.

## 3. Dasha systems and which person each applies to

### Takeaway
Vimshottari (from Moon's nakshatra) is the default; BPHS gives conditional nakshatra dashas each gated by a chart
condition, but practitioners treat those conditions as necessary-not-sufficient and say to use them alongside
Vimshottari. Tithi Pravesha is the "Vedic birthday" annual chart judged like a natal chart for one year.

### Cited Findings
Conditional nakshatra dashas (BPHS), per Saravali.github.io and Pijan's quotes of Santhanam's BPHS:
- Ashtottari (108 y): Rahu not in lagna, a kendra or trikona (one reading); BPHS excerpt also ties the variant to Rahu's
  placement from the lagna lord, with Ardradi and Krittikadi starting points; eligibility is disputed —
  [Saravali: Special Nakshatra Dasas](https://saravali.github.io/astrology/dasa_nakshatra.html);
  [Scribd BPHS dasa systems](https://www.scribd.com/document/460122993/BPHS-Dahsa-Systems);
  [Satyori: Ashtottari](https://satyori.com/jyotish/dasha/ashtottari/)
- Shodashottari (116 y): lagna in Moon's hora with Krishna paksha birth, or Sun's hora with Shukla paksha birth
  (Santhanam); periods of 11-18 years; Satyori phrases it as day birth in Krishna paksha / night birth in Shukla —
  translators differ — [Pijan: BPHS dasha systems](https://www.barbarapijan.com/bpa/VimshottariDasha/1Vimshottari_dasha_BPHS_dashaSystems47.htm);
  [Satyori: Shodashottari](https://satyori.com/jyotish/dasha/shodashottari/)
- Dwadashottari (112 y): lagna in a navamsa of Venus; variant reading: lagna vargottama —
  [Pijan](https://www.barbarapijan.com/bpa/VimshottariDasha/1Vimshottari_dasha_BPHS_dashaSystems47.htm);
  [Satyori: Dwadashottari](https://satyori.com/jyotish/dasha/dwadashottari/)
- Panchottari: lagna in Cancer and D12 lagna in Cancer (about 1 in 144 charts) — [Saravali](https://saravali.github.io/astrology/dasa_nakshatra.html)
- Shatabdika: lagna vargottama (rasi and navamsa lagna same sign) — [Saravali](https://saravali.github.io/astrology/dasa_nakshatra.html)
- Chaturashiti-sama: 10th lord in the 10th — [Saravali](https://saravali.github.io/astrology/dasa_nakshatra.html)
- Dwisaptati-sama: lagna lord in 1st or 7th — [Saravali](https://saravali.github.io/astrology/dasa_nakshatra.html)
- Shat-trimsha-sama: lagna in Sun's hora for day birth or Moon's hora for night birth —
  [Saravali](https://saravali.github.io/astrology/dasa_nakshatra.html); [Satyori: Shattrimsha-sama](https://satyori.com/jyotish/dasha/shattrimsha-sama/)
- Shashtihayani: covered by Satyori but its condition was not read this session — [Satyori: Shashtihayani](https://satyori.com/jyotish/articles/shashtihayani/)
- Note the Dwadashottari variant ("vargottama") collides with Shatabdika's condition — a source conflict to resolve
  against the translation.
- Conditional dashas "should only be used in combination with standard Dasas like Vimsottari and Kalachakra" —
  [Saravali](https://saravali.github.io/astrology/dasa_nakshatra.html)
- PVR Narasimha Rao (paper on conditional dasas): Parashara's conditions are "only necessary conditions and not
  sufficient conditions"; in many charts that meet a condition Vimshottari still dominates — per search summary of
  [vedicastrologer.org pp_cond_dasas.pdf](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_cond_dasas.pdf) (PDF text not
  extractable in this session; claim seen via search snippet only)

Annual charts:
- Tithi Pravesha (PVR): chart cast each year when the Moon is at the same angle from the Sun as at birth, in the same
  soli-lunar month; judged "just like the natal chart" but effective for one year —
  [PVR: pp_tp.pdf](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_tp.pdf) (via search snippet)
- Dasha in the TP chart: a Rao/Rath discussion transcript says the applicable dasha is Tithi Ashtottari dasha —
  [Scribd: Tithi Pravesh](https://www.scribd.com/document/347971271/Tithi-Pravesh-5);
  [Scribd: TP chart analysis](https://www.scribd.com/document/348629674/Tithi-Pravesha-Chart-Analysis) (secondary)
- PVR on Tajaka varshaphala: nakshatra dasa approach works well in natal and TP charts but not in Tajaka varshaphala
  charts; he had "not yet found a dasa system that works reliably" there —
  [PVR: pp_tajaka.pdf](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_tajaka.pdf) (via search snippet)
- Rath's "Dasa Pravesh Chakra" is not tied only to Narayana dasa —
  [Scribd: Dasa Pravesh Chakra](https://www.scribd.com/document/448743437/Dasa-Pravesh-Chakra)

Jaimini rasi dashas:
- Narayana dasa (Rath book; also called Padakrama dasa) starts from the stronger of lagna or 7th, the "satya-peethas" —
  [srath.com: Narayana Dasa](https://srath.com/page/63/)
- Narayana dasa sign-progression rule (count from dasa rasi as far as from lagna to dasa rasi) — unofficial Rath notes —
  [Scribd narayandasa](https://www.scribd.com/document/352108125/narayandasa-pdf)

### Inferences
- Product rule: run Vimshottari for everyone; compute which conditional dashas a chart qualifies for and show them only
  as an "also applies to you" cross-check, never as the headline timing.
- Tithi Pravesha is a native "your year" card (Hindu-calendar birthday), more culturally native than solar-return.

### Gaps
- What Rath says Narayana dasa is *for* (common teaching: events/environment, applied per varga e.g. D10 for career)
  is [UNVERIFIED - training knowledge]; srath.com excerpt did not state it.
- Chara dasa details, Mudda dasa (Vimshottari compressed into the Tajaka year) rules, total years of each conditional
  dasha beyond Shodashottari/Ashtottari/Dwadashottari were not fetched.
- Shashtihayani condition not confirmed.
- Explicit cross-checking methodology by named practitioners only partly sourced (Saravali + PVR snippet).

## 5. Ethical and legal cautions in India (health claims, remedies)

### Takeaway
The Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954 bars advertising "magic remedies" for listed
diseases, and ASCI actively refers health/magic-remedy ads to the AYUSH Ministry; no astrology-specific ASCI rule was
found, but "guaranteed" outcome claims are routinely struck down.

### Cited Findings
- DMR Act 1954 purpose: control drug advertising and prohibit advertising of remedies "alleged to possess magic
  qualities"; a section prohibits advertising magic remedies for treatment of specified diseases —
  [India Code: Act 21 of 1954](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/bitstream/123456789/1412/1/A1954-21.pdf);
  [Wikipedia: DMR Act](https://Www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Drugs_%26_Magic_Remedies_Act)
- ASCI 2024-25: 233 ads reported to the Ministry of AYUSH for potential DMR Act violations —
  [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/industry/pharma/asci-flags-233-health-ads-for-dmr-act-violation-reports-to-ayush-ministry-149226);
  [ASCI Annual Complaints Report 2024-25](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Digital-ASCI-Annual-Complaints-Report-2024-25.pdf)
- ASCI celebrity guidelines bar celebrities from endorsing products prohibited under the DMR Act —
  [ASCI celebrity guidelines](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/guidelines-for-celebrities-in-advertising.pdf)
- ASCI upholds complaints on "guaranteed" claims lacking evidence (e.g., guaranteed weight loss, 100% guaranteed job
  placement) — [ASCI Nov 2019 CCC release](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/asci_nov_2019_ccc_release.pdf);
  [ASCI complaint outcomes database](https://www.ascionline.in/codes-cases/complaint-outcomes)

### Inferences
- Never phrase a remedy (gemstone, mantra, puja) as treating or curing a disease; never "guaranteed" results.
- Health cards: describe constitutional tendencies and suggest routine/doctor, not diagnosis; keep longevity
  (ayurdaya) out of the product.

### Gaps
- No ASCI ruling specifically on astrology/gemstone ads found (need to search ASCI's outcome database directly).
- Not researched: state anti-superstition laws (Maharashtra 2013, Karnataka 2017), Consumer Protection Act 2019 /
  CCPA misleading-ads guidelines 2022, IT Rules for apps — all likely relevant.
