## 1. Vimshottari: riders and variants, and when each applies

### Takeaway
Rath's own Vimshottari lesson does not default to the Moon. For a natal chart it starts from the strongest of three nakshatras: lagna, Janma (Moon) or Utpanna (5th from Moon). For a horary chart it uses the stronger of the name and query (prashna) nakshatras. It falls back to the Moon's nakshatra when in doubt. Tara dasa replaces it when four or more planets are in kendras from lagna. All dashas run at once and differ only in which dominates. His book *Vimsottari and Udu Dasas* has a full chapter on choosing the dasha (Ch. 3), but its text was not available.

### Cited Findings
- **(A)** Vimshottari is the foremost udu dasha because its 120 years equal the full human life span in Kali yuga. Ashtottari (108) and Shodashottari (116) are named as alternatives. — [srath.com, Vimśottari Daśā](https://srath.com/?p=804)
- **(A) Five candidate starting nakshatras:** lagna nakshatra, Janma nakshatra (Moon), name nakshatra (first syllable of the name), prashna nakshatra (Moon at the time of the question), and Utpanna (5th from Janma). A footnote also names Kshema (4th) and Adhana (8th). — [srath.com/?p=804](https://srath.com/?p=804)
- **(A) Selection rule.** Natal chart: the strongest of the lagna, Janma and Utpanna nakshatras. Horary chart: the stronger of the name and prashna nakshatras. — [srath.com/?p=804](https://srath.com/?p=804); the same text is reproduced as a "Sri Sanjay Rath" lesson on a 2005 blog, [edstars.blogspot.com](https://edstars.blogspot.com/2005/01/vimsottari-dasa-sri-sanjay_110616653195301643.html) (C reproduction of A)
- **(A) Strength test between nakshatras:** count the planets in kendras from the sign holding each nakshatra. If tied, or if the signs are in mutual kendras, use conjunction or aspect of Jupiter, Mercury or the nakshatra's lord. If a nakshatra spans two signs, the lagna or Moon position fixes which sign counts (per the blog reproduction). — [srath.com/?p=804](https://srath.com/?p=804); [edstars blog](https://edstars.blogspot.com/2005/01/vimsottari-dasa-sri-sanjay_110616653195301643.html)
- **(A) Default:** when in doubt in human charts, prefer the Moon's nakshatra, because the Moon (mind) governs fortune. — [srath.com/?p=804](https://srath.com/?p=804)
- **(A) Tara dasa:** applies when four or more planets are in kendras from lagna. The strongest kendra planet starts, then the other kendra planets, then panapara, then apoklima. Periods are the Vimshottari years. The worked example uses bhava bala to rank kendra planets, because nodes cannot take graha bala. — [srath.com/?p=804](https://srath.com/?p=804)
- **(A) Simultaneity:** all dashas, Vimshottari variants included, operate at the same time and differ only in dominance. — [srath.com/?p=804](https://srath.com/?p=804)
- **(A, contents only) Book structure.** *Vimsottari and Udu Dasa's: Parasara's Key to Prognostication* (Sagar, 2002 edition per listing). Ch. 3 "Dasa applicability" (3.1 Parasara's advise, 3.2 Selection methodology, 3.3 Illustrations). Ch. 5.3 "Starting dasa", 5.6 "Ayur dasa". Ch. 6 "Vimsottari Variations": 6.1 Tara, 6.2 Moola, 6.3 Tribhagi, 6.4 Compressed Vimsottari. Ch. 7.5 Tithi Ashtottari. Ch. 8-15 each have an "Applicability" section. The listing describes Ch. 3 as the how and why of choosing the right dasha. — [Exotic India listing](https://exoticindiaart.com/book/details/vimsottari-and-udu-dasa-s-parasara-s-key-to-prognostication-naj126)
- **(A) Udu vs rasi dasha in timing practice.** Udu dashas are hard to use for a dasa pravesh chakra (a chart cast for the start of a dasha), because a few minutes' error in the Moon moves the start date a lot. Narayana dasa is convenient for this because it begins on the birthday and uses the solar ingress (varsha pravesh) chart. — *Narayana Dasa*, Judgment of Results, Dasa Pravesh Chakra paragraph (around p. 90), [PDF](https://scienceoflight.net/wp-content/uploads/extra/Narayana%20Dasa%20by%20Sanjay%20Rath.pdf)
- **(B) PVR's variants.** He reports dasha-from-lagna-longitude as recommended by "some authorities". It works better only when lagna is much stronger than the Moon. Utpanna (5th star) is preferred for general results when its sign is stronger than the Moon's sign. Kshema/Adhana lines (4th and 8th stars) are mainly for longevity: compare the Moon's sign with the signs of those two stars, and here maraka and malefic aspects make a sign stronger. He says the literature gives no clear guideline for comparing strength, and suggests Jupiter's aspect, planet count, or testing against known events. — PVR, *Integrated Approach*, Ch. 16.4.2 and 16.5.2 (pp. 185-186), [PDF](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/docs2/LakshmiNarayanLenasia.com/Astrology%20(Jyotish)%20collection/Vedic%20Astrology%20an%20Integrated%20Approach%20by%20P.V.R.%20Narasimha%20Rao.pdf)
- **(B) PVR's Kendradi condition differs from Rath's Tara condition.** If all kendras from the stronger of lagna and Moon hold planets, Kendradi Graha Dasa (Moola dasa) is more suitable than Vimshottari. In Ch. 19.1 he says some authors apply Moola dasa to everyone, and that with four planets in kendras from the stronger of lagna and Moon it gives much better results than Vimshottari. — PVR, *Integrated Approach*, Ch. 16.7 and 19.1. Rath's lesson counts kendras from lagna only and names the result Tara dasa (A, above).
- **(B) PVR on Rath's "Tripod of Life" principle.** Sun, Moon and lagna are the reference points. Use the Sun to judge a mahadasha, the Moon for an antardasha, and lagna for a pratyantardasha. — PVR, *Integrated Approach*, Ch. 16.5.3 (titled "Rath's 'Tripod of Life' Principle")

### Inferences
- An engine that implements "Rath Vimshottari" needs a seed-selection step: lagna vs Janma vs Utpanna by kendra-planet count, then a Jupiter/Mercury/lord tiebreak, with the Moon as fallback. It also needs a Tara-dasa switch at four or more planets in kendras from lagna. Kshema and Adhana belong to longevity use only.
- Rath's Tara condition (lagna kendras) and PVR's Moola/Kendradi condition (kendras from the stronger of lagna and Moon) must not be merged.

### Gaps
- The text of *Vimsottari and Udu Dasas* Ch. 3 (selection methodology), 5.3 (starting dasa) and 6 (Tribhagi, Compressed Vimshottari conditions) was not found online. When Tribhagi or compressed Vimshottari applies in Rath's scheme is unknown.
- The "Udu dasa" in the title means nakshatra dashas in general. No separate "Udu dasa" variant rule was found.
- The Ashtottari "both criteria" wording on srath.com/?p=804 is ambiguous; see section 2.

## 2. Conditional nakshatra dashas: conditions and precedence

### Takeaway
Rath's own online text gives conditions only for Ashtottari and Dvisaptati Sama (Dvisaptati is in the earlier notes), and it ranks Vimshottari first. His book has an "Applicability" section for each of the nine conditional dashas, but its text was not reachable. The fullest SJC-lineage precedence rule is PVR's 2014 "controlling planet" method: a condition is necessary but not sufficient. That is PVR's own deduction, not Rath's.

### Cited Findings
- **(A) Ashtottari conditions.** Criterion 1: Rahu in a kendra or trikona from the lagna lord's sign. Criterion 2: daytime birth in Krishna paksha, or night birth in Shukla paksha. Use Ashtottari only when the conditions are met, otherwise Vimshottari. The text says "if both", but treats the two elsewhere as alternatives. A footnote implies both must hold. The main text estimates Ashtottari in about 25% of charts, while a footnote says Vimshottari applies in 25%. — [srath.com/?p=804](https://srath.com/?p=804). Conflict unresolved in the source.
- **(A) Tithi Ashtottari exists in Rath's book** (Ch. 7.5-7.6, pp. 187-188), so it is his method or one he teaches. Its use is not described in the listing. — [Exotic India listing](https://exoticindiaart.com/book/details/vimsottari-and-udu-dasa-s-parasara-s-key-to-prognostication-naj126). This answers the earlier gap: Tithi Ashtottari *is* in a Rath book.
- **(A, contents only)** The book has applicability sections for Shodashottari (8.1), Dwadashottari (9.1), Panchottari (10.1), Shatabdika (11.1) and Shat-trimsha Sama (15.3). It also covers Chaturaseeti Sama (Ch. 12, starting dasha 12.2), Dvisaptati Sama (Ch. 13) and Shashtihayani (Ch. 14). — [Exotic India listing](https://exoticindiaart.com/book/details/vimsottari-and-udu-dasa-s-parasara-s-key-to-prognostication-naj126). This establishes that Rath teaches Chaturaseeti Sama, which the earlier report listed as having no Rath text.
- **(B) PVR's condition table (SJC/Jagannatha Hora convention):**
  - Shodashottari (116): lagna in Sun's hora in Shukla paksha, or in Moon's hora in Krishna paksha (about 50% of charts).
  - Dwadashottari (112): lagna in a Taurus or Libra (Venus) navamsa.
  - Ashtottari (108): Rahu in a kendra or trikona from the lagna lord, but not in lagna.
  - Panchottari (105): lagna in Cancer dwadashamsa. PVR calls "Cancer in both rasi and dwadashamsa" a mistranslation.
  - Shatabdika (100): lagna vargottama.
  - Chaturaseeti Sama (84): 10th lord in the 10th.
  - Dvisaptati Sama (72): lagna lord in the 7th, or 7th lord in lagna.
  - Shashtihayani (60): Sun in lagna.
  - Shat-trimsha Sama (36): lagna in Sun's hora by day, or Moon's hora by night.
  — [PVR, Unified Nakshatra Dasa Approach, 2014](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_cond_dasas.pdf)
- **(B) PVR on precedence.** Parashara gave no rule for choosing among Vimshottari and conditional dashas that apply at the same time, and several overlap. If a conditional dasha always overrode Vimshottari, Vimshottari would almost never be used. So a condition is "necessary" and not "sufficient". The dasha overrides Vimshottari only when its controlling planet is also strong. Controlling planets: Shodashottari Jupiter, Dwadashottari Ketu, Ashtottari Mars, Panchottari Venus, Shatabdika Sun, Chaturaseeti Saturn, Dvisaptati Rahu, Shashtihayani Moon, Shat-trimsha Mercury. He says this works better in annual Tithi Pravesha charts than in natal charts. It depends on his own "Pushya-paksha ayanamsa" and corrected varga calculations. — [same PDF](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_cond_dasas.pdf). This is PVR's innovation, not Rath's text.
- **(B) PVR's book (earlier) gives three competing views on Ashtottari:** it applies to all charts; it applies when Rahu (not in lagna) is in a kendra or trikona from the lagna lord; or it applies to day births in Krishna paksha and night births in Shukla paksha. He also notes its use as an ayur dasha by some and a phalita dasha by others. — PVR, *Integrated Approach*, Ch. 17.1 and 17.2.3 (pp. 195-198)
- **(C)** The Shodashottari condition is disputed between translators. Satyori treats the paksha-plus-hora rule as the most cited reading. — [Satyori, Shodashottari](https://satyori.com/jyotish/dasha/shodashottari/)

### Inferences
- Rath's own text gives this order: Vimshottari by default, a conditional dasha only when its condition holds. He gives no stated tiebreak among several qualifying conditional dashas in what was read. PVR's controlling-planet test is the only SJC-lineage tiebreak found, and it must be labelled B, PVR's own.
- Rath's Ashtottari Criterion 1 omits PVR's "not in lagna" clause. The two may differ in charts with Rahu in lagna.

### Gaps
- Rath's own applicability text for Shodashottari, Dwadashottari, Panchottari, Shatabdika, Chaturaseeti, Shashtihayani and Shat-trimsha (book Ch. 8-15) was not read.
- No Rath statement found on which conditional dasha wins when several qualify.

## 3. Rasi dashas: which question each answers

### Takeaway
Rath's *Narayana Dasa* gives an explicit division of labour, with these roles:
- **Narayana:** environment and the trend of events; deterministic.
- **Vimshottari:** mind, response and the power of yoga.
- **Sudasa:** prosperity (Sri Shakti).
- **Shoola or another ayur dasha:** health and longevity (Bhoo Shakti).
- **Drig:** spirituality.

Varga Narayana dashas cover specific life areas, and three varga areas get a different dasha (Paryaya, Manduka, Drig). He advises Narayana over Lagnamsaka for all charts. Chara is the movable-lagna form of Narayana (earlier notes).

### Cited Findings
- **(A) Narayana vs Vimshottari.** Narayana shows the environment's impact on the native. Vimshottari shows the native's reaction and experience. Vimshottari, as an udu dasha, gives the state of mind and personal preparedness. — *Narayana Dasa*, Preface, Trimurti section (pp. xiii-xiv)
- **(A) Shakti mapping.** Vimshottari judges the state of mind and self. Variation in Sri Shakti (prosperity) is examined from Su-dasa (Sudasa) or rasi dasha. Variation in Bhoo Shakti (health, long life) is examined from Shoola dasa "or any other Ayur dasa". The underlying saying: Brahma gives longevity, Vishnu wealth, Shiva marriage. — *Narayana Dasa*, Preface, Shakti section (p. xiv)
- **(A) Comprehensive examination.** For a full examination of a chart, examine Narayana, Su-dasa, Vimshottari, Shoola and Drig dashas. — *Narayana Dasa*, Preface, end (p. xv). This is the closest thing found to a Rath standard set.
- **(A) Narayana gives definite change.** Other dashas foresee several possible events that are hard to narrow. Narayana gives the most likely and definite change, shows the environment's effect, and is "largely deterministic". Vimshottari works by first fixing the influence on the mind, which then shapes life direction. Rath also writes that "a comparative study is useless" because each has merits; for him Narayana is the indispensable tool to broadly time an event. — *Narayana Dasa*, Order chapter, after the Sri Aurobindo illustration (around pp. 59-61)
- **(A) Vimshottari and Narayana are not in conflict.** When astrologers find Vimshottari results differ from Narayana, that is a limit of understanding. Vimshottari is a "Phalita Udu dasa" showing the power of yoga and the Moon's (conscious mind's) experience. Narayana sets the trend of the period and is more deterministic. — *Narayana Dasa*, Judgment of Results (pp. 89-90)
- **(A) Varga Narayana dashas.** The Narayana dasha of a divisional chart shows changes in the environment for that chart's area of life: D-10 for the professional environment (key in the working years), D-7 for children and dynasty, and so on. A varga Narayana starts from the sign occupied, in that varga, by the lord of the controlling house of the rasi chart. Where one varga governs several matters, a separate Narayana is run per matter. — *Narayana Dasa*, Varga Narayana Dasa chapter (pp. 114-115)
- **(A) Table 29, controlling houses** (D-chart: affair, rasi house). Remarks column read from the raw text extraction:

  | D-chart | Affair | Rasi house |
  |---|---|---|
  | D-2 | wealth and life force | 2nd |
  | D-3 | co-born | 3rd |
  | D-4 | property | 4th |
  | D-5 | subordinates | 5th |
  | D-6 | enemies | 6th |
  | D-7 | children | 7th |
  | D-8 | disease | 8th |
  | D-9 | spouse and dharma | 9th |
  | D-10 | career | 10th |
  | D-11 | destruction | 11th |
  | D-12 | parents | 12th |
  | D-16 | vehicles and comforts | 4th |
  | D-20 | spirituality | 8th |

  Remarks: for D-6, "Gochara dasa or Paryaya dasa is advised". For D-11 (Rudramsa), Manduka dasa is advised. For D-20, Drig dasa is advised.
  — *Narayana Dasa*, Table 29 (p. 119). Caveat: in the layout extraction the remarks column floats beside other rows, so the row each remark belongs to rests on the raw extraction. It is corroborated for D-20 by the next item and for D-11 by PVR's statement that Rudramsa Mandooka dasa is for wars and death.
- **(A) Spirituality.** For spiritual matters, Drig dasa in the rasi chart should be read first, before the Narayana dasa of the Vimshamsa (D-20). — *Narayana Dasa*, Varga illustrations, D-20 section (around pp. 157-159)
- **(A) Lagnamsaka vs Narayana.** Some hold that Lagnamsaka dasa (Narayana started from the rasi sign of the navamsa lagna) should replace Narayana for people without rajayoga. Rath rejects this as ambiguous: use Narayana in all charts. Lagnamsaka can be tried for the influence of dharma and the strength of a person's ideals. — *Narayana Dasa*, Lagnamsaka Dasa (pp. 187-188)
- **(A) Padanadhamsa dasa** (Narayana run in the navamsa from the arudha lagna lord's position) shows changes in image, the impact of maya, and largely fated changes. — *Narayana Dasa*, Padanadhamsa Dasa (pp. 189-192)
- **(A) Phalita vs ayur.** Exaltation signs of the nodes differ between the two classes: Rahu exalted in Gemini for phalita dashas such as Narayana, Parashara's node signs for ayur dashas. The second source of strength applies to Chara, Narayana and Navamsa dasa. Strength rule 1 (Atmakaraka veto) is used only in dashas reckoned from the Atmakaraka, such as Atmakaraka Kendradi rasi dasa. — *Narayana Dasa*, Ch. I-II (around pp. 35-42)
- **(A) Mula dasa** in the udu book examines past karma and when curses and bad karma take effect. Mandooka dasa (which uses Lagna Kendradi rasi dasa) is a separate system taught in another book. — [Exotic India listing description](https://exoticindiaart.com/book/details/vimsottari-and-udu-dasa-s-parasara-s-key-to-prognostication-naj126) (listing paraphrase of the book; A via C)
- **(A, contents only)** The *Upadesa Sutras* dasha list (Narayana/Padakrama, Sthira, Shoola, Brahma, Varnada, Mandooka, Paryaya/Gochara, Kendradi, Sudasa, Navamsa, Chara) is in the earlier notes. No new rule text was found for Sthira, Brahma, Varnada, Navamsa, Trikona or Niryana Shoola from Rath.
- **(A) Shoola dasa** is called an Ayur dasha for the period of death of self, spouse and relations, "amongst the most accurate" for longevity and health. — [srath.com, Shula Dasha excerpt](https://srath.com/category/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/dasa) (category excerpt, article not opened)
- **(B) PVR's purpose map:**
  - Vimshottari: nakshatra dasha, phalita and ayur.
  - Ashtottari: ayur or phalita.
  - Narayana: phalita, general.
  - Lagna Kendradi rasi dasa and Sudasa: material fortune.
  - Drigdasa: spirituality.
  - Niryana Shoola and Shoola: ayur.
  - Kalachakra: phalita nakshatra dasha.

  He calls the ten dashas "ways to look at 10 different aspects" of the same event, not alternatives. — PVR, *Integrated Approach*, Ch. 1.4 and the Part 2 list (around pp. 6, 180)
- **(B) PVR: Sudasa beats Lagna Kendradi.** Sudasa is "a superior dasa" for material success. Always check Narayana together with Sudasa, because Narayana gives power while Sri Lakshmi (Sudasa) gives prosperity. Example: Vajpayee's Sudasa Capricorn plus Narayana Scorpio at the time he became Prime Minister. — PVR, *Integrated Approach*, Ch. 19.4 and Ch. 20 (pp. 229-231)
- **(B) PVR on Mandooka.** A "noted author's" Mandooka dasa is really a Lagna Kendradi variant. True Mandooka uses the 3rd/11th jump from Kalachakra, and Mandooka dasa of the Rudramsa (D-11) is for wars and death. — PVR, Ch. 19.4. This may implicitly dispute another author's book; the author is unnamed.
- **(B) Shoola naming conflict.** Parashara's "Shoola dasa" is what PVR calls Niryana Shoola (it starts from the stronger of the 2nd and 8th). "Some scholars", footnote 60 naming "my guru Pundit Sanjay Rath", use "Shoola dasa" for a different dasha, which starts from the stronger of lagna and 7th. — PVR, Ch. 22.1 and footnote 60 (p. 239). So "Shoola" in Rath's books means the dasha from the stronger of 1st and 7th, not Niryana Shoola.
- **(B) Kalachakra applicability.** Some authors restrict Kalachakra to charts where the Moon is stronger in navamsa than in rasi. PVR holds it applies to everyone, because Parashara set no condition and called it the most respectable dasha. Focus: Vimshottari for the mind, Narayana for life direction and real happenings, Kalachakra for the inner self and sense of connectedness. — PVR, Ch. 24.5 (pp. 269-270). PVR does not attribute the restriction to Rath.

### Inferences
- The questions map to systems as follows in Rath's (A) text:
  - environment and events: Narayana, and varga Narayana for each life area;
  - mind and experience: Vimshottari;
  - wealth and prosperity: Sudasa;
  - longevity and body: Shoola and other ayur dashas;
  - spirituality: Drig (then D-20 Narayana);
  - curses and past karma: Mula;
  - dharma and ideals: Lagnamsaka;
  - image: Padanadhamsa;
  - enemies: Paryaya (D-6);
  - destruction: Manduka (D-11).
- No Rath (A) text was found for Yogini, Kalachakra (beyond a presentation title), Brahma, Navamsa dasa, Trikona or Niryana Shoola uses.

### Gaps
- Yogini: no Rath or SJC statement found. The book *Yogini and Kalchakra Dasa* is by Sumeet Chugh, not Rath ([Bagchee listing](https://www.bagchee.com/books/BB133849/yogini-and-kalchakra-dasa)).
- Kalachakra: no Rath text found. The KCD-SanjayRath Scribd presentation was not opened ([Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/presentation/620260194/KCD-SanjayRath)).
- Rath's Brahma dasa (longevity) and Navamsa dasa uses: names only.
- The page numbers of the D-20 Drig passage and the Order chapter are approximate.

## 4. Stated hierarchies

### Takeaway
Rath states no single ranking. He states purposes. The firm A-tier hierarchy statements are these:
- Vimshottari is the best udu dasha.
- Narayana is for the environment and Vimshottari for the mind; they are complementary, not ranked.
- Narayana goes in all charts, ahead of Lagnamsaka.
- Drig is read before the D-20 Narayana for spirituality.
- Narayana is easier than an udu dasha for a dasa pravesh chart.

"Sudasa when Moon sign stronger" and "Narayana over Vimshottari for marriage" were not found in any source.

### Cited Findings
- **(A)** Vimshottari is the foremost udu dasha; conditional dashas apply only when conditions hold. — [srath.com/?p=804](https://srath.com/?p=804)
- **(A)** Narayana is for the environment, Vimshottari for the reaction and mind. A comparative ranking is "useless"; each has merits. — *Narayana Dasa*, Preface and Order chapter
- **(A)** For the full picture, use Narayana, Sudasa, Vimshottari, Shoola and Drig together. — *Narayana Dasa*, Preface (p. xv)
- **(A)** Use Narayana in all charts regardless of rajayoga, not Lagnamsaka. — *Narayana Dasa*, p. 188
- **(A)** Read Drig in the rasi chart first, then the D-20 Narayana. — *Narayana Dasa*, D-20 section
- **(A)** Marriage: the Narayana book has a "Timing marriage" illustration using the Narayana dasha and antardasha, the darakaraka, the upapada and the dasa pravesh chakra. The text read does not rank it over Vimshottari. — *Narayana Dasa*, Judgment of Results, Illustrations (around p. 90 onward)
- **(B)** PVR: Sudasa ranks above Lagna Kendradi for wealth, and Narayana must be checked with Sudasa. Kalachakra is his own favourite for long-range life phases. Tajaka annual and monthly charts are his tools for one-to-two-week predictions. — PVR, Ch. 19.4, 20, 24.5 and footnote 69
- **(B)** PVR: when lagna and Moon kendras are all occupied, Kendradi/Moola is more suitable than Vimshottari. — PVR, Ch. 16.7

## 5. Agreement between a nakshatra dasha and a rasi dasha before predicting

### Takeaway
No Rath text was found that makes agreement between a nakshatra dasha and a rasi dasha a formal precondition for predicting. What exists:
- his statement that the two never truly conflict;
- his "examine all five" advice;
- the *Crux* children rule (prior notes), which uses an independent Narayana as a double check on the Vimshottari timing;
- worked examples in which dasha and antardasha within Narayana both connect to the event's significators "to confirm the event".

### Cited Findings
- **(A)** Apparent disagreement between Vimshottari and Narayana reflects the astrologer's limited understanding, not the tools. — *Narayana Dasa*, pp. 89-90
- **(A)** Within one rasi dasha, confirmation comes from the dasha and antardasha signs both linking to the event's significators. Tagore's 1883 marriage: the Darakaraka in the 7th from the dasha sign, the antardasha sign aspecting the upapada and Venus, so dasha and antardasha "connect... to confirm the event". — *Narayana Dasa*, Tagore illustration (around pp. 111-112)
- **(A, prior notes)** Children: Vimshottari conditions on the D-7 lagna lord, plus an independent Narayana from the 7th lord's sign in D-7 as a double check. — *Crux of Vedic Astrology*, sect. 8.6.2, recorded in `reports/Sanjay Rath method summary.md`
- **(B)** PVR: Vimshottari and Narayana "show the same events" with a different focus (mind vs happenings). Kalachakra adds the inner-self angle. Mixing dashas without knowing their differences gives vague readings. — PVR, Ch. 24.5
- **(B)** PVR: Narayana is checked alongside Sudasa for power and position (Vajpayee example). — PVR, Ch. 20
- **(C)** SJC-adjacent material recommends at least three timing methods together (for example Vimshottari, a rasi dasha and transits); it is not tied to a Rath text. — [Scribd, Dasa System](https://www.scribd.com/document/535059356/Dasa-System) (search snippet only; not opened)

### Inferences
- A defensible Rath-style confirmation rule for an engine: an event is "promised" when the question's rasi dasha (Narayana, or the varga Narayana for the area) and Vimshottari both point to its significators. This is an inference from his complementarity statements and the *Crux* double check. It is not a stated rule.

- *Vimsottari and Udu Dasas* sect. 4.7 (Dasa Pravesh Chakra), 4.8 (Gochara) and 4.12 (Dasa-antardasa) may hold such a rule; not read.
