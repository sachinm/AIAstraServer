## Q1. Yogas: which he emphasises, and how he tests that one will actually give results

### Takeaway
In Rath's school a yoga is never read alone. It is one factor out of ten (the 8th), read after the house, the lord, the dispositor, the arudhas and the karakas, and before argala (A, *Crux*, cross-referenced). Whether it gives results is then tested in four ways:
- **Strength.** The planets must be free of functional-malefic affliction, close in degree, not combust, debilitated or inimical, and they are scored by dignity across the ten vargas (B, Rao).
- **Varga confirmation.** A raja yoga must show in the D5 and D10 (A).
- **House and dasa timing.** Where the yoga sits decides when it acts, and a raja yoga given in Rahu dasa does not last (A).
- **Arudha check.** The AL and the A7 must not sit 2/12 or 6/8 from each other (B).

Jaimini yogas (Atmakaraka, Putrakaraka, Amatyakaraka, Hora Lagna and Ghatika Lagna) sit alongside the Parashari kendra-trikona yogas, not in place of them.

### Cited Findings

**Order and testing (cross-references, A)**

These come from the earlier report, which cites *Crux*.
- "Good and bad yogas" is factor 8 of 10 for any house. Argala is factor 9.
- In the worked Chart 11, a yoga is noted first and the planet that cancels it is named next.
- Yogas in the 2nd and 4th give results early. A raja yoga in the lagna acts late.
- Raja yoga must be confirmed in the D5 and D10.
- A debilitated planet in an exalted navamsa gives a sudden rise. If it is retrograde, the rise is short.
- A raja yoga given in Rahu dasa does not last.

These are cross-referenced, not re-researched.

**Atmakaraka as the gate for raja yoga (A)**
- Rath reads raja yoga potential through the Atmakaraka (AK) and the Karakamsa. The AK's link to the navamsa lagna sets the level of birth and rise:
  - If the navamsa lagna lord joins the AK, a person of humble birth can rise to rank.
  - If the AK aspects the navamsa lagna, royal links are present from birth.
  - In both cases the Sun's strength sets the scale.

  — (A) [srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- The house of the AK counted to the navamsa lagna names the dominant result, and the karaka of that house must be strong in both the Rasi and the navamsa:
  - 4th: Sun strength gives rajayoga.
  - 5th: a strong Sun gives many good yogas.
  - 11th: a strong Mars gives rajayoga.
  - 12th: great wealth.
  - 6th and 8th: trouble.

  He says Rasi results suit very great figures, and navamsa results apply to ordinary people. — (A) [same](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- The AK's own Vimshottari dasa usually brings suffering, especially when the AK is malefic. The Narayana dasa of the AK's sign, or of signs it aspects, can bring major achievement. — (A) [same](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)

**Gemstones and raja yoga (A)**
- Rath reports that the Agni Purana lists 36 gems for activating raja yoga in kings. He ties the number to the 36 drekkanas and suggests ordinary people can also benefit. The article gives no rule for which gem suits which chart. — (A) [srath.com, Thirty-Six Rajayoga Gemstones](https://srath.com/misc/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a-gems/thirty-six-rajayoga-gemstones/)

**Parashari raja yoga and its magnitude (B, Rao Ch. 11.7, pp.133-139)**
- **Basic raja yoga.** A kendra lord is associated with a trikona lord, by conjunction, mutual graha drishti or parivartana (sign exchange). The lagna counts as both a kendra and a trikona. The 9th and 10th lords together form the Dharma-Karmadhipati yoga, ranked best. — (B) [Rao, Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach §11.7.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Magnitude tests, all of which must be weighed:**
  - The planets are free of functional-malefic affliction.
  - The conjunction or aspect is within about 6 degrees. Beyond 5 to 6 degrees the yoga cannot give full results.
  - The planets are not combust, debilitated, in an inimical sign or in a bad avastha.
  - The count of good-dignity vargas in the ten-varga scheme sets the grade: Parijata at 2, Uttama at 3, Gopura at 4, Simhasana at 5, and so on up the scale.

  The final judgment weighs all the factors together, and no single factor decides. — (B) [Rao §11.7.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Viparita raja yoga.** The 6th, 8th and 12th lords are in, or join, dusthanas. Success comes after an initial struggle. The ideal case is the three lords together with no other planet. — (B) [Rao §11.7.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Jaimini and special-lagna raja yogas listed by Rao:**
  - The AK and Putrakaraka (PK) conjoined, plus the lagna and 5th lords conjoined. If only one condition holds, the results come partly.
  - The 9th lord and the AK in the 1st, 5th or 7th with benefic aspect.
  - Benefics in the 2nd, 4th and 5th from both the lagna lord and the AK.
  - Malefics in the 3rd and 6th from both.
  - One planet linked to the lagna, Hora Lagna (HL) and Ghatika Lagna (GL). An HL link gives wealth and a GL link gives power.
  - The same planet aspecting the lagna in all six shad-vargas.
  - The AL and A7 not in mutual 2/12 or 6/8. This makes the chart's raja yogas more effective.

  — (B) [Rao §11.7.3](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Raja sambandha yogas (association with power).**
  - The AK and Amatyakaraka (AmK) conjoined: a minister.
  - The AmK in a trine from the lagna: a famous minister.
  - The AmK in a kendra or trine from the AK: liked by the ruler.
  - The 10th lord with the AmK or its dispositor: important at court.

  Rao notes these yogas are very common, so magnitude depends on the planets' strength. — (B) [Rao §11.8](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Yogas inside divisional charts.** Many yogas apply inside a varga and are then read for that varga's matter. Rao also warns that yoga lists produced by software are sometimes wrong. — (B) [Rao §11.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Yogada status.** A planet owning, joining or aspecting both the HL and the lagna is a yogada for money. The same link with the GL makes a yogada for power. Yogadas bring good luck whatever their functional nature. — (B) [Rao §13.2, p.167-168](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf). This matches the earlier report's *Crux* Ch. II rule (A).

**Pancha Mahapurusha (B)**
- Rao ties the five Mahapurusha yogas to the five elements: Mars fire, Mercury earth, Saturn air, Venus water, Jupiter ether. — (B) [Rao §11.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- In Akbar's chart, Rao counts Malavya, Sasa and Ruchaka as adding to the raja yogas despite weaker varga grades. — (B) [Rao §11.7.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

**Gajakesari as a worked yoga test (B)**
- Sarbani Rath requires Jupiter to be joined or aspected by a benefic, and not debilitated, combust or in an enemy sign. Citing Jataka Parijata, she reads "benefic" as functional benefic.
- Jupiter in a kendra from both the lagna and the Moon makes the yoga strong.
- Jupiter in the 6th from the lagna neutralised the yoga in one chart, where litigation took the wealth.
- Her remedy to support the yoga is Balagopala worship.

— (B) [Sarbani Rath, Sakata, Mukuta & Gajakesari Yoga](https://sarbanirath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/saka%e1%b9%ada-muku%e1%b9%ada-gajakesari-yoga/)

### Inferences
- **Order of yoga judgment.** The combined order across tiers A and B runs:
  1. Identify the yoga.
  2. Check its cancellers and afflictions.
  3. Grade its strength (degree closeness, dignity, combustion, varga count).
  4. Confirm it in the relevant varga (D5 and D10 for power, D9 through the AK).
  5. Check the AL and A7 relation.
  6. Time it by house position and dasa (Narayana for the AK sign; Rahu dasa yogas do not last).

  Steps 1, 2, 4 and 6 are Rath's own (A). Steps 3 and 5 are Rao's (B). The sequence is assembled; neither author prints it.
- He tests Jaimini yogas through the AK and Karakamsa rather than through Upadesa Sutra yoga lists as such. The Upadesa Sutra text itself was not reachable.

### Gaps
- **Neecha bhanga rules.** No Rath text beyond the earlier report's "debilitated in an exalted navamsa" rule. Rao's book has no neecha-bhanga section.
- **Dhana yoga.** Rao's §11.9 Dhana Yogas list did not extract (the section was empty in the text). No Rath-bylined dhana-yoga list was found beyond the earlier report's HL and 2nd/11th rules.
- **Parivartana.** It is classed only as an association for raja yoga (B). Rath's "dainya parivartana", a dusthana lord exchanging with the 8th lord, appears as an affliction (Q2). No full Rath classification of the three parivartana types was found.
- **Jaimini Upadesa Sutras.** Rath's translation and commentary (Sagittarius) was not reachable online. The Jaimini raja yogas above are Rao's list, not checked against Rath's commentary.
- **srath.com Jaimini Scholar Program pages.** Not checked lesson by lesson.

## Q2. Afflictions: what he checks and in what order

### Takeaway
No single printed "affliction order" by Rath was found.

**What his own texts do show (A):**
- Argala, with its obstruction (virodhargala), is the decisive filter on any house.
- Papakartari on a house or its lord is the key squeeze.
- Badhaka is applied from the lagna, the AL and the Paka Lagna.
- Rahu on a weak 8th lord, and "dainya parivartana", mark the heavier afflictions.

**What Rao adds (B):** an ordered analysis for any matter.
1. Pick the varga.
2. Pick the house.
3. Pick the reference point.
4. Choose between the house and its arudha.
5. Read the influences: drishti, then argala, then house positions from the house, then badhaka.
6. Apply standard results, strength and yogas.

### Cited Findings

**Order of analysis (B, Rao §13.4.1, pp.169-170)**
1. **Varga.** Choose the varga for the matter: D16 vehicles, D30 a criminal's psychology, D9 marriage, D20 religion, D24 learning, D10 career.
2. **House.** Choose the house in that varga.
3. **Reference point.** Lagna for the true self, AL for the perceived self, or a karaka when the karaka is stronger.
4. **House or arudha.** Use an arudha for how a matter is perceived, such as A5 for awards.
5. **Influences.** Rasi and graha drishti, then argala. Then positions from the house:
   - kendras sustain it;
   - trines make it prosper;
   - upachayas let it grow;
   - dusthanas obstruct it;
   - a badhaka from it makes trouble.
6. **Standard results.** Then strength, avasthas, ashtakavarga and yogas.

— (B) [Rao §13.4.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

**Argala and virodhargala (B, Rao §10.5-10.7, pp.104-107)**

Rao calls argala decisive, beyond drishti.
- **Primary argala** comes from the 2nd, 4th and 11th. Benefics there give subhargala and malefics papargala.
- **Secondary argala** comes from the 5th.
- **Obstruction.** These are obstructed by planets in the 12th, 10th, 3rd and 9th, respectively.
- **Ketu exception.** If Ketu is in the sign, both are counted backward.
- **Three or more malefics in the 3rd.** Several malefics in the 3rd give argala instead of obstruction.
- **Deciding.** Use the house or its karaka. Count the planets causing argala and obstruction. If the counts are equal, compare strength.

— (B) [Rao §10.5-10.7](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

Rath's own application of argala to the karyesha, and of the Sun's and AK's unobstructed argala on the lagna, is in the earlier report (A, *Crux* Ch. IV, and srath.com divisional charts).

**Badhaka (B, A)**
- For a house in a movable, fixed or dual sign, the badhaka sthana is the 11th, 9th or 7th from it, and its lord is the badhaka.
- Rao applies this from every house and every arudha, in every varga. Example: for a Gemini D10 lagna, the dasas of Jupiter and of planets in Sagittarius trouble the career.

— (B) [Rao §13.3, pp.168-169](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

Rath's Paka Lagna and AL badhaka screen for the 10th is in the earlier report (A, COVA XIII preview).

**Functional nature as an affliction filter (B)**
- Trine lords are functional benefics. Lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th are functional malefics.
- A natural-benefic kendra lord turns functional malefic. A natural-malefic kendra lord is neutral.
- Lords of the 2nd, 8th and 12th are neutral, with the 8th the worst.
- A functional malefic in the 3rd or a dusthana does good by spoiling those houses. In a kendra or trine it harms unless it is very strong.

— (B) [Rao §13.2, pp.166-168](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

**Rahu, the 8th house and papakartari (A)**
- Rahu in the 8th does not always give the feared result. Exalted Rahu, or Rahu with an exalted planet there, can be a blessing.
- Rath takes Gemini as Rahu's exaltation and keeps Taurus for longevity matters.
- He lists these afflictions in his worked cases:
  - Rahu aspecting the 8th or its lord;
  - a weak, debilitated or marana-karaka-sthana 8th lord;
  - "dainya parivartana", an exchange between a dusthana lord and the 8th lord;
  - papakartari, with Rahu and malefics hemming the 8th lord or the Moon;
  - a dasa that activates the weakness.

  — (A) [srath.com, Rahu and Eighth House](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/amateur/rahu-and-eighth-house/)
- **[Sensitive]** The same article ties Rahu in Taurus, and the Moon with Rahu, to poor longevity, and reads a weak, Rahu-afflicted 8th lord as a life of sorrow. These are recorded as source rules only. — (A) [same](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/amateur/rahu-and-eighth-house/)

**AK afflictions (A)**
- The AK in the 6th or 8th from the navamsa lagna: illness and trouble.
- A retrograde Mars or Saturn as AK: compulsive drives.
- Saturn as AK: sorrow if one causes sorrow to elders.
- Rahu as AK: being cheated.

— (A) [srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)

**Maraka [Sensitive] (B)**
- The 2nd and 7th are the houses of death, being 12th from the 3rd and 8th. Their lords are marakas.
- A malefic with a strong graha drishti on them, or on their lords, also becomes a maraka.
- The Rasi chart decides, with the D11 and D30 as support.
- Death is timed by maraka signs in rasi dasas and maraka planets in graha dasas.

— (B) [Rao §14.2, pp.180-181](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf). Source rule only.

**Daridra (poverty) yogas (B)**
- The lagna lord and the 12th lord exchanged, or the lagna lord and the 6th lord exchanged, with a maraka linked.
- The lagna lord with a malefic in a dusthana, and the 2nd lord debilitated.

— (B) [Rao §11.10](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

**Curses [Sensitive]**
- Already covered in the earlier report: the IndiaDivine repost table and Sarbani Rath's timing rules (B, C).
- The same report records Rath's own order rule for children: capacity is checked before any curse is declared (A, *Crux* VIII 8.5.5).
- Rath's 2004 Serbia curse paper is no longer available on Scribd (HTTP 410). It was not read.

### Inferences
- The affliction order implied across tiers A and B runs:
  1. Functional nature of the lords.
  2. Planets in and aspecting the house.
  3. Argala, with obstruction counted.
  4. Papakartari.
  5. Badhaka from the house, AL or Paka Lagna.
  6. Dusthana and marana karaka positions of the lord and karaka.
  7. Node involvement.
  8. Dasa activation.

  This is assembled, not printed.
- Combustion and graha yuddha appear in Rao only as strength reducers for a yoga (§11.7.2). No Rath text found treats them as separate steps.

### Gaps
- **No source found.** No Rath or Rao rule was found for graha yuddha (planetary war), Gulika or Mandi, or combustion as a separate step. Rath's AK article states that a combust AK gives spiritual insight; it is the only combustion rule found.
- **Not repeated.** Rao's Rudra, Trishoola and Maheswara section (§14.3) is a longevity tool **[Sensitive]** and is not set out here.
- **Not read.** Rath's own badhaka text (the earlier report records only the Paka Lagna and AL screen) and his curse paper.

## Q3. Protections: what he counts as protecting a house or planet

### Takeaway
In the found texts, protection is mostly a mechanical counter-force:
- **Obstruction of papargala.** A planet in the 10th, 12th, 3rd or 9th blocks the malefic argala (B). Rath requires argala to be unobstructed before it helps (A, cross-reference).
- **Benefic argala on the house or karaka** (A, B).
- **A functional malefic placed in a dusthana** spoils the bad house (B).
- **The AK's own placement**, for example the 5th from the navamsa lagna gives the father's blessing (A).

A distinct "shubha kartari" or "Jupiter's aspect protects" rule under Rath's byline was not found.

### Cited Findings
- **Obstruction as protection.** Rao's example: Jupiter in the 10th from Mercury blocks Saturn's papargala from the 4th, and so averts the trouble. — (B) [Rao §10.6](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Unobstructed argala gives the good result.** The Sun's or AK's unobstructed argala on the lagna gives status and health (A, *Crux* Ch. IV, cross-reference).
- **Lagna argala protects wealth.** Benefic argala on the lagna gives profit, and papargala gives loss (A, *Crux* Ch. V, cross-reference).
- **Protections from the AK's position (A).**
  - The AK in the 5th from the navamsa lagna: the father's blessings protect.
  - In the 9th: piety and fortune.
  - In the 10th: a firm home base.
  - For a Mars AK, non-violence is named as the main protection.

  — (A) [srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **Jupiter as protector of the mother.** Jupiter in the 4th protects the mother's matters (A, *Crux* Ch. VII, cross-reference). Jupiter in a kendra from both the lagna and the Moon strengthens Gajakesari (B, Sarbani Rath, above).
- **Exalted Rahu protects.** Exalted Rahu in the 8th turns the house to blessing (A, Rahu and Eighth House, above).
- **Yogadas.** Planets that are yogadas (HL or GL with the lagna) bring luck regardless of their functional nature. — (B) [Rao §13.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Two senses of protection.** Rath's school treats protection mainly as (a) blocking malefic argala and (b) placements of the AK, the karaka and the lagna lord that carry a deity's blessing. The earlier report notes, for example, that the lagna lord in the 9th is blessed by Jupiter as Maha-Vishnu (A).
- **Deities as protection.** The deity layer (Q4) is the active form of protection: the Ishta for the soul, and the deity of a varga's lagna lord for a life area.

### Gaps
- No Rath or SJC text was found that defines shubha kartari as a protection rule, or Jupiter's aspect as a general shield.
- No Rath text was found that ranks protections, for example Ishta grace against benefic argala.

## Q4. Remedies: how he chooses the planet, the deity and the method, and his cautions

### Takeaway
**Rath's own view (A).** In Rath's own framing, a remedy follows diagnosis:
- The afflicted house or planet is identified first.
- The remedy is tied to that planet, to the AK's nature, or to the house in a named case.
- Muhurta (choosing a good moment) is itself a remedy.
- The astrologer's own faith and purity matter.
- Black-magic methods are condemned.
- Mantras must not be prescribed loosely.

**Rao's selection rules (B).**
- Gems only for functional benefics, or for a varga yogakaraka.
- Avoid functional-malefic gems, and maraka gems in maraka dasas.
- Worship the deity of the lagna lord in the varga for the area of life concerned.
- Worship the deity of the strongest planet occupying, owning or aspecting the 12th from the AK in the D9 for liberation (the Ishta).
- Choose the exact deity form from the planet's associations.

Palana and Dharma devata rules were not confirmed from any opened source.

### Cited Findings

**Rath's own text (A)**
- ***Vedic Remedies in Astrology*: what the retailer page shows.**
  - **Chapters:** Introduction; Astrology & Deity; Astrological Principles; Mantra Shastra; Compendium of Mantra; Gemology (p.271); Case Studies; appendices on Spiritual Discipline, Yantra, Kundalini, Nakshatra & Syllables, FAQ, and Rudraksha & Rosary.
  - **Ordering principle:** remedies are grouped by guna.
  - **Purpose:** a remedy removes an evil combination that hinders the good yogas.
  - **Sources of remedies:** jataka (natal), prasna (question), nimitta (omen) and muhurta literature, with muhurta itself a remedy.
  - **Deity of Jyotish:** Vishnu, with the Sun and Jupiter as karakas of dharma.
  - **Faith:** the astrologer needs unflinching faith and a pure, simple life.
  - **Black magic:** black tantric methods destroy good karma.
  - **Other remedies:** yantra corrections for Vaastu flaws, and general remedies for dasa-bhukti periods and Gandanta births.

  — (A, author's preface as shown by the retailer; not checked against print) [Exotic India listing](https://www.exoticindia.com/book/details/vedic-remedies-in-astrology-idj596)
- **Conduct remedies keyed to the AK.**
  - Sun AK: humility.
  - Moon AK: compassion.
  - Mars AK: non-violence.
  - Mercury AK: truthful, controlled speech.
  - Jupiter AK: respect for the guru and for children.
  - Venus AK: clean character.
  - Saturn AK: do not cause sorrow to elders.
  - Rahu AK: avoid guile.

  For the AK in the 6th or 8th: propitiate Saturn or Satya Narayana, fast on the full Moon, and keep truthfulness ("OM TAT SAT"). — (A) [srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **Remedy for 8th-house suffering.** Sri Rudra is named. — (A) [srath.com, Rahu and Eighth House](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/amateur/rahu-and-eighth-house/)
- **Charity by nakshatra.** Rath reproduces the Mahabharata (Anushasana Parva 64) list of gifts by birth star or day. — (A, title and snippet only; page not opened) [srath.com, Nakshatra Yoga (Donations)](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/nak%e1%b9%a3atra/nak%e1%b9%a3atra-yoga-donations/)
- **Earlier report cross-references (A, *Crux*):**
  - Remedies in *Crux* are case-bound: fasting on the weekdays of planets in the 12th, a Ganesha mantra, Surya and Shanti mantras, and a ruby for Saturn in the 9th.
  - He warns that mantras are prescribed too freely, and that a mismatched propitiation can endanger children.
  - Medical referral comes before any remedy on fertility capacity.

**Rao's selection rules (B, Ch. 34, pp.450-459)**
- **Four ways to pacify a planet:** gemstone, good deeds, mantra and stotra, and deity worship. Common-sense precaution in a bad period comes first. — (B) [Rao §34.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Gemstones.**
  - **Who qualifies:** only planets favourable in the chart, that is, functional benefics in the Rasi.
  - **Which to prefer:** the planet whose dasa is running, or one in a favourable yoga.
  - **By life area:** for a given area, strengthen a yogakaraka in that varga (D10 for career).
  - **Weak benefics:** a weak or badly placed functional benefic may still be strengthened. Example: Venus as yogakaraka for Capricorn lagna, placed in the 8th.
  - **What to avoid:** functional-malefic gems as far as possible, and maraka gems during maraka dasas.
  - **Combining stones:** two gems only if both planets are friends and functional benefics, ideally forming a yoga.
  - **When to start:** on the planet's weekday, with the planet strong at that moment.

  — (B) [Rao §34.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Good deeds, charity and fasting.**
  - Each planet is pleased by deeds in its own field and by gifts of its grain, for example chickpeas on a Thursday for Jupiter.
  - Fast on the weekday of the Upapada lord for marriage, and of the A10 (rajyapada) lord for career.

  — (B) [Rao §34.3](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Mantra cautions.**
  - Pronunciation and intonation must be exact.
  - Some sattvic mantras correct themselves over time, but some mantras harm when recited wrongly.
  - A stotra needs devotion, not intonation, and works slowly.
  - Rao prints mantras only in Devanagari to prevent mispronunciation.

  — (B) [Rao §34.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Choosing the deity.**
  - **Life areas:** worship the lagna lord of the varga for that area (D20 for spiritual progress, D16 for comforts).
  - **Ishta (liberation):** the strongest planet occupying, owning or aspecting the 12th from the AK in the D9.
  - **Planet-deity table:**
    - Sun: Shiva, Rama.
    - Moon: Gauri, Lalita, Saraswati, Krishna.
    - Mars: Hanuman, Rudra, Kartikeya, Narasimha.
    - Mercury: Vishnu, Narayana, Buddha.
    - Jupiter: Hayagriva, Vishnu, Dattatreya, any guru.
    - Venus: Lakshmi, Parvati.
    - Saturn: Vishnu, Brahma.
    - Rahu: Durga, Narasimha.
    - Ketu: Ganesha.

  — (B) [Rao §34.4-34.5, Table 78](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Choosing the exact form of the deity.** The form comes from the planet's associations:
  - Mercury or Saturn points to a Vaishnava deity, the Sun to a Shaiva deity, the Moon or Venus to a goddess, Mars to a fiery form, Rahu to an aggressive form.
  - Rahu with Mars, aspected by Saturn and Mercury, gives Ugra Narasimha.
  - Mars with Rahu gives Kartikeya.
  - Mars under Saturn's influence gives Hanuman.

  Rao calls the book tables only guidelines. — (B) [Rao §34.5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Remedy-selection order.** Combining tiers A and B, the order runs:
  1. Diagnose the afflicted house or karaka and the dasa (A).
  2. Apply common-sense precaution (B).
  3. Choose the planet to support: a functional benefic or varga yogakaraka. Do not strengthen functional malefics or marakas in maraka dasas (B).
  4. Choose the method: gem for support; mantra, stotra, deity or charity to pacify; conduct keyed to the AK (A).
  5. Choose the deity: varga lagna lord for the life area, Ishta from the 12th of the Karakamsa for the soul (B).
  6. Refine the deity's form from the planet's associations (B).

  This is assembled from both tiers, not printed by Rath.
- **Gemstone rule.** The brief's "gemstones only for functional benefics" is stated by Rao (B). It is not found in Rath's public text, though Rao defers to Rath's *Vedic Remedies* for detail.
- **Mantra mismatch danger.** Shown in Rath's own *Crux* children caution (A, cross-reference) and in Rao's pronunciation warning (B).
