## Q1. What does he do before judging anything, and in what order?

### Takeaway
No Rath text was found that prints a complete start-to-finish script. Tier A gives three pieces:
- a preparation list (in the earlier report);
- a ten-factor house checklist (in the earlier report);
- in one published case, birth-time rectification before any interpretation.

His student Rao prints the most explicit ordered checklist found (tier B). For any matter it runs: right divisional chart, then right house, then right reference point, then house or arudha, then influences (rasi drishti, graha drishti, argala, houses counted from the matter), then standard results. Panchanga is not a pre-reading step in either tier.

### Cited Findings
- **(A) Preparation and the ten factors.** Already recorded from *Crux* Ch. II sect. 7, approx. pp.30-31; see the earlier report "Sanjay Rath life area readings", section 1, steps 1-2. Not repeated here.
- **(A) Rectification comes first in a published case.** The srath.com reading of a former US President justifies the birth time through the Atmakaraka and the navamsa lagna before any interpretation, then moves through Upapada, D10, the other vargas, rajayogas, AL, and dasa and transit. Seen through a fetch summary only. [srath.com, Bill Clinton](https://srath.com/celebrity/usa/bill-clinton/), as recorded in the earlier notes `d1_and_style.md` Q1.
- **(B) How Rao rectifies.** Rao dismisses most quantum-based rectification formulas, which fail when tested on twins. He sets the Tattva method aside as beyond his book. His only accepted method is to find a time near the reported one that explains the native's nature and known past events. He calls this laborious and says there is no other way. Separately, he warns that fine sub-periods should not be used confidently on an unrectified time. [Rao IA, ch. 32 "Birthtime Rectification" approx. p.421-422; Vimsottari section approx. line "sookshma dasas … unless we rectify"](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Rao's printed six-step analysis order** (sect. 13.4.1 "Basic Guidelines", pp.169-171), paraphrased:
  1. **Divisional chart.** Pick the correct divisional chart for the matter: D16 for vehicles, D30 for criminal psychology, D9 for marriage, D20 for religion, D24 for learning, D10 for career. He adds that where marriage is not a dharmic union, the rasi chart may serve better than the D9.
  2. **House.** Pick the correct house within that chart.
  3. **Reference point.** Pick the correct reference to count from. This is lagna, AL or a karaka; see Q2.
  4. **House or arudha.** Decide whether the house or its arudha pada fits the matter better. Awards, for example, are maya of intelligence, so they are read from A5.
  5. **Influences.** Judge the influences on the house or arudha:
     - rasi drishti, graha drishti and argala;
     - houses counted from it: kendras sustain it, trines let it prosper, upachayas let it grow, dusthanas obstruct it;
     - baadhakas from it.
  6. **Standard results.** Apply the classical standard results.

  After the six steps, check planetary strength, avasthas, ashtakavarga and yogas. [Rao IA, sect. 13.4.1, pp.169-171](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) The rasi chart is one varga among others.** Rao treats the rasi chart as the divisional chart with one part per sign. Everything in his book applies to every divisional chart unless he names a chart. [Rao IA, ch. 6 "Divisional Charts", approx. p.50](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) No rule-of-thumb predictions.** Rao warns against predicting from rules of thumb and lookup tables. He says the houses a planet influences across the vargas outweigh its avasthas. [Rao IA, sect. 15.4 "Avasthas", end, p.201](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Panchanga.** Rao teaches tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga and karana in his first chapter as calendar background and for muhurta. They are not a step in chart judgement. [Rao IA, sect. 1.3.8 onward](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- **The order runs from the question outward.** In Rath's system the order is driven by the question rather than the chart. Choose the varga and the house for the matter first, then the reference point, then the influences. Rath's ten-factor list (tier A) and Rao's six steps (tier B) are compatible: the ten factors are the content of Rao's steps 2 to 6. This is an inference; neither text cross-references the other.
- **Rectification is a gate before fine timing.** It does not have to precede everything; Rao's caveat attaches to sub-periods, not to broad reading.

### Gaps
- **No tier A whole-chart script.** No Rath-bylined "do X first" checklist covering the whole chart was found. The Parasara Jyotisa Course and Jaimini Scholar Program lesson pages were again not reached.
- **Panchanga.** Panchanga as a pre-reading step has no source in any tier.
- **Rath's own rectification method.** His own method (Tattva, or the navamsa or D60 checks) was not found in text. The earlier report notes only that the D12 is "one of the key rectification vargas" (*Crux* intro).

## Q2. Which lagna governs which question, and is there a ranking when they disagree?

### Takeaway
Tier B (Rao) gives a clear map of reference point to question:
- **Lagna:** the true self.
- **Moon:** the mind.
- **Sun:** the soul and vitality.
- **AL:** perception, maya and status.
- **Paka lagna (the sign of the lagna lord):** the physical self.
- **Karakamsa:** the soul's inner self, read in the D9.
- **GL:** power and fame.
- **HL:** wealth.
- **Sree Lagna:** prosperity.
- **Karakas:** usable as references when they are stronger than the lagna.

Tier A agrees in pieces: AL for status, Varnada for community standing, HL with lagna as the wealth-giver, GL with lagna as the power-giver. No source in any tier gives a single ranking of lagnas for when they disagree. The stated rule is to pick the reference by the nature of the question. Where two references both apply, the stronger reference counts for more.

### Cited Findings
- **(B) Lagna, the default reference.** The lagna is the default reference and shows the true self. Intentions, knowledge and persistence belong to it. Status does not, because status belongs to the illusion of the world. [Rao IA, sect. 7.3.1, p.70](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Chandra lagna (Moon), the mind.** Houses from the Moon show things from the mind's point of view: happiness, ambition, and how one views one's career. His example is a routine job in the 10th from lagna, with an enterprising mind in the 10th from the Moon. [Rao IA, sect. 7.3.2, pp.70-71](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Ravi lagna (Sun), soul and vitality.** The Sun is the soul's perspective and an important reference for physical vitality. [Rao IA, sect. 7.3.3, p.71](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) AL, perception and status.** The AL shows how the native is perceived and the native's status. The same planet gives different results by reference:
  - in the 10th from lagna, developments in the profession;
  - in the 10th from the Moon, mental activity in the profession;
  - in the 10th from the AL, professional status.

  [Rao IA, sect. 7.3.4, p.71; ch. 9.3, pp.89-90](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Perception versus reality.** Rao says perception usually matters more than reality in material life, while internal matters need the true self. His list of perception questions is elections, competitive exams and promotion. [Rao IA, sect. 9.1, p.85](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Paka lagna, the physical self.** The paka lagna is the sign of the lagna lord. His D24 worked split:
  - success in competition is maya, so it is read from the 5th from the AL;
  - scholarship belongs to the conceptual self, so it is read from the 5th from lagna;
  - memory belongs to the physical self, so it is read from the 5th from the paka lagna.

  Saturn transiting the same three points gives obstruction (lagna), depression (Moon) and sickness (paka lagna). [Rao IA, sect. 7.3.5, pp.71-72](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Karakamsa, the inner self.** Karakamsa is the AK's sign in the navamsa and the reference for the inner self. The 12th from it shows liberation. [Rao IA, sect. 7.3.6, p.72](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf). Tier A agrees on the Karakamsa definition; see the earlier "method summary", section 1.
- **(B) GL and HL.** The GL is the self from the angle of power, authority and fame, and is key for promotions and politics. The HL is the self from the angle of wealth. [Rao IA, sect. 7.3.7-7.3.8, p.73](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf). The Sree Lagna is for prosperity and is used with Sudasa. [Rao IA, sect. 5.7](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(A) GL and HL as yoga-givers.** A planet linked to both the lagna and the HL is a dhana yogada. A planet linked to both the lagna and the GL is a rajya yogada. (*Crux* Ch. II sect. 2.2; already in the earlier report, section 1, step 7.) Rao repeats this and adds that yogadas bring good luck whatever their functional nature. [Rao IA, sect. 13.2, pp.167-168](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(A) Varnada.** The Sun and the AK are read from the AL for status, and from the Varnada for community standing and health. (*Crux* Ch. IV sect. 4.1; earlier report, section 1, step 4.)
- **(B) Karakas as references, by strength.** Each house is read both from the lagna and from its karaka, and the stronger of the two weighs more. If Mars is stronger than the lagna, the 3rd from Mars may matter more than the 3rd from lagna. His other pairs:
  - 4th from the Moon (mother);
  - 9th from the Sun (father);
  - 12th from Saturn (losses);
  - 5th from Jupiter (progeny);
  - 7th from Venus (marriage).

  [Rao IA, sect. 7.3.9, pp.73-74](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf). In the D24 he adds that when the karakas are stronger, they can replace the lagna as the reference. [Rao IA, sect. 13.4.1(3), pp.169-170](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(A) "Functional arudha" in one case, inferred.** In an srath.com reading of a former Indian President, the AL falls in Aries. The author treats the opposite sign, Libra, as the functional arudha because Venus occupies it. This is a one-case application; the fetch tool inferred the general rule from it, and the page states no rule. The byline was not confirmed in the fetch. [srath.com, Dr. Rajendra Prasad](https://srath.com/celebrity/india/dr-rajendra-prasad/)
- **(A) Parivritti varga lagnas.** A Rath-bylined article treats a varga lagna as the house of that varga's number: the navamsa lagna counts as the 9th house, so the 5th from it acts as the 1st. The navamsa is then rotated to make a bhagya lagna (the 5th), a jaya lagna (the 7th) and so on. This is a further reference-point rule specific to the D9. [srath.com, Parivṛtti Aṁśa Lagna](https://srath.com/?p=7691)

### Inferences
- **No fixed hierarchy of lagnas.** Rao's three pillars, read together, are true self, mind and perception. That suggests the school assigns references by question rather than ranking them. A disagreement between lagna and AL is therefore not resolved by choosing one; both are read, as reality against appearance. Only between a house-from-lagna and a house-from-karaka is the stronger one given more weight.
- **The functional arudha points to occupancy as the tiebreak.** The Rajendra Prasad case suggests an occupied, stronger 7th from the AL can take over the AL's role. That matches the occupancy-first strength rule in Q4, but it is only an inference.

### Gaps
- **No Rath statement ranks the lagnas.** None ranks lagna, Moon, Sun, AL, Karakamsa, HL, GL or Varnada for when they conflict.
- **Snippet-level claims, not used as findings.** The "satya peetha versus maya peetha" phrasing for lagna and AL came from search snippets of uncredited Scribd uploads and was not verified. The Atri class transcript was unavailable.
- **Upapada as the marriage reference.** This is covered in the earlier reports; no new precedence rule between the UL, A7 and 7th was found beyond "the A7 must also be seen".

## Q3. Rasi drishti against graha drishti, argala and virodhargala, and the three kinds of karaka: which takes precedence?

### Takeaway
- **Aspects (A).** Rath assigns the two aspect systems different meanings, not a ranking. Graha drishti is desire and comparatively temporary; rasi drishti is knowledge and permanent.
- **Strength of influence (B).** Rao ranks the influences: rasi drishti a small influence, graha drishti a more concrete one, and argala the one that decides parts of the matter.
- **Argala (A).** More planets wins. Then dignity or aspect decides. A tie gives bandhana (bondage). Malefics in the 3rd cannot be obstructed.
- **Karakas (B).** Rao is explicit that the three classes must not be mixed. Naisargika karakas serve general results. Chara karakas serve the people in one's sustenance, raja yoga and spiritual life. Sthira karakas serve the death of relatives.

### Cited Findings
- **(A) Graha drishti is desire.** It is comparatively temporary. All planets except Ketu cast it on the 7th. The special aspects are Mars 4th and 8th, Jupiter and Rahu 5th and 9th, and Saturn 3rd and 10th. Rahu also aspects the 2nd sign. No planet aspects the 2nd, 6th, 11th or 12th, apart from Rahu's 2nd. [srath.com, Tattva & Dristi](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/amateur/tattva-dristi/)
- **(A) Rasi drishti is knowledge.** It is a permanent feature of the signs:
  - movable signs aspect fixed signs, except the adjacent one;
  - fixed signs aspect movable signs, except the adjacent one;
  - dual signs aspect each other;
  - the aspect is mutual, and planets share the aspects of their sign.

  The article gives no rule for when the two systems disagree. [srath.com, Tattva & Dristi](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/amateur/tattva-dristi/)
- **(A) Sign aspects come first historically.** Rath argues that Parashara taught sign aspects before planetary ones, and that rasi drishti can cause lasting damage. (Earlier "method summary", section 1, citing srath.com Complexity of the Jaimini Sutra.)
- **(B) Rao's ranking by kind of influence.** Rasi drishti is like the influence of neighbours: the same for every planet in a sign, but varying in nature. Graha drishti is like a priest's influence in his own temple: specific to the planet. Planets with rasi drishti have a small influence and planets with graha drishti a more concrete one. Argala decides parts of the matter, like a bolt. [Rao IA, sect. 10.4-10.5, pp.103-104](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(A) Argala formation.**
  - **Primary argala:** from the 2nd, 4th and 11th, obstructed by the 12th, 10th and 3rd.
  - **Secondary argala:** from the 5th and 8th, weaker, obstructed by the 9th and 6th.
  - **Special argala:** malefics in the 3rd have argala that nothing obstructs. The 7th has special argala, obstructable from the lagna.
  - **Ketu:** counting runs in reverse when Ketu is involved, and for spiritual purposes.
  - **Reference:** argala can be judged from any planet or sign.

  [srath.com, Argala: Planetary Intervention](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/amateur/argala-planetary-intervention/)
- **(A) Argala against obstruction.**
  - The side with more planets wins, and an occupied sign beats an empty one.
  - Next come own sign, a benefic sign, or a planetary aspect; his example lets Jupiter's aspect decide.
  - An equal contest produces bandhana yoga. A malefic bandhana is severe; a benefic one can mean disciplined indoor work.
  - An intervening planet that is inimical to the house pulls the house's results astray, except for the 3rd-house special argala.

  [srath.com, Argala: Planetary Intervention](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/amateur/argala-planetary-intervention/)
- **(B) Rao on the same question.** Rao gives the same houses and the Ketu reversal. If a house has both argala and virodhargala, compare planet counts first, then strengths. [Rao IA, sect. 10.5-10.6, pp.104-106](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(A) Argala on the lagna as a karaka test.** The Sun's and the AK's unobstructed argala on the lagna gives status and health; an obstructed AK mars the chart. (*Crux* Ch. IV sect. 4.1; earlier report, section 1, step 4.) In a varga, argala on each karyesha decides which role thrives under the dasa (earlier report, section 2, step 4).
- **(B) Three karaka classes, not to be mixed.**
  - **Naisargika (9 planets, presided by Brahma):** general results, phalita.
  - **Chara (8 planets including Rahu, not Ketu; presided by Vishnu):** the people who shape one's sustenance and achievements, raja yoga, spiritual progress, and karma carried across lives.
  - **Sthira (7 planets; presided by Shiva):** the timing of relatives' deaths only.

  [Rao IA, sect. 8.1, pp.79-80](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Applying the karaka classes.**
  - **Marriage:** read the 7th from Venus, a naisargika karaka, for both sexes. Jupiter, the sthira karaka, is used only for the death of the spouse in a female chart.
  - **Chara and sthira karakas stand for the person directly.** The DK itself is the spouse; the 7th from the DK is not.
  - **Naisargika karakas are counted from.** The 5th from Jupiter gives the birth of children and simple events. The PK, a chara karaka, gives children-linked achievement or soul-punishing trouble. Jupiter as sthira karaka times a child's death.

  [Rao IA, sect. 8.3-8.4, pp.82-83](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Chara karaka computation.** Use eight planets. Rahu's advancement is measured from the end of his sign. The highest advancement is the AK, and the rest follow in descending order. [Rao IA, sect. 8.2, p.80](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf). This closes, at tier B, the earlier report's inference that the AK is chosen by highest degree; the earlier report already found the eight-karaka scheme at tier A.
- **(B) Sthira karakas of the parents.** The father's sthira karaka is the stronger of the Sun and Venus, and the mother's the stronger of the Moon and Mars. A footnote notes a day/night variant. [Rao IA, sect. 8.3, p.82](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Rasi drishti against graha drishti is a question of kind.** Neither tier makes one system override the other. Rath frames the difference as knowledge against desire and permanent against temporary. Rao frames it as small against concrete. A practical reading of both: rasi drishti for lasting links and relationships, graha drishti for active, dasa-driven influence. That reading is an inference. The Rajendra Prasad case uses rasi drishti for sibling links and graha drishti for influence on houses, but the fetch tool inferred that pattern.
- **Argala is the decider in both tiers.** It is the factor that settles what happens to a matter, which is why it appears as a test in so many Crux steps.
- **Spouse karaka tension.** *Crux* (tier A) uses Jupiter as the spouse significator in a female chart, in a worked example (Ch. II, p.37, earlier "method summary", section 4). Rao (tier B) says the 7th from Venus serves both sexes and keeps Jupiter for spouse-death timing. Whether that is a disagreement or a difference of purpose is unresolved.

### Gaps
- **Rath on argala against graha drishti.** No Rath text ranks argala against graha drishti for a house when they point opposite ways.
- **Rath on mixing karaka classes.** No Rath-bylined statement of the "do not mix karaka classes" rule was found; it is Rao's (tier B).

## Q4. Strength rules: how is the stronger sign or planet decided, and in what order are ties broken?

### Takeaway
- **Rath (A).** He takes the sign-strength order from the Jaimini Upadesa Sutras. His Narayana page says sources differ on the order, and his Mula dasa page credits its ordering to the Upadesa Sutras. His own step list was not retrieved.
- **Rao (B).** His student prints two ordered tie-break lists, one for signs and one for co-lords, plus a third, different order for Rudra.
- **Order varies by purpose.** The final tiebreak changes with the purpose: dasa length, arudha lord, or Rudra.
- **Shadbala and ashtakavarga (B).** Shadbala is kept only for deciding which planet in a yoga delivers it. Ashtakavarga measures whether a planet can deliver in harmony with the rest of the chart.

### Cited Findings
- **(A) Strength order credited to Jaimini.** Rath's Narayana Dasha page starts the dasa from the stronger of the lagna and the 7th. It says the strength rules come from BPHS and the Jaimini Sutras, and that sources differ on their order. His Mula dasa page credits its sign-strength ordering to Jaimini's Upadesa Sutra. (Earlier "method summary", section 2: [srath.com, Narayana Dasha](https://srath.com/misc/naraya%e1%b9%87a-dasa/); [srath.com, Mula Dasa](https://srath.com/?p=6254).)
- **(B) Stronger of two signs.** Used for rasi dasas, and for picking which of a planet's two signs is used to compute its graha arudha. Apply each rule only if the one before it ties:
  1. The sign with more planets wins.
  2. Count how many of Jupiter, Mercury and the sign's own lord occupy or aspect the sign, by rasi aspect only. More wins.
  3. A sign holding an exalted planet wins.
  4. A sign whose lord sits in a sign of different parity (odd or even) beats one whose lord sits in a sign of the same parity. This rule always settles a graha-arudha comparison.
  5. Natural strength: dual beats fixed, and fixed beats movable. This is useless for Narayana dasa, which compares a sign with its 7th.
  6. The sign whose lord is further advanced in its own sign wins. Rahu and Ketu are measured from the end of the sign. For Scorpio and Aquarius, use the stronger co-lord.

  [Rao IA, sect. 15.5.2, pp.203-205](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Stronger co-lord of Scorpio (Mars or Ketu) and Aquarius (Saturn or Rahu).** Used for arudha padas and Narayana dasa lengths.
  - **Base rule:** if one co-lord sits in the sign, the other is the lord.
  - **Otherwise**, apply each rule only if the one before it ties:
    1. The planet joined by more planets wins.
    2. Count how many of Jupiter, Mercury and the dispositor conjoin or aspect the planet, by rasi aspect. More wins.
    3. The exalted planet wins.
    4. Natural strength of the sign occupied: dual, then fixed, then movable.
    5. The final rule differs by purpose. (a) For a dasa length, take the planet that gives the longer dasa. (b) For an arudha lord, take the planet more advanced in its sign, with the nodes measured from the end.

  [Rao IA, sect. 15.5.1, pp.201-203](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) A different order for Rudra.** Rudra is the stronger of the 8th lords from the lagna and from the 7th, using a special table for the 8th. Rao's order here:
  1. more conjunctions;
  2. exalted or in own sign;
  3. joined with exalted planets;
  4. aspected by more planets by rasi aspect;
  5. further advanced.

  An override applies: a weaker 8th lord that is debilitated or in an enemy sign, and afflicted by Mars, Saturn, Rahu or Ketu, becomes Rudra anyway. [Rao IA, sect. 14 (Rudra), pp.182-183](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf). Rath's own Vimshottari #2 page uses a Rudra lord, the stronger of the 2nd and 8th lords; see the earlier "method summary", section 2. The two definitions differ and may serve different purposes.
- **(B) Why there are different strengths.** Rao uses an analogy to give each strength measure its own purpose:
  - **Shadbala:** when two or more planets join in a yoga, the shadbala-stronger one delivers it.
  - **Ashtakavarga:** shows whether a planet can deliver in harmony with the chart's other forces. Good shadbala with low ashtakavarga fails to deliver. Ashtakavarga strength can differ between vargas, so a planet gives its results through the area whose varga gives it a good score.
  - **Avasthas:** the planet's state modifies its output.

  [Rao IA, sect. 15.1-15.4, pp.187-201](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(A) Low bindus in the 10th.** Low ashtakavarga bindus in the 10th weaken the whole chart. (*Crux* Ch. XIII; earlier report, section 3, step 1.)
- **(A) Exaltation and debilitation in the D10 show success**, against ordinary dignity logic. (*Crux* Ch. XIII; earlier report, section 3, step 9.) This is a strength rule specific to one varga.

### Inferences
- **Occupancy first.** Across all three Rao orders, conjunction and occupancy count come first, ahead of dignity. ## Q5. Promise first, then timing: how is promise stated in D1 against the vargas?

### Takeaway
- **Rath (A).** He makes the relevant varga the confirming and final step of every house judgement: the tenth factor, backed by a citation of Saravali.
- **Rath, worked rules (A).** He states specific cases where the varga overrides or gates the D1:
  - the D7 has the final say on children;
  - raja yoga must be confirmed in the D5 and D10.
- **Rath on timing (A).** Timing comes from dasas chosen for the question. House position gates when a yoga matures.
- **Rao (B).** He states the dasa principle directly: a planet gives in its period what its positions in the various divisional charts promise.
- **Rao on death (B).** For marakas and death, he makes the rasi chart supreme over the D11 and D30.
- **No general rule.** No source in any tier states a general "D1 against varga when they disagree" rule.

### Cited Findings
- **(A) Varga as the final house factor.** The relevant divisional chart is checked last in the ten house factors, with a Saravali citation that astrology cannot proceed without vargas. (*Crux* Ch. II sect. 7; earlier report, section 1.)
- **(A) D7 decides, after the capacity check.** The result for children is given only after the D7 is examined, and capacity is checked before any curse is declared. (*Crux* Ch. VIII sect. 8.5.2, 8.5.5; earlier report, section 5.)
- **(A) Raja yoga must be confirmed** in the D5 and D10. (*Crux* Ch. XIII; earlier report, section 3, step 9.)
- **(A) Timing by house position.** Yogas in the 2nd or 4th fructify early, and a raja yoga in the lagna acts late. (*Crux* Ch. II p.19; earlier report, section 1, step 8.)
- **(A) Dasa by question.** Vimshottari, measured from the Moon, times the mind. Narayana times what the environment brings. For marriage, Narayana is the more accurate. (Earlier report, section 1, step 9, and section 8, step 5.)
- **(B) A dasa gives what the vargas promise.** Rao says a planet's Vimshottari period gives the results promised by its positions across the divisional charts. His examples:
  - the 5th lord's period in the D7 can give a child;
  - the 8th lord's period in the rasi chart can bring trouble;
  - the period of a planet exalted in the GL in the D10 can give power;
  - a further D9 example from the 12th from the AK.

  [Rao IA, sect. 16.5.1, p.214](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) The rasi chart is supreme for death.** Death concerns physical existence, so the rasi chart is of utmost importance. The D11 shows the forces of destruction, and the D30 shows evils and punishment, but the rasi chart remains the most important. [Rao IA, sect. 14.2, p.180](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf) **[Sensitive]**
- **(B) Rasi chart against D9 for marriage.** Rao makes the D9 the marriage chart, but says the rasi chart may be better where marriage is a living arrangement rather than a dharmic union. [Rao IA, sect. 13.4.1(1), p.169](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(A) Inside the varga.** Narayana dasa can be started inside a varga from the sign where the D1 lord of the house sits. (*Crux* Ch. VII; earlier report, section 2, step 6.) Rao's Kashinatha hora application has the same form (earlier report, section 2).

### Inferences
- **The working model.** Taken together: the D1 locates the matter (house, lord, karaka, arudha). The question-specific varga confirms it, qualifies it or, for children, overrides it. The dasa then delivers what both promise. The rasi chart keeps the last word only where the matter is physical, such as death or the body; this is Rao (tier B). This model is assembled from the findings above; no single text states it. The phrases "varga confirms D1" and "D1 is body, varga is fruit" were not found in any source.

### Gaps
- **D1 against a varga in general.** No rule in any tier says what to do when the D1 promises and the varga denies, except the D7 for children, where the D7 wins, and death, where the rasi chart wins (Rao).
- **Promise-then-timing as a principle.** No Rath-bylined statement of it as a principle was found.

## Q6. House as sign against bhava, maraka and badhaka, functional nature

### Takeaway
- **Houses as signs.** Both tiers use whole signs as houses throughout. No bhava chalit or cusp-based house system appears in Rath's material read or in Rao's book. No explicit statement of the stance was found.
- **Badhaka.** Defined by sign type: the 11th from a movable sign, the 9th from a fixed sign, the 7th from a dual sign. It is applied from any house, the AL and the paka lagna, not only the lagna.
- **Maraka.** The 2nd and 7th are maraka, being the 12th from the 3rd and 8th, the houses of life.
- **Functional nature (B).** Rao gives the standard Parashari functional scheme. Yogada status, from the lagna with the HL or GL, overrides functional nature.

### Cited Findings
- **(A) Badhaka in the career screen.** A 10th house or 10th lord in the badhaka sign from the paka lagna or AL obstructs the career; in a dusthana from them, the career is destroyed. (*Crux* Ch. XIII preview; earlier report, section 3, step 3.) The badhaka definition by sign type is in *Crux* Ch. II, pp.22-23 (earlier notes `d1_and_style.md` Q4).
- **(B) Badhaka from every house.** For a house in a movable, fixed or dual sign, the 11th, 9th or 7th from it is the baadhaka sthana, and its lord is the baadhaka for that house. His D10 example takes the baadhaka of the 9th, the boss, to show trouble with a manager. [Rao IA, sect. 13.3, pp.168-169](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Marakas.** The 3rd and 8th are the houses of life, so the 12th from each (the 2nd and 7th) are the houses of death. Their signs are maraka sthanas in rasi dasas, and their lords are maraka grahas. Good longevity wants the 3rd and 8th strong and the 2nd and 7th weak. [Rao IA, sect. 14.2, pp.180-181](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf) **[Sensitive]**
- **(A) The 7th as maraka.** Rath also lists the 7th as maraka (*Crux* Ch. X; earlier report, section 8). He reads the 8th as the maraka from the 7th in one example (earlier "method summary", section 4).
- **(B) Functional nature, by lordship from the lagna.**
  - **Trine lords:** functional benefics.
  - **Lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th:** functional malefics.
  - **Kendra lords:** a natural benefic becomes a functional malefic, and a natural malefic becomes neutral.
  - **Lords of the 2nd, 8th and 12th:** neutral, with the 8th the most malefic.
  - **A planet owning a kendra and a trine:** yogakaraka.
  - **The Moon:** depends on waxing or waning.

  [Rao IA, sect. 13.2, pp.166-167](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(B) Yogada beats functional nature.** Yogadas, from the lagna with the HL or GL, and planets in major yogas bring luck whatever their functional nature. Natural and functional nature are kept separate: what a planet is, against what it does to the native. [Rao IA, sect. 13.2, pp.167-168](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **(A) Planets from the dasa lord.** Planets in the 6th, 8th and 12th from the dasa lord act inimically, and those in the 7th give adverse results. (*Crux* Ch. II pp.22-23; earlier report, section 1, step 9.) This is a dasa-relative functional rule, alongside the lagna-relative one.

### Inferences
- **Every functional label is relative to a reference.** Badhaka, maraka and benefic or malefic can all be computed from any house or reference, not only the lagna. That follows from Rao's statement on badhaka and from Rath's dasa-lord rule, and is consistent with the school's multi-reference design.

### Gaps
- **Rath on bhava against rasi.** No explicit Rath statement was found on whole-sign houses against bhava chalit or Sripati houses. The whole-sign usage is inferred from all the worked material.
- **Functional nature in Rath's own words.** No Rath-bylined functional benefic and malefic table was found; the scheme above is Rao's (tier B).
