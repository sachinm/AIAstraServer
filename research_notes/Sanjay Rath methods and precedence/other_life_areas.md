# Sanjay Rath: methods for the life areas not yet covered

Scope: self and personality, health, education, spirituality and dharma, property and home, travel and foreign residence, enemies and litigation and debts, love before marriage, and general fortune. This file does not repeat the earlier report (`reports/Sanjay Rath life area readings.md`), which covers the D1 ten-factor checklist, varga principles, career, wealth, children, parents, siblings, marriage and the Subhapati. Where a rule here touches one of those, it is cross-referenced, not restated.

**Source tiers.** (A) Rath's own text: a book by him or an srath.com page with his byline. (B) Students and SJC teachers (Freedom Cole, Visti Larsen, Sarbani Rath, P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, SJC class notes). (C) Third-party.

**How the main source was read.** Most (A) rules come from *The Crux of Vedic Astrology: Timing of Events* (1998), read from a third-party full-text upload on archive.org, cited below as [Crux-1998](https://archive.org/stream/sanjayrathcruxofvedicastrologytimingofevents19982/Sanjay%20Rath%20-%20Crux%20of%20Vedic%20Astrology-Timing%20of%20Events%20(1998)_2_djvu.txt). It is a copyrighted book. It was read through a summarising fetch tool in 100,000-character blocks, so every rule is paraphrase from a summary, not a line-by-line read. Page numbers come from running headers. Within this copy they look consistent: for example, Ch. X runs from about p.251 to p.318. They differ from the other copies the earlier report used. Treat pages as locators, and check section numbers against print. srath.com pages were also read through the summarising fetch.

**Sensitive tag.** Rules about death, longevity, serious illness, curses and black magic are tagged **[Sensitive]**. They are recorded only as the source's rules, without elaboration, and are not for use in readings without a product-owner decision.

## 1. Self and personality (the "Self" board)

### Takeaway
Rath reads the person's nature from a blend of the Rasi and Navamsa lagna, with the Navamsa dominating. He then reads the lagna lord's house and the Paka Lagna (the sign the lagna lord occupies). Mind comes from the nakshatra lord and body from the sign lord. The Atmakaraka then gives the soul's lesson and spiritual level. No single printed "Self" order exists; the order below is the Ascendant chapter's sequence.

### Cited Findings

**(A) Rath's own text**
- Order inside the Ascendant chapter: (1) read lagna, AL and Varnada lagna separately; (2) dual karaka check, Sun and AK argala on the lagna; (3) sign temperament; (4) lagna lord by house; (5) Paka Lagna and the lagna lord's nakshatra lord; (6) yogas. Steps 1-2 are in the earlier report. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV §4.1-4.4, approx. pp.54-60
- **Lagna lord and Sun:** the lagna lord joining or aspecting the Sun gives good moral character and a cheerful disposition. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV §4.1
- **Sign temperament:** each lagna sign gets a character sketch. Examples: Aries energetic and impulsive, Taurus artistic and stubborn, Scorpio forceful but prone to anger, Capricorn steady but can be melancholic. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV §4.3.1-4.3.12, approx. pp.55-59
- **Blend rule:** the sign results blend the Rasi and Navamsa lagnas. The Navamsa traits predominate, and planets in the lagna modify both. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV §4.3 note, approx. p.59
- **Lagna lord in the 12 houses** (each conditional on strength), approx. p.59-60 — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV §4.4.1-4.4.12:
  - 1st: strong, brave and intelligent, but fickle-minded.
  - 2nd: scholarly, religious, honourable.
  - 3rd: courageous, fortunate, helpful brothers.
  - 4th: parental happiness, comfort, property, missionary zeal.
  - 5th: honourable, short-tempered, favoured by rulers (its children line is **[Sensitive]**).
  - 6th: debts, ill health and enemies; a benefic aspect or the lord's own period destroys enemies (its spouse line is **[Sensitive]**).
  - 7th: wandering or ascetic life unless the lagna lord is strong; strong gives great wealth (spouse lines **[Sensitive]**).
  - 8th: learned but diseased, short-tempered, a gambler **[Sensitive]**.
  - 9th: fortunate, popular, skilled, eloquent; blessed by Jupiter.
  - 10th: honourable, self-made, ambitious; blessed by parents.
  - 11th: good income and reputation, good qualities of head and heart.
  - 12th: self-caused misfortune, spendthrift, short temper.
- **Mind against body:** matters of the mind (intelligence, enemies, fortune) are seen mainly through the lord of the constellation (nakshatra lord); bodily matters through the sign lord. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV, approx. p.60
- **Paka Lagna:** the sign occupied by the lagna lord; the nakshatra-lord and sign-lord results above also apply to it. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV, approx. p.60. In the career chapter, the 10th or its lord in the badhaka or a dusthana from the Paka Lagna obstructs the career (earlier report, §3).
- **Worked example (Nehru, Chart 11):** Cancer Rasi lagna gives sensitivity and economy, Aquarius Navamsa lagna gives a strong will and scientific outlook, and the Navamsa is called dominant. The Moon, lagna lord in the lagna, is also the Atmakaraka, read as self-actualisation; its nakshatra (Aslesha, Mercury) carries the mental side. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV Chart 11, approx. pp.61-64
- **Atmakaraka as the soul's lesson:** the AK's sign and navamsa show what the soul wants. Each planet as AK carries a lesson:
  - Sun: humility over ego.
  - Moon: care and compassion.
  - Mars: non-violence.
  - Mercury: truthful speech.
  - Jupiter: respect for guru, husband and children.
  - Venus: clean character, no lust.
  - Saturn: cause no sorrow; will share the sorrow of many.
  - Rahu: may be cheated, must stay guileless.

  — [srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/) (A)
- **AK nature and spiritual level:** a malefic AK suggests higher spiritual development and a benefic AK lower. A retrograde AK marks a deep-rooted desire that drives the birth, and a combust AK gives spiritual insight. The AK's Vimshottari dasa often brings suffering, while the Narayana dasa of its sign can bring major achievement. — [srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/) (A)
- **AK counted to the navamsa lagna:** count from the AK to the navamsa lagna; that house's results predominate in life. Examples: 2nd spiritual; 4th karma yogi; 6th disease and trouble, with remedies; 9th pious and fortunate; 12th wealth. — [srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/) (A)
- **Karakamsa as Aries:** treat the Karakamsa as the 1st house of a natural zodiac. A navamsa lagna in the 12th from Karakamsa tends to make a person very spiritual, and in the 9th strict about dharma. The navamsa lagna lord linked with the AK gives raja yoga. — [srath.com, Karakamsa and Natural Zodiac](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/karakamsa-and-natural-zodiac/) (A)
- **Fifth house and mind:** the 5th lord in the lagna makes one intelligent and kind. The Sun in the 5th gives brilliance and creativity, and Saturn there melancholy. Skill and intelligence are also read from the 5th of the navamsa lagna or Karakamsa. — [Crux-1998], Ch. VIII §8.1-8.3, approx. pp.166-168

## 2. Health and disease **[Sensitive throughout]**

### Takeaway
In Rath's D30 article the 8th is disease and debt and the 6th is weaknesses; in *Crux* the 6th also carries enemies, boils and injuries. The "6th acquired, 8th self-caused" split is a student's classification (tier B). The D6 and D30 form the varga layer and the A6 for the body part. His D30 article gives an order: longevity houses first, then the 8th and 6th, then the 4th, then the A6 and Venus. Timing is Narayana dasa, Vimshottari sub-periods and, for the D6/D30, Paryaya dasa. No planet-to-dosha mapping by him was found.

### Cited Findings

**(A) Rath's own text**
- **D30 order of judgment** — [srath.com, Trimsamsa D-30 Chart](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/varga/trimsamsa-d-30-chart/):
  1. The 1st, 10th and 8th houses and lords, for longevity **[Sensitive]**.
  2. The 8th (disease and debt) and the 6th (weaknesses).
  3. The 4th, as the 9th from the 8th: happiness and laughter as medicine. Venus gives happiness, Mercury laughter, and the Moon controls good health.
  4. The arudha of the 6th shows the body part likely to be afflicted.
  5. The strength of Venus, which rules the Mrityunjaya mantra, for recovery.
- **D30 meaning:** the "shadow self" built from past wrongs. The Sun, Moon and nodes rule no trimsamsa. The 12th shows the way out of disease, as the 5th from the 8th. — same article
- **D30 timing:** use Paryaya dasa (credited to Jaimini for the D6), since the D30 is a higher harmonic of the D6. Rahu and Ketu in the 8th give disease in their periods. — same article
- **6th house:** covers enemies, service, relatives, mental tension, boils, injuries. Its karakas are Mars (enemies, injuries), Saturn (service, weakness) and Mercury (relatives, worry). — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.1, approx. p.204
- **Body parts by planet:** Sun head, Moon face, Mars throat, Mercury back, Jupiter stomach, Venus eyes, nodes lower body. The context is boils or disease that appear when the lagna lord joins the 6th lord, or through planets whose signs hold an arudha in the 6th. It is not a general anatomy table. Verify in print. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.5, approx. pp.209-210
- **Vargas for the 6th:** the D6 (Shastamsa) and D11; the D6 is used for disease, enemies and theft. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.4, approx. p.209
- **Disease in the 8th-house chapter:** disease is read from the Rasi, Navamsa and Trimsamsa, with planets in the 8th or its trines as key indicators. The navamsa sign of the badhaka lord gives the type of trouble. Timing uses Shoola dasas **[Sensitive]**. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XI, approx. pp.320-322, 355-364
- **Planets in the 6th:** Venus there gives health problems until marriage. Rahu eclipsing the Sun gives disease. Mars as AK in the 6th gives boils, and Venus aspecting Mars gives quick relief. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.2, §9.7
- **Lagna lord:** in the 6th or 8th it links to ill health. The 12th lord in the 1st shows loss of health. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV §4.4.6, 4.4.8; Ch. XV §15.10
- **AK and health:** an AK with unobstructed argala on the lagna gives good health (earlier report). The AK counted 6th or 8th to the navamsa lagna means trouble; remedy is Saturn or Satya Narayana, full-Moon fasts, truthfulness. — [srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **8th house and Rahu:** Rahu tied to the 8th lord or 8th house points to hard-to-cure illness. Six multiple-sclerosis charts show it alongside a weak 8th lord, an afflicted Mars (nerves), and a dasa that exposes the weakness. Sri Rudra worship is the remedy. — [srath.com, Rahu and Eighth House](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/amateur/rahu-and-eighth-house/)
- **Remedies in the chapter:** Shanti mantras and a crystal rosary for boils. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX
- **Subhapati:** the Moon's dispositor for health. See the earlier report, §2.

## 3. Education and learning

### Takeaway
Rath sets the 4th for schooling (Mercury as the karaka for formal education), the 5th for knowledge gained, and the 9th for higher education. He confirms all of them in the D24 (Siddhamsa). His Preface presents this as his own position and corrects the opposite view. Timing is the Vimshottari dasa of the relevant planet, with a D24 Narayana dasa for education abroad.

### Cited Findings

**(A) Rath's own text**
- **Order:**
  1. The 4th for basic schooling and the institution.
  2. The 5th for the knowledge gained.
  3. The 9th for higher education.
  4. Confirm in the D24.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. VII §7.7, approx. pp.159-161
- **Karaka:** Mercury signifies formal education among the 4th-house karakas (Moon mother, Venus vehicles, Ketu home, Mars land). — [Crux-1998], Ch. VII §7.1, approx. p.127 (from earlier notes, `research_notes/Sanjay Rath life area readings/family_parents_children.md`)
- **Preface statement:** the 4th governs formal education, the 5th intervenes in it, and the 11th from the 4th (speech and languages) describes the skills gained. — [Crux-1998], Preface
- **Yogas:**
  - Sharada yoga: the Moon in a kendra to Mercury, with a decanate condition by sign type. A 4th lord joining Mercury and the Moon also forms it.
  - Good learning: the 4th lord combined with Jupiter, the Sun or Mercury, or a Parijata-type combination of the 2nd, 4th and 11th lords.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. VII §7.3.2, §7.7, approx. pp.132, 160-161
- **Ninth and higher learning:** the 9th lord in the lagna supports higher learning, and the D24 is checked for it. Mercury (languages), Jupiter (Sanskrit) and Saturn (traditional knowledge) influencing the 9th show scholarship. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.9, §12.13, approx. pp.374, 388
- **Lagna lord:** the lagna lord in the 2nd or 9th links to scholarship. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IV §4.4
- **Third house:** Dhimantah yoga for skill. — [Crux-1998], Ch. VI §6.1, approx. pp.108-109
- **Karakamsa:** the 4th from Karakamsa holds parā vidyā (higher knowledge) and learning direction. The 3rd and 6th hold dhīmantaḥ yoga (intelligence). The 2nd and 7th hold aparā vidyā (worldly knowledge). — [srath.com, Karakamsa and Natural Zodiac](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/karakamsa-and-natural-zodiac/)
- **Varga cycle:** the D24 is learning, in the second harmonic cycle (earlier report, §2).
- **Timing:**
  - The dasa of the relevant planet: an afflicted Moon dasa disrupted studies, and a Rahu dasa yielded a scholarship.
  - An M.S. and a Ph.D. were timed to Jupiter dasa in a worked chart.
  - Narayana dasa of the D24 times education abroad.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. VII §7.7, approx. pp.161-162; Ch. IV Chart 15, approx. p.70; Ch. XV §15.13, approx. pp.565-568

## 4. Spirituality, dharma and moksha

### Takeaway
Rath's method has five parts:
- The Ishta devata (liberation) is the strongest influence on the 12th from Karakamsa in the D9.
- The Dharma devata is the 9th from Karakamsa.
- The Palana devata (livelihood) is the 6th from the Amatyakaraka in the D9.
- The Guru devata is the navamsa sign of the Bhratrikaraka.
- The D20 confirms the deity.

He states a three-step tie-break. Renunciation is timed by Drig dasa, and the AK's dasa.

### Cited Findings

**(A) Rath's own text**
- **Order** — [srath.com, Spirituality in Jyotish](https://srath.com/spirituality/spirituality-in-jyotish/):
  1. Identify the AK and the Karakamsa.
  2. Ishta devata from the 12th from Karakamsa.
  3. Dharma devata from the 9th from Karakamsa.
  4. Palana devata from the 6th from the AmK in the navamsa.
  5. Guru devata from the Bhratrikaraka's navamsa sign.
  6. Only then, a short-term remedy for the current problem, given with caution.

  The long-term tripod maps to the Sun (Ishta), the Moon (Palana) and Jupiter (Guru).
- **Deciding the strongest influence:**
  1. Planets in the sign. Prefer exalted or own-sign. If still tied, the page names the planet "with the highest longitude" the strongest, measured in the rasi chart ignoring signs.
  2. If the sign is empty, sign-based aspects only. A narrow re-fetch confirms that the page itself says the aspects "on the second house should be determined". The page does not say what "second house" refers to here. It may be a typo for the 12th, or a count from the 12th. Treat it as unresolved. Aspects show direction rather than the deity.
  3. If nothing aspects, the sign lord, and the stronger lord for dual-lord signs.

  — same page
- **Deity tables:**
  - Ishta as a Vishnu avatara: Sun Rama, Moon Krishna, Mars Narasimha, Mercury Buddha, Jupiter Vamana, Venus Parasurama, Saturn Kurma, Rahu Varaha, Ketu Matsya.
  - A second planet-to-deity table modifies the deity by sign or decanate. Examples: Ketu Ganapati, Rahu Durga, Jupiter Mahavishnu or Jagannatha.

  — same page
- **Ketu and moksha:** Ketu in the 9th or 12th from Karakamsa gives a wish for emancipation. Pisces or Cancer there strengthens it. — same page
- **Malefics in trines to Karakamsa:** two, three or four malefics give mantra, yantra and tantra knowledge. Some placements read as black magic **[Sensitive]**. — same page; also [Crux-1998], Ch. XV §15.5
- **Book version:** the Ishta devata is read from the 12th from Karakamsa, with Jaimini's planet-deity list. The 6th from the AmK gives material support and the Bhratrikaraka the teacher. The D20 should also be examined for the deity. Ketu is the moksha karaka, with Ganapati as its deity. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XV §15.5, approx. pp.548-549
- **Internal inconsistency to check:** the ninth-house chapter says the AK *is* the Ishta devata and must be strong in a kendra or trikona. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.17, approx. p.415. This conflicts in wording with §15.5 and the srath.com page, which use the 12th from Karakamsa. It is probably a shorthand within a case, but it needs checking in print.
- **D20:** the 9th is the strongest trine. Its lords and the Moon shape devotion to a deity, and the 5th lord links to prayer and mantra. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.15-12.16, approx. pp.403-409. A workshop syllabus lists D20 bhavas with the devata types Kula, Gotra, Sthana, Dharma and Ishta; no rules are given. — [srath.com, Spiritual Weekend in California](https://srath.com/learn/workshops/spiritual-weekend-in-california/)
- **Karakamsa houses as deities:**
  - 1st, 5th and 8th: Surya.
  - 2nd and 7th: Devi.
  - 3rd and 6th: Ganesha.
  - 4th: Shakti.
  - 9th: Vishnu as dharma.
  - 10th and 11th: Shiva and Rudra.
  - 12th: Vishnu as Ishta, liberation while living.

  — [srath.com, Karakamsa and Natural Zodiac](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/karakamsa-and-natural-zodiac/)
- **5th-house worship and mantra:**
  - The strongest planet in the 5th gives the deity worshipped.
  - The mantra is chosen in this order: the planet in the 5th, then an aspecting planet, then the 5th lord if well related to the lagna lord, else its dispositor.
  - Mantras of planets unrelated to the 5th fail in their periods.
  - The A5 (Mantra pada) is a reference point for remedies.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. VIII §8.2, §8.4, §8.10, approx. pp.167-170, 194
- **Renunciation markers:**
  - Benefics in the 3rd or 6th from the AL, or malefics in the 2nd, 4th or 7th from it.
  - Rahu in the 7th or 12th from the AL gives deep spirituality.
  - Saturn in dignity in the 9th gives a renunciant attitude.
  - The Moon in the 12th forms Shiva yoga.
  - Sun, Jupiter and Ketu on the lagna or 10th mean low materialism.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.6, §12.8; Ch. XV §15.4, approx. pp.372-373, 547-548
- **Planet to type of worshipper:** e.g. Jupiter saints, Venus Vaishnavas, Saturn severe penance. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.6, approx. p.372
- **Timing:** renunciation is timed by Drig dasa from the 9th, the AL Drig dasa, and the AK's Vimshottari dasa. Ketu's transit through trines to the lagna or AL shows renunciation. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.6, §12.14-12.15; Ch. V §5.5, approx. p.89

## 5. Property, vehicles and home

### Takeaway
The judgment runs:
- The 4th house and its karakas: Mars land, Venus vehicles, Ketu buildings, Jupiter property.
- The A4 counted from the AL.
- The D4 for immovable property and residence.
- The D16 for vehicles.

Residence changes are timed by a Narayana dasa in the D4 started from the D4 sign of the D1 4th lord. Vehicle purchases fall in the sub-sub-period of the 4th lord's D16 position.

### Cited Findings

**(A) Rath's own text**
- **4th-house matters:** mother, land, vehicles, mines, inheritance. Supporting vargas: D4 home, D16 vehicles and luxuries; real-estate income from the D2 and D10. — [Crux-1998], Ch. V §5.4, approx. p.83; Ch. VII §7.1, approx. pp.127-128
- **Planets in the 4th:**
  - Mars gives land with real-estate disturbance.
  - Jupiter gives property, home and vehicles.
  - Ketu is good for buildings.
  - Venus and Jupiter give happiness.
  - Rahu harms unless exalted or in its own sign.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. VII §7.2.3-7.2.9, approx. pp.129-131
- **4th lord:** in the 10th it destabilises the home. In the 12th it denies happiness from the father. In the 8th it is bad, except the Sun in Sagittarius, which gives property abroad. — [Crux-1998], Ch. VII §7.3.1-7.3.4, approx. pp.131-133
- **A4:** the A4 in a kendra or trikona from the AL brings property and home blessings. The A4-to-AL readings on the mother's relationship are in the earlier report. — [Crux-1998], Ch. VII §7.3.3, approx. p.132
- **D4:**
  - Without the wealth-giving Jupiter there, wealth from property is unlikely.

## 7. Enemies, litigation and debts

### Takeaway
Enmity is read from the A6 (Shatrupada) and the Varnada of the 6th. Battles are decided by comparing the 3rd and 6th from the AL (the native) with the 3rd and 6th from the A6 (the enemy). Litigation uses the AL, A6, A7 and the D6. Debt is Mars and the 8th, with the 6th for loans. Timing is Narayana dasa (in thirds), Vimshottari sub-periods and the transit Moon.

### Cited Findings

**(A) Rath's own text**
- **Order** — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.1-9.3, approx. pp.204-209:
  1. The 6th house and its karakas.
  2. The A6 and the Varnada of the 6th for the enmity itself.
  3. The 3rd and 6th from the AL for the native's fighting spirit, and the 3rd and 6th from the A6 for the enemy's strength.
  4. The 6th from the Varnada lagna for the native's supporters, and the 6th from the Varnada of the 6th for the enemy's supporters.
- **Who wins:**
  - Benefics in the 3rd or 6th from the AL mean the native gives up or loses; benefics there from the A6 mean the enemy is beaten.
  - The Sun in trines to the AL favours the native, and in trines to the A6 the opponent.
  - Mars or Saturn in the 6th is good for competition. Many malefics there make powerful enemies.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.1-9.2, illustrations of Hitler, Roosevelt and Hirohito, approx. pp.209-223
- **6th lord's direction of enmity:**
  - In the 5th, children or friends become hostile. In the lagna, the native is hostile to his own family.
  - Counting from the 6th to its lord harms the enemy's significations; counting from the lord to the 6th harms the native's.
  - Vipareeta raja yoga (6th lord in a dusthana) gains from others' losses. Its family side-effects are **[Sensitive]** (earlier report, §3).

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.3, approx. pp.207-209
- **Litigation:** the 6th is read through the AL, A6 and A7. Mercury in the 6th of the D6 signals litigation. Court success comes from propitiating the AK. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.10, approx. pp.246-250
- **Theft:** the thief is read from the A6 lord and the Rahu or Saturn placement, and in the D6 from the 2nd and 6th. Recovery is timed by the transit Moon, with the Surya ashtakshari mantra as the remedy. — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.6, approx. pp.224-228
- **Hidden enemies:** people shown by planets in the 12th from the AL are secret enemies whom the native likes. Malefics with the 12th bring trouble from secret enemies and loss of sleep. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XV §15.7-15.9, approx. pp.550-552
- **Competitor via A7:** in one beauty-pageant case, the Darapada (A7), not the A6, showed the competitor. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XIII, approx. p.470
- **Debts:**
  - Mars signifies debt (earlier report, §4). The 6th is loans and service; the 8th is business loans.
  - The lagna lord in the 6th gives debts, ill health and enemies.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. V §5.4, approx. p.83; Ch. IV §4.4.6; Ch. XI Charts 96-97, approx. pp.363-367

  - Vimshottari sub-periods; transit Moon; Bhrigu transits.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. IX §9.7-9.10; Ch. IV Chart 16, approx. pp.71-72; Ch. VI Chart 36

## 9. General fortune and luck (bhagya)

### Takeaway
The 9th lord is "divinity in the chart". The 9th from the AL is the main giver of material benefit and protection. The Sun and Jupiter in the 9th escape the karaka-in-own-house harm. Timing uses Narayana dasa and Sudasa, plus a printed table of planetary "years of fortune".

### Cited Findings

**(A) Rath's own text**
- **9th lord:** the 9th lord represents the divine in the chart. In the lagna it gives intelligence, good breeding, public service and higher learning. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.9, approx. p.374
- **9th from the AL:** planets there protect and bring fortune and money; it is the main contributor of material benefit. The Moon there gives lifelong fortune through the mother's blessings. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.8, approx. pp.373-374
- **The 9th as protector:** the 9th is the protector, Vishnu house, and its argala on the 8th prevents calamity. The 7th, as 11th from the 9th, affects fortune. Malefics in the 9th bring misfortune. — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII opening, approx. pp.368-369; Ch. X §10.1.2-10.1.3, approx. pp.252-253
- **Yogas:**
  - Dharma-karmadhipati yoga (9th and 10th lords linked) is the strongest raja yoga.

  — [Crux-1998], Ch. XIII, approx. pp.447, 455; Ch. IV §4.4.9
- **Navamsa:** planets in the 9th from the navamsa lagna show passion (Mars, Venus), obedience to the guru (Jupiter), and dutifulness to the father (Sun). — [Crux-1998], Ch. XII §12.8, approx. p.374
