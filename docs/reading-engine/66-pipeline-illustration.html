Self board pipeline, end to end

How one reading of the Self board is made, from the tap that starts it to the cards on screen. Numbers are measured from live replay run 2 (9 Nov 2026, one cold build, three profiles) unless marked as estimates.

The flow

1 · Trigger
First open of the boardFree trial build, once per board
"Get a fresh reading" tapOnly after "good until"; uses 1 question
Clock switchConsent sheet; uses 1 question
↓
2 · Build event
One background run per person and clockIts own Lambda invocation. The page shows the last reading (or a veil) and polls every 10 s. A marker stops two builds racing; a failed build gives the question back.
↓
3 · Data pulls
Chart digestD1–D60 houses and planets, dignities, avasthas, strength, yogas, arudhas, karakas
Dasha activationEvery stored clock, levels 1–3, with the engine's period charter
Sky overlay and pressureTransits, Sade Sati; the engine's pressure inventory (not deployed yet: windows run degraded)
PersonWizard facts, places, confirms, mood, plan

All four are rows the engine and the app already stored. One read each, in parallel. Measured 9.0 s on the live database; per-query timing is being added to find the slow one.

↓
4 · Sub-techniques
S1 ClockWhich dasha system reads this chart
S2 PeriodsPrevious, running, next; sub and sub-sub
S3 Windows6 months back and ahead; by life matter
S4 Promise ledgerGifts, knots, graces from yogas
S5 Risk ledgerWhat could go wrong, from where, who, when, what holds it
S6 Mind factsFeeling, thinking, peace, judgement
S7 Body factsFooting, wear, shape, live pressure
S8 Presence factsBearing, style, best-dressed window
S9 YearThis year's chart and clock
S10 ProfileAge band, gender, facts, mood

Each runs once per build and is shared by every area. Node only selects and joins engine rows with reviewed words tables; it computes nothing astrological. Outputs are stored in technique_outputs, keyed by the boundary stamps that can change them, so an unchanged stamp is a cache hit.

↓
5 · Area briefs
12 briefs: the hero plus 11 areasRight now · Where you get stuck · This chapter · In short · Who you are · How you think · Body & energy · Drive & ambition · How you come across · Gifts & knots · Chapters · This year. Each brief is a selection of fact tags, evidence keys, dates and ledger rows. No prose.
↓
6 · Cache prep
Slices built from the contextEach agent gets a stable prefix (cached) and a small suffix (today, confirms). Never read from the database again.
Prefixes warmed in parallelEvery region gets an llm_cache_entries row so the 45-minute sweep can delete it. Warm-up measured 3.4 s.
↓
7 · Family readers
RasiNatal houses
VargaDivisional charts
DashaPeriods
YearAnnual charts
SkyTransits

The linemen. Five parallel calls on gemini-3.1-flash-lite, each returns plain findings for its family. Measured: 99% of input cached, about 6 s wall, $0.015 for all five. The v1 lineman that joined them is retired in v2: the ledgers do that join in code.

↓
8 · Oracle
Call ANow group areas
Call BYou group areas
Call CTime group and patterns

Three parallel calls on gemini-3.8-flash over one shared cached prefix (natal slice, ledgers, briefs). Each writes its areas in the strict Vedic register as JSON: headline, paragraphs, dates, actions, certainty, evidence keys. It reads the risk ledger and confirms or rejects each row; it never recomputes a date. Measured: 89.7% cached, about 27 s wall, $0.05.

↓
9 · Validate
Oracle validatorEvery evidence key must be in the input; every date must be an exact stored window; caps; engine names redacted. A failed or partial Oracle stores nothing.
↓
10 · Personalizer
Builds a portrait, then writes the cardsBeing moved to the Oracle's model (gemini-3.8-flash). Reads the Oracle document plus the person portrait: personality, character, biography, birth place and current city, past month, mood, health, the state of career, love, self, home, family, business, health, fortune, work, gains and sacrifices, and the register (plain or technical).
Personalizer validatorEvery sentence cites an Oracle paragraph; dates only from the Oracle; no chart terms, engine names, medical words; profile-fit words; gender words only when stated. A failing card shows "Not enough in your chart to say this with confidence."

Run 2 on flash-lite: 3 of 3 profiles, about 13 s each, about $0.007 each. Estimate on the Oracle's model: about $0.02–0.03 per profile. A profile change (wizard answer, mood, birthday) re-runs only this step.

↓

Stored readingHero and cards per clock and profile; Oracle document; briefs and frame
GET /api/self/v212 areas in order, ranges, refresh dates, clock list; engine names redacted
Self pageSelfie and "Self", hero with strip and question, carousel, Fresh reading button

What is not working yet

Double build. With the switch on, the old Oracle and lineman run first and the new page never reads them. Fix in progress.
9-second data load. Expected under 1.5 s; per-query timing is being added to find the slow read on the live database.
Engine pressure inventory not deployed. Every window has equal strength until it ships; there is no documented engine release path.
Readings go stale between fresh taps. "Since May" and "eases 12 Dec" are relative to the build date; the page needs an "as of" date and a nudge when a window has passed.
Love and Work don't share the sub-techniques. Clock, periods, windows, ledgers and profile are the same for all three boards.
