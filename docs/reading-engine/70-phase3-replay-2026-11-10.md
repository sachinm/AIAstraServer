## What failed or was left out

1. **Two clock sheets were partial** (Yogini and the Tithi Pravesha year): the reader gave a tone the cited rows did not
   allow in three cells ("tone mixed against the cited rows (light); allowed light"). The sheets' other cells stand.
2. **Card checks that dropped content:** `chapter` cited an evidence key that is not in the frame
   (`pressure:dasha:chathuraaseethi_sama/2/Ma@2025-11-03`, a row from before the frame); `mind` cited no ledger cell for
   all three spans (3 issues); `yogas` and `learning` each had a "confirm" dropped because its dates (2025-08-16 to
   2026-01-18) are outside the frame. 5 cards had issues in all; all 21 still stood after repair.
3. **Run log write failed:** `agent_runs` hit foreign key `agent_runs_agent_fkey`. The new agents (`reading.clock`,
   `reading.concordance`, the card agents) are not in the staging `agents` registry table, so their runs are not logged.
   Not a replay problem; it will happen on staging.
4. **A card says "agreement 4 of 5" while its tone is mixed and one clock disagrees** (the sleep card, s1): the agreement
   line counts the span tone against five clocks. That reads correctly; no fault found.

## What it means

- **Speed.** Waiting about 100 s for the first card is too long. The clock layer (readers, then a 44 s concordance)
  runs before any card. Options: show the board's cards from the chart alone while the layer runs (no agreement line yet);
  or give the concordance a smaller input; or cache the clock layer per kundli and per day, so only the first open
  pays it. The third needs no new design: the layer's output is a stored ledger.
- **Cost.** At $0.70 for all 21 cards, `CARDS_READ_REST` (the other 16 cards, $0.43) should stay off until the cost is
  cut or Sachin accepts it. The board of five alone is about $0.27 with the layer.
- **"Confirmed".** 28 of 36 cells confirmed. The stopgap cut heavy tone but the count still sees most cells as agreed.
  Whether that is because the clocks do agree, or because they share planet labels (the engine's flags are per planet),
  is the open question in `docs/reading-engine/70-heavy-clocks-2026-11-10.md`.

## Fixes made (2026-11-10, after the replay; Sachin: "fix speed and cost")

No model was called for any of this. Every number below the measured column is arithmetic on the replay's own calls
(`replays/70-phase3-2026-11-10/summary.json`, prices `gemini-3.8-flash` $0.75 in, $0.075 cached, $3.75 out per M), not

### What changed

| # | Change | Commit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clock layer cache.** The sheets and the ledger are stored once per kundli in `kundli_slices` (row `layer.clock`), keyed on the as-of day, the clock set, the model, the prefix hash, every clock slice hash, the frame hash, the three prompts' hashes and the input version. A warm open reads one row and makes no reader and no concordance call (the cache region the cards read is still warmed). M48 is not applied on staging: with no table the read is null and the write does nothing, silently; the per-sheet and ledger rows in `technique_outputs` still serve a warm open there. | `e24a4d4` |
| 2 | **Cards no longer wait for the concordance.** With the per-card reading on, the layer returns as soon as the sheets are counted. The counted ledger already holds every cell's status, tone, agree/of and evidence (all code); the concordance only adds words, in parallel with the cards, folded in after. A card still needs a confirmed or leaning cell for `build` and must cite its cells, exactly as before. The card store key holds the grounding, not the words, so a card read before the words is served on the next open. | `eee5b32` |
| 3 | **Concordance trimmed.** Input: per cell only the agreeing clocks' sentences (others by tone), every clock for a contested cell; no evidence keys, strengths or pair table. | 4 | **Readers read their slice cut to the frame** (rows ending before 2026-10-27 or starting after the frame's end can never ground a cell), and are asked for 15-word sentences and at most three keys (the validator still accepts 25 words). | `7c1f62d`, `6399f94` |
| 5 | **Card input trimmed.** The three matter slices merged into one `MATTER ROWS` block: each row once, only rows overlapping the frame, rows and promise entries already in the cached prefix (`sky.window`, `year.tp`, `natal.core`) named by key only. | 6 | **Pre-check before a repair call** (`precheckCard`): a key the card was never given is dropped from a span that keeps another key (the replay's `chapter`); a span citing a dated row one of its ledger cells rests on, but not the cell, gets that cell (the replay's `mind`, three spans). Never on a planet or house key alone. Both replay repairs would have been fixed in code. | `e55c9e2` |

The replay's FK failure came from its storage-dry proxy swallowing the seed's raw SQL. Now a run that hits `agent_runs_agent_fkey` re-seeds and writes once more, and the replay seeds with the plain client first. | `da11df5` |
| 8 | **Replay script**: new estimate, and `--warm` (a second pass on the layer cached in memory, board only). | `27d0e43` |
