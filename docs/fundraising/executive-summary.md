# AdAstra AI Executive Summary
November 2026 | Confidential | Investor review
## A personal Vedic astrology companion
AdAstra gives people a place to check daily timing, understand a life question in the context of their own chart, and return to it as their plans change. The initial audience is people already familiar with Jyotish in the United States and India. The June plan identified three recurring needs: accessible timing, specialist chart depth, and continuity across questions. AdAstra brings those needs into one consumer experience.
## Product today and what comes next
The signed-in web app has free-entry Panchang timing under Happy Hour(s), Self, Love and Work dashboards, a Work question composer, a birth-chart sidebar and a Life timeline. Self has the latest dashboard design. Love and Work are next. Longer Life Reading is in development.
Users will choose and change card topics as their priorities shift. Each card gives them context before they ask a focused question. The response can draw on the card, relevant chart factors and useful details from prior conversations. Planned modes include a light check-in, a detailed Research view and a calmer response for stressful moments.
AdAstra calculates chart data from the birth date, time and place supplied by the user. Language models can give plausible but incorrect placements when asked to infer celestial geometry from text alone. AdAstra gives the reading workflow calculated chart facts first. Specialist agents can examine natal factors, planetary periods and transits for deeper questions. Reference-chart checks and practitioner review will test calculation agreement and interpretation quality.
## A reason to come back
Free Panchang is the entry point. The user might check a timing window, open a Love card before a conversation about engagement, then return to discuss a change of plans. Private Life Projects will let people collect inspiration, notes, dates and chart insights for trips, career moves, business goals, engagements and weddings. AdAstra will test whether users return to update those plans. The companion is positioned for reflection and planning, with future outcomes understood as uncertain.
# Business and financing
## Two markets with distinct offers
Lead with subscriptions in the United States and one-time question packs in India. The configured Plus catalog is $50 or INR 2,499 per month. The launch plan tests optional dashboard upgrades at $5.99 monthly in the US and INR 199 for a 30-day pass in India. India pack test prices are INR 99 for one question, INR 299 for five and INR 499 for ten. Verify receipts, renewals and repurchases before treating any offer as validated. Easy cancellation, free timing and a reason to resume a project are part of the retention strategy.
## Distribution and the $100K MRR milestone
Launch in both markets after iPhone, iPad and Android are approved and tested. Acquire early users through question-led search, Jyotish educators, US diaspora communities and India-based creators. Track repeat timing checks, card-to-chat use, paid renewal and pack repurchase before increasing spend.
At the configured Plus prices, 1,800 US and 400 India monthly subscribers imply about $100,634 MRR at a planning rate of INR 94 per USD. That is approximately $1.2M annualized recurring revenue. At a 5% subscriber share of meaningful monthly active users, this scenario requires 44,000 active users. One-time sales, sponsorships and API revenue are tracked separately. This is a scale milestone with a 24–30 month planning horizon from financing, subject to demonstrated demand, retention and funding.
## Financing and proof
The recommended seed target is $2M in preferred equity. The base budget covers 18 months of planned operations and a $250K reserve. A $1.5M close supports a narrower release with slower feature expansion and tighter marketing. The round funds mobile apps and billing, chart and reading quality, the US and India launch, and the first cohorts that can demonstrate repeat use and payment.
## Competitive position
Co-Star demonstrates personalized astrology and social discovery. Astrotalk and AstroSage already offer timing tools, and AstroSage sells AI readings. Jagannatha Hora is a specialist calculation reference. Dating products such as Tinder and The Pattern add astrology or compatibility to discovery. AdAstra is testing whether users value the continuity from a timing check to a specific chart-based question and an evolving private plan.
