# Financing and Consumer Launch

November 2026 | Internal decision memo

**Recommend a $2.0M target, a $1.5M minimum close for the scoped plan, and a $2.5M stretch only if hiring or proven distribution justifies it.** Treat preferred equity as the current investor round proposal. Accelerator capital and its SAFE terms are a separate decision. Budget against cash wired. Underwrite the build and release without assuming consumer revenue, ads or conditional accelerator follow-on.

| 18-month base uses | USD |
|---|---:|
| Founder and core engineering, including mobile and billing | $960,000 |
| Product design, QA, localization and practitioner review | $270,000 |
| Cloud, inference, analytics and support | $180,000 |
| Creator, search and consumer launch tests | $230,000 |
| Legal, privacy, finance and store operations | $110,000 |
| Contingency and runway reserve | $250,000 |
| Total | $2,000,000 |

Operating envelope before reserve is $1.75M, averaging about $97K/month. A $1M raise at that burn covers roughly 10 months before reserve, $1.5M roughly 15 months, and $2M roughly 21 months with full reserve available. Plan for 18 months of work and keep the reserve ring-fenced. The $1.5M version requires a $1.35M operating cap and $150K reserve, targeting roughly $75K/month for 18 months. Use a founder-led team, shared mobile code and focused contract support. Defer additional dashboards beyond the first three, broader paid acquisition, partner integrations and ads until release and early cohorts. The $1M version funds a narrower beta or needs follow-on capital before the full plan. This is a staffing and cash model, with vendor quotes, compensation, opening cash, cap table and liabilities to be confirmed before fundraising.

| Months from close | Milestone | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Baseline web funnel, charter the mobile build, finish dashboard and question flow scope | Instrumented activation, representative demo, practitioner rubric, test entitlements |
| 3–6 | iPhone, iPad and Android private beta in US and India | Real-device checkout sandbox, cross-device sign-in and history, Love and Work updated, verified timing and chart fixtures |
| 6–9 | Coordinated two-country consumer release | Both stores approved, purchases and cancellation verified, opt-in communications, support coverage, first paid cohorts |
| 9–12 | Establish repeat behavior and economics | D30 cohorts, US first renewal, India pack repurchase, quality and contribution by offer |
| 12–18 | Scale qualified channels and ship the next proven feature | Growth checkpoints with CAC and churn, Life Projects pilot, partner API design partner evidence |

The $100K MRR target is a scale milestone. Plan for a 24–30 month post-close scenario if commercialization begins around month nine and the subscriber build takes about 18 months. Track the first renewals and contribution before raising growth spend. If the base plan has fewer than nine months of cash remaining and repeat cohorts support expansion, prepare a next-round process. If the cohorts are weak, reduce discretionary acquisition, tighten onboarding and retest the offer before expanding the team.

At an illustrative $10M pre-money, a $2M priced round sells 16.7% before option-pool changes, existing convertibles and transaction terms. At $8M or $12M pre-money the same raise sells 20.0% or 14.3%, respectively. Use fully diluted ownership when comparing scenarios. An eventual institutional valuation needs actual cap-table review and investor discussions.
