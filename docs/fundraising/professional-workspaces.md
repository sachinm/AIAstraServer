## What practitioners pay for

Start with a secure workspace that lets a practitioner open a client-authorized Kundli or compute one from details the client provided for that purpose. Support an expiring client invitation, chart annotations, natal and divisional charts, planetary periods, transit comparisons, and session briefs that show which chart factors support each interpretation. The practitioner reviews and edits every brief before sharing. At the next session they can reopen the chart and prior notes. The value to measure is time saved in preparation and the share of sessions where the practitioner comes back to use the workspace.

Research gets structured side-by-side chart comparison, saved methods and reproducible exports with calculation conventions and version. Start with synthetic, public-use licensed or explicitly consented records. Use de-identified exports by default. Keep private client notes and identifying information out of research exports. Team and Enterprise add roles, access logs, shared templates and onboarding. Enterprise can add negotiated SLA, SSO and contract terms as they are built.

Access is scoped to each workspace and each authorized chart. Never grant lookup of arbitrary AdAstra users. Separate chart computation from AI usage. The chart cap counts distinct subjects opened for analysis in a billing month, including existing subjects. The same chart can be reopened repeatedly without consuming another subject slot. AI runs have their own cap. Failed jobs do not consume a run. Access revocation prevents future access to the linked chart and sharing. Address retention, derived reports and deletion in the client consent and workspace terms. No client or research data feeds advertising.

## Launch quotes

US and international prices are in USD. India pricing is in INR. These are proposed monthly prices before applicable taxes, with pricing and demand to be tested.

| Plan | US and international | India | Monthly scope |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Practitioner | $149 | ₹3,999 | 1 seat, 100 authorized client charts, 150 AI briefs |
| Researcher | $149 | ₹4,999 | 1 seat, 250 eligible charts, 150 AI runs, batch comparisons and structured exports |
| Studio | $499 | ₹14,999 | 5 seats, 500 authorized charts, 750 AI briefs, shared templates and access controls |
| Enterprise | From $1,500 | From ₹49,999 | 10 seats, negotiated chart volume, starting at 2,000 AI runs, onboarding and contractual support |

Lead with caps while costs are measured. Offer negotiated uncapped manual chart opens only where infrastructure and storage costs support it. Keep batch compute and AI runs metered. Test prepaid blocks of 100 AI runs at $49 or ₹1,999, with no automatic overage. Price to a 70% contribution margin target after variable compute, delivery, support and fees, including heavy-use accounts. At full included usage the India Practitioner tier has about ₹8 per brief available for all variable costs at that margin. Measure real delivery cost before promising the quota or change the cap and quote together. Consider annual billing after first-renewal and usage evidence. Partner API access stays on its own contract.

## An alternate path to $100K MRR

Use ₹94 per USD as a planning conversion. Count only active recurring plans, after recurring discounts and before variable costs. A customer can buy a consumer plan and a professional plan, but each charge appears once and professional counts below refer to distinct paid workspaces.

| Recurring line | Active count | Approximate MRR |
|---|---:|---:|
| US consumer Plus at $50 | 901 | $45,050 |
| India consumer Plus at ₹2,499 | 300 | $7,976 |
| US Practitioner at $149 | 150 | $22,350 |
| India Practitioner at ₹3,999 | 200 | $8,509 |
| International Researcher at $149 | 75 | $11,175 |
| USD Studio at $499 | 10 | $4,990 |
| Total | 1,201 consumer subscriptions and 435 professional workspaces | $100,049 |

Totals use unrounded figures. This is a scenario for roughly $1.2M annualized recurring revenue, not a sales forecast. Professional subscriptions contribute about $47K MRR. Advertising, one-time question packs, overages and API revenue are excluded. At 5% consumer subscriber penetration, the consumer side implies approximately 24,000 meaningful monthly active users. At a hypothetical 20% qualified-demo-to-paid rate, winning 435 workspaces takes at least 2,175 qualified demos at that rate before churn. Schools and studios are a way to reach multiple practitioners through one workspace sale.

Recruit 12 paid design partners across India, the US and international English-speaking markets, with representation from solo practices, teachers and studios. Use targeted demonstrations for practitioners, research workshops for Jyotish schools and creator partnerships based on public professional work. Run a six-week pilot. Log time to prepare a comparable session, chart authorizations, repeated use after week one, actual AI cost, paid renewal and support time. Use working pilot targets of at least 8 of 12 using the workspace in two separate weeks and at least 6 renewing at the quoted price. Treat the small sample as directional evidence. Expand outreach after confirming consent flow, contribution margin and repeated use. Keep the existing consumer-first financing case until professional renewals support including it in the forecast.
