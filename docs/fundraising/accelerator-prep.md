# AdAstra Accelerator and Investor Preparation

Prepared November 12, 2026. Internal working guide for YC, a16z speedrun, Bay Area accelerator applications, and seed investor meetings.

## Company story

AdAstra is a Vedic astrology companion for people making plans and working through everyday questions. A free Panchang timing check brings people in. Life dashboards give people context before they ask their own questions, and the assistant explains the relevant chart factors and timing. AdAstra calculates the chart from the user's birth details before the AI interprets it. Deeper readings can use specialist agents to examine different chart factors and reconcile their findings. Verify each technical claim against the current demo build.

Lead with English-speaking Vedic astrology users in the US and India. A conversation about dating, a relationship milestone, or planning an engagement makes the companion easy to understand. The same workflow applies to work, family, or personal goals. Keep matching and the future API out of the live demo unless they are explicitly labeled as roadmap items.

## Investor deck outline

1. **Company and customer.** A Vedic astrology companion for people who seek guidance on timing, work, relationships, and personal plans.
2. **Problem.** People often have to interpret complex chart output or restate their situation when seeking follow-up guidance. Use one customer observation with permission or in anonymized form.
3. **Product.** Show free Panchang timing, the Self dashboard, and a focused Love follow-up. Call out which screens and interactions are live. Work and Love have dashboards and are planned to receive the newer Self design.
4. **Why the answers are different.** Show chart computation, relevant chart factors, and the explanation or research view. Use one verified example and describe practitioner review as a milestone.
5. **Market and competition.** Define reachable US diaspora and India segments from the bottom up. Compare Co-Star, Astrotalk, AstroSage, Jagannatha Hora, and the relationship experience offered by dating apps.
6. **Demand and distribution.** Show qualified search, educators, and communities. Use cohorts for activation, repeat timing checks, question completion, and return. Put both sample size and date on each number.
7. **Revenue.** Start with US subscriptions and India question packs as regional offer hypotheses. Show optional paid dashboards, easy cancellation and return, and the math behind the $100,000 MRR target. Report pack sales alongside recurring revenue as a separate measure.
8. **Launch and capital.** Publicly launch the US and India together once iPhone, iPad, and Android releases pass the launch gates. Show what the round funds, runway, and the milestones that justify wider distribution.
9. **Team and next step.** Name the team, relevant experience, and the specific roles or partnerships the round enables.

The deck accompanies the 1–3 page executive summary and business plan. The first meeting needs the story, evidence, and live demo. Keep the source inventory and detailed codebase checklist for internal prep or diligence when requested.

## MVP demo and evidence

Use a synthetic demo account and consented or synthetic fixtures. Test the canonical sign-in and account flow in a clean browser and pre-authenticate the demo account before recording. Capture a short backup clip on the same release.

| Time | On-screen action | Verify before recording |
|---|---|---|
| 0–15s | Open Happy Hour(s) and show a populated local Panchang timing window. | Date, location, local time, and activity produce the expected view. |
| 15–30s | Open the current Self dashboard and one card. Switch to Love for the example if that entry is visible and working. | Current dashboard version and card interaction. |
| 30–60s | Ask “I'm thinking about getting engaged next year. What relationship themes should I reflect on as we plan?” Use a verified contextual chat entry. | The finished answer addresses the question, ties its interpretation to actual chart factors, and describes timing as a traditional interpretation. If only General Reading works, identify it as that in the demo. |
| 60–90s | Ask a follow-up such as “What should we talk through before deciding on timing?” Show the available chart or research detail. | The follow-up uses the prior exchange. Show the chart sidebar or reading stages only if visible. Identify longer-horizon Life Reading, configurable cards, and response modes by their verified release status. |

For YC, record the founder introduction separately. Explain what the product does, why the founder understands the user, and one measured product result or learning. For speedrun, use a founder-led account of what shipped and what the team learned, with the current form's video length and format. Use the archived [SR008 prep](../a16z-speedrun-sr008-application.md) for historical prompts. Recheck its dated product and application facts before reuse.

Before sharing the application, assemble a dated claims sheet with each metric's definition, region, time window, source, and owner. Define activation as completing a first timing check or a first chart-based answer, and report the two paths separately. Report day 7 and day 30 returns using the same denominator, plus paid renewal, pack repurchase, refund rates, and purchase conversion where charging is verified. Exclude staff, demo and test accounts. Show $100,000 MRR as a target and identify the time window and mix in its model. A program reviewer can inspect a real demo without seeing customer birth details or conversation history.

The public launch gate includes successful platform billing and restores, account deletion, regional price display, text and layout on phone and tablet, accessibility, slow-network recovery, and a reviewable privacy and support flow. Verify an iPhone, an iPad, and representative Android devices in both regions. Run closed tests on TestFlight and Google Play before coordinating the two-country release. The accelerator demo can use the current verified web MVP while the native apps are still in development.
