## 1. Executive summary

**The story in one paragraph.** People have always looked to the sky before the events of their lives. A full Vedic reading has been out of reach for almost everyone, because it needs a reader who knows the complete original sources, works through a whole chart and applies every rule as written. Very few people can do that, and they serve the very rich. A machine can do it for everyone. AdAstra AI is building that reader, and placing it where people already plan their lives: a layer they check each day, alongside their own judgement, to see what the sky says about what is coming and how to prepare for it.

**What exists.** A working web product, in testing: registration, three personal dashboards (You, Love and Work), a daily timing board built on the Panchang, and conversation with the AI from any card or freely. Behind it is a catalogue of written techniques, 25 of which have been run through a benchmark, a techniques lab, a research lab and an automated audit that traces every claim in a reading to a computed chart fact. It was built by one founder directing AI agents. **Built.**

**What does not exist.** Customers, revenue, outside funding, hires, payments, mobile apps, and any review of the readings by practising astrologers. We make no claim of accuracy.

**The competition is real.** Human astrologers, Astrotalk, AstroSage and Co-Star are large and established, and several are adopting AI. We are a new entrant.

**The barrier.** People pay a great deal for a human astrologer and still hesitate to pay for an AI reading. We expect adoption to be slow until we can publish a success rate for our readings.

**The ask.** $2.0M in preferred equity for 18 months, to do three things: prove the AI method of reading and publish the result, win adoption of a lifestyle app in the US and India, and hire people.

## 2. An old need

"People are not looking for horoscopes. They are looking for answers."

People look to the sky before a job, a marriage, a move, a child, or the right day to begin something. That has been true for thousands of years and is true now. In the United States, 27% of adults say they believe in astrology and 28% consult astrology or a horoscope at least once a year (Pew Research, May 2025). In India, 44% of people say they believe in astrology (Pew Research, 2021), one marketplace for human astrologers reported revenue of ₹1,214 crore in a single year, and one Panchang website reports 58 million visitors in 2024.

What people find is uneven. "The astrology market is fragmented across apps, websites, software tools, YouTube personalities, marketplaces, and independent practitioners. As a result, users frequently seek multiple readings from multiple astrologers to validate previous opinions. The same questions are asked repeatedly, often generating conflicting answers and additional expense."

"People do not purchase astrology for entertainment alone." They bring real questions, and they pay again when the first answer does not satisfy them.

## 3. Why only a few have ever had a full reading

The limit has never been curiosity. It has been the reader.

A full reading asks three things of an astrologer.

- **The complete sources.** They must know the original texts whole, and not an abridged or second-hand version.
- **The whole chart.** One birth moment yields about 45,000 records of chart data. **Measured, provisional.**
- **Every rule, as written.** They must apply each rule however hard the chart looks, without softening what it shows to please the person in front of them.

Very few people can do all three, and none can do it for every client and every question. "Many practitioners cannot consistently apply the full depth of the tradition during every consultation." So a careful reading has been the life's work of a few astrologers, serving the very rich.

Everyone else has made do with less. Much of what now passes for astrology is internet shorthand, such as the "Mercury retrograde" scare, far removed from the source texts.

## 4. The thesis: a problem made for superintelligence

Nobody learns assembly language to use a computer. The machine handles the mechanics, and people get the benefit. Astrology has never had that layer. The mechanics have stayed with the specialist, and so has the benefit.

In July 2025 an AI system solved five of the six problems at the International Mathematical Olympiad, graded by the Olympiad's own coordinators (Google DeepMind). That is sustained, exact reasoning over many steps. A Vedic reading asks for the same quality: a large body of rules, each applied exactly. The rules are written down. The chart is computed. What was missing was a reader with the whole source, unlimited patience and no reason to flatter.

"For the first time, AI can synthesize vast amounts of astrological data, classical rules, chart relationships, and historical context simultaneously on a personalized basis for every individual." A system can read every chart the same way, "applying the same diligence, preparation, and analytical rigor to every user regardless of income or location."

**This is a thesis, and it is not yet a result.** Today's general AI models know the classical texts, but they do not read a chart this way on their own. Asked to work out a chart from a birth date, they can produce plausible and wrong positions. Our work is to give them exactly computed charts and make them apply the rules one technique at a time. Sections 6 and 7 describe that work.

**The layer.** The only way to make this useful is to make it ordinary. Our own design papers put it this way: the product is the operating system for a person's life. We think of AdAstra AI as a layer added to everyday life, in the way a calendar or a weather forecast is: something a person checks before a contract, a first meeting, a journey or a new venture, to see what the planets and the nakshatras say about what is coming and how to prepare for it. It is one more input alongside a person's own judgement, for reflection and planning. It does not make the decision. **Vision.**

Elon Musk has said humanity should "become a spacefaring civilization, a multi-planet species" (CNBC, April 2021). Our own view is the other half of that thought. A civilization reaching for other planets should carry the best of its Eastern knowledge traditions with it, correctly and from the source.

## 5. How the product works

"Most AI products begin with a blank conversation. AdAstra begins with a structured model of a person's life."

A person moves through the product in five steps. All of it runs on the web in our test environment. **Built**, except where marked. Section 8 says how finished each part is.

**Step 1. Registration.** A person signs in with a code sent by email, then gives a birth date, time and place. The chart is computed once and kept.

**Step 5. Life Reading.** A longer reading of purpose and direction across a life, with a timeline of confirmed events. The timeline is built. The reading is in development. **Planned.**

**What makes it a daily layer.** The free timing board answers "what is today good for". The dashboards answer "where do I stand". The conversation answers "what about this one thing". Together they are meant to be opened the way a person opens a calendar. Whether people do return daily is untested.

No human astrologer is in the loop. Software computes the chart, and written techniques and AI agents produce the readings.

## 6. How a reading is produced

"Before every answer, AdAstra performs structured chart research similar to the preparation process of an experienced astrologer."

| Stage | What happens | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Calculation | Ordinary software computes the chart from the birth details: positions, divisional charts, planetary periods, transits and the annual chart. No AI is involved, so there is nothing to guess. | Built |
| 2. The record | The results are stored as about 45,000 records for the chart. | Measured, provisional |
| 3. Technique | Each card and each kind of question has a written technique: which parts of the chart to read, under which classical rules, and what to return. | Built: 142 catalogue entries of all kinds, including timing, chat and research; 25 exercised in the benchmark |
| 4. Reading | The technique is applied to the records that bear on the question: by written rules where the rule is exact, and by AI agents where judgement and words are needed. One answer was measured reading about 800 records. | Measured, 2 November |
| 5. Cross-check | Where several timing methods bear on the same question, the reading reports how many agree. | Built |
| 6. Writing | A writer turns the findings into plain words, with dates. | Built |
| 7. Audit | An automated check confirms that every claim points at a computed chart fact. Claims that do not are removed. | Built |

**What it costs.** The cost depends on how much of a dashboard the AI writes. In the design benchmarked on 8 November, a freshly built dashboard cost about 3 to 9 cents in model fees (**Measured**). In a fuller design tested on 10 November, where the AI writes every one of 21 cards, one person cost 70 cents (**Measured**); that design is switched off until its cost comes down. One chat answer is estimated at 6 to 12 cents (**estimate**, October, widened for a price change due on 1 December).

## 7. The research and accuracy programme

This is where most of the work has gone, and it is where we intend to compete. We are tackling research in more than one way.

- **Every dashboard card is fed by its own technique.** Because each technique is a separate written contract, each can be studied, corrected and tuned on its own, without disturbing the others.
- **A techniques lab** runs one technique on one chart and shows what went in, what each step did and what came out.

[Screenshot 6 to add: the techniques lab, one technique run]

- **A research lab** holds sets of synthetic charts across age groups, so a technique can be examined across many lives and not only one.

[Screenshot 7 to add: the research lab, synthetic charts]

- **An automated audit** runs on every reading, as described above.
- **AI does the research.** AI agents write the techniques, run them, audit the results and fix what fails, under the founder's direction. Then the cycle repeats.
- **Confirmation from people.** The dashboards ask a person whether a past period felt the way the chart suggests. Those answers are designed to become calibration points. **Built** as a feature; no outside person has used it.

**What we have measured.**

| What | Result | Date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benchmark | 30 dashboard readings on 10 test charts, using 25 techniques. Every claim shown traced to a chart fact. | 8 November 2026 |
| Model cost of the whole benchmark | $0.35 | 8 November 2026 |

**What this does and does not show.** The benchmark shows that the readings are grounded: each claim shown pointed at a computed chart fact. It does not show that the readings are right. Only practising astrologers and the people themselves can show that.

**Milestone one.** Readings that follow the classical rules of Vedic astrology completely and without abridgement, confirmed by blind review from practising astrologers. "Investors should treat accuracy engineering as co-equal with GTM." Until that review is done we hold to one rule: "No accuracy claims in marketing until benchmark is advisor-validated."

**Milestone two.** A success rate we can publish: how often reviewers judge a reading sound, and how often people confirm what a reading told them. **Planned.**

**What we know is wrong today.** We list these because an investor will find them.

- **Speed.** A full You reading took 35 seconds when it worked, and two runs failed after several minutes (8 November). Building the cards one by one took 275 seconds (10 November). Our target is 15 seconds.
- **Chart defects.** Four open defects can produce a wrong reading: one annual period table is wrong, some birth dates can be read with day and month swapped, a time-zone fallback can give a wrong chart, and a few values are still worked out in the wrong part of the system.
- **Security and privacy.** An internal security review found 12 issues: 4 high, 7 medium and 1 low. Personal data is not yet encrypted field by field.
- **Capacity.** The current hosting allows 10 requests to run at once, and the database holds about 150 to 200 charts. Both are set up for testing, not for a launch.
- **Reliability.** In the past three weeks: sign-up broke on an email limit, a bulk job filled the database and took the service down, and the model account ran out of credit on 13 November, which stops live readings until it is topped up.

**Where we stand against them.** We are a new entrant with no customers. We do not claim the field is empty, and we expect every one of these companies to compete on every feature we build.

**Why a person might choose AdAstra AI, in terms of what the product does today.**

- **It starts from the person, not from a blank chat box.** The dashboards tell a person where they stand before they ask anything.

- **It is one companion, not a marketplace.** There is no meter running by the minute and no choosing between astrologers.
- **Every card is fed by its own technique, which we can study and correct.** That is a method, and it is hard to see from outside.

None of this has been tested with customers. Whether it is enough is what the first cohorts will show.

## 10. What stands in the way: adoption

People will pay a learned astrologer as much as $300 for an hour: two well-known Vedic astrologers publish rates of $270 to $405 for an hour and $245 to $345 for 90 minutes. In our experience, many come away with only one to three questions answered, and the feeling that the session fell short. The same people still hesitate to pay for a reading produced by AI. That hesitation is the real barrier, for us and for every company in this field.

We expect adoption to be slow until we can publish a success rate for our readings. Until that record is public, we ask no one to take accuracy on trust.

A published record does two things. It gives people a reason to try an AI reading. It also lets us price on what a reading is worth. We plan to launch at the prices below, and to raise them toward what the most expensive human astrologers charge as the published record grows. That is a plan. Nothing yet shows that a published record will support higher prices.

The operating milestone is $100,000 in monthly recurring revenue. It is a scale milestone, on a horizon of 24 to 30 months from financing. It is not what this round alone delivers.

**One way to reach it.** 1,800 US subscribers at $50 and 400 India subscribers at ₹2,499 come to $100,634 a month, at ₹94 to the dollar. That is 2,200 subscribers.

| Subscribers lost each month | New subscribers needed each month | Total to add over 18 months | Cost at $50 each | Cost at $100 each |

**What the stress test says.** The launch budget of $230,000 covers one case only: $50 a subscriber with 8% lost each month. In every other case it does not buy this on paid acquisition alone. The cost of acquiring a subscriber is an assumption; we have no data.

## 17. Risks, and the standards we hold

| Risk | What we do about it |
| --- | --- |
| **The readings are wrong.** A practitioner review may find the readings unsound. | It is milestone one, before any marketing spend. Each technique can be corrected on its own. |
| **A calculation defect.** An error in the chart makes every reading built on it wrong. | Checks against reference charts; calculation is ordinary software and can be tested exactly. |
| **Model error.** An AI model states something the chart does not support. | The audit removes unsupported claims. It cannot catch a wrong interpretation of a true fact. |
| **Speed.** A reading that takes minutes will not be used daily. | Readings are built ahead of time and kept; the 15-second target is a launch gate. |
| **Security and privacy.** Birth details and conversations are sensitive. | The four high findings and field encryption are fixed before any public release. |
| **One person.** The company depends on the founder. | Hiring is one of the three uses of this round. The work is documented as it is built. |
| **People do not come back.** AI apps lose subscribers faster than others. | A free daily reason to return; the model is tested to 15% monthly loss. |
| **People will not pay for AI readings.** | A published success rate; low-cost packs to make a first purchase easy. |
| **Larger competitors copy the product.** | Assume they will. Compete on method and on the published record. |
| **Store review.** Apple or Google may reject or delay the apps. | Weeks reserved for review; a reviewer account and demo. |
| **Model costs rise.** | Cost per reading is tracked; a known increase on 1 December is already in the estimate. |
| **Regulation and reliance.** Astrology is not a scientific method, and a published "success rate" may invite scrutiny. | Legal review of every public claim. The product is for reflection and planning. |

**Our standards.**

1. No accuracy claim before a practitioner has validated it.
2. One number per quantity, with a source and a date.
3. Every status carries a date.
4. We promise only what the stated team and money can deliver.
5. We never sell or infer sensitive personal signals.
