## Figures and claims to reconcile

- The original financing proposal was $1M–$1.5M in preferred equity (pp. 3, 30–31). The original six-month targets were 750 payers and about $12K MRR (pp. 19–20), equivalent to $16 per active subscriber. Treat these as historical plan assumptions. The revised commercial milestone is $100K MRR, or $1.2M annualized recurring revenue.
- The original uses competing market counts (cover, pp. 2 and 13), question allowances and prices (pp. 2 and 16), and Year 3 subscriber counts (pp. 14 and 26). Its six-month 750 payers and 10,000 registrations imply 7.5% if measured with the same denominator, while its assumption says 5% (pp. 19–20, 25).
- The original modeled $290 LTV and $20 blended diaspora CAC, a 14.5× ratio under those inputs (pp. 3, 17). The Year 3 revenue mix includes other revenue lines while the projection is also described as subscription-only (pp. 26, 29). Build a new model from regional pricing, cohort retention, discounts, platform fees, and service costs.
- Practitioner validation is a future milestone (pp. 21–23). Verify chart computation and evaluate interpretation quality separately. Treat dated competitor valuations, acquisition scenarios, and inferred life-event advertising (pp. 14, 21, 31–33) as historical proposals outside the base case. For advertising, study contextual placements by region and voluntarily chosen broad interests.
