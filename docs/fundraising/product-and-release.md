# Product and consumer release plan

November 2026 · Internal operating plan

## Start and launch goal

The signed-in web app has Panchang timing, Self, Love, Work, a Work-specific chat entry, a Kundli sidebar, and a Life timeline. Self has the latest dashboard design. Work and Love are next for the same design. The longer Life Reading, user-selected card topics, card-specific follow-ups, and response modes are in development. The product roadmap uses the web MVP for private testing and fundraising while iPhone, iPad and Android are built. The first public consumer campaign opens the US and India in the same release window after all three device experiences and both regional offers are approved and tested.

The launch product needs to answer three questions quickly: what is relevant today, what context helps me understand it, and what specific question should I ask next? Keep basic timing free. Let users choose which topics appear as cards and change them as their needs change. Each card gives an interpretation and visible chart context before opening a question composer. A follow-up can draw on the selected topic, relevant computed chart factors, and conversation details the user has chosen to retain. Offer a light check-in, a detailed Research mode, and a calmer mode for stressful moments. User-selected modes adjust tone and depth. They do not create an emotional profile.

## Six dashboard candidates

The North Indian chart format fixes the positions of the twelve houses, counted from the ascendant. The signs in those positions vary by chart. The houses below are traditional interpretive anchors. The same life topic can draw on several houses, chart factors and timing methods. The app organizes those factors into user-facing subjects. | --- | --- | --- |
| Home and Family | 4 home and roots, 2 family and resources | “Making room for family.” Ask about themes to consider while planning a move or family gathering. |
| Money Habits and Resources | 2 accumulated resources, 11 gains, 12 expenses | “A spending habit to review.” Reflect on budgeting priorities and compare practical milestones the user sets. Keep investment and credit decisions outside the assistant’s remit. |

| Routines and Wellbeing | 6 routines, service and obstacles, 1 self and vitality, 12 rest and retreat | “A routine to revisit.” Use reflection prompts and practical routines. Keep disease, clinical treatment, and crisis response outside astrology content. |
| Community and Life Transitions | 11 communities and aspirations, 8 transitions and shared resources | “A change in my support network.” Ask which priorities and practical support might be worth considering in a transition. |

Self covers 1 as a primary anchor, Love covers 5 and 7 for romance and partnership, and Work covers 10 for vocation with relevant supporting houses. Together the candidates and current boards cover all twelve anchors. Default new boards to a common card design and share the same research and conversation components. Pick Home and Family plus Travel and Learning for the first post-launch tests. Verify demand before building the other four. Treat new boards as potential US monthly add-ons or India one-time 30-day passes with separately priced questions, subject to the regional offer tests. Show a preview and the exact paid benefit before the purchase prompt.

## Life Projects

Create private visual boards inspired by pinning and collecting. A project starts with a goal, an optional target date, and a template such as a vacation, promotion, business milestone, engagement or wedding. Users can save inspiration links and notes, collect relevant dashboard cards and traditional timing windows, and turn them into editable milestones. The AI astrologer helps compare dates, surface the chart factors it used, and suggest practical questions or steps. It distinguishes an astrological interpretation from a calendar constraint or a fact supplied by the user.

Begin with a small pilot for trip planning and engagement or wedding planning. The first version supports creating, pinning, rearranging, editing and archiving a board. It keeps dates and action items under user control and uses one project-scoped conversation. Users can remove items and delete a project. Sharing is a later, opt-in feature with explicit review of what is shared. Keep projects separate from advertising data. Use original product design, naming and artwork. The goal is to test whether users return to update a board and discuss a decision over time. ## Mobile delivery and launch gates

| Stage | Work and evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–4 · Scope and prototype | Choose one cross-platform client with native navigation and store integration, connected to the existing web service. Build a vertical slice of sign-in, time and place capture, free Panchang and a single card to chat flow. Design iPad for split-view navigation and reading, and verify a low-cost Android device on a slow connection. Start Apple and Google developer setup and check available release controls. |
| Weeks 5–12 · Private device builds | Deliver iPhone and iPad through TestFlight, Android through Play testing. Cover accurate birth time and time-zone handling, accessibility, localized USD and INR offers, store payments, cancellation and account deletion. Retest with synthetic charts and both storefronts. Verify how stored chart and chat context resume across web and native. |
| Weeks 13–20 · Beta and review | Invite both regions to test the same core workflow. Test failed and retried purchases, duplicate transactions, subscription renewal and cancellation, credit balances after reinstall, refunds, deletion, error and offline states. Submit with a review account or demo mode, sample data, and notes explaining the distinctive Panchang, chart context, and dashboard to chat flows. Reserve several weeks for review and fixes. |
| Weeks 21–24 · Release candidate and earliest launch | Coordinate Apple and Google availability in both regions. Give marketing the go-ahead when the store pages, purchase SKUs and first-run flow work on iPhone, iPad and Android in both countries. Ramp campaigns and new-user volume in both regions together. Have support, on-call ownership, cost limits and server-side rollback controls ready. |

Treat the 24-week schedule as the earliest delivery path. The financing plan budgets public launch in months 6–9. The release gate is working functionality and both store approvals. For purchases of digital content in the native apps, default to StoreKit and Google Play Billing. Configure distinct regional products with a shared account entitlement record, verified store transactions, idempotent grants and refund reversals. Keep purchased question credits nonexpiring, and make subscription renewal and cancellation terms explicit. Validate any web or alternative billing arrangement separately before adding it. Offer in-app account deletion and the Google-required web request path, complete App Privacy and Play Data safety disclosures, and test support and deletion end to end. Apple's minimum functionality and spam guidelines make a differentiated native experience and credible reviewer demo important. [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) · [Apple TestFlight](https://developer.apple.com/testflight/) · [Google Play payments](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en) · [Google Play testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en) · [Google Play account deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en)

Use internal beta targets of at least 50 activated testers per region, at least 99.5% crash-free beta sessions by platform, zero open blockers on the critical journeys, and successful purchase and deletion acceptance tests in both markets. These are release criteria, not forecasts. Report activation from first completed timing check or card-to-chat session, and track 7- and 30-day repeat use, US renewal, India pack repurchase, paid conversion, refunds, and cost per answer. Hold acquisition spend if users cannot complete the main flow or observed contribution fails to cover channel cost within the agreed test period.

## Partner API and sponsorship

Prototype a read-only partner API for location-and-date Panchang data and embeddable educational content. Give two design partners a synthetic-data sandbox and a short paid pilot. Define versioned responses, rate limits, uptime expectations and attribution. Consumer chart and conversation storage is outside the partner API. Evaluate an explicit, narrowly scoped user-authorized flow separately before offering personalized partner functions.

Start with contextual sponsorship inventory rather than a user data product. Example placements include a travel publication sponsoring a public travel-planning guide in India and an events partner sponsoring a public calendar in the US. Advertisers select broad storefront region, language, public placement and campaign dates. The app can rotate approved creatives using those public page attributes. The advertiser receives aggregate impression and click counts above a minimum reporting threshold, with sparse cells suppressed. Scope the first pilot to direct sponsorships and fixed prices. No third-party ad SDK, tracking identifier, personal chart, private chats, project content, location precision, stress mode, or inferred feelings or priorities belongs in the ad system. Obtain legal and store-policy review and validate reporting isolation and minimum cohort sizes before expanding.
