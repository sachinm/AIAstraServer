# AdAstra AI: Executive Summary

November 2026 | Draft for founder approval | Confidential

**Your personal Vedic astrologer, for everyday questions.**

AdAstra AI is a new entrant with no customers yet, in a market where the competition is real. We are raising $2.0M to do three things we cannot do without investment: prove our AI method of reading, win adoption of a lifestyle app, and hire people. This summary tells the story in order: the need, why only a few people have ever had it met, why AI changes that, what we have built, what stands in the way, and what we ask for.

## 1. An old need

"People are not looking for horoscopes. They are looking for answers."

People have always looked to the sky before the events of their lives: a job, a marriage, a move, a child, the right day to begin. That has not changed in thousands of years. What they find today is uneven. "The astrology market is fragmented across apps, websites, software tools, YouTube personalities, marketplaces, and independent practitioners. As a result, users frequently seek multiple readings from multiple astrologers to validate previous opinions. The same questions are asked repeatedly, often generating conflicting answers and additional expense."

## 2. Why only a few have ever had a full reading

The limit has never been curiosity. It has been the reader.

A full reading asks three things of an astrologer. They must know the original sources complete, and not an abridged or second-hand version. They must work through the whole chart, which for one birth moment comes to about 45,000 records. And they must apply every rule as written, however hard the chart looks, without softening what it shows.

Very few people can do all three, and none can do it for every client and every question. "Many practitioners cannot consistently apply the full depth of the tradition during every consultation." So a careful reading has been the life's work of a few astrologers, serving the very rich. Everyone else has made do with less, and much of what now passes for astrology is internet shorthand, such as the "Mercury retrograde" scare, far removed from the source texts.

## 3. Why this is a problem made for superintelligence

AI is now set to work on hard problems in mathematics, where the task is to hold a large body of rules in mind and apply each one without error. A Vedic reading is a problem of the same shape. The rules are written down. The chart is computed. What was missing was a reader with the whole source, unlimited patience and no reason to flatter.

"For the first time, AI can synthesize vast amounts of astrological data, classical rules, chart relationships, and historical context simultaneously on a personalized basis for every individual." A system can read every chart the same way, "applying the same diligence, preparation, and analytical rigor to every user regardless of income or location." It does not tire, it does not skip a rule, and it has no interest in telling a person what they hope to hear.

This is our thesis, and it is not yet a result. Today's general AI models do not read a chart this way on their own. That is the work described in sections 4 and 5, and the first milestone in section 9.

Elon Musk has said humanity should "become a spacefaring civilization, a multi-planet species" (CNBC, April 2021). Our own view is the other half of that thought. A civilization reaching for other planets should carry the best of its Eastern knowledge traditions with it, correctly and from the source. We want a reading of the sky to become an ordinary part of life: checked before a contract is signed, a first meeting, a journey or a new venture, as easily as people now check the weather.

## 5. How we make each reading follow the rules

"Before every answer, AdAstra performs structured chart research similar to the preparation process of an experienced astrologer."

This is where most of the work has gone, and it is where we intend to compete.

- **Every dashboard card is fed by its own technique.** A technique is a written contract: which parts of the chart it reads, under which classical rules, and what it returns. Each can be studied, corrected and tuned on its own.
- **A techniques lab** runs one technique on one chart and shows what went in, what each step did and what came out.
- **A research lab** holds sets of synthetic charts across age groups, so a technique can be examined across many lives and not only one.
- **An automated audit** checks that every claim in a reading points at a computed chart fact.
- **AI does the research.** AI agents write the techniques, run them, audit the results and fix what fails, under the founder's direction, and then the cycle repeats. As people confirm what was right, those confirmations are designed to become calibration points.

## 6. We are not alone: the competition is real

"Most astrology products fall into one of two categories: Horoscope and content apps [and] Pay-per-session astrologer marketplaces." Both are large and established, and several are already adopting AI.

- **Human astrologers** remain the standard people trust, and the service people already pay for.
- **Astrotalk** is a leading marketplace for them. It reported revenue of ₹1,214 crore for FY25, up 85%. Its home page lists astrologers at ₹34 to ₹82 a minute, which is ₹340 to ₹820 for ten minutes.
- **AstroSage** now sells AI astrologers alongside human ones. Its site says the AI astrologers have answered 10 crore questions.
- **Co-Star** says it uses "NASA data, real human astrologers, and AI technology". In July 2026 it was reported to have been acquired by Midjourney, an AI company.

We are a new entrant with no customers. We do not claim the field is empty, and we expect every one of these companies to compete on every feature we build. Our bet is focus and method. One personal companion for everyday questions, where every dashboard card is produced by its own written technique and every claim is checked against the computed chart. We study and correct those techniques in our own research tools, one at a time.

## 7. What stands in the way: adoption will be slow until our results are public

In our experience, people will pay a learned astrologer as much as $300 for an hour. Many come away with only one to three questions answered, and the feeling that the session fell short. The same people still hesitate to pay for a reading produced by AI. That hesitation is the real barrier, for us and for every company in this field.

We expect adoption to be slow until we can publish a success rate for our readings: how often practising astrologers, reviewing blind, judge a reading sound, and how often people confirm what a reading told them. Until that record is public, we ask no one to take accuracy on trust.

A published record does two things. It gives people a reason to try an AI reading. It also lets us price on what a reading is worth. We plan to launch at the prices in section 10, and to raise them toward what the most expensive human astrologers charge as the published record grows. Earning that record takes time, people and money, which is why adoption cannot be won by engineering alone.

## 8. Where we stand today

We are a new entrant. There are no customers, no revenue, no outside funding and no hires. Practising astrologers have not yet reviewed the readings, so we make no claim of accuracy. Payments and mobile apps are not built. What exists was built by one founder directing AI agents: the chart computation, the reading pipeline, three dashboards, lucky times, and the research tooling to improve them.

## 9. What we ask for, and why

We cannot take this further without investment. One founder with AI agents can build a product. That is not enough to prove the method with outside experts, to put the product in people's hands, or to compete with companies of this size. We are raising $2.0M in preferred equity, for 18 months, to do three things.

1. **Prove the AI method of reading, and publish the result.** Our first milestone is readings that follow the classical rules of Vedic astrology completely and without abridgement, confirmed by blind review from practising astrologers, with a success rate we can publish.
2. **Win adoption of the lifestyle app.** People must first become comfortable paying for an AI reading. That means iPhone, iPad and Android apps, payments, and a release in the US and India, starting with invited users and a capped test of creators, search and app-store ads.
3. **Hire people.** The first engineers, product design and quality, and astrologers as reviewers. Today there is one person.

## 10. The business, and where the story goes

Everyone starts free. The catalog is Plus at $50 or ₹2,499 a month and Unlimited at $200 or ₹9,999, with question packs from ₹99 planned for India. "Most astrology companies market astrology. AdAstra markets answers." The operating milestone is $100,000 in monthly recurring revenue. One scenario reaches it with 1,800 US and 400 India subscribers, on a horizon of 24 to 30 months from financing. It is a scale milestone, not what this round alone delivers.

**The vision.** Astrology is the first layer. The same companion can grow into how people plan their lives, understand their relationships and find the people they are compatible with, with a careful reading folded into every event that matters to them. "Technology changes. Human behavior does not."
