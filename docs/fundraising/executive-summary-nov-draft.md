# AdAstra AI: Executive Summary

November 2026 | Version 3, draft for founder approval | Confidential

**Your personal Vedic astrologer, for everyday questions.**

AdAstra AI is building a layer for everyday life: a place a person checks each day, alongside their own judgement, to see what the sky says about what is coming and how to prepare for it. We are a new entrant with no customers, in a market where the competition is real. We are raising $2.0M.

## The need is old

"People are not looking for horoscopes. They are looking for answers." People have looked to the sky before a job, a marriage, a journey or a new venture for thousands of years. They still do, and they pay for it again and again, because the answers they get conflict.

## Only a few have ever had a full reading

A full Vedic reading asks three things of the reader: the complete original sources, the whole chart, and every rule applied as written, however hard the chart looks. One birth moment yields about 45,000 records. "Many practitioners cannot consistently apply the full depth of the tradition during every consultation." So a careful reading has been the work of a few astrologers serving the very rich. Everyone else gets internet shorthand, such as the "Mercury retrograde" scare, far removed from the source.

## It is a problem made for superintelligence

Nobody learns assembly language to use a computer. Astrology has never had that layer. In July 2025 an AI system solved five of the six problems at the International Mathematical Olympiad (Google DeepMind): sustained, exact reasoning over many steps. A reading asks for the same quality, and a machine has no reason to tire, skip a rule or flatter. "For the first time, AI can synthesize vast amounts of astrological data, classical rules, chart relationships, and historical context simultaneously on a personalized basis for every individual."

This is our thesis, and it is not yet a result. Today's general models do not read a chart this way on their own. Making them do it is our work.

Elon Musk has said humanity should "become a spacefaring civilization, a multi-planet species" (CNBC, April 2021). Our view is the other half of that thought: such a civilization should carry the best of its Eastern knowledge with it, correctly and from the source.

## What we have built

"Most AI products begin with a blank conversation. AdAstra begins with a structured model of a person's life."

- **Dashboards.** You, Love and Work each open as a reading written for one person, with dates and reasons.
- **Daily timing.** Built on the Panchang. A person names a plan, and sees the good and the difficult hours for it.
- **Conversation.** Ask the AI about any card, or ask anything freely.

No human astrologer is in the loop. Software computes the chart, and written techniques and AI agents produce the readings.

## How we make each reading follow the rules

Every dashboard card is fed by its own written technique, which we can study, correct and tune on its own. A techniques lab runs one technique on one chart. A research lab runs it across many synthetic lives. An automated audit traces every claim to a computed chart fact. AI agents do this research in a loop, under the founder's direction.

In a benchmark on 8 November 2026, 30 dashboard readings on 10 test charts passed that audit, at a total model cost of $0.35.

## Where we stand

There are no customers, no revenue, no outside funding and no hires. Practising astrologers have not yet reviewed the readings, so we make no claim of accuracy. Payments and mobile apps are not built, and readings are still too slow: 35 seconds or more against a target of 15. One founder built what exists, directing AI agents.

The competition is real. Human astrologers are the standard people trust. Astrotalk reported revenue of ₹1,214 crore in FY25. AstroSage sells AI astrologers. Co-Star was acquired by Midjourney, an AI company, in 2026. All will compete with us.

## What stands in the way

People will pay a learned astrologer as much as $300 for an hour, and in our experience often come away with one to three questions answered. They still hesitate to pay for an AI reading. We expect adoption to be slow until we can publish a success rate for our readings. A published record gives people a reason to try, and lets us raise prices toward what the best human astrologers charge.

## The ask

$2.0M in preferred equity, for 18 months, to do three things we cannot do without investment.

1. **Prove the AI method of reading, and publish the result.** Blind review by practising astrologers comes first.
2. **Win adoption of the lifestyle app.** iPhone, iPad and Android apps, payments, and a release in the US and India.
3. **Hire people.** Engineers, design and quality, and astrologers as reviewers.

Everyone starts free. Plus is planned at $50 or ₹2,499 a month, with question packs from ₹99 in India. The operating milestone is $100,000 in monthly recurring revenue, on a horizon of 24 to 30 months from financing. It is a scale milestone, not what this round alone delivers.

**Where it goes.** Astrology is the first layer. The same companion can grow into how people plan their lives and understand their relationships. "Technology changes. Human behavior does not."
