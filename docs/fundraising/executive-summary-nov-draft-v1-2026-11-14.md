# AdAstra AI: Executive Summary

November 2026 | Draft for founder approval | Confidential

**Your personal Vedic astrologer, for everyday questions.**

AdAstra AI is a new entrant with no customers yet, in a market where the competition is real. We are raising $2.0M to do three things we cannot do without investment: prove our AI method of reading, win adoption of a lifestyle app, and hire people.

## The need

"People are not looking for horoscopes. They are looking for answers."

People have always looked to the sky for guidance on work, marriage, family and timing. What they find today is uneven. "The astrology market is fragmented across apps, websites, software tools, YouTube personalities, marketplaces, and independent practitioners. As a result, users frequently seek multiple readings from multiple astrologers to validate previous opinions. The same questions are asked repeatedly, often generating conflicting answers and additional expense."

The limit has never been curiosity. It has been preparation. A careful reading means working through a person's whole chart, and "many practitioners cannot consistently apply the full depth of the tradition during every consultation."

## Why now

"For the first time, AI can synthesize vast amounts of astrological data, classical rules, chart relationships, and historical context simultaneously on a personalized basis for every individual." The preparation that once limited a reading to those who could pay for an expert's time can now be done for every question, at a price most people can afford.

This could not have been built before. One birth moment produces about 45,000 records of chart data. No person can work through that for every client and every question. A system can, "applying the same diligence, preparation, and analytical rigor to every user regardless of income or location."

Elon Musk has said humanity should "become a spacefaring civilization, a multi-planet species" (CNBC, April 2021). Our own view is the other half of that thought. A civilization reaching for other planets should carry the best of its Eastern knowledge traditions into everyday life. A careful reading of the sky has been the life's work of a few astrologers serving the very rich. AdAstra AI exists to make it available to everyone.

## The competition is real

"Most astrology products fall into one of two categories: Horoscope and content apps [and] Pay-per-session astrologer marketplaces." Both are large and established, and several are already adopting AI.

- **Human astrologers** remain the standard people trust, and the service people already pay for.
- **Astrotalk** is a leading marketplace for them. It reported revenue of ₹1,214 crore for FY25, up 85%. Its home page lists astrologers at ₹34 to ₹82 a minute.
- **AstroSage** now sells AI astrologers alongside human ones. Its site says the AI astrologers have answered 10 crore questions.
- **Co-Star** says it uses "NASA data, real human astrologers, and AI technology". In July 2026 it was reported to have been acquired by Midjourney, an AI company.

We are a new entrant with no customers. We do not claim the field is empty, and we expect every one of these companies to compete on every feature we build. Our bet is focus and method. One personal companion for everyday questions, where every dashboard card is produced by its own written technique and every claim is checked against the computed chart. We study and correct those techniques in our own research tools, one at a time.

## Adoption will be slow until our results are public

In our experience, people will pay a learned astrologer as much as $300 for an hour. Many come away with only one to three questions answered, and the feeling that the session fell short. The same people still hesitate to pay for a reading produced by AI. That hesitation is the real barrier, for us and for every company in this field.

We expect adoption to be slow until we can publish a success rate for our readings: how often practising astrologers, reviewing blind, judge a reading sound, and how often people confirm what a reading told them. Until that record is public, we ask no one to take accuracy on trust.

A published record does two things. It gives people a reason to try an AI reading. It also lets us price on what a reading is worth. We plan to launch at the prices below, and to raise them toward what the most expensive human astrologers charge as the published record grows. Earning that record takes time, people and money, which is why adoption cannot be won by engineering alone.

## What AdAstra AI is

A lifestyle companion for everyday questions, built to earn the confidence of ordinary people one answer at a time. It is for reflection and planning. It does not promise outcomes.

"Most AI products begin with a blank conversation. AdAstra begins with a structured model of a person's life."

- **Registration.** A person gives a birth date, time and place. The chart is computed once.
- **Dashboards.** You, Love and Work each open as a reading written for that one person.
- **Life Reading.** A longer reading of purpose and direction, now in development.
- **Conversation.** Ask the AI about any card on a dashboard, or ask anything freely.
- **Daily timing.** A free daily check that gives a reason to return.

No human astrologer is in the loop. Software computes the chart, and AI agents produce every reading. The product itself was built by one founder directing AI agents.

## How every answer is prepared

"Before every answer, AdAstra performs structured chart research similar to the preparation process of an experienced astrologer."

Each answer draws on the records that bear on the question, out of about 45,000 computed for the chart. We estimate the model cost of one answer at 6 to 12 cents. At the planned prices a customer who uses a full monthly allowance would pay about $0.50 a question in the US, and about ₹60 a question in India on a five-question pack. A human session at the rates above costs ₹340 to ₹820 for ten minutes. The prices are planned and not yet tested.

## Research, done by AI, in a loop

This is where most of the work has gone, and it is where we intend to compete.

- **Every dashboard card is fed by its own technique.** A technique is a written contract: which parts of the chart it reads, under which classical rules, and what it returns. Each can be studied, corrected and tuned on its own.
- **A techniques lab** runs one technique on one chart and shows what went in, what each step did and what came out.
- **A research lab** holds sets of synthetic charts across age groups, so a technique can be examined across many lives and not only one.
- **An automated audit** checks that every claim in a reading points at a computed chart fact.
- **AI does the research.** AI agents write the techniques, run them, audit the results and fix what fails, under the founder's direction, and then the cycle repeats. As people confirm what was right, those confirmations are designed to become calibration points.

Our first milestone is readings that follow the classical rules of Vedic astrology completely and without abridgement, confirmed by blind review from practising astrologers.

## Where we stand

We are a new entrant. There are no customers, no revenue, no outside funding and no hires. Practising astrologers have not yet reviewed the readings, so we make no claim of accuracy. Payments and mobile apps are not built. What exists was built by one founder directing AI agents: the chart computation, the reading pipeline, three dashboards, and the research tooling to improve them.

## Why we need investment

We cannot take this further without it. One founder with AI agents can build a product. That is not enough to prove the method with outside experts, to put the product in people's hands, or to compete with companies of this size. We are raising $2.0M in preferred equity, for 18 months, to do three things.

1. **Prove the AI method of reading, and publish the result.** Blind review of the readings by practising astrologers, the research loop that acts on what they find, and a success rate we can publish. This is the first milestone.
2. **Win adoption of the lifestyle app.** People must first become comfortable paying for an AI reading. That means iPhone, iPad and Android apps, payments, and a release in the US and India, starting with invited users and a capped test of creators, search and app-store ads.
3. **Hire people.** The first engineers, product design and quality, and astrologers as reviewers. Today there is one person.

## Business

Everyone starts free. The catalog is Plus at $50 or ₹2,499 a month and Unlimited at $200 or ₹9,999, with question packs from ₹99 planned for India. "Most astrology companies market astrology. AdAstra markets answers." The operating milestone is $100,000 in monthly recurring revenue. One scenario reaches it with 1,800 US and 400 India subscribers, on a horizon of 24 to 30 months from financing. It is a scale milestone, not what this round alone delivers.

**The vision.** Astrology is the first layer. The same companion can grow into how people plan their lives, understand their relationships and find the people they are compatible with. "Technology changes. Human behavior does not."
