# Bhava analysis method (bhāva vicāra)

> Bhava analysis is the core natal judgement. It decides whether a house (a life area) is promised, how strongly, and in what tone. Five factors are read for that house:
> 1. the house itself (sign, occupants, aspects received);
> 2. its lord (placement, dignity, strength, associations);
> 3. its karaka (natural significator);
> 4. derived houses (bhavat bhavam);
> 5. confirmation from the same house counted from the Moon and from the karaka.
>
> Every Parashari school uses this. All other techniques (yogas, strengths, vargas, dashas) are refinements of it.

## 1. Sources
- **BPHS ch. 11 "Judgement of houses"** (Santhanam numbering): a house prospers when aspected or joined by its lord or benefics, and declines when its lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th or afflicted (R; chapter title and rule recalled, verse numbers not checked).
- **BPHS ch. 12-23**: the effects of each house, 1st to 12th (R).
- **BPHS ch. 24 "Effects of the bhava lords"**: the lord of house X placed in house Y, 144 combinations (R). In BPHS-GCS this is ch. 27 (V, wisdomlib table of contents: https://de.wisdomlib.org/shop/books/jyotisha/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra).
- **BPHS ch. 32 "Karakas"**: the natural significators of houses (R).
- **BPHS ch. 34 "Yoga karakas"**: functional nature by lordship (kendra and trikona lords, kendradhipati dosha, the 8th lord) (V that the chapter exists in BPHS-ET; rules R).
- **Phaladeepika ch. 15 "Bhava chinta"** (V. Subrahmanya Sastri 1950), all (R; PDF was 403 this session):
  - judging a house from the lord, karaka and occupants;
  - a house is strong when its lord is in a kendra or trikona, in its own or exaltation sign, or aspected by benefics;
  - it is weak when its lord is in a dusthana, combust, debilitated or with malefics;
  - also the derived-house rule.
- **Bhavat bhavam** (bhāvāt bhāvam): the house as many houses from itself as it is from the lagna (e.g. the 9th from the 9th = the 5th, for father's fortune). Widely attributed to Phaladeepika ch. 15 and Uttara Kalamrita (R; verse not located, U).
- **Karako bhava nashaya** (kārako bhāva nāśāya): a karaka placed in its own house harms that house (e.g. Jupiter in the 5th for children, Venus in the 7th for spouse, Saturn in the 8th not harmful for longevity). Quoted as an adage. No BPHS verse was located (U). Tag [COMM]/[MODERN], with Mantreswara and Kalidasa-tradition attributions unverified.

## 2. When it applies
- Every question about any life area, natal first.
- Apply it in D1, then confirm the same house in the relevant varga (file 11) and from the Moon (Chandra lagna).

## 3. Inputs required
For the house H being judged:
- the sign in H and its lord L;
- occupants of H, with dignity, strength and functional nature;
- graha drishti on H and on L;
- L's house placement from the lagna and from H, plus its dignity, combustion, retrogression, Shadbala ratio, Vimshopaka and avastha;
- associations of L (conjunction, mutual aspect, exchange);
- the karaka of H, with its placement, dignity and strength;
- the same factors for H counted from the Moon and for the karaka-as-lagna;
- the dispositor of L;
- Bhava bala rank;
- the functional benefic/malefic/neutral list for this lagna;
- yogas involving H or L.

## 4. Calculation / rule
1. **House state**. Are there benefic or malefic occupants? Benefic or malefic aspects? Is the lord aspecting its own house (a strong plus)?
2. **Lord placement from the lagna**:
   - kendra or trikona: strong;
   - 2nd or 11th: good for wealth;
   - 3rd or 6th: effort or struggle;
   - 6th, 8th or 12th: the house suffers.

   One exception: lords of dusthanas in dusthanas can give Viparita results (file 19).
3. **Lord placement from H** (bhavat bhavam view). L in the 6th, 8th or 12th *from H* also weakens H.
4. **Lord's dignity and strength**: exalted, MT or own beats friend, which beats neutral, which beats enemy or debilitated. Also read combustion, defeat in war, Shadbala ratio, Vimshopaka and Baladi.
5. **Lord's associations**:
   - with functional benefics and yogakarakas: up;
   - with the lords of the 6th, 8th or 12th, or Rahu and Ketu: down.
6. **Karaka**:
   - strong and well placed: confirms;
   - afflicted: denies or delays.

   The karaka in the very house it signifies can harm that house (karako bhava nashaya [COMM]). This applies especially to Jupiter in the 5th, Venus in the 7th and the Sun in the 9th.
7. **Derived houses**: read H from H (bhavat bhavam). For a relative's matters, use the relative's house as a new lagna (e.g. the 3rd from the 7th for the spouse's siblings).
8. **Moon and karaka views**: repeat 1-6 with the Moon as lagna. A result confirmed from both the lagna and the Moon is firm.
9. **Verdict**:
   - Promise = (house state, lord state, karaka state).
   - All three good: strong promise.
   - Two of three: moderate.
   - One or none: weak, delayed or denied.

   Dashas and transits time only what is promised.

Strength from the lord's placement [TEXT, Phaladeepika 15 (R)]:

| Lord in (from lagna) | Effect on its house |
|---|---|
| Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) | Flourishes |
| Trikona (1, 5, 9) | Flourishes, with fortune |
| 2, 11 | Gainful |
| 3 | Moderate, through effort |
| 6, 8, 12 | Suffers, unless Viparita or own sign |

## 5. Interpretation rules
- [TEXT] A house aspected or occupied by its own lord, or by benefics, prospers (BPHS 11, R; Phaladeepika 15, R).
- [TEXT] A house whose lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th, debilitated or combust, and which is aspected by malefics without benefic aspect, is destroyed in its significations (BPHS 11, R).
- [TEXT] Lords of trikonas are always auspicious. Natural benefics owning kendras lose their beneficence (kendradhipati dosha). Lords of the 3rd, 6th and 11th are malefic. The 8th lord is malefic unless it also owns the lagna (BPHS 34, R).
- [TEXT] A planet owning both a kendra and a trikona is a yogakaraka (BPHS 34, R).
- [COMM] Bhavat bhavam: the house as far from H as H is from the lagna also signifies H's matters (R).
- [COMM/MODERN] Karako bhava nashaya: a karaka in its own house harms that house (U for a classical verse). K.N. Rao and SJC both use it with exceptions (e.g. Saturn in the 8th gives long life).
- [MODERN] K.N. Rao: never predict an event from one factor. At least three confirmations are needed: lagna, Moon and the relevant varga.

## 6. Variants and disputes
- **Whole sign vs bhava chalit (cusp) occupancy**: SJC and K.N. Rao use whole sign for occupancy; Raman and KP-influenced readers use cusps. The engine should give both, with a shift flag.
- **Moon vs Sun as secondary lagnas**: BPHS and others add Surya lagna for some matters.
- **Rahu and Ketu lordship** (Aquarius and Scorpio co-lords) is disputed. See file 04.
- **Karako bhava nashaya scope**: some limit it to Jupiter in the 5th and Venus in the 7th. Others apply it to all karakas.

## 7. Worked micro-example
- Career (10th) for a Leo lagna. The 10th is Taurus; its lord Venus is in the 9th (trikona) in Aries.
- Venus is in Aries, a neutral sign: Dina in Deeptadi, Swapna in Jagradadi. The Sun (the 10th karaka, also lagna lord) is exalted in Aries with Venus.
- House state: Jupiter aspects the 10th from the 2nd.
- Two of three factors are strong, so career is firmly promised. Venus as a neutral, combust-prone lord tempers it. Check combustion (file 05).

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra
- **The engine must emit a "house card" per house per chart** (D1, Moon chart, and key vargas), with:
  - sign, lord, occupants (with dignity), and aspects received (with sphuta drishti);
  - the lord's house from the lagna and from H, plus its dignity, combustion, war, Shadbala ratio, Vimshopaka, Baladi and Jagradadi;
  - lord associations (conjunction, mutual aspect, exchange);
  - the karaka set with each karaka's state;
  - the `karaka_in_own_house` flag;
  - Bhava bala rank;
  - the functional nature of each planet for this lagna;
  - the cusp-shift flag;
  - bhavat bhavam: the derived house number.
- **Precomputed summary flags**:
  - `lord_in_dusthana`;
  - `lord_aspects_house`;
  - `benefic_aspect_count` and `malefic_aspect_count`;
  - `confirmed_from_moon`.

  Each flag carries a text tag.
- **Board use**: this is the backbone of `general.promise` and of the Self, Love and Work boards. Every board sub-area maps to a house set.
- **Weight**: primary. Yogas and strengths modify it; they do not replace it.
- **Pitfalls**:
  - Do not let the model derive "lord in the 6th from H". The engine sends it.
  - Keep functional nature per lagna in the engine, not as a Node lookup.
