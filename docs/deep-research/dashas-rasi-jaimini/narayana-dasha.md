# Narayana dasa (nārāyaṇa daśā), also Padakrama dasa; rasi chart and divisional charts

> The SJC school's main general-purpose sign dasa. It starts from the stronger of lagna and the 7th, moves in one of
> three patterns depending on whether that sign is movable (regular), fixed (every 6th) or dual (kendra-trine
> jumps), and gives each sign years by the count-to-lord rule. Read in D1 it shows the "progressed lagna"; computed for
> a varga (D9, D10, D4, D7, D12, D24...) it times that varga's matters. The sutras behind the sequence (Jaimini
> 2.4.7-10) are textual; the name "Narayana" and the varga method are Rath's and PVR's school. Widely used in SJC and
> Jagannatha Hora; little used outside it.

## 1. Sources
- [TEXT] JS-BSR 2.4.7 (start from stronger of 1st/7th), 2.4.8 "prathame prak-pratyaktvam" (movable start -> regular),
  2.4.9 "dvitiye ravitah" (fixed -> 6th, 6th...; katapayadi "ravi" = 6), 2.4.10 (dual -> kendra/panapara/apoklima;
  BSR), 2.4.31-32 (odd/even of karaka or sign sets forward/back), 2.4.33 (Saturn, "some acharyas"), 2.4.34 (same for
  antardasas), 2.4.35-36 (good/bad results); 1.1.28 (years), 1.1.34 (antardasa). BSR calls these "Padakrama Dasas".
- [TEXT] BPHS 46.155-167 (years, footed groups, 9th-house direction), ch. 51 vv. 5-12 / BPHS-R 50.30-31 (antardasa).
- [MODERN] Rath, *Narayana Dasa* (Sagar) — rules (start, Trimurti sequences, 9th-house direction, Saturn and Ketu
  exceptions, years, dual lords, second cycle, antardasa, judgement); via repo note
  `research_notes/Sanjay Rath open method gaps/chara_dasha.md`.
- [MODERN] PVR-VA ch. 18 (18.2 computation, Table 40 sequences, 18.3 antardasa, 18.4 interpretation, 18.5 vargas,
  18.6 Sun-based sub-division).

## 2. When it applies
- [MODERN] Every chart; "phalita" (results) dasa for general events (PVR-VA 18.1). In D1 it is the progression of the
  lagna (or of the 7th if the 7th is the seed). Varga versions per matter: D4 residence/property, D9 marriage and
  married life, D10 career, D7 children, D12 parents, D24 learning, D16 vehicles/comforts (PVR-VA 18.5).
- [MODERN] Rath: shows the environment's impact on the native, while Vimsottari shows the native's response.

## 3. Inputs required
Rasi chart (or the target varga) with all nine planets; strength data for "stronger of two signs" (00-overview C4);
exaltation table incl. Rath's phalita node table (Rahu ex. Gemini, Ketu ex. Sagittarius; own signs Aq/Sc); for
vargas, the rasi-chart house numbered as the varga's "seed" and that lord's position in the varga.

## 4. Calculation
1. Seed = stronger of lagna and 7th (JS 2.4.7; Rath; PVR). [MODERN] Rath rejects "lagna for men / 7th for women".
2. Sequence by seed type [TEXT JS 2.4.8-10 / MODERN names]: movable -> 1,2,3...; fixed -> 1,6,11,4... (every 6th);
   dual -> 1,5,9 then 10,2,6 then 7,11,3 then 4,8,12 (PVR: trines of seed, trines of 10th, of 7th, of 4th).
3. Direction = footedness of the 9th from the seed: odd-footed forward, even-footed backward (BPHS 46.167; Rath; PVR).
4. Exceptions [MODERN Rath; PVR]: Saturn in the seed -> regular zodiacal order for any seed type (JS-R 2.4.33). Ketu
   in the seed -> reverse the direction, keep the jump pattern (JS-R 1.1.8).
5. Years = count from dasa sign to its lord (forward if odd-footed, backward if even-footed), minus one; own sign
   = 12; lord exalted +1, debilitated -1; maximum 12 (Rath, PVR-VA 18.2.2). Scorpio/Aquarius: BPHS 46.158-166 ladder
   (Rath: both in sign 12; both together elsewhere -> count to them; one in sign -> count to the other; else stronger
   lord; tie -> more years).
6. Second cycle: same order, years = 12 minus first-cycle years (Rath; PVR). A 12-year sign gives 0 in cycle two.
7. Antardasa [MODERN Rath, PVR-VA 18.3]: 12 equal parts; antardasa seed = stronger of dasa sign and its 7th; begin
   from the sign holding that seed's lord; count 1,2,3... (no jumps); forward if that start sign is odd, backward if
   even (plain odd/even). Saturn in the seed -> forward; Ketu -> reversed. Pratyantar and lower levels split the same
   way (PVR-VA 18.6).
8. Year length [MODERN PVR-VA 18.6]: equal arcs of the Sun's motion; a dasa year ends when the Sun returns to its natal
   longitude.
9. Varga Narayana [MODERN PVR-VA 18.5]: seed house for D-n = nth house (reduce by 12s: D16 -> 4th, D24 -> 12th,
   D30 -> 6th); take that house's lord in the rasi chart (stronger lord for Sc/Aq); the sign that lord occupies in
   the varga is treated as lagna; compute Narayana dasa on the varga as if it were a rasi chart. PVR warns the varga
   dasa is not a progression of the lagna, so do not read dasa sign as lagna there. Rath gives a "controlling house"
   per varga (e.g. 10th lord in D10).

## 5. Interpretation rules
- [TEXT] JS-BSR 2.4.35-36: benefic contact or a strong/exalted lord -> prosperity in that dasa.
- [TEXT] JS-BSR 2.4.2-6: dwara (dasa sign) and bahya sign; malefics there -> confinement, disease, unless in own sign
  or near Jupiter.
- [MODERN] Rath: read dasa sign and paka rasi (sign of its lord); count from the dasa sign as far as it is from lagna;
  health from Sun, AK and lagna; benefic/malefic argala (2nd/12th and 5th/9th) on dasa or paka rasi -> shubha/papa
  kartari; badhaka from dasa sign (11th movable, 9th fixed, 7th dual); chara karakas in kendras to the dasa sign
  strongest, aspecting karakas make their themes felt.
- [MODERN] PVR-VA 18.4: dasa lagna = dasa sign (or its 7th if seed was the 7th); natural malefics in 3rd/6th from it
  give success; benefics in trines and 8th give happiness; any planet in 11th gives gains; Rahu in 8th/12th fear;
  malefics in 4th discomfort; benefics in 2nd/5th fame; affliction to 7th from dasa lagna and paka rasi -> marital
  trouble. Natal references too: dasa of A10 (career), UL (marriage), 2nd/7th from UL (marital trouble), GL (power);
  antardasas aspected by GL bring promotions. Three-part split: sign rules first third, lord the second, occupants and
  aspecting planets the last.

## 6. Variants and disputes
- K.N. Rao and Iranganti do not use the name; Iranganti applies padakrama to "Chara dasa" with lagna oddity setting
  direction (SUT). K.N. Rao uses plain order (see chara-dasha.md).
- Antardasa start: Rath/PVR start from the lord's sign; a critic (Sutramritam "Shanmukha") holds JS 2.4.13 means start
  from the dasa sign itself; BPHS ch. 51 starts from the stronger of dasa sign and its 7th with padakrama jumps.
- ±1 for exaltation: Rath and PVR book yes; PVR-2014 (Parasara's Chara) no.
- Varga seed rule (nth house's lord) is PVR/SJC practice; no text read states it.

## 7. Worked micro-example (PVR-VA Example 63, paraphrased)
Lagna Taurus, Scorpio stronger -> seed Scorpio (fixed, every 6th). 9th from Scorpio = Cancer (even-footed) ->
backward. Order: Sc, Ge, Cp, Le, Pi, Li, Ta, Sg, Cn, Aq, Vi, Ar.
