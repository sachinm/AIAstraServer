# 12. Chart verification and birth-time rectification

How Rath checks that a chart is the right chart before he predicts from it, how his senior student disagrees, how the
consultation is run around that check, and what was not found. The product side (the questions themselves, their fact
keys and order) is in [13-user-questions.md](13-user-questions.md); the technique mapping is in
[14-technique-mapping.md](14-technique-mapping.md).

Main source: *Crux of Vedic Astrology: Timing of Events*, Ch. II sect. 8 "Birth Time Rectification", pp.34-39, read in
full from an OCR PDF, with worked Chart 7 (male, 23 May 1955; the OCR misprints the year once as 1995). Tier B is P.V.R.
Narasimha Rao (PVR), *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*, ch. 32, and Visti Larsen. Detail and links:
`research_notes/Sanjay Rath methods and precedence/verification_and_questions.md`.

Tiers: **[A]** Rath's own text · **[B]** his students and SJC teachers · **[C]** third party · **[Inferred]** our
reading of the sources, stated by no one. **[Sensitive]** marks rules about death, illness or loss; they are named here
as steps only, without their mechanics (see [15](15-sensitive-rules-and-gaps.md)).

## 1. Rath's worked order (Crux Ch. II sect. 8)

Rath states the principle on p.34 [A]: before the divisional charts are judged, rectify the birth time from the
Navamsa, the Dwadasamsa and other key vargas, then fine-tune with the vighati rule of the Jaimini Sutras. Chart 7 shows
the order in practice. Each step cuts the candidate range, and each is tested against a fact the native confirms aloud
before the next step starts.

| Step | What is fixed | Chart factor used | What the native was asked to confirm | Range after the step | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Rising sign | Rasi lagna sign against build, height and complexion | Average height, slim, dark: accepted Aquarius from a reported 1:30-2:00 AM window | Aquarius rising | [A] p.36 |
| 1a | Elder siblings | 11th from the Arudha Lagna (AL); direction set by odd or even AL | Eldest child; any elder sibling would not have survived **[Sensitive]** | — | [A] p.36 |
| 1b | Younger siblings | 3rd from AL, its lord and aspects | Many younger siblings, at least one short-lived **[Sensitive]** | — | [A] p.36 |
| 1c | Drekkana (D3) lagna | Two candidate D3 lagnas (Gemini, Libra) tested against the fate of the next younger sibling, then the sex of each later sibling read in sequence from successive lords | The next sibling's early death **[Sensitive]**; the brother/sister sequence | Aquarius 20° to 28°06' | [A] pp.36-37 |
| 2 | Navamsa (D9) lagna | Upapada (UL) and the 2nd from the 7th as spouse indicators; D9 lagna that puts named planets in dusthanas; a character check (Saturn in the D9 lagna: earns by own skill) | Spouse's fate, volunteered by the native **[Sensitive]**; earns by own skill | Aquarius 23°20' to 26°40' | [A] pp.37-38 |
| 3 | Saptamsa (D7) lagna | Two candidate D7 lagnas (Cancer, Leo); D7 5th lord for the first child, 7th lord for the second | First child a son with a health condition **[Sensitive]**; second child a daughter, which chose Leo | Aquarius 25°43' to 26°40' | [A] p.38 |
| 4 | Cross-checks | 8th from UL and 8th from the 7th for a second marriage; 8th from the D7 5th, counted by "frog-jump" (Manduka gati), for the second wife's children | Second marriage; sex and order of the later children | unchanged; confirmation only | [A] pp.38-39 |
| 5 | Sex check | Birth time from sunrise or sunset in vighatis, divided by nine, remainder counted from the Sun in weekday order; the planet reached gives male, female or neither | — (a sanity check) | unchanged | [A] pp.35-36 |
| 6 | Kunda, last | Ascendant longitude × 81, multiples of 360° removed; must fall in a trine to the natal Moon's star or the name star | — | Rectified to 1:54 AM IST (cusp moved by 40') | [A] p.39 |
| 7 | Proof by forecast | Predictive value is "the final test of a corrected chart" | A forecast that came true: the father's death **[Sensitive]** | — | [A] p.39 |

What this order means [Inferred, from the case text]:
- **Family facts lead.** Siblings come first because the window spanned two drekkanas, not because siblings always come
  first. The rule underneath is that each question is a discriminator: it is asked because two candidate varga lagnas
  predict different answers.
- **Each answer narrows the range before the next question.** Siblings (D3), then spouse (D9), then children (D7); in
  Chart 7 each step roughly halved the range.
- **Formula tools come last.** The vighati sex check is a sanity check and Kunda is fine tuning. Neither leads.
- **Most of Rath's discriminating facts in this case are deaths and illnesses of relatives.** The neutral halves (count,
  order, sex, earns by own skill, second marriage) can be used without the sensitive halves; see [13](13-user-questions.md).
- The D12 is named among the first rectification vargas on p.34 but has no worked step in the section [A, gap].

Other Rath cases [A]:
- **Chart 35 (Crux Ch. VI, pp.119-120).** He placed the native as youngest of seven from the AL, read each elder
  sibling's sex in order from the D3 (twins included), and offered one detail about the eldest sister (temperament,
  troubled marriage) "to confirm the readings". Recorded as confirmed.
- **Clinton reading (srath.com).** The birth time is justified through the Atmakaraka and the navamsa lagna before any
  interpretation (summarising fetch only; see `research_notes/Sanjay Rath life area readings/d1_and_style.md`).
- **Rani Rasmani (srath.com).** No recorded time: a prashna chose the lagna and navamsa lagna from three candidates,
  then the chart was tested against marriage age, the husband's profile, the number and sex of children, and the year
  of the husband's death **[Sensitive]**, timed by dasha.

Student additions on Kunda and Tattva [B], Visti Larsen (Scribd snippet only; the full article did not load and a
reviewer calls its arithmetic inconsistent):
- Kunda moves 81 times faster than the lagna and should fall in a trine or the 7th from the rasi lagna.
- A lagna correction of more than about 3° also moves the D60 and D10 lagnas, which must be re-checked.
- Kunda "cannot be used independently" of varga rectification.
- Name-based rectification (srigaruda.com): a name sign by Katapayadi, a time adjusted so the lagna degree links to
  it, checked against the D60 lagna, the Hora chakra of the first syllable and Ketu's dispositor. No life events asked.

## 2. Rao's dissent and his own order [B]

PVR calls Rath "my guru" and departs from him here (*Integrated Approach* ch. 32, pp.421-431):

| Point | PVR's position | Against Rath |
|---|---|---|
| Formula methods | Tattva, Pranapada lagna in navamsa and Kunda are "reasonable", but Pranapada and Kunda fail the twins test; Tattva is outside his book | Rath uses Kunda as the last step in Chart 7 |
| What decides | Only a nearby time that explains the person's nature, credentials, attitude, aptitude and known past events; laborious, no other way | Agrees that known events decide |
| Client times | "Seldom accurate": unadjusted clocks, a time noted late, a parent's memory, differing ideas of when birth happens | Rath's intake asks for the hospital record or the mother |
| Order | Fix the D9 or D10 lagna first, then higher vargas (D20, D24, Kalachakra); be ready to go back to the D9. Married: use the marriage for the D9; unmarried: duty, basic skills, how they deal with others | Rath leads with siblings (D3) in Chart 7 |
| Events | D4 move abroad (timed by dasha), D9 temperament, D10 career read from the D10 AL, D24 education (timed by dasha) | Rath's own cases use family facts and livelihood |
| Sensitivity | The lagna moves about 1° in 4 minutes; the D10 lagna changes sign about every 12 minutes, the D24 about every 5. A planet on a varga border must be tested both ways. A 2-minute error shifts Vimshottari dates by about (n×m)/4 days | — |
| Fine timing | Do not use fine sub-periods confidently on an unrectified time | Consistent with Rath's "final test" |

Where they agree and differ [Inferred]: both say known events decide the time. They differ on Kunda as a final tool.
For a product, PVR's position is the safer default: no rectification by formula alone, and any Kunda or Tattva result
is at most a weak tiebreaker.

## 3. Consultation stance

Rath's published process [A] (srath.com "The Process" and "About Your Future"):
- **The client leads the topics.** The person sends birth data and a form choosing the life matters to cover; the
  reading goes where the client leads.
- **Birth data first.** The time should come from a hospital record or the mother. Religion is asked, and whether the
  person objects to gemstone advice.
- **Name study.** The name and any name changes are studied (the name star is a Kunda target).
- **A detailed study of the past is made before the forecast.** In the book cases the past is confirmed aloud ("This was
  confirmed"), so verification is a dialogue step, not a silent calculation.
- **Remedies wait for verification.** Before naming a curse and its remedy for children, physical capacity is checked
  first (*Crux* 8.5.5) **[Sensitive]**.
- **Service menu.** Separate products for a vague time (correction), date and place only (determination), month and
  place only, and year only (lost data).

Which star starts Vimshottari [A], two Rath texts that do not match:
- *Crux* Introduction (pp.4-6): Jataka Parijata lists the lagna star, the Moon star, the name star and the Moon star at
  the time of the question. Rath rules that natal work starts from the **stronger of the lagna and the Moon**,
  "ignoring" the birth-related (utpanna), name and question stars.
- srath.com Vimshottari lesson (`?p=804`): natal work starts from the **strongest of the lagna, Moon (Janma) and Utpanna
  (5th from the Moon) stars**, by planets in kendras, then Jupiter, Mercury or the star lord; the Moon's star when in
  doubt; the name and question stars only for horary charts.
- Both are tier A. The difference is unresolved (the book chapter that would settle it, *Vimsottari and Udu Dasas*
  Ch. 3, was not read). Either way, the question-time Moon is not used for a natal chart. See
  [04-dasha-selection-and-ranking.md](04-dasha-selection-and-ranking.md).

What the product takes from this [Inferred]: confirm the past before predicting (AGENTS.md rule 2), let the person lead
the topic (Clarify's bucket and sub-bucket), and treat the moment of a question as a fallback chart only when birth data
is missing, never as a replacement for the natal chart.

## 4. Prashna for lost birth data [A]

- Prashna is listed among Rath's services, and he used it to recover the rasi and some varga charts for a person with
  no recorded time (Rani Rasmani), then tested the result against life events.
- He describes a devotional prashna device (seven verses on folded slips, chosen after prayer); seen as a search
  excerpt only.
- His student Larsen teaches Prashna-kendra and Prashna-arudha "as per" Rath's teaching [B]; the computation was not
  retrieved. No text presents prashna as a third confirmation of a natal prediction.
- For the product: `general.prashna` already answers eligible yes/no questions from the question-moment chart. Using
  prashna to reconstruct a missing birth time is a different job with no technique and no engine path today (see
  [14](14-technique-mapping.md)).

## 5. What the product can and cannot do today

- **Stores that exist:** `person_facts` (fixed `FACT_CATALOG`) and `life_events` (52 dated categories). The ledger is the
  natural home for confirmed past events, but no confirm answer writes to it today, and no technique uses it to
  rectify (`existing_techniques_inventory.md` §2.3, §4 items 12 and 22).
- **Gates that need a birth-time quality fact:** `work.d10_check` lets D60 confirm only when the time is "recorded to
  the minute", but nothing records that. Filed in `docs/TODOS.md` (2026-11-18 section).
- **Engine inputs a rectification step would need,** none of them exported as a time-window search today: the set of
  candidate D3, D9, D7 and D10 lagnas across a time window, the time of each sign change of those lagnas, the vighati
  count from sunrise or sunset, and the Kunda longitude. All of it would be an engine addition; nothing may be computed
  in Node (CLAUDE.md).

## 6. What was not found

- No Rath primary text on Pranapada rectification, on D60 or D150 (nadi-amsa) rectification, or on a separate Moon pada
  rule. The only star link found is Kunda in trine to the natal Moon's star or the name star.
- No worked D12 step, though the D12 is named among the first rectification vargas.
- No Rath text asking about job changes, house moves or the end of education as rectification events; those are PVR's
  [B].
- No srath.com intake form listing required events; the "Vedic Form" was not read.
- No first-hand account of a live Rath consultation, and no Rath statement on how to word health or death forecasts,
  how to avoid fear, or when to refuse a question. A third-party ethics page holding death prediction unethical was
  seen as a snippet only and is not tied to Rath [C].
- Nothing on prashna arudha computation, judging the questioner's intent, or a mismatch between the stated concern and
  the chart's indication.
