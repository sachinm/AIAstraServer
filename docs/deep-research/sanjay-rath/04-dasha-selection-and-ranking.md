Purpose: Record which dasha system the Rath sources assign to which question, how Vimshottari is started, when conditional dashas apply, how systems combine, and which earlier claims are now corrected or unconfirmed.

## 1. Which dasha answers which question

Rath frames dashas by purpose, not by rank. He calls a comparative ranking "useless" because each has merits. [A] *Narayana Dasa*, Order chapter, around pp.59-61.

## 2. Narayana per varga (seed rules)

- The Narayana dasha of a divisional chart shows changes in the environment for that chart's area (D10 professional environment, D7 children and dynasty). It starts from the sign occupied, in that varga, by the lord of the controlling house of the rasi chart. Where a varga covers several matters, run a separate Narayana per matter. [A] *Narayana Dasa*, Varga Narayana Dasa chapter, pp.114-115. Also demonstrated on the D4 (*Crux* Ch. VII, approx. pp.155-157).
- Table 29 controlling houses [A] (*Narayana Dasa* p.119): D2 2nd (wealth, life force); D3 3rd; D4 4th; D5 5th (subordinates); D6 6th; D7 7th; D8 8th; D9 9th (spouse, dharma); D10 10th; D11 11th; D12 12th; D16 4th; D20 8th; D24 4th; D30 6th; D40 4th; D45 9th; D60 12th.
- Note on D7: Table 29 gives the 7th house as controller, and *Crux* runs the children double check from the D7 sign of the D1 7th lord; the life area report notes the general varga rule would give the 5th lord for children and that the book's reason was not found. [A, unresolved]
- Hora Narayana from the D2 sign of the D1 2nd lord, in the Kashinatha hora. [B] Rao, Using Kashinatha Hora Chart (2003), reposted.

Engine: not computed. Dashas cannot be cast on a varga or from a chosen seed.

## 4. Conditional nakshatra dashas

- Rath's order: Vimshottari by default; a conditional dasha only when its condition holds. No tie-break among several qualifying conditional dashas was found. [A] srath.com/?p=804.
- Ashtottari [A]: Rahu in a kendra or trikona from the lagna lord's sign; and day birth in Krishna paksha or night birth in Shukla paksha. The text says "if both" but elsewhere treats them as alternatives, and the 25% share is given once for Ashtottari and once for Vimshottari. Unresolved in the source.
- Dvisaptati Sama [A]: lagna lord in the 7th or 7th lord in lagna. [srath.com/?p=75](https://srath.com/?p=75).
- The udu book has applicability sections for Shodashottari (8.1), Dwadashottari (9.1), Panchottari (10.1), Shatabdika (11.1), Shat-trimsha Sama (15.3), and chapters on Chaturaseeti Sama (Ch. 12, starting dasha 12.2), Dvisaptati Sama (13), Shashtihayani (14), Tithi Ashtottari (7.5-7.6, pp.187-188). [A, contents only] [Exotic India listing](https://exoticindiaart.com/book/details/vimsottari-and-udu-dasa-s-parasara-s-key-to-prognostication-naj126).
- PVR condition table [B] ([Unified Nakshatra Dasa Approach, 2014](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_cond_dasas.pdf)): Shodashottari, lagna in Sun's hora in Shukla or Moon's hora in Krishna paksha; Dwadashottari, lagna in a Venus navamsa; Ashtottari, Rahu in kendra or trikona from lagna lord, not in lagna; Panchottari, lagna in Cancer dwadashamsa; Shatabdika, lagna vargottama; Chaturaseeti Sama, 10th lord in the 10th; Dvisaptati Sama, lagna lord in 7th or 7th lord in lagna; Shashtihayani, Sun in lagna; Shat-trimsha Sama, lagna in Sun's hora by day or Moon's by night.
- PVR precedence [B, his own innovation]: a condition is necessary, not sufficient; the conditional dasha overrides Vimshottari only when its controlling planet is also strong (Shodashottari Jupiter, Dwadashottari Ketu, Ashtottari Mars, Panchottari Venus, Shatabdika Sun, Chaturaseeti Saturn, Dvisaptati Rahu, Shashtihayani Moon, Shat-trimsha Mercury). Works better in Tithi Pravesha charts; depends on his own ayanamsa.
- PVR's book gives three competing Ashtottari views. [B] Ch. 17.1, 17.2.3.

Engine: all nine conditional dashas computed; `dasha.applicable` lists them with their rule (which convention it follows was not checked against Rath or PVR). Controlling-planet strength test not computed.

## 5. Kendradi and Tara, Moola and Mandooka

- Rath [A]: four or more planets in kendras from lagna triggers Tara dasa (srath.com/?p=804).
- PVR [B]: all kendras from the stronger of lagna and Moon occupied makes Kendradi Graha (Moola) dasa more suitable than Vimshottari; some apply Moola to everyone (Ch. 16.7, 19.1).
- These are different triggers and different replacements; do not merge them.
- Mula dasa sequence and periods [A] (srath.com/?p=6254): start from the stronger of lagna and a kendra with planets; parity of lagna decides whether panapara or apoklima follows; Saturn and Ketu in lagna override; period = Vimshottari years minus (signs to mulatrikona minus one), plus or minus one for exaltation or debilitation; second cycle to 120 years.
- Mandooka: Rath teaches it in another book (listing paraphrase); PVR says a noted author's Mandooka is really a Lagna Kendradi variant and true Mandooka is the Kalachakra frog jump; D11 Mandooka for wars and death [B] Ch. 19.4.

## 6. Chara as movable Narayana

- Narayana has three types, "Chara etc", for movable, fixed and dual lagnas. Under JS 2.4.8 a movable lagna gives a regular sequence, which Rath notes are "also called Chara dasa". [A] *Narayana Dasa* Preface; Order chapter rule (8).
- Fixed lagna: next dasha is the 6th sign; dual lagna: kendra then its trines. Saturn in the starting sign forces the regular (Chara) order for every lagna type; Ketu in the dasha sign reverses direction. [A] Order rules (9), (10), (14), (15).
- Shared rules [A]: start from the stronger of lagna and 7th; direction by footedness of the 9th from the start (BPHS 46.167); years = count to the lord by footedness minus one, plus one exalted, minus one debilitated, cap 12; Scorpio/Aquarius dual-lord ladder (BPHS 46.158-163); second cycle 12 minus first; 12 equal antardashas starting from the sign of the lord of the stronger of dasha sign and its 7th, direction by odd or even dasha sign. Rahu exalted in Gemini and Ketu in Sagittarius for phalita dashas; Parashara's node signs kept for ayur dashas.
- No Rath rule says when to prefer Chara over Narayana: in his scheme they are the same system for a movable lagna. [A] chara_dasha.md section 2.
- Differences from K.N. Rao: K.N. Rao makes no exaltation adjustment and runs a plain regular sequence in his example [C, other school]. PVR 2014 "Parasara's Chara Dasa" is his own departure [B].
- Engine: chara and narayana computed separately; whether the engine's chara follows Rath's (movable-lagna Narayana) or K.N. Rao's rules was not checked.

## 7. How systems combine

| Statement | Tier | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Narayana and Vimshottari are not in conflict; apparent disagreement reflects the astrologer's limited understanding | [A] | *Narayana Dasa* pp.89-90 |
| A nakshatra dasha such as Vimshottari pinpoints an event, but its occurrence needs confirmation from a rasi dasha such as Narayana | [A] | *Crux* Introduction, p.3 (verification_and_questions.md) |
| Children: Vimshottari conditions on the D7 lagna lord, with an independent Narayana from the D7 sign of the 7th lord as a double check | [A] | *Crux* sect. 8.6.2, pp.177-178 |
| Within one rasi dasha, dasha and antardasha signs both linking to the event's significators confirm the event (Tagore's marriage) | [A] | *Narayana Dasa*, Tagore illustration, around pp.111-112 |
| Vimshottari and Narayana show the same events with different focus; mixing dashas without knowing their differences gives vague readings | [B] | PVR Ch. 24.5 |
| Check Narayana with Sudasa for power with prosperity | [B] | PVR Ch. 20 |
| At least three timing methods together | [C, Scribd snippet, not opened] | [Scribd, Dasa System](https://www.scribd.com/document/535059356/Dasa-System) |

Working rule [Inferred]: an event is promised in time when the question's rasi dasha (Narayana, or the varga Narayana for the area) and Vimshottari both point to its significators. The *Crux* p.3 line supports this direction; no quantitative or required-agreement rule was found.

## 8. Corrections to earlier claims

- **Tithi Ashtottari and Chaturaseeti Sama do appear in Rath's book contents** (udu book Ch. 7.5-7.6 and Ch. 12). The earlier method summary listed them as having no Rath text. They are therefore taught by him, but their use and rules are still unread; the contents establish topics, not rules.
- **"Sudasa is preferred over Narayana only when the Moon sign is stronger": not confirmed this round.** The earlier life area report states it as Rath's, citing *Crux* Ch. I sect. 4, Ch. V sect. 5.5, Ch. VII sect. 7.5, Ch. XIV, from chapters read partly through a summarising tool. This round found no such condition in any tier: Sudasa starts from Sree Lagna (PVR Ch. 20) and no Moon-strength condition was seen. The two reports conflict; until the *Crux* passage is checked in print, treat the condition as unverified.
- **"Narayana over Vimshottari for marriage": not confirmed this round.** The earlier life area report says *Crux* Ch. X (Charts 71-79) calls Narayana more accurate because Vimshottari alone shortlists too many periods. This round's direct read of *Narayana Dasa* found a marriage-timing illustration that does not rank Narayana over Vimshottari, and found Rath calling comparative ranking useless. The two reports conflict; the earlier claim rests on a summarised chapter and needs a print check.
- **"Shoola" naming.** In Rath's books "Shoola dasa" starts from the stronger of lagna and 7th; PVR calls Parashara's Shoola "Niryana Shoola" (stronger of 2nd and 8th) and notes Rath uses the name differently (PVR Ch. 22.1, footnote 60). *Crux* uses spouse-related shoola dashas from the stronger of 1st and 7th, nine years each (Ch. VII p.139). The engine exposes both `shoola` and `nirayana (shoola)`; which matches Rath's usage was not checked.

## Gaps and conflicts

- No single Rath ranking of dashas; only purposes.
- *Vimsottari and Udu Dasas* text unread: selection methodology, Tribhagi, compressed Vimshottari, Tithi Ashtottari use, conditional applicability in Rath's words, dasa pravesh and gochara sections.
- Ashtottari "both criteria" ambiguity and the 25% inconsistency in Rath's own page.
- No Rath tie-break among qualifying conditional dashas; PVR's controlling-planet test is his own.
- Table 29 remarks rest on a floating column in the extraction.
- D7 Narayana seed: 7th lord (*Crux*) vs the general controlling-house rule.
- Yogini, Kalachakra, Kaala, Brahma, Navamsa dasa, Trikona: no Rath rule text.
- The two earlier claims in section 8 conflict with this round.

## Use in the product

- Choose the dasha by the question, not by a global rank: environment and events from Narayana, inner experience from Vimshottari, prosperity from Sudasa (checked with Narayana), spiritual matters from Drig. Do not use Shoola, Mula, Manduka or the Vimshottari #2 lines in readings without an owner decision.
- For any dated window, present it as stronger when Narayana and Vimshottari agree, and as tentative when only one supports it; this follows *Crux* p.3 and the children double check.
- Keep Vimshottari on the Moon by default (Rath's fallback). A seeded start (lagna or Utpanna by kendra count) needs engine support and should be shown as a variant until then.
- Use a conditional nakshatra dasha only where its condition holds, and label which convention (Rath or PVR) the condition follows. Do not let it silently replace Vimshottari.
- Career, children and other area boards should eventually time inside their varga (D10, D7 Narayana); until the engine can seed a dasha in a varga, say the area timing comes from the D1 Narayana.
- Do not cite "Sudasa when the Moon sign is stronger" or "Narayana is more accurate for marriage" as Rath rules until the *Crux* passages are checked in print.
- Engine work implied: dasha seeded from a chosen sign and cast on a varga; Vimshottari from a chosen star; a check of the chara and shoola implementations against Rath's definitions.
