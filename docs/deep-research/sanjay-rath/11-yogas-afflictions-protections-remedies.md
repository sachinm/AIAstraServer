## 1. Yogas: which count and how fructification is tested

### Order of judgment
Provenance: assembled across tiers [Inferred]; neither Rath nor Rao prints it. Steps 1, 2, 4 and 6 are Rath's [A]; steps 3 and 5 are Rao's [B]. A yoga is never read alone: it is factor 8 of the ten house factors, after house, lord, dispositor, arudhas and karakas and before argala [A].
1. Identify the yoga.
2. Check its cancellers and afflictions.
3. Grade its strength: degree closeness, dignity, combustion, varga count.
4. Confirm it in the relevant varga (D5 and D10 for power; D9 through the AK).
5. Check the AL and A7 relation.
6. Time it by house position and dasa.

### Timing method
House position gates the age of fructification (1.3); dasa choice gates permanence (1.4) and achievement (1.6). Narayana dasa activates raja yoga [A] ([Crux-1998], Ch. I sect. 4; Ch. XIII). Engine: computed on D1.

### What to confirm with the person
1. Did your biggest rise in standing come early in life, in the middle years, or later?
2. Did a big break come after a hard start, as if difficulties turned into an advantage?

## 2. Afflictions

### Order of judgment
No single printed affliction order by Rath was found.
**Rao's ordered analysis for any matter [B]** (Rao sect. 13.4.1, pp.169-170):
1. Choose the varga for the matter.
2. Choose the house in it.
3. Choose the reference point: lagna for the true self, AL for the perceived self, or a karaka when stronger.
4. House or arudha (arudha for how the matter is perceived, such as A5 for awards).
5. Influences: rasi and graha drishti, then argala, then positions from the house (kendras sustain, trines prosper, upachayas grow, dusthanas obstruct, a badhaka from it troubles).
6. Standard results, then strength, avasthas, ashtakavarga and yogas.

**Implied affliction order across tiers [Inferred]:** functional nature of the lords; planets in and aspecting the house; argala with obstruction; papakartari; badhaka from the house, AL or Paka lagna; dusthana and marana karaka positions of lord and karaka; node involvement; dasa activation.

## 3. Protections

### Order of judgment
No ranking of protections was found. What counts as protection in the texts [Inferred grouping]:
1. Obstruction of papargala.
2. Unobstructed benefic argala on the house or karaka.
3. A functional malefic in a dusthana spoiling the bad house.
4. Placements of the AK, karaka or lagna lord that carry a deity's blessing.
5. The deity layer (Ishta for the soul; the varga lagna lord's deity for a life area).

## 4. Remedies

### Order of judgment
Provenance: assembled from both tiers [Inferred]; Rath does not print it. His own frame is that a remedy follows diagnosis [A].
1. Diagnose the afflicted house or karaka and the running dasa [A].
2. Apply common-sense precaution in a bad period first [B].
3. Choose the planet to support: a functional benefic or a varga yogakaraka; do not strengthen functional malefics, or marakas in maraka dasas [B].
4. Choose the method: gem to support; mantra, stotra, deity worship or charity to pacify; conduct keyed to the AK [A].
5. Choose the deity: the varga lagna lord's deity for a life area; the Ishta for the soul [B]; Rath's own Ishta order is in file 07 [A].
6. Refine the deity's form from the planet's associations [B].
