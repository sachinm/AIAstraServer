# Sanjay Rath: the Jaimini apparatus

Purpose: Record what the Rath sources say about chara karakas, karakamsa, arudha padas, upapada, rasi drishti, argala, the special lagnas and Jaimini yogas, and what is still unstated.
Sources: research_notes/Sanjay Rath method summary/jaimini_methods.md; research_notes/Sanjay Rath open method gaps/karakamsa_profession.md, indu_lagna.md, chara_dasha.md; research_notes/Sanjay Rath methods and precedence/procedure_and_precedence.md, yogas_afflictions_remedies.md, existing_techniques_inventory.md section 3; reports/Sanjay Rath method summary.md (Karakamsha and marriage sections); reports/Sanjay Rath life area readings.md (sections 1, 3, 4, 8).
Informs: reference points used by techniques; marriage, career and wealth boards; self board (Atmakaraka themes); confirm questions built on arudhas.

Tier key: [A] Rath's own text; [B] student (Rao IA = P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*, https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf); [C] third party or other translator; [Inferred]. All srath.com pages were read through a summarising fetch.

- Chara karakas stand for the person directly: the DK is the spouse, not the 7th from it. [B] Rao IA 8.3-8.4.

## 4. Upapada and marriage padas

All from [A] [srath.com, Darapada and Upapada](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/) and *Crux* Ch. X unless marked. Engine: UL and A7 computed.

- Venus signifies marriage and the A7; Ketu signifies renunciation and moksha.
- The UL and the 7th alone cannot settle whether marriage occurs; the A7 must be read too.
- The UL is the marriage and spouse; its planets show the spouse's family; Sun in the 12th from it strains relations with the father-in-law; Moon in the 7th from it, a hostile mother-in-law. (*Crux* Ch. X)
- UL in the 12th from the AL: the native may tend to ignore the spouse. UL in Libra with the 7th lord Jupiter: a very good marriage and spiritual growth (single-chart readings).
- Harmony between the 7th and 12th and their arudhas, especially from karakamsa, benefits the native.
- 2nd from UL or from the 7th for sustenance of marriage (*Crux* Ch. V p.79; the natural-significator reference is ambiguous in the scan).
- UL in the 2nd brings wealth through the spouse (*Crux* Ch. V sect. 5.3.4).
- Second marriage from the 8th from the 7th and the 8th from the UL; third from the 9th; where an earlier bond is strong, the 8th from UL stands as the UL of the second marriage (*Crux* Ch. VII p.143; Ch. X pp.167-169).
- Look for a link between the 12th and/or UL and the Sun, Venus and Rahu; effects not stated. ([srath.com, Complexity](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/complexity-of-jaimini-sutra/))
- Venus in the 12th from lagna or AL, Ketu in the 12th, Venus in the 6th from AL, and Venus/Ketu/Rahu combinations across 7th, A7, 12th and UL carry hedged readings of delay, separation, two spouses, loss or renunciation. [Sensitive] source rule (spouse loss, renunciation), not for readings without owner decision.
- 3rd from UL as place of the spouse's death. [Sensitive] source rule, not for readings without owner decision.

Not found: the UL and A7 derivation.

## 5. Rasi drishti

- Movable aspects fixed (not adjacent); fixed aspects movable (not adjacent); duals aspect each other; mutual; planets share their sign's aspects. [A, fetch] [srath.com, Tattva & Dristi](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/amateur/tattva-dristi/). Engine: computed.
- Rasi drishti is knowledge, permanent, and can cause lasting damage; Parashara taught it before graha drishti. [A] Complexity of the Jaimini Sutra.
- In Jaimini strength tests only rasi drishti counts (aspect of Jupiter, Mercury or the sign lord on a sign). [A] *Narayana Dasa* Ch. I-II; [B] Rao IA 15.5.
- The jaimini_methods note found no mechanics on the pages it checked; the mechanics above come from the later Tattva & Dristi fetch. The two notes are consistent.

## Gaps and conflicts

- Chara karakas: tie rule; 7-karaka mundane claim (critic only); Rath's own AK selection statement; DK and PK selection or reading rules.
- Karakamsa: Rath's swamsa definition; which chart each is read in; any profession rule; the purpose of the "four signs from the 10th" count.
- Arudhas: no Rath calculation or exception rule; the "functional arudha" rests on one case with an inferred rule.
- Upapada and A7: derivation not stated; many marriage readings are loss or renunciation and are sensitive.
- Indu Lagna is not a Rath technique; the engine computes it, but any Rath label would be invented.
- Yamada lagna appears in Rath's factor list but is not in the engine inventory.
- Jaimini yogas: Rao's list only; Rath's Upadesa Sutras commentary unread.
- Spouse karaka conflict (Jupiter vs 7th from Venus) carries into marriage reading; see file 02.

## Use in the product

- Use the eight-karaka scheme the engine already computes; label the AK choice by degree as "Rath school (Rao)". Do not build any reading that depends on the DK or PK beyond naming it, since no selection or reading rule is stated by Rath.
- Self board: the AK lesson (what the soul must learn) and the 12th and 9th from karakamsa are Rath [A] and can carry Self content; frame them as themes, never as events.
- Career and reputation: read status and titles from the AL and A10, the visible field from AL occupants. Confirm question form: ask how the person's work is seen or titled before predicting status change.
- Marriage: read UL, A7 and Venus together, as Rath requires the A7 alongside UL and 7th. Confirm with in-law relations or partner traits the person has already shared before saying anything about the marriage itself. Do not show loss, second-marriage or renunciation readings without owner decision.
- Wealth: HL for money and Sree Lagna with Sudasa for prosperity are the school's points; Indu Lagna may appear only labelled as classical (Uttara Kalamrita), not Rath.
- Sibling confirm questions (elder and younger counts from the AL) are a tier A verification route; avoid the parts about siblings' deaths.
- Engine work implied: Yamada lagna, Paka lagna as a named point, arudhas counted from the Sun, karakamsa in vargas other than D9, AK-to-navamsa-lagna raja yoga flags, Rao's Jaimini raja yogas, and the natural-zodiac karakamsa view.
