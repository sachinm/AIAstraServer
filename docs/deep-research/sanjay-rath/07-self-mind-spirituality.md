# 07. Self, mind, fortune and spirituality in Sanjay Rath's method

Purpose: Give the order and rules Rath uses for a person's nature, mind, soul lesson, fortune and spiritual direction, with tiers, citations and engine status, so the Self board and general chat can read them from stored chart facts.
Sources: research_notes/Sanjay Rath methods and precedence/other_life_areas.md (sect. 1, 4, 9), yogas_afflictions_remedies.md (Q1, Q3, Q4); reports/Sanjay Rath life area readings.md (sect. 1); research_notes/Sanjay Rath life area readings/d1_and_style.md; research_notes/Sanjay Rath open method gaps/karakamsa_profession.md (Karakamsa definition); existing_techniques_inventory.md sect. 3.
Informs: Self board (temperament, mind, soul lesson), general chat (fortune, spiritual questions), and the deity layer used by remedies (file 11).

**Tiers.** [A] Rath's own text. [B] named student or SJC teacher. [C] third party or unattributed. [Inferred] assembled by the notes. **Citations:** "[Crux-1998]" pages follow the archive.org copy used in other_life_areas.md; "*Crux*" pages follow the earlier report's copies. Both were read through a summarising fetch, so page numbers are locators only. **Engine:** status from the engine inventory sect. 3.

## 1. Temperament and mind

### Order of judgment
Provenance: no printed "Self" order exists. Steps 1-6 follow the Ascendant chapter's sequence ([Crux-1998], Ch. IV sect. 4.1-4.4); step 7 comes from srath.com articles. The chain as a whole is [Inferred].
1. Read the lagna, the AL and the Varnada lagna separately, with their strength.
2. Dual karaka check: the Sun's and the AK's unobstructed argala on the lagna.
3. Sign temperament, blending the D1 and D9 lagnas, with the D9 dominant and planets in the lagna modifying both.
4. Lagna lord by house (each result conditional on its strength).
5. Paka lagna (the sign the lagna lord occupies); the lagna lord's nakshatra lord for the mind, its sign lord for the body.
6. Yogas, then what cancels them.
7. Atmakaraka: its lesson, its nature, and the house it falls in counted to the D9 lagna.

### Timing method
- Vimshottari and other nakshatra dasas time the state of mind, counted from the Moon; Narayana dasa times what the environment brings. [A] *Crux* Preface.
- Vimshottari starts from the strongest of four stars: lagna star, Moon star, name-initial star, or the Moon's star at the query. [A] *Crux* Ch. I sect. 6, approx. p.5. Engine: partial (Vimshottari from the Moon is computed; other seeds are not).
- The AK's own Vimshottari dasa often brings suffering, especially for a malefic AK; the Narayana dasa of the AK's sign, or of signs it aspects, can bring major achievement. [A] srath.com, Atmakaraka & Karakamsa. Engine: computed (Vimshottari, Narayana on D1).

### What to confirm with the person
1. Do people who know you well see you as more driven and strong-willed than you first come across?
2. Is there one lesson that keeps coming back in your life, such as learning humility, speaking only the truth, or not being taken advantage of?
3. Are you more at ease with ideas and planning than with physical routine, or the other way round?

### Gaps
- No Rath reading of temperament from the Moon sign or Moon nakshatra on its own; the Moon enters only through the nakshatra-lord rule, the dasa choice and examples.
- No Rath table of the 27 nakshatras for personality (srath.com "Nakshatra Shakti" listed, not read).
- No rule for weighing lagna, Moon and AK against each other when they disagree.
- His Swamsa definition, and which chart each of Karakamsa and Swamsa is read in.

## 2. Fortune (bhagya)

### Order of judgment
Provenance: assembled from the ninth-house chapter [Inferred].
1. The 9th house and lord: the divine in the chart, the protector.
2. Planets in the 9th from the AL: the main giver of material benefit and protection.
3. Yogas of the 9th lord (Dharma-Karmadhipati, Lakshmi).
4. Planets in the 9th from the D9 lagna.
5. Time with Narayana dasa, Sudasa (when the Moon sign is stronger) and the years-of-fortune table.

## 3. Spirituality, dharma and the personal deities

### Order of judgment
Provenance: steps 1-6 are printed by Rath as one sequence on srath.com, Spirituality in Jyotish [A].
1. Identify the AK and the Karakamsa (D9).
2. Ishta devata (liberation) from the 12th from the Karakamsa.
3. Dharma devata from the 9th from the Karakamsa.
4. Palana devata (livelihood, support) from the 6th from the Amatyakaraka in the D9.
5. Guru devata from the Bhratrikaraka's navamsa sign.
6. Only then a short-term remedy for the current problem, given with caution.
Then confirm the deity in the D20, and time renunciation with Drig dasa.

### Timing method
- Renunciation: Drig dasa from the 9th, the AL Drig dasa, and the AK's Vimshottari dasa; Ketu's transit through trines to the lagna or AL. [A] [Crux-1998], Ch. XII sect. 12.6, 12.14-12.15; Ch. ### What to confirm with the person
1. Is there a form of the divine, a teacher or a practice you have felt drawn to since childhood without being taught to?
2. Have you had periods when you wanted to step back from worldly goals altogether, and when were they?
3. Does someone in your life act as a guide or teacher whose advice you keep returning to?
