# 08. Career and wealth in Sanjay Rath's method

Purpose: Give the order of judgment, rules, timing and open gaps for career (field, service against business, titles, job changes) and wealth (stored, earned, lost, timed), with tiers, citations and engine status.
Sources: reports/Sanjay Rath life area readings.md (sect. 3, 4); research_notes/Sanjay Rath life area readings/career.md, finance_wealth.md, varga_charts.md; research_notes/Sanjay Rath open method gaps/karakamsa_profession.md, d10_deities.md, indu_lagna.md; research_notes/Sanjay Rath methods and precedence/yogas_afflictions_remedies.md (Q1, Q4); existing_techniques_inventory.md sect. 3.
Informs: Work board (career field, job change, promotion, service or business), money sections of the Work and Self boards, general chat on jobs and money.

**Tiers.** [A] Rath's own text. [B] named student or SJC teacher. [C] third party, unattributed, or another translator's text. [Inferred] assembled by the notes. **Citations:** "*Crux*" refers to *The Crux of Vedic Astrology: Timing of Events*; pages are approximate locators from several copies. ## 1. Career

### Order of judgment
Provenance: assembled from the tenth-house chapter's section order and about twenty worked charts [Inferred]; Rath does not print it as a list. His chapter runs: 10th house; 10th from the Moon and Hora Lagna; navamsa and Karakamsa; unemployment and ill fame; professional determination and fame; professions.
1. Rasi 10th house, its lord, karakas and ashtakavarga.
2. Argala on the 10th.
3. Paka lagna and AL screen (badhaka and dusthana).
4. 10th from the Moon and from Hora Lagna.
5. Navamsa layer: 10th from D9 lagna and Karakamsa; the 10th lord's navamsa dispositor and sign type.
6. Unemployment and ill-fame checks.
7. AL, A10 and A7: the visible career, titles, business.
8. Amatyakaraka (case use only).
9. D10 as a full chart: field, raja yoga confirmation, dignity.
10. Service against business in the D10.
11. Time with D10 house lords, Vimshottari, Narayana and transits.

### What to confirm with the person
1. Do you work for an employer, or do you run something of your own, and which have you leaned towards over the years?
2. When did you last start a new job or role, and did it come through someone below or beside you rather than above you?
3. Is your work mostly at a desk in one place, or does it keep you moving around?

## 2. Wealth

### Order of judgment
Provenance: assembled from *Crux* chapters II, V, XIV and XV [Inferred]; no numbered checklist by Rath found.
1. Promise: the 2nd house and Jupiter (Hora Lagna as the wealth point).
2. Argala on Jupiter and on the lagna.
3. Arudha checks on the 2nd (A2, Upapada, Varnada).
4. Visible income: the 11th from the AL.
5. Drains: the 12th from the AL.
6. Confirm with special lagnas, sahams and vargas.
7. Time with Sudasa, Narayana and Vimshottari.
8. Debt, speculation and inheritance.

**Windfalls.** No Rath rule for windfalls, lotteries or sudden 8th-house gains was found. Nothing here should be presented as his method for sudden money.

**Remedies (case-bound).** Fast on the weekdays of planets in the 12th (*Crux* Ch. XV sect. 15.7) [A]; a Ganesha mantra in an income and job-change case (Ch. VI, Chart 33) [A]. Lakshmi remedies appear only on commercial blogs [C], not his. See file 11.

### What to confirm with the person
1. Does most of your money come from your own work, from family, or from a partner or business deals?
2. Do you tend to earn well but find it slips away, or is money slow to come but stays?
3. Was there a year when your finances clearly turned up or down, and roughly when?

- The Artha and other natal sahams are not exported by the engine (annual sahams only).
- A wealth remedy targeting the SL lord, the HL-linked planet or planets in the 12th from AL is plausible within his frame but stated nowhere [Inferred].
