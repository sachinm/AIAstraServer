## 1. Who Rath is, as his own pages present him

- He presents himself as carrying a Puri family tradition traced to Sri Achyuta Dasa, taught by his uncle Pt. Kasinath Rath. [A] [srath.com, about](https://srath.com/series/about), as recorded in the life area report, Voice section.
- He frames his approach as rooted in the classical texts (BPHS, Jaimini's Upadesa Sutras, Brihat Jataka, Saravali), argues Jaimini is not a separate school, and invites criticism of his explanations (*Crux* Preface). [A] Life area report, Voice section.
- He presents the Upadesa Sutras as explaining Parashara's teaching. The page carrying this is of mixed authorship (sections credited to Andrew Foss). [A, mixed authorship] [srath.com/?p=3437](https://srath.com/?p=3437).
- He states some methods are taught only inside Jyotish families. [A] Life area report, Voice section.

The organisation is the Sri Jagannath Center (SJC); its Jaimini Scholar Program and Parasara Jyotisa Course lesson pages were never reached in any round.

## 4. Source tiers and how much weight each gets

| Tier | Definition used in this set | Weight in the product |
|---|---|---|
| [A] | A book by Rath or an srath.com page with his byline. Co-credited pages are A with the flag stated | May be labelled "Rath". Still paraphrase; srath.com wording to be checked before any quote is reused |
| [B] | A student or SJC teacher (PVR, Sarbani Rath, Larsen, Cole, Poddar, Sood) | May be labelled "Rath school", never "Rath". Where B is the only source for an order (for example PVR's six steps), it is the working order but must carry the B label |
| [C] | Third party: listings, Scribd uploads with unverified bylines, general sites, critics | Context only; supports no rule |
| [Inferred] | Assembled by the research notes from A and B; no source states it | May shape product defaults, but must be shown as an assumption, never as a cited rule |
| Unpinned | Seen only in a search summary with no URL tied to it | Not a finding; listed only under gaps |

Reading-status modifiers that lower confidence inside a tier: "read through a summarising fetch", "search snippet only", "contents only", "scan of unconfirmed provenance", "page numbers approximate".

## 6. Accuracy and critique record

- No systematic or independent evaluation of Rath's predictions was found in any round. [Method summary report, Accuracy]
- Critique is anecdotal forum material: a 2003 message attributed to K.N. Rao saying most *Crux* charts were wrong, with no chart shown ([IndiaDivine](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1216566-knrao-about-sanjay-rath/)); a former client describing two 2004 predictions that failed, with no chart or transcript ([IndiaDivine](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1183996-sanjay-raths-failure/)). [C]
- Supporter material: a 2005 blog post listing claimed hits and one miss, seen as a search snippet ([jyotish-blog](https://jyotish-blog.blogspot.com/2005/04/predictive-success-etc.html)). [C]
- Several *Crux* worked forecasts were written in 1997-98 and the text gives no confirmed outcome. An srath.com article carries a box of past predictions presented as fulfilled (tone reading from a fetch summary). [A]
- Rath's own stated limits: explanations may be inadequate (*Crux* preface); overlooking Vimshottari riders gives wrong predictions (*Crux* introduction); the srath.com disclaimer says astrology does not replace professional advice and remedies carry no guarantee ([srath.com, disclaimer](https://srath.com/misc/media/disclaimer/)). [A]
- A general paper notes accuracy cannot be estimated from isolated predictions picked after the fact; it is not about astrology ([arXiv 1602.01013](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.01013)). [C]

No accuracy claim, either way, may be made for any rule in this set.

## Gaps and conflicts

- Authorship is unsettled for several srath.com pages (Tithi Pravesha co-credit, India 2008, Rajendra Prasad, Rani Rasmani, ?p=3437, Medical Astrology I).
- Book dates conflict: Narayana Dasa (1998 on srath.com; 2001 and 2019 in other listings); Upadesa Sutras (1997 vs 2002).
- *Crux* pagination differs across the three copies used (tenth-house chapter near p.242 in one copy, pp.433-490 in another).
- The earlier life area report and this round disagree on two dasha claims; see file 04.
- Students differ from Rath on Kunda, Shoola naming, the Kendradi/Tara trigger, Chara dasha and conditional dasha precedence (section 5).
- No accuracy evidence exists beyond anecdote.

## Use in the product

- Label each rule by tier when it reaches a technique or a reading: "Rath" only for [A], "Rath school" for [B], and mark [Inferred] orders as product assumptions.
- Where only PVR states an order or tie-break, use it as the working default, but keep it replaceable if Rath's unread books later disagree.
- Never present any rule as tested or accurate; the record has no test.
- Where students disagree with Rath (Kunda, Chara, conditional dasha precedence), prefer Rath's [A] rule as default and keep the student variant as a labelled alternative, not a silent merge.
- Rectification confidence feeds question design: because PVR rejects formula rectification, the assistant should confirm known life facts before relying on fine sub-periods (see file 02).
