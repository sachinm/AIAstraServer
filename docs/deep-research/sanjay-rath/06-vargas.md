# 06. Divisional charts (vargas) in Sanjay Rath's method

Purpose: Set out how Rath's school picks, casts, reads and times a divisional chart, which construction variants it uses, and what is still unsourced, so every life-area file (07-11) can point here for varga mechanics.
Sources: reports/Sanjay Rath life area readings.md (sect. 2); research_notes/Sanjay Rath life area readings/varga_charts.md, career.md; research_notes/Sanjay Rath open method gaps/varga_variants.md, d10_deities.md; research_notes/Sanjay Rath methods and precedence/other_life_areas.md, yogas_afflictions_remedies.md; existing_techniques_inventory.md sect. 3 (engine status).
Informs: every board that reads a varga (Self via D9, Love via D9, Work via D10, money via D2, family via D3/D7/D12, home via D4/D16, learning via D24, spirituality via D20, health via D6/D30) and the engine-request list.

**Tiers.** [A] Rath's own text (a book by him or an srath.com page under his byline). [B] a named student or SJC teacher (mostly P.V.R. Narasimha Rao). [C] third party, unattributed, or a classical text quoted without Rath's commentary. [Inferred] assembled or extended by the research notes, not stated by anyone.

**Citation note.** Two different copies of *Crux of Vedic Astrology: Timing of Events* were used upstream and their page numbers differ. "*Crux*" pages follow the earlier report's copies; "[Crux-1998]" pages follow the archive.org copy used in other_life_areas.md. Treat every page number as a locator to check in print. srath.com pages and several *Crux* chapters were read through a summarising fetch, so all statements are paraphrase.

**Engine key.** "computed" means the calculation engine exports it today. "partial" means the inputs are exported but the rule's own output is not. "not computed" means neither the output nor a direct input exists. Status is from the engine inventory, section 3.

### Order of judgment
1. Name the matter and pick the varga that carries it (table below).
2. Note the varga's plane (physical, conscious, sub-conscious, super-conscious, supra-conscious); deeper planes need more care in wording and a more exact birth time.
3. If the matter lives on two planes (for example property in D4 and comforts in D16, its higher octave), read both.

## 2. Reading any varga: karya bhava, karyesha and D1 rules inside the varga

### Order of judgment
Provenance: steps 1-4 are stated by Rath with the D10 as his only worked example; applying them to other vargas extends that example [Inferred]. Step 5 is Rao's [B].
1. Cast the varga (section 3 for variants).
2. Read it from its own lagna. Find the karya rasi (karya bhava), the house that is the varga's subject; his example is the 10th in the D10.
3. Check that the D1 lord of that house is well placed in the varga.
4. Find the karyesha(s): natural, chara and fixed karakas of the activity. Argala on each decides which role thrives or fails as dasas change.
5. Apply all D1 tools inside the varga: rasi and graha drishti, argala, yogas, arudhas, special lagnas, dignities.
6. [Sensitive] Subhapati check (below).
7. Time inside the varga (section 4).

### Rules

| # | Rule (paraphrase) | Tier | Citation | Engine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Casting: divide each sign into N equal parts and map each part to a fixed sign; his worked D3 sends the three 10-degree parts to the sign, its 5th and its 9th. | [A] | srath.com, Principles of Divisional Charts ("dividing a sign") | computed |
| 2.2 | Karya rasi is the house that is the varga's focus (10th in D10); its D1 lord should be well placed in the varga. | [A] | same article (technical terms) | partial (varga placements and dignities exported; no karya-rasi output) |
| 2.3 | Karya bhava for other vargas (2nd in D2, 3rd in D3, 4th in D4, 5th in D7, 7th in D9, 9th or 4th in D12 by parent) extends the D10 example. | [Inferred] | varga_charts.md, Inferences | partial |

## 4. Timing inside a varga: Narayana dasa per varga

### Order of judgment
1. Take the D1 lord of the matter's house.
2. Find the sign it occupies in the varga.
3. Start a Narayana dasa from that sign and read the varga as a separate chart.
4. Read transits from the varga lagna and varga Moon [B].

### Rules
