## 2. Marriage

### Order of judgment
Provenance: assembled from the seventh-house chapter and the Darapada article [Inferred]. Rath states that the Upapada and 7th alone cannot settle whether marriage occurs, so the A7 must also be read [A].
1. The 7th house, its lord and Venus (Jupiter too in a woman's chart).
2. The Upapada (marriage and spouse) and the A7.
3. Spouse's home region from the 7th lord counted from Venus.
4. The D9 and the Darakaraka as confirmers.
5. Time: Vimshottari linked to the D9, Narayana dasa (more accurate), transits of Jupiter and the Sun, Jupiter progression, Sudarshana chakra and Vivaha saham.
6. Second and third marriages.
