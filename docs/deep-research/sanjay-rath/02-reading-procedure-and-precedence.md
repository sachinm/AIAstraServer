# Sanjay Rath: reading procedure and precedence

Purpose: Set out the order in which the Rath school judges a matter, which reference point governs which question, and how conflicts between factors are resolved, with each rule's tier and engine status.
Sources: research_notes/Sanjay Rath methods and precedence/procedure_and_precedence.md (main), dasha_selection.md, verification_and_questions.md, yogas_afflictions_remedies.md, existing_techniques_inventory.md section 3; reports/Sanjay Rath life area readings.md sections 1-3 and 8; reports/Sanjay Rath method summary.md.
Informs: reading order for every board and chat reading; which reference point a technique counts from; confirm-before-predict questions; promise-before-timing gating.

Tier key: [A] Rath's own text; [B] student (mostly P.V.R. Narasimha Rao, *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*, cited "Rao IA" with section and page as the notes give them; PDF: https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf); [C] third party; [Inferred] assembled by the notes. *Crux* = *Crux of Vedic Astrology: Timing of Events*, read from scans; page numbers approximate.

## 2. The order of judgment for one matter

### 2.1 Rath's preparation list and ten house factors [A]

Preparation (*Crux* Ch. II sect. 7, approx. p.30): cast D1, D9, D3 and the relevant vargas, plus the arudha, Yamada and upagraha charts; mark the special lagnas (especially HL and GL); identify Karta, Kevala and Yogada planets; tabulate the eight chara karakas with Rahu counted from the end of its sign.

The ten factors for any house (*Crux* Ch. II sect. 7, approx. pp.30-31), checked in this order:

| # | Factor | Engine |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The house, its sign, planets in or aspecting it | computed |
| 2 | House lord, its strength and sign | computed |
| 3 | The lord's dispositor | computed (placements) |
| 4 | Navamsa lord of that dispositor | computed (D9) |
| 5 | Arudha and Yamada of the house | arudha computed; Yamada not listed in the inventory |
| 6 | Fixed, temporary and natural karakas of the house | chara and natural computed; sthira karakas not listed |
| 7 | The house from the AL and from the Yamada lagna | AL counts computed; Yamada not listed |
| 8 | Good and bad yogas in the house or with its lord | partial (engine yoga classes: raja, dhana, mahapurusha, viparita, neecha bhanga, dosha) |
| 9 | Argala and virodhargala on the house | partial (2, 4, 5, 11 with 12, 10, 9, 3; 3rd malefic and 7th/8th argala not exported) |
| 10 | The relevant saham, then a detailed check of the relevant divisional chart (Saravali cited: no astrology without vargas) | sahams computed only in the annual export; vargas computed |

### 2.2 Rao's six-step order [B]

The only explicit "do this first" order found in any tier. Rao IA sect. 13.4.1, pp.169-171:

1. Choose the divisional chart for the matter (D16 vehicles, D30 criminal psychology, D9 marriage, D20 religion, D24 learning, D10 career; the rasi chart may serve better than D9 where marriage is not a dharmic union).
2. Choose the house in that chart.
3. Choose the reference point: lagna, AL or a karaka (section 3).
4. Choose house or arudha: the arudha for how a matter is perceived (awards from A5).
5. Judge the influences: rasi drishti, graha drishti, argala; houses counted from it (kendras sustain, trines prosper, upachayas grow, dusthanas obstruct); badhakas from it.
6. Apply the classical standard results.

Then check strength, avasthas, ashtakavarga and yogas. Engine: all inputs computed except ashtakavarga beyond D1 and the missing argala types.

### 2.3 How the two fit [Inferred]

The ten factors are the content of Rao's steps 2-6; neither text cross-references the other. The order runs from the question outward: varga and house first, then reference, then influences. Rath's worked readings reorder steps by topic, so the sequence is a pattern, not a fixed script (life area report, section 1).

### 2.4 Rath's other printed ordering constraints [A]

- Read lagna, AL and Yamada lagna independently, then the dual karaka check (Sun and AK argala on the lagna; both read from AL for status and from Varnada for standing). *Crux* Ch. IV sect. 4.1, approx. p.54. Varnada is computed; Yamada not listed.
- Children: capacity before any curse; the D7 last and decisive. *Crux* Ch. VIII sect. 8.5.2, 8.5.5.
- Marriage: the UL and the 7th alone cannot settle whether marriage occurs; the A7 must be seen. [srath.com, Darapada and Upapada](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/).
- Yogas: noted first, then the planet that cancels them (Chart 11).

## 4. Rasi drishti against graha drishti

- Graha drishti is desire and comparatively temporary; all planets except Ketu aspect the 7th; special aspects for Mars (4, 8), Jupiter and Rahu (5, 9), Saturn (3, 10); Rahu also the 2nd sign. [A, fetch] [srath.com, Tattva & Dristi](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/amateur/tattva-dristi/). Engine: computed.
- Rasi drishti is knowledge and permanent: movable signs aspect fixed (not the adjacent), fixed aspect movable (not the adjacent), duals aspect each other; mutual; planets share their sign's aspects. No rule for disagreement is given. [A, fetch] same page. Engine: computed.
- Parashara taught sign aspects before planetary ones; rasi drishti can cause lasting damage. [A] [srath.com, Complexity of the Jaimini Sutra](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/complexity-of-jaimini-sutra/).
- Influence ranking: rasi drishti a small influence, graha drishti a more concrete one, argala decides parts of the matter. [B] Rao IA 10.4-10.5, pp.103-104.
- Working reading: rasi drishti for lasting links, graha drishti for active, dasha-driven influence. [Inferred]

## 7. Strength tie-break orders

Rath credits his sign-strength order to the Jaimini Upadesa Sutras and says sources differ on the order (*Narayana Dasa* and Mula Dasa pages, [A]). His own step list was not read. Rao's orders [B], applied only on a tie at the previous step:

| Purpose | Order | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Stronger of two signs (rasi dashas; graha arudha) | 1 more planets; 2 more of Jupiter, Mercury, own lord occupying or rasi-aspecting; 3 holds an exalted planet; 4 lord in sign of different parity; 5 dual > fixed > movable (useless for Narayana); 6 lord more advanced (nodes from end; stronger co-lord for Scorpio, Aquarius) | Rao IA 15.5.2, pp.203-205 |
| Stronger co-lord (Scorpio: Mars/Ketu; Aquarius: Saturn/Rahu) | if one co-lord sits in the sign, the other rules; else 1 more conjunctions; 2 more of Jupiter, Mercury, dispositor by rasi aspect; 3 exalted; 4 dual > fixed > movable; 5 for dasha length the longer dasha, for arudha lord the more advanced | Rao IA 15.5.1, pp.201-203 |
| Rudra | conjunctions; exalted or own; with exalted planets; more rasi aspects; advanced; with an affliction override | Rao IA 14, pp.182-183. [Sensitive] source rule, not for readings without owner decision |

Shadbala is used only to decide which planet in a yoga delivers it; ashtakavarga shows whether a planet can deliver in harmony with the chart; avasthas modify. [B] Rao IA 15.1-15.4, pp.187-201. Low bindus in the 10th weaken the whole chart [A] *Crux* Ch. XIII. Exaltation or debilitation in the D10 shows success, against ordinary dignity logic [A] *Crux* Ch. XIII.

Occupancy before dignity across all orders is the school's signature. [Inferred]

Engine: shadbala, ashtakavarga (D1 only), avasthas and dignities computed; no sign-versus-sign or co-lord comparison export is listed (partial).

## Use in the product

- Reading order for any matter: confirm the question, pick the varga and house, pick the reference by question type (section 3 table), decide house versus arudha, judge influences, then yogas, then varga confirmation, then timing. Label the order "Rath school, after Rao" and the ten factors "Rath".
- Before fine timing (sub-periods below antardasha), ask confirm questions that test the chart against known facts, in Rath's rectification order: siblings, then spouse, then children, then career. Skip any fact the person has not offered when it touches death or loss.
- Give perception questions (promotion, exams, reputation) to the AL; inner and mind questions to lagna and Moon; money to HL and Sree Lagna; power to GL.
- Do not predict a matter whose varga denies it; for children the D7 decides, and for any other matter treat varga denial as "uncertain" and ask rather than predict.
- Treat a timing claim as stronger when a rasi dasha agrees with Vimshottari, and say so plainly when they do not.
- Keep the spouse-significator conflict visible: read Venus for both sexes and present Jupiter in a female chart as an additional view, not a replacement.
- Engine work implied: 3rd-house malefic and 8th-house argala, badhaka from AL and Paka lagna, sign and co-lord strength comparison, Yamada lagna, sthira karakas, dashas seeded inside a varga.
