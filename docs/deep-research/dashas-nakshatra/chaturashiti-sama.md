# Chaturashiti-sama dasha (caturaśītisamā daśā)

> An 84-year "equal" dasha: seven planets, 12 years each, counted from Swati, for charts with the 10th lord in the 10th house. PVR links it to work (controlling planet Saturn). Research use; of possible interest for the Work board as agreement evidence.

## 2. When it applies

The lord of the 10th sign from lagna occupies that 10th sign (whole-sign houses). [TEXT]

## 3. Inputs required

Lagna sign; positions of the 10th lord; Moon longitude.

## 4. Calculation

Count from Swati (15) to the birth star, ÷ 7. Engine table (`chathuraaseethi_sama.py`, seed 15):

| Lord | Years | Nakshatras |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | 12 | 15, 22, 2, 9 |
| Moon | 12 | 16, 23, 3, 10 |
| Mars | 12 | 17, 24, 4, 11 |
| Mercury | 12 | 18, 25, 5, 12 |
| Jupiter | 12 | 19, 26, 6, 13 |
| Venus | 12 | 20, 27, 7, 14 |
| Saturn | 12 | 21, 1, 8 |

Total 84. Balance by 46.16. AD = 12 × 12 / 84 = 1.714 years each (equal ADs).
