# Dwisaptati-sama dasha (dvisaptatisamā daśā)

> A 72-year equal dasha of eight lords (Ketu left out), 9 years each, counted from Mula, for charts where the lagna lord is in the 7th or the 7th lord is in lagna. PVR ties it to desire and partnership (controlling planet Rahu). A candidate for agreement evidence on the Love board.

## 2. When it applies

Lagna lord in the 7th sign from lagna, or 7th lord in the lagna sign (46.38). [TEXT] An English summary of AE read it as "lagna lord in lagna or 7th"; the Sanskrit does not support that.

## 4. Calculation

Count from Mula (19) to the birth star, ÷ 8. Engine table (`dwisatpathi.py`, seed 19):

| Lord | Years | Nakshatras |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | 9 | 19, 27, 8, 16 |
| Moon | 9 | 20, 1, 9, 17 |
| Mars | 9 | 21, 2, 10, 18 |
| Mercury | 9 | 22, 3, 11 |
| Jupiter | 9 | 23, 4, 12 |
| Venus | 9 | 24, 5, 13 |
| Saturn | 9 | 25, 6, 14 |
| Rahu | 9 | 26, 7, 15 |

Total 72. AD = 9 × 9 / 72 = 1.125 years each.
