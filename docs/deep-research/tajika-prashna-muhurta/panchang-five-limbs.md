# The five limbs of the panchang (pañcāṅga: tithi, vāra, nakṣatra, yoga, karaṇa)

> The panchang ("five limbs") describes any moment by five lunar-solar factors: tithi (lunar day), vāra (weekday),
> nakṣatra (the Moon's asterism), yoga (Sun + Moon sum) and karaṇa (half-tithi). Every muhurta text grades a moment
> by these first. Each limb has classes with recommended and forbidden uses. It is the most widely used Vedic timing
> tool and appears in every Indian almanac. The limbs can be computed exactly. Their use-tables come from
> *Muhūrta-cintāmaṇi* (Rāma Daivajña, c. 1600) and similar texts, and differ in details by region and lineage.

## 2. When it applies

Every day and place: the shared Lucky Times day table. Every election (muhurta) for an event.

## 3. Inputs required

Sun and Moon sidereal longitudes; sunrise for the place and date (the limb "of the day" is the one at sunrise, with
change times); weekday from sunrise to sunrise.

## 4. Calculation

| Limb | Formula | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Tithi | (Moon − Sun) / 12° | 30 (Śukla 1–15, Kṛṣṇa 1–15/Amāvāsyā) |
| Vāra | Weekday, sunrise to sunrise | 7 |
| Nakṣatra | Moon / 13°20′ | 27 (28 with Abhijit in some muhurta uses) |
| Yoga | (Sun + Moon) / 13°20′ | 27 |
| Karaṇa | Half-tithi (6°) | 11: 7 movable (Bava, Bālava, Kaulava, Taitila, Gara, Vaṇija, Viṣṭi) ×8, and 4 fixed (Śakuni, Catuṣpada, Nāga, Kiṃstughna) |

## 5. Interpretation rules (good and bad uses)

**Tithi groups [TEXT, MC ch. 1]:** Nandā (1, 6, 11): joy, festivities. Bhadrā (2, 7, 12): good works, building.
Jayā (3, 8, 13): victory, contests, legal. Riktā (4, 9, 14): "empty", avoided for auspicious starts, used for
destructive or aggressive acts. Pūrṇā (5, 10, 15): completion. Amāvāsyā: avoided for auspicious starts.
Nandā on Friday, Bhadrā on Wednesday, Jayā on Tuesday, Riktā on Saturday and Pūrṇā on Thursday give siddha (success)
combinations [COMM]. Some tithi–weekday pairs are *dagdha* (burnt) and avoided.

**Vāra nature [TEXT, MC ch. 1–2]:** Sunday fixed (sthira/dhruva), Monday movable (cara), Tuesday fierce (ugra),
Wednesday mixed (miśra), Thursday light or swift (laghu/kṣipra), Friday soft (mṛdu), Saturday sharp (tīkṣṇa/dāruṇa).
Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday (saumya-vāra) are generally preferred for auspicious acts.

**Nakṣatra classes [TEXT, MC ch. 2 Nakṣatra-prakaraṇa]:**

| Class | Stars | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| Dhruva / sthira (fixed) | Rohiṇī, U.Phalgunī, U.Āṣāḍhā, U.Bhādrapadā | Foundations, house-building, coronation, permanent things, planting |
| Cara / cala (movable) | Svātī, Punarvasu, Śravaṇa, Dhaniṣṭhā, Śatabhiṣā | Travel, vehicles, gardens, movement |
| Ugra / krūra (fierce) | P.Phalgunī, P.Āṣāḍhā, P.Bhādrapadā, Bharaṇī, Maghā | Destruction, fire, weapons, confrontation |
| Miśra / sādhāraṇa (mixed) | Kṛttikā, Viśākhā | Fire work, mixed affairs |
| Kṣipra / laghu (swift) | Aśvinī, Puṣya, Hasta, (Abhijit) | Trade, medicine, learning, quick ventures, travel |
| Mṛdu / maitra (soft) | Mṛgaśirā, Citrā, Anurādhā, Revatī | Friendship, arts, clothing, romance, music |
| Tīkṣṇa / dāruṇa (sharp) | Mūla, Jyeṣṭhā, Ārdrā, Āśleṣā | Separation, exorcism, harsh acts |

Also face classes: ūrdhva-mukha (upward), adho-mukha (downward), tiryaṅ-mukha (sideways), used for building, digging and travel [TEXT, MC ch. 2].

**Yoga [TEXT, MC ch. 1]:** avoid Viṣkambha, Atigaṇḍa, Śūla, Gaṇḍa, Vyāghāta, Vajra, Vyatīpāta, Parigha and Vaidhṛti.
[COMM] Several authorities avoid only the opening ghaṭīs of the milder ones and the whole of Vyatīpāta and Vaidhṛti
(exact ghaṭī counts unverified). Also the weekday + star "Ānandādi" yogas and the Amṛta-siddhi / Sarvārtha-siddhi
combinations (good) and Mṛtyu / Utpāta (bad) [TEXT, MC ch. 1].

**Karaṇa [TEXT, MC ch. 1]:** Viṣṭi (Bhadrā) is avoided for auspicious acts (see `muhurta-doshas.md`). Śakuni, Catuṣpada
and Nāga are inauspicious for starts. Kiṃstughna (first half of Śukla 1) is good. The six movable karaṇas apart from Viṣṭi are acceptable.

## 6. Variants and disputes

- Which limb counts for the day: at sunrise (traditional almanac) vs. at the moment of the act (muhurta). Both are needed.
- Tithi kṣaya and vṛddhi (a tithi skipped or repeated across sunrises): regional almanac rules.
- Yoga ghaṭī exclusions differ by author.

## 7. Worked micro-example

Thursday, Śukla Pañcamī (Pūrṇā), Puṣya nakṣatra (kṣipra) at sunrise: Pūrṇā on Thursday is siddha, and Thursday +
Puṣya is the famous Guru-Puṣya (Amṛta-siddhi). This is an excellent day to start trade or learning, unless Viṣṭi or
an avoided yoga covers the chosen hour.
