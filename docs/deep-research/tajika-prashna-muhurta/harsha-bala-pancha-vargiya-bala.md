# Harsha bala and Pancha-vargiya bala (harṣa-bala, pañcavargīya-bala)

> These are the two Tajika strength measures for planets in an annual chart. *Pañcavargīya bala* ("five-dignity
> strength") adds up a planet's standing in its sign, exaltation, term (haddā), drekkāṇa and navāṃśa on a 20-point
> scale. It decides the year lord and grades every yoga. *Harṣa bala* ("joy strength") gives 5 points for each of four
> conditions of "joy" (a house of joy, own or exalted sign, a gender-matched sign group, day or night). Both are
> standard in Tajika and found in all major texts. Point values and friendship fractions differ by author.

## 2. When it applies

Annual and monthly charts and Tajika praśna: choosing the year and month lords, grading Itthaśāla and Kamboola, and judging whether a significator can deliver.

## 3. Inputs required

Planet longitudes (D1), drekkāṇa, navāṃśa, the haddā table, exaltation degrees, Tajika friendship (`maitrī-cakra`, HR ch. 2 §4),
house positions in the annual chart, and day or night at the revolution.

## 4. Calculation

### 4.1 Pañcavargīya bala [TEXT]

| Dignity | Full points (Grahajñābharaṇa) |
|---|---|
| Gṛha (sign lord) | 30 |
| Ucca (exaltation) | 20 (graded, below) |
| Haddā (term) | 15 |
| Drekkāṇa | 10 |
| Navāṃśa | 5 |
| Sum | 80. **Divide by 4, so the maximum is 20** |

- Exaltation strength (Vāmana): the distance from the debilitation degree (folded to ≤180°) ÷ 9, so 0–20.
  Exaltation degrees (Yādava 4.16): Sun Ar 10, Moon Ta 3, Mars Cp 28, Mercury Vi 15, Jupiter Cn 5, Venus Pi 27, Saturn Li 20.
- Sign, haddā, drekkāṇa and navāṃśa: the full value if the planet is in its own division, and a fraction if it is in a
  friend's, neutral or enemy's division. Fractions disagree:
  Saṃj. 1.40 as summarised is internally inconsistent (marked **unverified**). *Tājikatilaka*: great friend ¾, friend ½,
  neutral ¼, enemy ⅛, great enemy 1/16. *Paddhatibhūṣaṇa* 30–31: friend ½, enemy ¼.
  (*Tājikasāra* 74–75 swaps the haddā and drekkāṇa values to 10 and 15. Also flagged in HR.)
- Haddā table (Saṃj. 1.33–38), each as planet and degrees in order:
  Ar Ju6 Ve6 Me8 Ma5 Sa5 · Ta Ve8 Me6 Ju8 Sa5 Ma3 · Ge Me6 Ve6 Ju5 Ma7 Sa6 · Cn Ma7 Ve6 Me6 Ju7 Sa4 ·
  Le Ju6 Ve5 Sa7 Me6 Ma6 · Vi Me7 Ve10 Ju4 Ma7 Sa2 · Li Sa6 Me8 Ju7 Ve7 Ma2 · Sc Ma7 Ve4 Me8 Ju5 Sa6 ·
  Sg Ju12 Ve5 Me4 Ma5 Sa4 · Cp Me7 Ju7 Ve8 Sa4 Ma4 · Aq Me7 Ve6 Ju7 Ma5 Sa5 · Pi Ve12 Ju4 Me3 Ma9 Sa2.
  (Gemini and Sagittarius differ from Ptolemy's Egyptian terms. HR footnote.)
- Thresholds: *Tājikasāra*: ≤5 powerless, ≤10 middling, ≤15 excellent, 20 best. Yādava 4.40 (Hillāja): <5 none,
  =5 little, >5 middling, >10 full. Balabhadra for the year lord: ≤6 little, >6 middling, >12 full.

### 4.2 Harṣa bala [TEXT] (Tejaḥsiṃha, *Daivajñālaṃkṛti* 7.1–2)

Four places of joy. Each is worth 5 points (*Paddhatibhūṣaṇa* 20), for a maximum of 20:

1. **Sthāna (house of joy)** in the annual chart: Sun 9th, Moon 3rd, Mars 6th, Mercury 1st, Jupiter 11th, Venus 5th,
   Saturn 12th. (Pairing by order in the source. The same as the Hellenistic joys.)
2. **Own sign** (Samarasiṃha) or own and exaltation sign (Tejaḥsiṃha).
3. **Gender group** of houses from the lagna: female planets (Moon, Venus) in houses 1–3 and 7–9. Male planets (Sun,
   Mars, Jupiter) in houses 4–6 and 10–12. (Mercury and Saturn treatment varies. The fetched HR page did not list it.
   The standard modern form follows.)
4. **Day/night**: male planets in a day revolution, female planets in a night revolution.

Variants: Yādava 4.36–37 counts five places at 4 points each. Tuka counts 1 unit each. *Praśnavaiṣṇava* 2.30 adds
extra joy houses (Sun: lagna; Moon: 10, 4, 2; Mars: 7; Mercury: 4; Saturn: 9).

## 5. Interpretation rules

- [TEXT] "Without dignity a planet is not strong" (Samarasiṃha). Use pañcavargīya to choose the year lord and month
  lord and to judge whether a yoga's planets can deliver.
- [TEXT] Joy shows a planet "rejoicing" and able to give its good results. Full joy in all four places is the best.
- [MODERN] Many software packages and books also compute Dvādaśavargīya bala (12 dignities) and Tajika ṣaḍbala
  (HR ch. 2 §6–8). They are less used.

## 6. Variants and disputes

Friendship fractions (above). Exaltation in the joy list (Tejaḥsiṃha yes, Samarasiṃha no). 4 vs. 5 places of joy. Point values per joy.

## 7. Worked micro-example

Venus at 28° Pisces in a night revolution, in the 5th house of the year. Sign: Jupiter's sign; Jupiter is an enemy in
Tajika friendship, say, so 30 × ¼ = 7.5. Exaltation: 28° Pi is 1° from the 27° peak, so about 179/9 = 19.9. Haddā (Pi
28°): Saturn's term, an enemy, so 15 × ¼ = 3.75. Drekkāṇa and navāṃśa: per tables. The sum ÷ 4 is about 8, middling.
Harṣa: the 5th house is Venus's joy, so +5. Night and female, so +5. Pisces is not own but is exaltation, so +5 by
Tejaḥsiṃha only. The 5th house is in the male group, so 0. Total 10–15.

- Engine (exists): `strength.pancha_vargeeya_bala`, `strength.harsha_bala`, `dwadhasa_vargeeya_bala`
  (`horoscope/chart/strength.py`). `const.hadda_lords` matches Saṃj. 1.33–38 in the rows checked (Ar, Ta, Ge).
  `const.hadda_points = [15, 7.5, 3.75]` (own, friend, enemy).
- **Check:** `const.harsha_bala_houses = [8,2,5,0,10,11,11]` (0-based, Sun..Saturn) gives Venus's joy house as the
  **12th**. The texts give the **5th** (Daivajñālaṃkṛti 7.1–2 via HR, and the Hellenistic source). Likely an engine
  error. File it for the engine-rule-verifier. `harsha_bala_feminine_houses = [0,1,2,6,7,8]` (houses 1–3, 7–9) and
  masculine `[3,4,5,9,10,11]` match the standard form.
- Serve per person-year: `strength.pancha_vargiya{planet: {griha, ucca, hadda, drekkana, navamsa, total_20, grade}}`
  and `strength.harsha{planet: {sthana, swa, gender, day_night, total_20}}`, on the varṣa chart (not natal). Today
  `annual.tajaka` does not export them.
- Weight: infrastructure for the year lord and yogas. It can also give a simple "planet strong this year" fact.
