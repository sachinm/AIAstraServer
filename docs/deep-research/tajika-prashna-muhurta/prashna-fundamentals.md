# Prashna (praśna, horary astrology): fundamentals

> Prashna answers a specific question from a chart cast for the moment the question is put, not from the birth
> chart. The rising sign (praśna lagna), the Moon, an "ārūḍha" sign chosen by the querist's position or touch, and
> the lords of the houses involved decide yes or no, how, and when. Three traditions matter. (1) Kerala: *Praśna Mārga*
> (1649), with ārūḍha, Trisphuṭa and Aṣṭamaṅgala. (2) Tajika: Nīlakaṇṭha's *Praśnatantra*, which uses the Tajika yogas
> between the lagna lord and the matter's lord. (3) The short classical *Ṣaṭpañcāśikā* of Pṛthuyaśas (son of
> Varāhamihira), 56 verses. Prashna is used widely in India. It needs the querist's moment, which suits a chat assistant.

## 2. When it applies

Any concrete question asked at a known moment: "will I get this job?", "will the relationship resume?", "where is
the lost item?", "will the sick person recover?". *Praśna Mārga* covers marriage (ch. XVII), progeny (XVIII),
disease (XII–XIII, XXIII), lost articles (XXIX), travel and return, longevity (IX–XI) and more.

## 3. Inputs required

Exact time and place of the question (or when the astrologer receives it). The praśna chart (lagna, planets, Gulika,
Moon) with panchanga at that moment. Optionally the querist's natal chart. For ārūḍha: the querist's choice or position
(a number, a direction, a touched sign). For Aṣṭamaṅgala: three numbers (the cowrie count).

## 4. Calculation / rules

**Praśna Mārga (chapter level) [TEXT, verses not verified]:**

| Element | Ch. | Rule (paraphrase) |
|---|---|---|
| Ārūḍha rāśi | II, IV | The sign the querist faces, sits towards or touches on a 12-sign diagram (or a gold-piece placement). It is read like a second lagna for the matter |
| Udaya (praśna) lagna | V | The sign rising at the query, with Kunda correction to refine it |
| Moon | V, VIII | The Moon's sign and nakṣatra show the mind and the matter's course. Chandra kriyā, velā and avasthā (appendices 4–6) |
| Trisphuṭa | V–VI | Lagna + Moon + Gulika longitudes (sum, mod 360°). Its nakṣatra and position show disease, danger and death timing. Catuḥsphuṭa adds the Sun. Pañcasphuṭa adds Rāhu |
| Aṣṭamaṅgala | IV, VII | 108 cowries are divided into three heaps, each counted in 8s. The remainders, mapped to the 8 directions, planets and dhvaja etc., give the reading |
| Five sūtras | VI | Ashtamangala, ārūḍha, udaya, Moon and planets read together. **Exact list to verify** |
| Timing | X, XIV | Dasha from the praśna. Transits of Saturn, Jupiter, the Sun and the Moon (niryāṇa); fructification |

**Tajika praśna (Nīlakaṇṭha, Praśnatantra) [COMM]:** take the lagna lord (the querist) and the lord of the
house of the question (the matter). An Itthaśāla between them means yes. Nakta or Yamayā means yes through
others. Īsarāpha means it has gone. Maṇaū, Radda or Khallāsara means a block or no. The Moon is co-significator
(Kamboola). Timing is by the degrees still to perfection (`tajika-yogas.md`).

**Ṣaṭpañcāśikā [COMM, verse not verified]:** a lagna strong and aspected by benefics, with benefics in angles, means
success. Malefics in the angles or the 8th mean failure. A movable lagna or sign means quick results or movement, a
fixed one means delay or staying, a dual one means mixed. The rising navāṃśa and the lagna lord's position help time results.

## 5. Interpretation rules

- [TEXT] The Moon is central in every prashna school. A void, afflicted or 6/8/12 Moon weakens any "yes".
- [TEXT] Ārūḍha and udaya lagna together: agreement (benefics, strong lords) gives a clear answer. Disagreement gives a mixed answer.
- [REGIONAL] Aṣṭamaṅgala praśna is a Kerala temple and household ritual. It is not a technique to automate.

## 6. Variants and disputes

- Kerala (ārūḍha, sphuṭas, Gulika) vs. Tajika (yogas) vs. North Indian KP-style horary [MODERN, out of scope].
- Gulika's longitude: rise at the start of Saturn's portion vs. middle (*Praśna Mārga* appendix 1). The same issue as in natal work.

## 7. Worked micro-example

"Will I get the job?" asked at 10:05. The lagna is Gemini, with Mercury (lagna lord) at 12° Virgo. The 10th lord is
Jupiter at 15° Pisces. Mercury is faster and behind, and Virgo and Pisces are 7th from each other (open enemy). This
is an applying Itthaśāla through a hostile aspect, so yes, after struggle. If the Moon also applies to Jupiter, it is
Kamboola, a stronger yes.

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra

- Engine (exists): `prashna_export.run_prashna` serves a chart at the query moment with `hora_at`, `panchanga_at`
  and chart tables. Engine additions for a horary feature: Tajika yogas for a named house (the same addition as in
  `tajika-yogas.md`), Moon condition flags (void, waning, combust, 6/8/12), Gulika and Trisphuṭa / Catuḥsphuṭa /
  Pañcasphuṭa longitudes and nakṣatras, and the rising navāṃśa. Ārūḍha and Aṣṭamaṅgala need user ritual input and
  should not be built.
- Product fit: chat questions about a specific event ("will X happen") could be answered with natal promise + dasha
  + transit, **plus** a praśna chart for the moment of asking as a second witness. That fits "confirm before
  predict". Keep it off the boards. Weight: secondary to natal for life questions. Primary only for one-off matters
  (lost items, a pending call).
- Pitfall: prashna needs the exact moment and place of asking. The server must record the message timestamp and the
  user's place, and the engine computes from them.
