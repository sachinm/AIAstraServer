# Muhurta for specific events: marriage, travel, starting a business (vivāha, yātrā, vyāpāra-ārambha)

> *Muhūrta-cintāmaṇi* gives each life event its own chapter. Each lists which tithis, weekdays, nakṣatras, lagnas
> and planetary conditions suit that act. Then the general doṣas and the personal filters (tārābala, candrabala,
> and for marriage the planetary strengths of bride and groom) are applied. Indian families and priests use this
> daily. The tables are traditional and stable in their core, with regional differences at the edges.

## 2. When it applies

Choosing a date and time window for the person's event, at the event's place.

## 3. Inputs required

The place-day panchang with change times, the lagna table for the day (udaya-lagna), planet longitudes (combustion,
the 7th and 8th of the lagna), the person's (and partner's) natal Moon star and sign, and for marriage the natal
positions for Guru-bala, Sūrya-bala and Candra-bala.

## 4. Rules

### 4.2 Travel (Yātrā-prakaraṇa) [TEXT, chapter level, verses unverified]

- Stars: Aśvinī, Punarvasu, Anurādhā, Mṛgaśirā, Puṣya, Revatī, Hasta, Śravaṇa and Dhaniṣṭhā are best (cara and kṣipra classes).
  Avoid Bharaṇī, Kṛttikā, Ārdrā, Āśleṣā, Maghā, Viśākhā, Jyeṣṭhā and Mūla for starting out [COMM].
- **Diśā-śūla** (weekday direction ban) [COMM]: East: Saturday and Monday. South: Thursday. West: Sunday and Friday. North: Tuesday and Wednesday. Nakṣatra-śūla by direction also exists.
- Yoginī direction by tithi, and the Moon's direction (candra-vāsa): the Moon in front or to the right is good, behind or to the left is bad.
- Pañcaka: no southward travel. Avoid Riktā tithis, Viṣṭi, and the 8th tithi.
- Personal: tārābala and candrabala. Lagna: movable or dual, with benefics, the lagna lord strong.

### 4.3 Starting a business or shop, buying and selling [TEXT, Nakṣatra-prakaraṇa, verses unverified]

- Opening a shop (āpaṇa/vipaṇi): kṣipra (Aśvinī, Puṣya, Hasta), mṛdu (Mṛgaśirā, Citrā, Anurādhā, Revatī) and dhruva
  (Rohiṇī, the three Uttarās) stars. Weekdays Wednesday, Thursday, Friday (Monday acceptable).
- Buying (kraya) vs. selling (vikraya) stars are listed separately in MC ch. 2 [COMM]. Commonly, buy in Aśvinī, Svātī,
  Citrā, Revatī, Śatabhiṣā, Śravaṇa, and sell in Bharaṇī, Kṛttikā, Āśleṣā, Viśākhā and the three Pūrvās. **Verify.**
- Lagna: a fixed lagna for lasting ventures. Strong 2nd, 10th and 11th. Benefics in angles. The Moon not in 4/8/12 from the lagna.
- Tithi: avoid Riktā, Amāvāsyā and the 8th. Prefer Nandā, Bhadrā, Pūrṇā (Śukla).
- Horā (`hora.md`) and choghadiya Labh/Shubh/Amrit as fine layers [REGIONAL].

## 5. Interpretation rules

- [TEXT] Order of judgement (secondary, citing MC, Dharmasindhu, Raman): (1) no Venus or Jupiter combustion (marriage);
  (2) nakṣatra; (3) lagna as corrector; (4) tithi, yoga, karaṇa and vāra; (5) personal tārā and candrabala.
- [COMM] When no ideal day exists in a range, choose the day with the fewest and mildest doṣas, and repair it with a strong lagna, Abhijit or Godhūli.

## 6. Variants and disputes

The marriage star list (MC vs. modern), Sunday for marriage (MC yes vs. Raman no), diśā-śūla tables (regional variants), and buying/selling stars.

  muhurta says *when to start*.
