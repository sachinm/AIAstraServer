# The sixteen Tajika yogas (ṣoḍaśa tājika-yoga)

> Tajika "yogas" are configurations between two significators, typically the lagna lord and the lord of the matter
> (kārya-pati). Each depends on Tajika aspects, orbs, relative speed and dignity. Together they answer: will the
> matter happen this year (or for this question), how (directly, through a third person, after trouble), and will it
> last? Gansten and Wikander (2011, *Annals of Science* 68) show the sixteen derive from Sahl ibn Bishr, through
> intermediaries, into Nīlakaṇṭha and Gaṇeśa. Itthaśāla, Īsarāpha, Nakta, Yamayā, Kamboola and Radda are used
> everywhere. The rest are rarely used, and texts define them differently.

## 2. When it applies

Annual, monthly and daily charts, and Tajika praśna. For annual life areas, the significators are the varṣa-lagna lord
and the lord of the house of the matter (7th for marriage, 10th for career, etc.). The sahams can stand in as well.

## 3. Inputs required

Longitudes, speeds, retrograde and combustion flags, house positions, dignity (own, exalted, haddā, drekkāṇa,
navāṃśa, friend or enemy sign), and Tajika aspects with orbs (`tajika-aspects-deeptamsha.md`).

## 4. Calculation / rule (each yoga)

Notation: L = lagna lord, K = lord of the matter (kārya). "Faster" means greater daily motion.

| # | Yoga | Definition (source) | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ikkavāla | All planets in angles and succedents (1,4,7,10 / 2,5,8,11) (Saṃj. 2.17). Yādava 6.5: two such planets in aspect | Dominion and happiness. Reverses an expected misfortune [TEXT] |
| 2 | Induvāra | All planets in cadent houses (3,6,9,12) (Saṃj. 2.17) | Never praised [TEXT] |
| 3 | Itthaśāla (muthaśila) | The faster planet is behind the slower one, within orbs, in Tajika aspect, and applying (Tājikālaṃkāra, quoted). Sub-kinds: *pūrṇa* when the gap is ≤ ½′ (Yādava 6.7); *bhaviṣyat* (future) when the faster is at a sign's end about to enter orb (Vāmana) or beyond orb (Samarasiṃha). It works across signs (Daivajñālaṃkṛti 9.10). A slower planet retrograde ahead counts; a faster planet retrograde behind does not | The matter happens. Strong when helped by benefics and friendly aspects (Saṃj. 2.21–23). About 12 days per degree to perfection (Yādava 6.35) |
| 4 | Īsarāpha (muśarif) | The faster has passed the slower by ≥1° (TBh 4.10). Under 1° it is still Itthaśāla | Evil. The matter has gone. Hillāja: if formed by benefics, not destructive. Yādava 6.12: good beyond 12° |
| 5 | Nakta | L and K have no aspect. A **faster** third planet aspects both and carries light from one to the other (Saṃj. 2.25–27) | The matter happens through another person. Yādava 6.14 allows it even with a sign aspect beyond orb |
| 6 | Yamayā | As Nakta but the carrier is **slower** than both (TBh 4.13; Saṃj. 2.29–30 example) | Through a third party, e.g. a counsellor |
| 7 | Maṇaū | Mars or Saturn makes a hostile aspect (1, 4, 7) within orb to the faster significator, cutting an Itthaśāla (Yādava 6.19; Samarasiṃha MJ 3.8) | The promised matter fails. Fear from enemies |
| 8 | Kamboola | L–K in Itthaśāla **and** the Moon in muthaśila with one or both (Saṃj. 2.36). 16 grades: Moon's dignity × other's dignity (superior = own or exalted; middling = haddā, drekkāṇa or navāṃśa; inferior = enemy or fall; neutral) (Saṃj. 2.37–47) | Strengthens the Itthaśāla by grade. Insignificant if one is debilitated and the other undignified (2.53–54) |
| 9 | Gairi-kamboola | Itthaśāla present, the Moon unaspected by L and K, void, but entering the next sign to join a planet in its own or exaltation sign (Yādava 6.25–26; Saṃj. 2.58–60 example) | Like Kamboola if that planet is dignified. Otherwise evil. Gain through another |
| 10 | Khallāsara | The Moon has no aspect or muthaśila with L or K (Yādava 6.27) | Destroys the matter, like kemadruma |
| 11 | Radda | Itthaśāla in which a significator is retrograde, combust or setting, in 6/8/12, or overcome by a malefic (Yādava 6.28). TBh 4.26: faster in a cadent and slower in an angle gives delay then success; the reverse gives success then ruin | Spoils or reverses results |
| 12 | Duḥphāli-kuttha | The slower planet is in own, exaltation or triplicity sign and in muthaśila with an undignified faster planet (TBh 4.27) | Success all the same |
| 13 | Dutthottha-davīra | Weak L and K in a yoga, one of them also joined to a dignified planet; or two dignified faster planets apply to the weak slower lord (*Jīrṇatājika*) | Success with another's help |
| 14 | Tambīra | No L–K yoga, but one, strong at a sign's end, passes its light to a strong planet in the next sign within orb (*Hāyanasindhu*) | Very favourable. The matter is done |
| 15 | Kuttha | A strength condition. The planet is dignified, in an angle, joined to or aspecting the lagna, with benefics, free of malefics, risen, direct and time-strong (TBh 4.29a) | Full results from that planet |
| 16 | Duruḥpha | A weakness condition. Set, retrograde, with or aspected by malefics, in 6/8/12, in its detriment (7th from own), in Itthaśāla with a fallen planet, or with Rāhu or Ketu (TBh 4.30). Moon special case (Saṃj. 2.73–74): 12th from the Sun, void, waning, at a sign's end, or under a Mars or Saturn kṣut aspect | Weak and harmful |

## 5. Interpretation rules

- [TEXT] *Jīrṇatājika* summary (HR ch. 3 §3): Itthaśāla, the native makes it happen. Yamayā or Nakta, it comes from
  another. Īsarāpha, the native destroys it. Maṇaū, another's hand prevents it. Khallāsara, nothing comes.
- [TEXT] Itthaśāla between the lagna lord and a house lord brings gain from that house unless Maṇaū, Khallāsara or
  Radda block it. Yamayā, Nakta or Tambīra can deliver even without an Itthaśāla (HR ch. 6 §1).
- [TEXT] Tājikasāra 93: a full Itthaśāla of benefics in the varṣa lagna brings comfort, wealth and beauty.
- [MODERN] Today's practice (Raman, K.N. Rao and others) uses mainly the first eleven, and treats Kuttha and Duruḥpha
  as strength flags, not yogas.

## 6. Variants and disputes

- Ikkavāla and Induvāra: all planets by house class (Saṃj.) vs. a two-planet aspect (Yādava). HR rejects Balabhadra's
  preference for Yādava.
- Nakta's direction of light (Saṃj. vs. Gansten's note that it lacks Arabic support). Yādava's relaxation.
- Kamboola: four grades (Saṃj.) vs. five categories (Yādava 6.23–24). Moon with one vs. both significators.
- Maṇaū: whether the same-sign aspect counts (*ekagataḥ*, tentative).
- Secondary web summaries reverse or merge definitions. One calls Yamayā "prohibition", which is wrong. Use only the texts above.

## 7. Worked micro-example (Saṃj. 2.29–30, as quoted)

Libra lagna. Venus (L) at 16° Aries. The Moon (10th lord, K) at 6° Taurus. Venus and the Moon have no aspect (adjacent
signs). Jupiter at 10° Cancer is slower than both, aspects both and carries the Moon's light to Venus: Yamayā. The
querent attains dominion through a counsellor.
