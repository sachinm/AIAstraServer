# 06 · The overpowered benefic

A benefic that is strong by nature but defeated: in planetary war, combust, in an enemy sign, or hemmed by malefics.
Doc 13 has no row for it. Read-status labels are as in `01`.

## 2. Timing

- **The dasha of the defeated benefic itself** gives "only untoward results" if it is in an enemy sign or
  debilitation (PD 20.27). Combust counts as debilitation (PD 20.30).
- **Recovery:** a planet "eclipsed or posited in its depression or inimical house" that occupies an auspicious sign or
  amsa gives mixed results, and good results "in the latter half of the Dasa" (PD 20.57; already in doc 13 §2.3).
- No source times a war defeat separately.
