## 3. Verdict on doc 13's mapping

**Change it.**

- **Keep the 7th** (trade and partners: BPHS 11.8; Uttara Kalamrita V adds "arguments"). **Add the A7** for business
  partners [A Rath].
- **Replace the 3rd with the 2nd.** Phaladeepika 1.10 puts documents and the spoken word in the 2nd. No text found
  makes the 3rd the house of signatures or documents. Rath's only 3rd-house contract rule (rule 1.7) is about short
  contracts in a career, not about breaking them.
- **Keep Mercury** as the significator of documents and writing (Uttara Kalamrita V), labelled [TEXT].
- **Add the Mars–Saturn conjunction** (Brihat Jataka 14.3) as the nearest classical marker of a person who does not
  keep a compact. Tag it [TEXT] for the verse and [INFERRED] for the contract reading. It describes the person's own
  trait.
- **For the dispute that follows**, add Rath's litigation reading: the 6th through the AL, the A6 and the A7
  [A Rath], with the 6th from the AL as [B student].
- **Row tag in doc 13:** "Text (PD 1.10; BPHS 11.8); Text via our gloss (BJ 14.3); Rath (A7; litigation); Ours
  (combining them)".
