**An observation, [INFERRED].** Rath's primary years of fortune (7.4) match the folk maturity ages (7.5) for six of
seven planets, give or take a year: Sun 22, Moon 24, Mars 28, Mercury 32, Venus 25-26, Saturn 36. Jupiter is the
exception (33-34 against 16). The two unlabelled rows, 41-42 and 45-46, sit near the nodes' 42 and 48. So Rath's
table is probably a version of the same tradition. If it is confirmed in print, the maturity table gains a Rath tag
for those six planets, and Jupiter should follow Rath (33-34), not 16.

## 2. Timing

- PD 20.33(a) gives a phase of life (the middle), not ages. The text sets no lifespan, so it stays a label.
- PD 20.33(b), if it is a dasha rule, places a planet's results at the start, middle or end of its own period. That
  is useful for "within this maha dasha", but not for life ages.
- Rath's yoga-placement rule (7.3) gives early, mid or late life, not exact ages.

## 3. Verdict on doc 13
