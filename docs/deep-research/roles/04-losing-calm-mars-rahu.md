No classical verse was found that ties **Rahu** to losing calm through the Moon. Rahu's classical link is falsehood
and gambling (`02`, `03`). Ketu, not Rahu, carries "instability of mind" in Uttara Kalamrita.

## 2. Timing

- No source times loss of calm.
- BPHS 45 says the agitated planet damages the house it occupies. By the general dasha rule, that planet's period
  would carry the damage [INFERRED].

## 3. Verdict on doc 13's mapping

**Narrow it and re-tag it.**

- **Keep the Moon** as the seat of calm [TEXT: PD 2.2, 15.15].
- **Make Mars the main disruptor** [TEXT: PD 2.10; UK V anger; PD 8.9 Mars in the 5th]. **Add Saturn** alongside
  Mars. Saturn has its own anger and 5th-house rules (PD 2.14, 8.22), and one of BJ 23.13's two combinations pairs
  Saturn in the lagna with Mars in a trine or the 7th. (The other pairs Mars in the 7th with Jupiter in the lagna.)
- **Rahu stays Ours** (or Modern). No text found. Consider adding **Ketu** for scattered focus [TEXT: UK V].
- **The 5th is supported** by PD 8.9 and 8.22 (Mars or Saturn in the 5th spoils the reasoning), so it is now Text.
  For the 4th, BPHS 11.5 gives happiness (*saukhya*, with the mother), so the 4th as **happiness** is Text. **"Peace
  of mind"** is our gloss; it was not found in BPHS 11.5, PD 1.11-12 or the UK lists read. Tag the 4th "Text
  (happiness); Ours (peace of mind)".
- **Use the BPHS 45 states** (kshobhita, kshudhita, trishita) as the planet-level test. They are already exported by
  the engine (`avasthas_export.py`).
- **Do not use BJ 23.13's "madness"** in any reading. It is the extreme form of the same pattern. Record it as a
  source rule only (the sensitive-rules policy of `sanjay-rath/15`).
