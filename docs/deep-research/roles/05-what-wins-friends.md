## 3. Verdict on doc 13's mapping

**Change it, and correct a citation.**

- **Citation error in doc 13.** The 11th row is tagged Text and cites `natal-techniques/02` L26. L26 is the
  **6th-house** row. The 11th-house row (L31) lists "Gains, income, elder siblings, fulfilment of desires" and no
  friends. BPHS 11.12 and PD 1.15 confirm there are no friends in the 11th. Re-tag the 11th as **[MODERN]**.
- **Name one planet: Mercury** [TEXT: PD 15.15, PD 2.4]. Jupiter is a second reading for close or dear friends
  [TEXT: UK V].
- **Add the 4th house** as the classical house of friends [TEXT: PD 1.11-12]. Keep the 3rd as secondary [TEXT: UK V].
- **Keep the A7 rule** [TEXT, Jaimini] and **Rath's A4-from-AL rule** [A Rath] unchanged.
- **New row:** "Wins friends | Mercury (karaka); the 4th house; A7 from the lagna; A4 from the AL | Text (PD 15.15,
  1.11-12; JUS 1.3.20-21); Rath (A4 from AL); Modern (11th)".
