## 3. Verdict on doc 13

**The "Not a rule" row is half right. Refute it at the Text level; keep it at the Rath level.**

- **Correct:** no Rath or SJC rule makes Jupiter's aspect or shubha kartari a **general** shield. This pass found
  none either.
- **Wrong as stated:** shubha kartari **is** a classical rule.
  - It protects the lagna, and with it the person (PD 6.8, 6.11).
  - It improves a dasha lord's results (Saravali 41.22-24).
  - Benefic aspect makes a house flourish (PD 15.1, 15.5) and removes the condition of several evil yogas
    (BJ 23.11, 23.17).
  - None of these rules is specific to Jupiter. Each is about benefics as a group.
- **Replace the row** with: "Protector (limited) | Shubha kartari on the lagna protects the person (PD 6.8, 6.11). A
  dasha lord hemmed by benefics gives moderately good results (Saravali 41.22-24). A benefic's aspect lets a house
  give its good results (PD 15.1, 15.5) and is the missing condition in several evil yogas (BJ 23). Jupiter adds to
  this as one benefic, not as a general shield. Rath's own protections stay as in §2.1 (argala and its obstruction;
  the 9th) | Text; no Rath rule".
- **Never say "Jupiter's aspect protects you"** as a blanket statement. Name the house or planet, and the rule.
