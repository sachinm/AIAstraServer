No classical verse found makes the 5th the house of speculation or gambling. The gloss "5th = speculative gains" in
`natal-techniques/14-yoga-dhana.md` L6 is an unsourced summary. The 5th does have one Rath link to speculation
(rule 3.3b), as the field of a career.

## 3. Verdict on doc 13's mapping

**Replace most of it.**

- **The habit:** Rahu (UK V) and the Mars–Venus conjunction (BJ 14.3) as [TEXT]; the 8th from the D10 lagna as
  [A Rath].
- **The loss:** Rath's wealth-loss rules (rule 3.4: the 12th from the AL, 12th links to the 2nd and 11th) as
  [A Rath], plus the 12th from the lagna [TEXT].
- **Re-tag the 5th.** It is not a Text signification (no verse found). It carries a Rath tag only through the 10th
  lord in the 5th (speculation as work, rule 3.3b), and Modern otherwise.
- **Keep the 8th from the lagna** only as Ours or Modern for sudden reversal. The sourced 8th is the D10 8th.
- **Row tag in doc 13:** "Text (BJ 14.3; UK V, Rahu); Rath (D10 8th; 10th lord in the 5th; 12th from AL for loss);
  Modern (5th as gambling)".
- **Fix `sanjay-rath/08` W7**: "only" is wrong, given rule 1.2 of the same file.
