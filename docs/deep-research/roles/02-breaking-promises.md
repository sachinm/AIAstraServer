## 2. Timing

- No source times a broken promise.
- By the general dasha principle, the periods of the 2nd lord, of planets in the 2nd, and of a Mars–Saturn pair would
  carry it. That is [INFERRED] from the general BPHS dasha rule. No verse applies it to this role.

## 3. Verdict on doc 13's mapping

**Change it.**

- **Keep the 2nd and its lord** as the primary house, now **[TEXT]** (BPHS 13.13; Uttara Kalamrita V). The 2nd and its
  lord afflicted by malefics is a sourced rule, so the row moves from Ours to Text for that part.
- **Replace Jupiter with Mercury** as the significator of truthful speech (Phaladeepika 2.4). Name **Mars, Rahu and
  Saturn** as the planets of falsehood (PD 2.3, 2.33; UK V). Add the Mars–Saturn conjunction (BJ 14.3).
- **Demote the 9th** to a secondary reading of conscience and dharma, tagged Ours. No verse ties the 9th to breaking
  one's word.
- **Add the AK conduct rule** [A Rath] as the "what helps" line. It is Rath's remedy framing, not a predictive rule.
- **Wording:** the classical words are "liar" and "slanderer". The product states a tendency to be watched, never a
  verdict on the person (SOUL.md).
