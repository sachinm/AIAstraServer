# Chara karakas (cara-kāraka, "movable significators")

> The chara karakas rank the planets by how far each has advanced within its own sign; the most advanced is the Atmakaraka (AK, the self/soul), and the rest in descending order signify the minister/advisor, siblings, mother, father, children, kin/rivals and spouse. The ranking changes from chart to chart, hence "movable". It is the foundation of nearly all Jaimini work (karakamsha, raja yogas, longevity, Maheshvara) and is also taught by Parashara. It is universally used in Jaimini-style reading; the live disputes are the 7- versus 8-karaka scheme, Rahu's degree, and ties.

## 1. Sources

- **JUS-R** (Rao 1949): 1.1.11 *ātmādhikaḥ kalādibhir nabhogaḥ saptānām aṣṭānāṃ vā* (romanised in the OCR as "Atmadhikaha kaladibhir nabhogas saptanam ashtanamva") = the planet with most degrees (by kala etc.) among seven or eight is AK; 1.1.12 AK governs bondage and liberation; 1.1.13-18 Amatya, Bhratri, Matri, Putra, Jnati, Dara; 1.1.19 some merge Matri and Putra. No Pitri sutra. [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/Astrology_Books_by_B_Suryanarayana_Row)
- **JUS-A** (Abhyankar 1951): 1.1.10 AK; 1.1.11 bondage/liberation; 1.1.12-18 AmK, BK, MK, **PiK (1.1.15)**, PK, GK, DK; 1.1.19 some take MK as PK "taking the number as seven and not eight, omitting Rahu" (translator's parenthesis). [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/jaimini-kva-a-4-size)
- **BPHS-ELS** ch. 32 vv. 1-17 (site numbering, read via summary): 7 or 8 karakas; Rahu included as eighth; ties by minutes then seconds; only degrees within the sign count; Rahu's degrees taken from 30; order AK ... DK; vv. 16-17 when two planets share the same degrees, one karakatva is lost and the sthira karaka is used for it. [chapter 32](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-32)
- **PVR** §8.1-8.2: eight chara karakas (Sun-Saturn + Rahu, Ketu excluded), Rahu measured from the end of the sign, exact-tie rule. [PDF](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Rath web**: treats Rahu as AK in two named charts (so uses Rahu at least there); karakamsha defined from AK. [srath.com atmakaraka-karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)

## 2. When it applies

Every natal chart. Life areas: self and soul purpose (AK), career advisors and work (AmK), siblings (BK), mother (MK), father (PiK), children and followers (PK), rivals/kin (GK), spouse (DK). It feeds karakamsha, Jaimini raja yogas, Jaimini longevity, Maheshvara, Navamsa/Chara dasha interpretation, and spiritual readings.

## 3. Inputs required

- Sidereal longitude (degree, minute, second within sign) of Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn and Rahu (mean or true node; must be stated).
- Scheme flag: 7 or 8 karakas.
- For ties: the sthira karaka table and planet strengths.

## 4. Calculation

1. For each of Sun ... Saturn take degrees advanced in the occupied sign (0-30). Sign number is ignored [TEXT BPHS-ELS 32.4; COMM Rao note on 1.1.11].
2. Rahu (8-karaka scheme only): 30° minus Rahu's degrees in its sign, because it moves backward [TEXT BPHS-ELS 32.5; MODERN PVR §8.2].
3. Sort descending. 1st = AK, 2nd AmK, 3rd BK, 4th MK, 5th PiK, 6th PK, 7th GK, 8th DK (8-scheme). In the 7-scheme there is no PiK (Rao text) or MK doubles as PK (the "some say" sutra); the order then runs AK, AmK, BK, MK, PK, GK, DK.
4. Compare minutes, then seconds, on equal degrees [TEXT BPHS-ELS 32.3].
5. Exact ties: the tied planets share one karakatva, the next role is left without a chara karaka, and the sthira karaka for that relative is used instead [TEXT BPHS-ELS 32.16-17; MODERN PVR §8.2]. Rao's note on 1.1.11 reads the eighth (Rahu) as the filler: "vacancies ... supplied by Rahu", i.e. Rahu is brought in only when a tie removes a slot [COMM]. Ketu is never a chara karaka [MODERN PVR §8.1].

## 5. Interpretation rules

- AK is the king of the chart; other karakas give their good results only if favourable to AK; a malefic or afflicted AK spoils them [TEXT BPHS-ELS 32.7-12; JUS 1.1.12].
- AK in exaltation/benefic dignity frees from bondage; debilitated or afflicted AK binds [COMM Rao note on 1.1.12].
- Chara karakas show **people** who shape one's sustenance and achievement; DK itself is the spouse (do not take the 7th from DK) [MODERN PVR §8.3].
- Persons: AmK advisors/ministers (career), BK siblings, MK mother, PiK father (also boss), PK children, followers, subordinates, GK cousins/rivals, DK spouse/partner [MODERN PVR §8.2 table].
- Devotion is read from the planet following AmK (i.e. BK) and its house [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.43].
- Rahu as AK: can show a saint or an outcast/revolutionary [MODERN PVR §8.2 example].

## 6. Variants and disputes

| Question | Readings |
|---|---|
| 7 or 8 | Text says "seven or eight" in both editions [TEXT]. Rao's text lacks a Pitri sutra, giving seven [TEXT JUS-R]; Abhyankar's text has Pitri, giving eight [TEXT JUS-A 1.1.15]. BPHS says Rahu is included for eight; "some" use seven [TEXT BPHS-ELS 32.1-2]. Abhyankar's own gloss associates seven with merging MK and PK and omitting Rahu [COMM]. PVR and SJC use eight for people [MODERN]. A search snippet says Rath uses seven for mundane charts; unverified (repo note). |
| When is Rahu used | (a) always in the 8 scheme [MODERN PVR]; (b) only to fill a slot vacated by a tie [COMM Rao, who cites Parashara for it]. BPHS 32.2 as summarised ("if two planets have equal degrees, consider Rahu, giving eight") is text-level support for (b) [TEXT BPHS-ELS, summary-read; verify in print]. |
| Rahu degree | 30 − degrees [TEXT BPHS; MODERN PVR]. No dissent found. |
| Ties | Share karakatva, missing role to the sthira karaka [TEXT BPHS; MODERN PVR] vs Rahu fills the gap [COMM Rao]. |
| Node type | Mean vs true node changes Rahu's degree and can flip AK; no classical rule found. |

## 7. Worked micro-example

PVR Example 28: Sun 12Ge47, Moon 20Ar28, Mars 13Ge51, Mercury 25Ge18, Jupiter 5Ta40, Venus 17Ge21, Saturn 2Ta28, Rahu 1Cn43. Rahu = 30° − 1°43' = 28°17' → AK Rahu, AmK Mercury, BK Moon, MK Venus, PiK Mars, PK Sun, GK Jupiter, DK Saturn. In the 7-scheme the same chart gives AK Mercury.

- **Engine status:** `AIAstra_Radha/src/jhora/horoscope/chart/house.py::chara_karakas` ranks Sun..Rahu (8), Rahu as 30 − degree, Python `sorted` (stable) so exact ties fall back to planet order silently; no lopa/sthira substitution; no 7-karaka option. Chara karakas appear in `export.py` (`_build_chara_karaka_json`); not found in `natal_export.py`. Which export path feeds the server is not established here.
- **Engine additions:** (1) export `chara_karakas` with `scheme: 8|7`, the degree used per planet (Rahu as 30 − x), and `node_type`; (2) `ties: [{planets, role}]` and `substitute_sthira` when degrees tie to the arc-second (or to a configurable tolerance, flagged); (3) a 7-scheme run alongside so readings can show "AK changes under the 7 scheme" when it does; (4) `ak_margin_deg` (gap between AK and AmK) so low-margin charts are flagged as birth-time sensitive.
- **Boards:** You/Self (AK = soul lesson; lead card), Work (AmK), Love (DK), family cards (MK, PiK, PK, BK).
- **Weight:** high for "who is this person" and spiritual framing; medium for event prediction (always pair with lagna/house lords).
- **Pitfalls:** AK can change with a few minutes of birth time when two planets are close; Rahu inclusion changes every downstream Jaimini output; do not mix chara with naisargika roles (PVR §8.1).
