# Argala and virodhargala (argalā, "bolt/intervention"; virodhārgalā, "obstruction")

> Argala is the decisive intervention that planets in the 2nd, 4th and 11th (and, secondarily, the 5th) from a house or planet exert on it; planets in the 12th, 10th and 3rd (and 9th) obstruct that intervention (virodhargala). Several malefics in the 3rd give argala instead of obstructing. For Ketu (and, by commentary, Rahu) the counting is reversed. Argala answers "what decides the affairs of this house or person, and is it blocked?" It is used in both Jaimini and Parashari work; SJC treats it as more decisive than aspects.

## 1. Sources

- **JUS-R** 1.1.5-10 (Rao 1949): 1.1.5 "Darabhagyasulastha argala nidhyatuh" (romanised OCR): planets in 4th, 2nd, 11th from the aspecting body are argalas; 1.1.6 "Kamastha tu bhuyasa papanam": malefics in the 3rd give (evil) argala; 1.1.7 "Riphanichakamastha virodhinah": planets in 12th, 10th, 3rd obstruct; 1.1.8 "Na nyuna vibalascha": fewer or weaker obstructors do not obstruct; 1.1.9 "Pragvat trikone": trines (5/9) likewise; 1.1.10 "Viparitam ketoh": for Ketu, reversed [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/Astrology_Books_by_B_Suryanarayana_Row).
- **JUS-A** 1.1.4-9 (Abhyankar 1951): same content; 1.1.5 malefics in the 3rd form argala "provided they are malefic and in greater number than the benefics in that house"; 1.1.9 reverse with Ketu [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/jaimini-kva-a-4-size).
- **BPHS-ELS** ch. 31 (site numbering, via summary): v. 3 argala from 4th, 2nd, 11th, obstruction from 10th, 12th, 3rd; v. 4 weak or fewer obstructors ineffective, malefics in the 3rd troublesome; v. 6 5th creates argala and 9th obstructs, shadow planets (Rahu and Ketu) reverse the count; v. 7 one planet inferior, two medium, more excellent; v. 8 argala from sign and from planet; unobstructed fruitful, obstructed fruitless; vv. 13-16 results of argala on each house; v. 17 benefic, malefic, mixed argala; v. 18 argala on lagna, 5th and 9th makes a king [chapter 31](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-31).
- **PVR** §10.5-10.7 [PDF](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf).

## 2. When it applies

Any house, sign, planet or special point (lagna, AL, UL, HL, karakas, karakamsha) in any varga. Life areas: all; examples in the texts are wealth (2nd), siblings (3rd), home and kin (4th), children and intelligence (5th), spouse (7th), fortune (9th), honour/career (10th), gains (11th), expenses (12th).

## 3. Inputs required

Planet signs per varga; sign of the reference house/point; benefic/malefic classification of each planet (Moon by paksha, Mercury by association); planet strength (for the "fewer or weaker" test); position of Ketu and Rahu.

## 4. Calculation

1. Pick the reference sign R (a house or the sign of a planet/point).
2. Primary argala: planets in the 2nd, 4th, 11th from R. Secondary argala: 5th from R [TEXT JUS 1.1.5, 1.1.9; BPHS-ELS 31.3, 31.6; MODERN PVR §10.5].
3. Virodhargala pairs: 2nd ↔ 12th, 4th ↔ 10th, 11th ↔ 3rd, 5th ↔ 9th [TEXT JUS 1.1.7, 1.1.9; MODERN PVR §10.6].
4. Third-house exception: if malefics in the 3rd from R outnumber benefics there, they cause (malefic) argala on R instead of obstructing the 11th [TEXT JUS-A 1.1.5; JUS-R 1.1.6; MODERN PVR §10.6].
5. Compare each pair: obstruction prevails only when the obstructors outnumber the argala planets, or are equal in number and stronger; fewer or weaker obstructors do not obstruct [TEXT JUS 1.1.8 (R) / 1.1.7 (A); MODERN PVR §10.7].
6. Reversal: count anti-zodiacally. See section 6 for who it applies to.
7. Grade: one planet weak, two medium, three+ excellent [TEXT BPHS-ELS 31.7]. Classify each as subha (benefic) or papa (malefic) argala [TEXT BPHS-ELS 31.17; MODERN PVR §10.5].

## 5. Interpretation rules

- Unobstructed argala makes a house's and a planet's results firm; obstructed argala is fruitless [TEXT BPHS-ELS 31.2, 31.8].
- Argala on the lagna, 5th and 9th (unobstructed) gives high status [TEXT BPHS-ELS 31.18].
- Argala on a house/karaka shows what decides its matters: 2nd = sustenance ingredient, 4th = driver of state and mood, 11th = catalyst for gains, 5th = contributing factor [MODERN PVR §10.7].
- Argala on Saturn (karaka of livelihood) shows decisive influences on career (PVR examples of P. V. Narasimha Rao and Reagan) [MODERN PVR §10.7].
- Argala on the AL / its 7th, unobstructed, makes one lucky; benefic argala gives wealth [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.22-23].

## 6. Variants and disputes

| Question | Readings |
|---|---|
| Which node reverses | Sutra names only Ketu ("ketoḥ") [TEXT JUS 1.1.10 R / 1.1.9 A]. BPHS names the shadow planets (both) [TEXT BPHS-ELS 31.6]. Rao: "Rahu is not mentioned in the original, but some commentators" include him; concludes both reverse [COMM Rao]. PVR: only when the **reference sign contains Ketu** are its argalas counted anti-zodiacally [MODERN PVR §10.6]. |
| What is reversed | (a) argala **on** a sign or planet that is (or holds) Ketu: Rao's note counts from Ketu as the reference ("the 9th from Ketu becomes the 3rd"; benefics in the 2nd and 11th from Ketu do not form argala, malefics in the 3rd and 8th from Ketu do not form evil obstruction), which supports this reading [COMM Rao]; PVR applies the same idea to the sign containing Ketu [MODERN]. (b) argala **caused by** a node (a node in the 12th acting as if in the 2nd) is a reading some modern teachers use; no source read here states it [unsourced, unresolved]. |
| 3rd-house rule | "malefics in excess of benefics" [COMM Abhyankar]; "several malefics" [MODERN PVR]; Rao: malefics in the 3rd give evil argala [COMM]. |
| Trine sutra 1.1.9 | 5th gives argala, 9th obstructs [TEXT BPHS-ELS 31.6; MODERN PVR]; Rao reads planets in 5/9 as counteracting argala generally [COMM Rao]. |
| Net strength | No text gives a numeric score. |

## 7. Worked micro-example

PVR §10.6: Mercury in Gemini, Saturn in Virgo (4th from Gemini) gives malefic argala; Jupiter in Pisces (10th from Gemini) obstructs it. Venus in Aries (11th) gives benefic argala; unobstructed if Leo (3rd) is empty.

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra

- **Engine status:** `natal_export.py::_build_argala` / `argala_table` exports for each house 1-12 the planets in 2/4/5/11 and the paired 12/10/9/3 with subha/papa nature (PVR method). It does **not** apply the Ketu/Rahu reversal, does **not** turn 3rd-house malefics into argala, gives no net verdict, and is house-based only (no argala on planets or special points).
- **Engine additions:** (1) `argala_on_points` for AL, UL, HL, GL, SL, KL and each planet; (2) `third_house_malefic_argala: true/false` per reference; (3) `node_reversal` variants (`ketu_sign_reversed` PVR, `both_nodes`, `none`) with the variant name in the output; (4) `net: argala|obstructed|tie` using counts then a declared strength rule, with the rule name.
- **Boards:** Self (argala on lagna/AL), Love (on 7th, UL, Venus, DK), Work (on 10th, AL 10th, Saturn, AmK), Lucky/money (on 2nd, 11th, HL).
- **Weight:** high in SJC readings; treat as "what decides this matter" rather than as an event trigger.
- **Pitfall:** without the reversal and 3rd-house rule, outputs differ from Jagannatha Hora; readers must not compute these themselves.
