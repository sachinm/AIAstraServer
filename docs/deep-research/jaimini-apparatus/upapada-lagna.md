# Upapada lagna (upapada, UL; also gauṇapada, A12)

> The upapada is the arudha pada of the 12th house. Jaimini (Adhyaya 1 Pada 4) and Parashara (BPHS ch. 30) use it, with the 2nd house from it, as the main marriage reference: the kind of spouse, whether marriage survives, loss or renunciation of the spouse, the spouse's illnesses, and, through the 7th and other houses from it, children and siblings. It is the most specific classical marriage tool in the Jaimini system and is heavily used by SJC. Disputes: whether rules apply to UL itself or to its 2nd, and whether the 7th/8th from UL also show the end of marriage (modern addition).

## 2. When it applies

Every chart, both sexes. Life areas: marriage and spouse (primary), continuity/break of marriage, spouse's health, children (7th from UL and combinations in 1.4.24-31), siblings (from pada).

## 3. Inputs required

UL sign (A12, same computation as all padas incl. exception); occupants of UL and its 2nd, 7th, 8th; their dignity (exalted/debilitated, navamsa dignity per BPHS 30.8-9); lord of UL and its dignity; Venus's dignity; rasi aspects on UL and its 2nd; benefic/malefic classification (Sun special rule).

## 4. Calculation

UL = pada of the 12th house: count from the 12th sign to its lord, same count onward, 1st/7th exception as for all padas (see `arudha-padas.md`). Then read UL, 2nd from UL, 7th from UL (and the 7th from lagna and pada) [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.1, 1.4.23; BPHS-ELS 30.23].

## 5. Interpretation rules

- Benefics in/aspecting UL or its 2nd (or benefic lord) → happiness from spouse, beautiful/virtuous spouse [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.11; BPHS-ELS 30.3, 30.15].
- Malefic in or owning UL/2nd, without benefic aspect → renunciation (pravrajya) or loss of spouse [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.2, 1.4.4; BPHS-ELS 30.4-5]. Sun is not malefic for this rule [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.3]; BPHS limits that exemption to the Sun in exaltation, own or friendly sign [TEXT BPHS-ELS 30.6].
- Debilitated planet there → loss of spouse; exalted → many spouses; Gemini → many [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.5-7].
- Lord of UL in its sign or Venus in own sign → spouse lost late in life [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.8; BPHS-ELS 30.12-13].
- Lord of UL or Venus exalted → spouse from a noble family; debilitated → the reverse [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.9-10; BPHS-ELS 30.14].
- Saturn + Rahu → separation through scandal or loss [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.12; BPHS-ELS 30.16].
- Planets in UL describe the spouse: Mercury intelligent or indecisive, Ketu spiritual or short-tempered, Sun charming/authoritative from a respectable family, Mars bold or quarrelsome [MODERN PVR §9.4].
- 8th from UL = longevity of marriage; 2nd and 7th from UL = end of marriage; malefics there → troubles or divorce. Nature of spouse (UL) and length of marriage (8th) are separate questions [MODERN PVR §9.4].
- 2nd from UL as the "sustenance" of marriage is a common SJC explanation of why the texts stress the 2nd [unsourced in this pass; consistent with the TEXT emphasis on the 2nd].

## 6. Variants and disputes

- **"Upapada" meaning:** pada of the 12th (house "adjacent" to lagna) is standard [TEXT JUS-A 1.4.1]. Abhyankar records other explanations by scholars [COMM]; not extracted.
- **UL or its 2nd:** Abhyankar reads most sutras as "UL or the second from it" [COMM]; BPHS puts weight on the 2nd for loss of wife [TEXT BPHS-ELS 30.7-8]. SJC treats the 2nd as decisive for continuation.
- **Divorce reading from 7th/8th of UL:** PVR [MODERN]; not found as such in the sutras read.
- **Female charts:** texts speak of "wife"; modern schools apply UL to both sexes [MODERN].
- **Subsequent marriages:** a rule for later marriages from UL is reported in SJC practice but no source was read for it [unsourced; see §9].

## 7. Worked micro-example

PVR §9.4: Mars and Saturn in UL with Jupiter in the 8th from UL → long marriage to an argumentative spouse; Jupiter in UL with Mars and Saturn in the 8th → noble spouse but rough marriage, possibly divorce.

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra

- **Engine status:** UL is exported as A12 in `natal_export.py::_build_arudhas`. No UL-relative houses, occupants or aspects are exported.
- **Engine additions:** `upapada: {sign, lord, lord_sign, lord_dignity, houses_from_ul: 1..12 with occupants, dignity, rasi_aspects}`, plus `ul_flags` (malefic_in_ul_or_2nd, benefic_aspect_on_ul_or_2nd, sun_exempt_applied, debilitated_in_ul_or_2nd, exalted_in_ul_or_2nd, gemini_ul_or_2nd, saturn_rahu_in_ul_or_2nd) each with the planets and rule citation. The same block for D9.
- **Boards:** Love board lead technique for spouse nature and marriage stability; pairs with DK, Venus, 7th lord, A7 and the 7th from karakamsha.
- **Weight:** high for "what kind of spouse/marriage"; timing still comes from dashas and transits over UL.
- **Pitfalls:** many sutra results are medical (spouse's diseases) and harsh (loss of spouse, illegitimacy); keep them out of user copy and use only as internal pressure flags. Confirm with the user before predicting (product rule).
