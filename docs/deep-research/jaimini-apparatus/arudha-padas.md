# Arudha padas (ārūḍha-pada; bhāva-pada and graha-pada)

> The arudha (pada) of a house is the sign reached by counting from the house to its lord and then the same number again from the lord. It shows the house's matters as they manifest and are perceived in the world (status, image, tangible results), as opposed to the house itself (reality). The arudha of the lagna (AL, pada lagna) is the person's public standing; the 11th and 12th from AL show gains and expenses; A7 (dara pada) shows partners and associates; A12 is the upapada (marriage). Planets also have padas (graha padas). Both Jaimini and Parashara teach it; it is central to SJC practice. Disputed points: the exception when the pada falls in the house itself or the 7th from it, and the lord of Scorpio/Aquarius.

Adhyaya 1 Pada 3: results of planets from the pada lagna [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/Astrology_Books_by_B_Suryanarayana_Row).
- **JUS-A** 1.1.29-31; 1.3.1-23 (pada lagna results: 11th = wealth, 12th = expenses by planet, 7th and 2nd, A7 position, argala on AL) [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/jaimini-kva-a-4-size).
- **BPHS-ELS** ch. 29 (site numbering, via summary): v. 2 counting rule; v. 3 same for all houses, lagna pada is principal; vv. 4-5 special cases; v. 6 graha pada; v. 7 two-lord signs by strength; vv. 8-22 11th and 12th from pada (gains, expenses, by planet); vv. 23-28 7th and 2nd from pada; v. 29 same yogas from karakamsha; vv. 31-36 dara pada vs lagna pada; v. 37 read lagna pada with other padas [chapter 29](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-29).
- **PVR** ch. 9 (§9.2-9.6), §11.7.3(18) [PDF](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf).

## 2. When it applies

All charts and all vargas (PVR applies padas in D10, D16, D24, D20 etc.). Life areas: status and reputation (AL), wealth and expenditure (11th/12th from AL, A2, A11), partners and business associates (A7), marriage (UL = A12), career image and workplace (A10, especially in D10), education institution (A4 in D24), siblings/communication output (A3).

## 3. Inputs required

Sign of each house cusp (whole sign); sign of each house lord in that varga; for Scorpio (Mars, Ketu) and Aquarius (Saturn, Rahu), a stronger-lord test; for graha padas, the stronger of a planet's two own signs; positions of all planets for reading results.

## 4. Calculation

**Bhava pada of house H** [TEXT JUS 1.1.29; BPHS-ELS 29.2-3; MODERN PVR §9.2]
1. Find the lord L of H's sign (Scorpio/Aquarius: the stronger of the two lords) [TEXT BPHS-ELS 29.7; MODERN PVR §9.2].
2. n = count from H's sign to L's sign, zodiacally (always forward, per PVR).
3. Pada = n-th sign counted from L's sign.
4. Exception (see §6 for readings). PVR / engine rule: if the pada is the 1st or 7th from H, take the 10th sign from the pada.

**Graha pada of planet P** [TEXT BPHS-ELS 29.6; MODERN PVR §9.5]: count from P's sign to its own sign (the stronger if two), then as many again from that sign; PVR applies the same 1st/7th exception. Rahu uses Aquarius, Ketu Scorpio. Sun and Moon padas of houses (counting houses from the Sun or Moon) exist in the library but are not in the texts read.

Names (PVR Table 18): A1 Arudha/Pada lagna; A2 Dhana pada; A3 Bhratri/Vikrama pada; A4 Matri/Sukha/Vahana pada; A5 Mantra/Putra pada; A6 Roga/Shatru pada; A7 Dara pada; A8 Mrityu pada; A9 Bhagya/Pitri pada; A10 Karma/Rajya pada; A11 Labha pada; A12 Upapada.

## 5. Interpretation rules

From AL (pada lagna):
- Planets in or aspecting the 11th from AL bring wealth; benefics by fair means, malefics by foul; exalted/own-sign planets give more [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.1-4; BPHS-ELS 29.8-10].
- Planets in or aspecting the 12th from AL bring expense: Sun, Rahu, Venus through government (definite with Moon's aspect); Mercury through kin or quarrels; Jupiter through taxes; Mars/Saturn through brothers; the same planets in the 11th become sources of income [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.5-12; BPHS-ELS 29.16-22].
- 11th from AL aspected and 12th not: unobstructed gains [TEXT BPHS-ELS 29.12].
- Rahu or Ketu in the 7th from AL: stomach trouble; Ketu in the 2nd: premature ageing; Moon, Venus, Jupiter or an exalted planet in the 2nd: wealth [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.13-16; BPHS-ELS 29.23-28].
- Malefics in the 3rd/6th from AL: perceived as bold, materially successful; benefics there: gentle, saintly image [MODERN PVR §9.3].
- A malefic in the 6th from the arudha without benefic help makes a thief [TEXT BPHS-ELS 30.37].

AL and A7 (dara pada):
- A7 in a kendra or trikona from AL (or from lagna, Abhyankar reads "from the Ascendant") makes wealthy; elsewhere poor [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.18-19; BPHS-ELS 29.31-32].
- A7 in kendra/trikona/upachaya: friendship between spouses; in 6/8/12: enmity [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.20-21; BPHS-ELS 29.34].
- AL and A7 mutually in kendra, 3/11 or trines: king [TEXT BPHS-ELS 29.36]. AL and A7 not in 2/12 or 6/8 makes other raja yogas work [MODERN PVR §11.7.3(18)].
- Same test for other padas against AL (e.g. A5 from AL: friendship and gain vs enmity) [TEXT BPHS-ELS 29.35].

Graha padas show how the native feels and the illusions under which he acts in that planet's dasha [MODERN PVR §9.6].

## 6. Variants and disputes

**The special cases (1.1.30-31 and BPHS 29.4-5). Three readings:**

| Reading | Lord of lagna in 4th | Lord in 7th | Lord in 1st | Lord in 10th |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rao, literal [COMM JUS-R 1.1.30-31] | 7th is pada (no exception) | lagna itself is AL | lagna (no exception stated) | 7th |
| Abhyankar [COMM JUS-A 1.1.30-31] and BPHS [TEXT BPHS-ELS 29.4-5] | the 4th itself is pada | the 10th is pada | not stated | not stated |
| PVR / SJC / engine [MODERN PVR §9.2] | pada falls in 7th → 10th from it = 4th | pada falls in 1st → 10th | pada in 1st → 10th | pada in 7th → 10th from it = 4th |

Abhyankar/BPHS and PVR agree wherever both speak; PVR generalises. Rao's literal reading is a minority view and conflicts with BPHS.

- **Scorpio/Aquarius lord:** stronger lord [TEXT BPHS-ELS 29.7; MODERN PVR]; the strength rules used (more conjunctions, then dignity, etc.) come from PVR's strength chapter, not from the arudha verses.
- **Counting direction:** PVR counts zodiacally always; some Jaimini readers apply odd/even sign direction (JUS 1.1.25-27) to padas too. Not resolved in sources read.
- **Graha pada exception:** PVR applies it; whether the texts intend it for planets is unstated.
- Gautama Samhita / "Arudha Padadyayi" reportedly gives only 8 padas (search snippet, unverified).

## 7. Worked micro-example

PVR Example 29: Virgo lagna, Mercury in Pisces. Virgo to Pisces = 7; 7 from Pisces = Virgo, which is the 1st from Virgo → take the 10th → AL in Gemini.
