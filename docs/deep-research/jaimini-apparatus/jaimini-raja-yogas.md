# Jaimini raja yogas (rāja-yoga by kārakas, padas and special lagnas)

> Jaimini-style raja yogas judge status and power from the chara karakas (AK with PK, AmK, 9th lord), from the pada lagna and dara pada, from the planets around the lagna lord / 7th lord / AK (benefics in 2/4/5/8, malefics in 3/6), and from one planet connecting the lagna, hora lagna and ghati lagna (or the lagna in several vargas). Parashara repeats several of them in BPHS ch. 39. They answer "does this chart promise rank, authority, wealth?" and are widely used by SJC; strength depends on dignity and on the AL-A7 relation.

## 1. Sources

- **JUS-A** Adhyaya 1 Pada 3 (Abhyankar 1951): 1.3.18-19 A7 in kendra/trikona from lagna → wealth, else poverty; 1.3.22-23 unobstructed argala on AL or its 7th → lucky, benefic → wealthy; 1.3.24 the same planet aspecting janma, hora and ghatika lagna → king; 1.3.25-27 lagna or 7th in rasi, navamsa and drekkana aspected by one planet → royal glory, less if one chart lacks it; extend to other vargas; 1.3.28-29 Venus and Moon aspects → vehicles; Mars, Venus, Ketu → sacrifices/royal canopy; 1.3.30 benefics in 2nd, 4th, 5th, 8th from the AK's sign → king; 1.3.31-32 malefics in 3rd and 6th (from AK, or from lagna lord / 7th lord) → royal glory, general; 1.3.33-34 equal numbers → prosperous; reverse → poverty; 1.3.35-36 Jupiter/Venus/Moon in 5th from lagna lord or 7th lord → high official; malefic in 3rd/6th → army general; 1.3.37-41 aspects on AK and lagna by lords of 3/6/5 (wisdom), 4th (happiness), 8th (poverty), 12th (spending), lagna lord or AK's dispositor (power); 1.3.42-44 equal planets on both sides (2/12, 5/9, 3/11, 4/10, 6/12) of lagna or AK → misery, imprisonment (benefics mild) [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/jaimini-kva-a-4-size). Numbering approximate (OCR).
- **BPHS-ELS** ch. 39 (site numbering, via summary): vv. 3-6 karakamsha and AK with PK, with lagna and 5th lords; v. 8 karaka in lagna, 5th, 7th; v. 9 from karaka with lagna lord; v. 22 karaka in good sign and amsa; v. 23 lagna pada and dara pada in mutual kendra/trikona/3-11; v. 24 lagna, HL, GL aspected by exalted planet; vv. 26-29 pada with exalted planet/Moon/Jupiter and the 11th from pada [chapter 39](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-39). Also BPHS-ELS 29.36 (AL and A7 mutual kendra, 3/11 or trine → king) and 31.18 (argala on lagna, 5th, 9th).

## 2. When it applies

All charts. Life areas: career rank, authority, public power, wealth; also spiritual stature (AK-based). Results come in the dashas of the participating planets/signs (BPHS 33.96: yoga results at the end of the forming planet's dasha, via summary).

## 3. Inputs required

Chara karakas (AK, AmK, PK) with scheme; lagna, 5th, 7th, 9th lords; AL and A7; HL, GL; varga lagnas (D1, D2, D3, D9, D12, D30); rasi aspects; dignities (own, exaltation, mooltrikona, own navamsa); benefic/malefic classes; argala on AL.

## 4. Calculation and list

Each yoga is a boolean with participating planets:
1. **AK + PK conjunction** and lagna lord + 5th lord conjunction → raja yoga; one condition alone gives partial results [TEXT BPHS-ELS 39.4-5; MODERN PVR §11.7.3(1)].
2. Lagna lord in 5th, 5th lord in lagna, AK and PK in lagna or 5th, in own sign/amsa/exaltation or benefic-aspected → great king [TEXT BPHS-ELS 39.6; MODERN PVR (2)].
3. 9th lord and AK in lagna, 5th or 7th, benefic-aspected [TEXT BPHS-ELS 39.8; MODERN PVR (3)].
4. Benefics in 2nd, 4th, 5th (and 8th, Jaimini) from AK (and from lagna lord) → king [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.30; MODERN PVR (4)].
5. Malefics in 3rd and 6th from AK / lagna lord / 7th lord → king, general [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.31-32, 1.3.36; MODERN PVR (5)].
6. One planet joining or aspecting lagna, HL and GL [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.24; BPHS-ELS 39.24; MODERN PVR (6) adds ownership and partial forms].
7. One planet aspecting lagna in the six shad-varga charts (D1, D9, D2, D3, D12, D30); JUS-A 1.3.25-27 states rasi, navamsa, drekkana [TEXT; MODERN PVR (7)].
8. Lagna, HL and GL each occupied by own/exalted planets [MODERN PVR (8)].
9. AL and A7 mutually in kendra, trikona or 3/11 [TEXT BPHS-ELS 29.36, 39.23]; AL and A7 not in 2/12 or 6/8 makes all raja yogas effective [MODERN PVR (18)].
10. Unobstructed (benefic) argala on AL or its 7th [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.22-23]; argala on lagna, 5th and 9th [TEXT BPHS-ELS 31.18].

## 5. Interpretation rules

- Rank is shown at the AL (status), not only at the lagna; read raja yogas together with the 10th and 11th from AL [MODERN PVR ch. 9-11].
- A karaka yoga needs AK to be well placed: a malefic or afflicted AK blocks other karakas' good results [TEXT BPHS-ELS 32.10-12].
- Close degrees strengthen; PVR: more than 5-6° separation loses full results for conjunction yogas [MODERN PVR §11.7 n.38].
- Equal planets on both sides of lagna or AK (papa/subha kartari-like symmetry) → bondage/imprisonment, mild if benefic [TEXT JUS-A 1.3.42-44].

## 6. Variants and disputes

- Whether "AK" here is chara (Jaimini) only or the lagna as "bhava karaka" (BPHS 32.31 calls the lagna the atmakaraka of the native, via summary): some read BPHS yogas with the lagna.
- 7 vs 8 karaka scheme changes PK and AmK, so yoga 1-3 can appear or vanish.
- Aspect type: rasi drishti in Jaimini reading; PVR allows ownership and partial association.

## 7. Worked micro-example

PVR uses Akbar's chart to show conjunction raja yogas with close degrees (§11.7), and a Scorpio-lagna Prime Minister's chart with a raja yoga involving AK Mercury in the lagna (Sudasa chapter). Details were not extracted.

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra

- **Engine status:** `natal_export.py` emits one Jaimini yoga, `atmakaraka_putrakaraka_raja_yoga` (around line 410); library `horoscope/chart/raja_yoga.py` has Parashari pair yogas (dharma-karmadhipati, vipareeta, neecha bhanga) and `check_other_raja_yoga_1..3`, which use AK and PK from `chara_karakas` (likely BPHS 39.4-6 forms; not reviewed line by line). The HL/GL, AL-A7, benefics/malefics-from-AK and varga-lagna yogas above were not found in the export.
- **Engine additions:** a `jaimini_raja_yogas` list with id, rule text id (e.g. `JUS-A 1.3.24`), participating planets/points, strength (dignity, degree gap), `karaka_scheme`, and an `al_a7_relation` gate; equal-planets-both-sides flag for lagna and AK.
- **Boards:** Work (rank, authority), Self (stature), not Love.
- **Weight:** medium-high for status questions; never state rank as certain (product rule); timing must come from dashas.
- **Pitfalls:** "king" language must be translated to modern scale; many yogas are common in charts, so weight by dignity and by count.

## 9. Open questions

- Rao's numbering for Pada 3 not extracted; JUS-A numbers are approximate.
- Rath's specific Jaimini raja yoga set (e.g. AK-AmK conjunction as a raja yoga, widely cited online) was not found in the texts read: neither JUS-A 1.3 nor BPHS ch. 39 (as summarised) names AK with AmK. Treat AK-AmK as modern until a source is found.
