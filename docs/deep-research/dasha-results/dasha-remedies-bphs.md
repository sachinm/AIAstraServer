# Remedies prescribed for dashas in BPHS (daśā-śānti)

> BPHS attaches a remedy (japa, recitation, worship, havan or a gift) to most antardasha entries, mostly inside the
> maraka branch (when the bhukti lord is the 2nd or 7th lord, or sits in those houses) and sometimes to bad-branch
> entries. Pratyantar, sukshma and prana chapters give none. This file lists only what the fetched text says, with
> its condition.

## 1. Sources

BPHS e-text (archive.org, 97-ch.), ch. 47.85-89 (Venus MD as 2nd lord: Shatarudriya, Mrityunjaya japa, gift of cow or
buffalo), ch. 52-60 antardasha chapters; 65.32 (Maharishi yajnas destroy evil of inauspicious dashas). Verse numbers
±2 (OCR). Coverage note: the opening of ch. 52 was cut off in the fetch; for Rahu MD (ch. 55) only five entries had a
remedy visible; "none stated" below means the text read had none, "not read" means not seen.

## 2. When it applies

Per BPHS: when the antardasha lord is a maraka (2nd/7th lord, sometimes with 8th/12th) or badly placed.

## 3. Inputs required

The antardasha lord's maraka status (2nd/7th lord, sometimes 8th/12th), its placement branch (good/bad) from the
antardasha template, and the MD/AD pair.

## 4. Rule procedure

1. Engine marks the AD as maraka branch or bad branch (antardasha file).
2. Look up the (MD, AD) cell below.
3. If the cell is "none stated" or "not read", show no remedy.

## 6. Variants and disputes

- Only one BPHS e-text was read; other translations were not compared. In the e-text, remedies sometimes follow the
  bad branch rather than the maraka branch.
- **Saravali ch. 44 "Antidotes for evils of dashas" is not ritual**: it says the evils of a dasha are nullified if, at
  the dasha start, a planet is in a benefic's or close friend's division and benefic-aspected, or is exalted/
  mulatrikona/own and joined or aspected by a benefic (44.1-3). It belongs in the condition file (dasha-start sky),
  not in a remedy table.
- Animal gifts are not practical today; no modern-school substitution source was read in this pass.

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra

- Remedies are optional content; the product must not imply guaranteed outcomes, and animal gifts should not be
  suggested literally. Store as data with condition + source; show only if the product decides to offer remedies.
- Engine only supplies the trigger (AD lord is maraka / bad branch); text is a static table.
