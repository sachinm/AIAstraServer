# Antardasha results per planet pair (antardaśā-phala / bhukti-phala)

> How a sub-period (bhukti) inside a mahadasha is judged. BPHS gives 81 entries (9 x 9), each written as a rule: good
> results if the bhukti lord is in a kendra/trikona/11th from lagna, exalted/own, or joined with the lagna/9th/10th
> lord; bad results if it is 6/8/12 from the dasha lord or from lagna, debilitated, combust or with malefics; physical
> distress or death risk if it is the 2nd or 7th lord; plus a remedy. Phaladeepika compresses this into a handful of
> relational rules (position from the dasha lord, friendship, mutual kendra/trikona). This is the core of month-scale
> prediction in every Parashari school.

## 2. When it applies

Every Vimshottari antardasha. Life area = houses owned/occupied by the bhukti lord, counted both from lagna and from
the dasha lord (Phaladeepika 20.29).

## 3. Inputs required

- bhukti lord: house from lagna, house **from the dasha lord**, dignity, combustion, conjunctions, houses owned;
- whether it is the 2nd/7th lord (maraka), 8th/12th lord, or joined to them;
- natural and temporary (tatkalika) friendship with the dasha lord, and natural friendship with the lagna lord
  (Phaladeepika 20.28);
- functional relation between dasha and bhukti lords (conjunction, mutual aspect, exchange, kendra/trikona lords).

## 4. Rule procedure (BPHS entry template, observed across ch. 52-60)

1. **Good branch** [TEXT]: bhukti lord in kendra/trikona (often also 11th, 2nd, 4th, 9th named) from lagna, or exalted/
   own sign, or joined with the lagna/9th/10th lord; some entries add "in kendra/trikona/3rd/11th from the dasha lord".
2. **Bad branch** [TEXT]: bhukti lord 6/8/12 from the dasha lord (e.g. 52.13-14 Moon in Sun MD; 53.62 Venus in Moon
   MD; 57.79), or in 6/8/12 from lagna, debilitated, combust, with malefics, or weak.
3. **Maraka branch** [TEXT]: bhukti lord is lord of the 2nd or 7th (sometimes 8th, 12th named) = bodily distress or
   premature death risk; a remedy follows (e.g. 53.63-64 Venus in Moon MD; 57.61-62 Mars as lord of 2/7/8).
4. **Phase** [TEXT]: many entries split the bhukti into start, middle, end.
5. **Phaladeepika overlay** [TEXT]:
   - bhukti lord 6/8/12 from the dasha lord = unhappiness; any other position = good, and the house it occupies
     counted from the dasha lord names the area (20.29);
   - bhukti of the dasha lord's enemy, of a planet in the 6th, or of the lagna lord's enemy = danger from foes, loss of
     position (20.28);
   - mutual dasha/bhukti of 6/8/12 owners or occupants = bad (20.22);
   - kendra lord's dasha with trikona lord's bhukti, and the reverse, good; related planets help each other (20.42,
     20.48-49); a planet's dasha shows itself most clearly in the bhuktis of planets related to it (20.44).
6. **Malefic dasha + nakshatra-lord bhuktis** [TEXT] Phaladeepika 20.23: in a malefic dasha, the bhukti of the lord of
   the 3rd, 5th or 7th nakshatra from the birth star (vipat, pratyak, vadha), or of the janma-rashi lord or 8th from
   it, brings enemies and thieves.

## 5. Interpretation rules

Compressed matrix (only the rule, not the 81 descriptions):

| Relation of bhukti lord | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| kendra/trikona/11 from lagna, strong | good for its owned houses | BPHS ch. 52-60 good branches |
| kendra/trikona/3/11 from dasha lord | good | BPHS (several entries); PD 20.29 |
| 6/8/12 from dasha lord | bad (shadashtaka / dvirdvadasha) | BPHS 52.13-14, 53.62, 57.79; PD 20.29 |
| natural/temporary friend of dasha lord | good | BPHS 50.89; PD 20.28 (enemy = bad) |
| 2nd/7th lord (maraka) | health distress, remedy prescribed | BPHS ch. 52-60 maraka branches |
| owns or occupies 6/8/12 and so does dasha lord | bad | PD 20.22 |
| trikona lord in kendra lord's dasha (or reverse) | good | PD 20.42, 20.48-49; BPHS 48.12-13 |

Per-pair descriptions (Phaladeepika ch. 21) are short natural-signification lists, e.g. Sun-Moon: defeat of enemies,
agriculture, house building; if malefic, wasting illness (21.4). Use them for colour only.

## 6. Variants and disputes

- BPHS counts the bad branch from both lagna and dasha lord; Phaladeepika 20.29 makes position from the dasha lord
  decisive. Common modern usage reads both and names the 6/8 and 2/12 axes shadashtaka and dvirdvadasha (a secondary
  lesson page says so; no school-level source read).
  [MODERN]: whether 2/12 is bad is a modern extension; the fetched verses name 6/8/12.
- Laghu Parashari adds: a bhukti lord unrelated to the dasha lord gives results by its own functional nature; related
  ones give the dasha lord's results (not fetched; see functional-nature file).
- Phaladeepika ch. 21 (Jupiter MD, Mercury bhukti, about 21.41) records that "some" call it bad and "others" good: the
  texts admit disputes.
- **Saravali ch. 42 (Moola dasha sub-periods, read).** Sub-periods go to planets by position from the dasha lord
  (conjunct 1/2 share, 5th/9th 1/3, 7th 1/7, 4th/8th 1/4; 42.1-5), and their results follow position from the dasha
  lord: conjunct planets bring confinement and loss unless the dasha lord is in a kendra/trikona from lagna (42.6-7);
  7th from the dasha lord: trouble to or through the spouse (42.8); 8th from it: death-like danger, loss, confinement
  (42.9-10); 4th from it: gains through friends, honour (42.11). Benefic sub-lords give good, malefic sub-lords bad
  (42.2-3). Per-pair descriptions follow (42.12 ff.). This is a different sub-period scheme from Vimshottari, but it
  states the same principle as Phaladeepika 20.29: **position from the dasha lord** decides the bhukti.
- **Saravali ch. 43**: a malefic sub-period in a malefic dasha brings enemies and doubts; Mars dasha Saturn bhukti is
  named as dangerous even with long-life indications; the lagna lord's bhukti inside the dasha of a planet hostile to
  the lagna lord is dangerous (attributed to Satya) (43.1-4).

## 7. Worked micro-example

Aries lagna, Saturn MD, Mercury AD. Mercury owns 3 and 6, sits in the 12th; Saturn in the 7th. Mercury is 6th from
Saturn: bad (PD 20.29). Mercury owns 3/6 (BPHS 48.18): bad. Output: a pressured bhukti; area = 12th (expense, travel)
and 6th (disputes).

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra

- Engine must export, for each (MD, AD) pair: `ad_from_md_house` (1-12), `ad_from_lagna_house`, `friendship_natural`,
  `friendship_temporal`, `relation` (conjunct/aspect/exchange/none), `ad_is_maraka_lord`, `both_dusthana` flag,
  `ad_nakshatra_tara` (vipat/pratyak/vadha from janma star). **Engine addition** if any is missing; Node derives none.
- This is the main monthly driver for Lucky and Today views and for the dated windows on Love/Work.
- Weight: AD relation rules > AD per-pair description; AD never overrides a strongly bad MD lord fully (BPHS 50.36:
  a benefic AD eases, not cancels, an unfavourable dasha, stated for rasi dashas).
- Pitfall: the death/maraka branch must be rendered as health caution only.
