# Judging a dasha by its lord's condition (daśā-phala-nirṇaya: sthāna, bala, avasthā, sambandha)

> The general principles that decide whether a Vimshottari mahadasha (and, by the same logic, an antardasha) is good,
> bad or mixed, before any per-planet description is read. The classical texts agree on the core: the period gives
> the results of (a) the houses its lord owns, (b) the house it occupies, (c) its dignity and strength, (d) the planets
> it is joined with or aspected by. Phaladeepika gives the cleanest quantitative scale; BPHS adds "condition at the
> start of the dasha" and the dreshkana timing rule. This is the most widely used and most reliable rule set for
> dasha judgement; every school starts here.

## 2. When it applies

Every graha dasha at every level (BPHS 61.2 says pratyantar effects are judged "in this manner"; Phaladeepika
21.84 extends bhukti logic to antara and antarantara). All life areas: the house(s) owned and occupied pick the area,
the condition picks the polarity.

## 3. Inputs required

Per period lord, from the engine:
- sign, house from lagna, houses owned (both signs), dignity (deep exalt / exalt / mulatrikona / own / great friend /
  friend / neutral / enemy / great enemy / debil / deep debil), combustion, retrogression, vargottama flag, D9 sign;
- degree within sign (for dreshkana timing, BPHS 47.3-4) and sign rising-type (shirshodaya etc.);
- conjunctions and graha drishti received (with benefic/malefic and functional status of each);
- shadbala or an equivalent strength ratio; Moon paksha bala (Phaladeepika 19.8);
- **sky at the dasha start** (transit sign/house/dignity of the lord on the start date) for BPHS 47.5-6 and 48.8.

## 4. Rule procedure

1. **Houses owned** (BPHS 48.2-7; Phaladeepika 20.2-20). Read the lord-of-house list for each sign it owns.
2. **House occupied**. Kendra/trikona/11th good; 6/8/12 bad (BPHS 47.5-6; Phaladeepika 20.14, 20.22, 20.27;
   Saravali 41.66-68). 3/6/11 **lords** bad (BPHS 34.4, 48.3-7). Planets **in** 3/6/11 or joined to their lords: the
   e-text renders BPHS 48.18 as unfavourable and adds that Rahu/Ketu there are favourable; the Sanskrit page shows only
   the opening clause (*triṣaḍāyagatānāṃ ca triṣaḍāyādhipair yujām*) with the result cut off. See §6 conflict.
3. **Dignity scale** (Phaladeepika 20.30): weight = 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25, ~0 for exalt / MT / own / friend / enemy-debil;
   combust counts as debilitated. Reverse polarity for natural malefics ("scale reversed for malefics": read as the
   harm a malefic does shrinks as dignity rises).
4. **Direction of movement** (Phaladeepika 20.56): arohini = improving, avarohini = declining, madhya = steady good,
   adhama = poor.
5. **Association** (BPHS 48.9-17; Phaladeepika 20.21): joined to or exchanging with the 5th or 9th lord lifts the
   dasha; joined to a maraka lord or sitting in a maraka house spoils even a benefic (BPHS 48.19).
6. **Condition at the dasha start** (BPHS 47.5-6, 48.8): repeat steps 2-3 for the lord's transit position on the start
   date. Birth and start both good = good; one bad = mixed; both bad = bad (the same two-moment logic is stated
   explicitly for rasi dashas at BPHS 50.11-17).
7. **Timing inside the dasha**: dreshkana 1/2/3 = results at start/middle/end, reversed if retrograde, always reversed
   for Rahu/Ketu (BPHS 47.3-4); sign rising-type gives a second three-way split (Saravali 41.75 for planetary dashas;
   BPHS 50.88 states it in the rasi-dasha chapter). No text found ranks the two methods.
6b. **Sky at the dasha start, second check** (Saravali 41.6, 44.1-3): a planet in a benefic/friend's division and
   benefic-aspected, or one dignified planet joined/aspected by a benefic, at the start moment softens or cancels the
   dasha's evils.
8. **Natural significations** (BPHS 47.2; Phaladeepika ch. 19): colour the result with the planet's karakatwa
   (see `mahadasha-results-per-planet.md`).

## 5. Interpretation rules

Lord-of-house dasha results (paraphrase; [TEXT] BPHS 48.2-7, strong/weak split from Phaladeepika 20.2-20):

| Lord of | BPHS 48.2-7 (general) | Phaladeepika strong (20.2-13) | Phaladeepika weak (20.15-20) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | reputation, bodily comfort | eminence, health, rising prosperity | fear, illness, loss of position |
| 2 | distress, fear of death | family success, earnings by speech | broken word, heavy spending, eye trouble |
| 3 | evil results | help of siblings, courage, honour | loss of brother, internal treachery |
| 4 | joy of home and land | vehicles, land, house | harm to mother, property, danger from water |
| 5 | learning, joy of children | a son, councillor's post, merit | loss of son, confusion, royal displeasure |
| 6 | bodily pain, fear of enemies | defeat of enemies, health | thieves, defeat, disease, wounds |
| 7 | suffering to spouse, fear of death | marital pleasure, travel | separation, disease |
| 8 | fear of death, loss of wealth | debt relief, end of disputes | sorrow, poverty, possible death |
| 9 | gain and renown | family prosperity, royal favour | loss of father/elder, penury |
| 10 | honour in royal assemblies | success, fame, stable position | fruitless effort, dishonour, absence abroad |
| 11 | obstacles to gains, illness | steady income, reunion with kin | bad news, sibling/child trouble |
| 12 | considerable suffering | spending on good causes, honours | disease, bondage, wealth drains |

Tagged rules:
- [TEXT] Results follow the period lord's strength at birth (Phaladeepika 20.1; BPHS 47.2).
- [TEXT] A planet good at birth but placed badly at the dasha start gives weaker or mixed results (BPHS 47.5-6, 48.8).
- [TEXT] 6/8/12 lords become favourable in their dasha when joined to a trikona lord (BPHS 48.14).
- [TEXT] Mutual dasha/bhukti of two planets that own or occupy 6/8/12 is unfavourable (Phaladeepika 20.22).
- [TEXT] Benefic association or aspect softens a weak lord; malefic aspect worsens it (BPHS 47.12-15, Sun entry; same
  pattern for every planet in ch. 47).
- [TEXT] Vargottama lord = favourable dasha (Phaladeepika 20.22).
- [TEXT] Exalted or strong lord = incomparably good; own/mulatrikona/own navamsa or hemmed by benefics = moderately
  good; moving toward exaltation or in a friend's sign = feebly good (Saravali 41.22-24).
- [TEXT] Enemy navamsa or debilitated navamsa spoils a good rasi placement to "mixed" (Saravali 41.19).
- Design inference (not a text claim): the texts name dignity, rays and placement, not shadbala; using shadbala as the
  "strength" input is an engineering choice to be labelled as such.

## 6. Variants and disputes

- **BPHS ch. 50 vs graha dashas.** The eighteen-state list, arohini/avarohini naming and retrograde rule sit in the
  e-text's chapter on Chara and other rasi dashas (50.73-87). Mantreswara states the same four-way naming for graha
  dashas (Phaladeepika 20.56). Use Phaladeepika as the graha-dasha locus; treat BPHS 50.73-87 as supporting.
- **Avasthas.** BPHS ch. 45 (avasthas) gives deeptadi and lajjitadi states but, in the e-text read here, ties them to
  bhava results, not to "during its dasha". Saravali ch. 41-44 (read) judge dashas by dignity, rays and strength and do
  not use the deeptadi names. Do not ship deeptadi dasha texts as [TEXT] until a verse is found.
- **Brihat Jataka ch. 8** describes dashas computed from longevity (amsha/pinda ayurdaya), not Vimshottari; its
  strength rules (strongest of lagna, Sun, Moon starts) belong to that system. Map with care.
- **Number of states.** BPHS 50.73-77 lists 18 conditions; Phaladeepika 20.56 compresses to 4; 20.30 grades 5 steps;
  Saravali 41.22-24 grades 3 (incomparable / moderate / feeble good).
- **Unresolved conflict: the 11th and the 3rd/6th houses.** BPHS ch. 47 entries treat a planet in the 11th (and for
  Saturn the 3rd) as good; the e-text of BPHS 48.18 calls planets in 3/6/11 unfavourable; Saravali 41.66 calls the 6th
  evil. Design must not encode both "11th good" and "in 11th bad". Proposed default until the Sanskrit of 48.18-19 is
  read: lordship of 3/6/11 bad; occupation of 3/6/11 neutral for natural malefics and nodes, of 11th good for all.
- **Saravali's chapter is about Moola dasha** (an ayurdaya-based order: strongest of lagna/Sun/Moon first, then kendra,
  panaphara, apoklima planets; 41.3-5). Its judging rules (41.6-8, 41.19, 41.22-24, 41.66-75, 44.1-3) are stated for
  dashas in general and match BPHS/Phaladeepika, so they are used here; its order and durations are not Vimshottari.
- **Retrograde**: Phaladeepika 20.14 treats retrograde as strong; Saravali 41.68 says retrograde malefics give
  wandering and enmity (benefics exempt). Keep both as flags; do not assume retro = good for malefics.

## 7. Worked micro-example

Leo lagna, Jupiter (5th and 8th lord) in Sagittarius (5th, own sign, also mulatrikona degrees possible) at birth.
Jupiter dasha: owns 5 (good: learning, children) and 8 (hidden, debts); occupies a trikona; dignity 0.5-0.75
(Phaladeepika 20.30). If on the dasha start date Jupiter transits Capricorn (debilitated, 6th from lagna), BPHS 47.5-6
marks the start as weak: expect slow early years, good results later.

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra

- Engine output per period lord (all levels): `owned_houses[]`, `occupied_house`, `dignity_step` (Phaladeepika 5-step
  weight), `combust`, `retro`, `vargottama`, `dasha_kind` (arohini/avarohini/madhya/adhama), `start_transit_dignity`,
  `start_transit_house`, `timing_third` (start/middle/end from dreshkana, reversal flags), `associations[]` with
  functional status. **Engine addition:** the dasha-start transit placement and `timing_third` are not known to be
  exported today; both must be computed by the engine, never in Node.
- This is the base polarity layer for every board. Self board uses lord-of-1 and Moon; Love uses 5/7 and Venus; Work
  uses 10/6/2/11 and Saturn/Sun/Mercury.
- Weight: highest of all dasha-results rules; transit and pratyantar only modulate it.
- Pitfalls: double-counting a lord that owns a good and a bad house (report both themes, do not average);
  combust = debilitated only per Phaladeepika; retrograde is treated as strong in Phaladeepika 20.14.
