# Functional benefics, yogakaraka and relation rules (Laghu Parāśarī / Jātaka-candrikā; BPHS ch. 34)

> The lordship rules that decide whether a planet's dasha is functionally good or bad for a given lagna, independent of
> whether it is a natural benefic: trikona lords good, 3/6/11 lords bad, kendra lordship neutralises (kendrādhipatya
> doṣa), 2/12/8 lords give results by association, yogakaraka = owner of a kendra and a trikona, and "a planet gives
> results through the planets it is related to". Laghu Parashari is the classic condensation; its rules are verified
> here through their parallels in BPHS ch. 34 (Sanskrit checked) and Phaladeepika 20.41-54. This is the most used
> rule set in North and South Indian dasha practice.

## 2. When it applies

All graha dashas and bhuktis; it sets the functional sign (good/bad) of each lord before condition is read. Rahu/Ketu
get their functional status from occupation and association (34.16-17).

## 3. Inputs required

Lagna sign; for each planet: both owned houses, occupied house, conjunctions, full graha drishti exchanged, sign
exchanges (parivartana); natural benefic/malefic class with waxing/waning Moon (34.9); whether Mercury is with
malefics.

## 4. Rule procedure

1. Classify each planet by owned houses: trikona (1, 5, 9) good; 3/6/11 bad; kendra (4, 7, 10; 1 is both) neutralises
   (benefic loses good, malefic loses bad); 2/12 neutral, follow association and the other owned house; 8th bad.
   Exceptions to the 8th-lord fault: Phaladeepika 20.41 exempts an 8th lord that is also the lagna lord; the BPHS 34.6
   gloss read here instead names co-lordship of the 3rd, 7th, 11th or a trikona (gloss inconsistent; recheck the
   Sanskrit). Sun/Moon exempt from the 8th-lord fault (BPHS 34.7; PD 20.41).
2. If one planet owns a kendra and a trikona: **yogakaraka** (BPHS 34.13). Derive per lagna from the rule (e.g. Saturn
   for Taurus and Libra, Mars for Cancer and Leo, Venus for Capricorn and Aquarius follow arithmetically); do not cite
   the BPHS 34.22-44 lagna glosses for these, since the page's English was inconsistent about which lagna each verse
   names.
3. Relations: kendra lord and trikona lord joined, exchanging, or in mutual full aspect = raja-yoga pair (34.11-13;
   PD 20.45-47). Cancelled if either also owns a dusthana and the link is mere mutual lordship (34.15).
4. Kendradhipati dosha scale: rises Moon < Mercury < Jupiter < Venus (34.10). Jupiter and Venus as kendra lords become
   malefic, and as 2nd/7th lords can kill (PD 20.50-51).
5. Dasha-bhukti relation: a bhukti lord related to the dasha lord expresses the dasha lord's results; yogakaraka-
   related bhuktis give the yoga (PD 20.44, 20.48-49).

## 5. Interpretation rules

- [TEXT] Trikona lords always good; 3/6/11 lords bad (BPHS 34.2-4; PD 20.41).
- [TEXT] Benefic kendra lords not good, malefic kendra lords not bad (BPHS 34.2; PD 20.41).
- [TEXT] 2/12/8 lords give results by association (BPHS 34.5). 8th lord bad; also-lagna-lord exception per PD 20.41
  only; Sun/Moon exempt (BPHS 34.7; PD 20.41).
- [TEXT] Yogakaraka dasha and the bhuktis of planets related to it bring prosperity (PD 20.48-49).
- [TEXT] Rahu/Ketu act as yogakarakas when in kendra/trikona and related to a lord (BPHS 34.17; PD 20.52-53).
- [COMM/MODERN] "A planet gives the results of its dispositor and associates": the association half is [TEXT]
  (BPHS 34.5, 34.16; PD 20.39, 20.44). The **dispositor** half (sign lord) was not found in a fetched verse; it is
  commonly taught (e.g. for Rahu/Ketu) and is tagged [MODERN] here until a verse is located.
- [TEXT] A planet's dasha shows itself most clearly in the bhuktis of planets related to it (PD 20.44); planets related
  to a yogakaraka give the yoga in their bhuktis, unrelated planets do not harm it (PD 20.48-49).

## 6. Variants and disputes

- **Laghu Parashari verse numbering** varies by edition (42-verse core vs editions that number the lagna lists as
  further verses; Verma's contents run to ~73 numbered items). Do not cite LP verse numbers until checked.
- **2nd and 12th lords**: neutral by association in BPHS 34.5; the 2nd is also a maraka house (BPHS 44.2-3), so its
  lord carries a health flag even when functionally neutral.
- **Kendra dosha order**: BPHS 34.10 gives a rising order Moon < Mercury < Jupiter < Venus; the page's English
  gloss of 34.9-10 was summarised loosely; recheck the Sanskrit before encoding the order.
- **Lagna lists** (BPHS 34.22-44) differ in places from Laghu Parashari's lagna lists and from modern tables (e.g.
  Jupiter for Libra); the page's English glosses were inconsistent about which lagna each verse names.

## 7. Worked micro-example

Libra lagna: Saturn owns 4 and 5 = yogakaraka. Venus (lagna and 8th lord) not bad (lagna lord exempts 8th fault).
Jupiter owns 3 and 6 = functional malefic. Mars owns 2 and 7 = maraka by lordship (BPHS 44.2-3). Saturn MD Venus AD:
yogakaraka dasha, related lagna lord bhukti = good (PD 20.48).
