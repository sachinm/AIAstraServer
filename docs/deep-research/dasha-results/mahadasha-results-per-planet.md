# Mahadasha results per planet (graha-daśā-phala)

> The per-planet result lists for the nine Vimshottari mahadashas: what a Sun, Moon, ... Ketu period gives when its
> lord is strong/well placed versus weak/afflicted. These are descriptive lists, but BPHS ch. 47 states each one as a
> conditional (if exalted / own / kendra / with 9th-10th lord ... else ...), which makes them usable as rules.
> Phaladeepika ch. 19 gives a shorter list of natural significations. Use these only after the condition rules in
> `dasha-lord-condition-principles.md` have set polarity.

## 1. Sources

- BPHS, archive.org e-text (97-chapter numbering, = enjoylearningsanskrit numbering), ch. 47 "Effects of Dashas":
  47.7-15 Sun, 47.16-22 Moon, 47.27-32 Mars, 47.34-39 Rahu, 47.45-48 Jupiter, 47.52-56 Saturn, 47.61-70 Mercury,
  47.71-77 Ketu, 47.78-89 Venus (verse ranges from an OCR e-text, ±2). Sharma (wisdomlib) numbers this chapter 49.
- Phaladeepika, Subrahmanya Sastri tr. 1950 (wisdomlib OCR), ch. 19 "Dasas and their effects": 19.5-6 Sun,
  19.7-8 Moon, 19.9 Mars, 19.10 Mercury, 19.11 Jupiter, 19.12 Venus, 19.13 Saturn, 19.14-16 Rahu, 19.17 Ketu; a second
  pass at 19.18-26 repeats each planet with more health detail.
- Brihat Jataka ch. 8 (8.10-18 per-planet): Sanskrit only read; not used for content here.
- Saravali (English translation PDF, translator not named), ch. 41 "Moola Dasa": 41.25-51 favourable vs adverse
  dasha of each of the seven planets (no nodes; the Moola scheme has none); 41.53-59 Sun's dasha by five placements
  (exaltation, fall, mulatrikona, kendra, enemy sign; Sun in lagna gives rulership, in the 8th bodily affliction and
  wandering); 41.60-65 Moon's dasha by six states (exalted, debilitated, friend, enemy, waning, waxing; Moon in the
  8th: humiliation and hardship).

## 2. When it applies

Vimshottari mahadasha (and the antardasha lord's natural colour). Life areas follow the planet's karakatwa plus the
houses it owns.

## 3. Inputs required

Same as the condition file: sign, house, dignity, combustion, waxing/waning Moon, conjunctions/aspects, which house
lords it is linked with (BPHS names the 9th, 10th, 5th, 2nd, 3rd lords explicitly).

## 4. Rule procedure

1. Decide strong vs weak with BPHS 47.5-6 (and Phaladeepika 20.14/20.30).
2. Read the matching column below.
3. Add the lordship links BPHS names for that planet (e.g. Sun linked with 9th/10th lord).
4. Apply the start/middle/end notes where the text gives them.

## 6. Variants and disputes

- Phaladeepika ch. 19 lists are mostly unconditional (good Sun vs bad Sun at 19.5-6 is the main conditional), and
  19.18-26 is a second pass without stated reason; the translator does not explain the doubling.
- BPHS phase notes (start/middle/end) differ by planet and conflict with the generic dreshkana rule (47.3-4) when both
  are applied; no text ranks them. Proposed: dreshkana rule first, planet note as colour.
- Saravali's per-planet lists (41.25-51) follow the same favourable/adverse split and broadly agree with BPHS ch. 47
  in themes (e.g. favourable Jupiter: honours, advisers, wealth; adverse Saturn: separation, windy illness,
  confinement). Saravali's dashas are Moola (ayurdaya-based) dashas; the lists are reusable as natural colour.
- Brihat Jataka per-planet verses (8.10-18) were seen in Sanskrit only; its dashas are ayurdaya-based.

## 7. Worked micro-example

Cancer lagna, Saturn (7th, 8th lord) exalted in Libra (4th). BPHS 47.52-56: exalted Saturn = recognition, property;
but it owns 7 and 8 (BPHS 48.5: fear of death to self/spouse, loss of wealth) and is a maraka (7th lord). Output:
"strong period for property and standing; strain around spouse and joint money".

## 8. Design notes for AI Astra

- Engine supplies the condition flags; the prompt layer owns wording. Store the table above as a technique contract
  (planet x strong/weak x phase), not as model free-text.
- Board mapping: Self (lagna lord, Sun, Moon dashas), Love (Venus, Moon, 5th/7th lords), Work (Sun, Saturn, Mercury,
  10th lord).
- Weight: per-planet description < house-ownership result. A strong Saturn owning 8 still gives 8th-house themes.
- Pitfall: health and death phrases in the texts (poison, imprisonment, death) must never reach users raw; translate
  to tone ("demanding", "closing").
