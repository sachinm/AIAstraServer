# How personality, strengths and executive-coaching products present a profile, strengths, weaknesses and growth plan

Researched 2026-11-09. Scope: CliftonStrengths, 16Personalities, Enneagram (Institute, Truity), Hogan (HPI/HDS/MVPI), BetterUp, PrinciplesYou, Big Five / Understand Myself, Insights Discovery, MBTI official reports, executive 360s (Leadership Versatility Index). Target use: the "You" board of a Vedic astrology app (who you are, mind, body, soul's aim, gifts and knots, struggles, this year), for women 25-45, professionals, executives.

Method note: several primary sample PDFs (Gallup CliftonStrengths 34 sample, Hogan Challenge sample) downloaded but could not be text-extracted in this environment (no PDF tooling); the Hogan Insight HDS sample URL returned 404; the Enneagram Institute Levels page returned 404. Findings rely on vendor product/help pages, vendor-hosted search snippets and partner-hosted samples. Section lists not verified page by page are marked. Quotes are under 15 words. DISC, EnneaApp and Truity report section orders were not covered by primary sources (see Gaps).

## 1. Section names and order: what comes first

### Takeaway
Every product opens with an identity anchor (a named type/archetype/theme list plus a one-line tagline), then strengths, then a framed "risk" section (blind spots / weaknesses / derailers), then relationships or communication, then development suggestions. Executive products add a values/drivers layer and split "normal day" from "under pressure" behaviour.

### Cited Findings
- **CliftonStrengths 34**: ranks all 34 themes "in order of how frequently and intensely they show up"; personalised insights for themes 1-5, detailed information for 1-10, plus explanations of potential blind spots. Each top theme gets a page headed "Why Your [Theme] Is Unique"; action items "you can implement immediately". The report answers "How am I unique, and what gives me an edge?" — [Gallup 34 report page](https://gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/403127/cliftonstrengths-34-report.aspx); [Gallup product finder](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/253868/popular-cliftonstrengthsassessment-products.aspx); [Gallup Top 5 sample PDF](https://www.gallup.com/file/cliftonstrengths/en/547511/CliftonStrengths_Top_5_Report_Sample-prelaunch.pdf)
- **CliftonStrengths Top 5 report** contents (newer report, from search snippet): "Custom strengths insights, Your areas of excellence, Navigating blind spots, Managing your lesser strengths, Unique domain distribution" — [Gallup Top 5 report page](https://gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/512510/cliftonstrengths-top-5-report.aspx)
- **16Personalities type profile** order: Introduction -> Strengths & Weaknesses -> Romantic Relationships -> Friendships -> Parenthood -> Career Paths -> Workplace Habits -> Conclusion -> Premium upsell. Opens with type code + nickname ("INFJ (Advocate)") and a one-line tagline: "Advocates are quiet visionaries, often serving as inspiring and tireless idealists." Intro sub-heads are thematic ("Seeking Purpose", "A Personal Mission") — [16Personalities INFJ](https://www.16personalities.com/infj-personality)
- 16Personalities premium is sold as three areas (career, personal growth, relationships) with strengths and weaknesses per life area; career guide sold separately — [16Personalities premium](https://16personalities.com/academy/consul); [Career suite](https://www.16personalities.com/academy/logician)
- **PrinciplesYou**: up to three archetypes (top + two others); 30 archetypes in 10 groups (e.g. Advocates: Inspirer, Coach, Campaigner); "Archetype Archipelago" map; traits and facets as 0-100 percentiles vs a 3,000+ norm group; three "Personality Orientations" (Cognitive, Interpersonal, Motivational); written "Insights" readouts — [PrinciplesYou results FAQ](https://support.principlesus.com/principlesyou-results-faq); [Assessment overview](https://support.principlesus.com/assessment-overview). Third-party: three parts (archetype, traits/facets, insights) and also the archetype you "least identify with" — [Marlee blog](https://getmarlee.com/blog/principals-ray-dalio). Official page says 17 core dimensions; a third-party blog says 48 — conflicting, official preferred.
- **Insights Discovery** Foundation Chapter (always first): Overview, Personal Style, Interacting with Others, Decision Making, Key Strengths & Weaknesses, Value to the Team, Communication, Possible Blind Spots (newer versions: "Possible Weaknesses"), Opposite Type, Suggestions for Development; optional chapters (management, sales, etc.) assigned by the practitioner — [Insights support: chapters](https://support.insights.com/how-to-guides/understanding-and-assigning-chapters-for-insights-discovery-personal-profiles); [Colourworks sample profile](https://www.thecolourworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Insight-Discovery-Sample-Profile.pdf)
- Insights identity anchor = wheel position + type name, e.g. "26 Motivating Inspirer (Classic)" (from partner-hosted sample file names) — [Colourworks sample](https://www.thecolourworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/HollyMiller-26-Motivating-Inspirer-Classic-002.pdf)
- **Hogan Leadership Forecast Series** = three lenses: HPI "bright side" normal personality; HDS behaviours "that emerge during times of stress"; MVPI core values and drivers. Summary report: Performance Strengths (HPI), Values and Drivers (MVPI), Performance Challenges (HDS), tips for career development. Flash report = two-page score summary — [Peter Berry LFS](https://peterberry.com.au/leadership-forecast-series/); [Performance Programs LFS](https://performanceprograms.com/?p=232208); [Hogan Summary sample](https://performanceprograms.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/Hogan-Summary-Report-Sample.pdf)
- **Hogan HDS** report opens with a background section: under pressure most people show counterproductive tendencies, called "risk factors"; 11 scales grouped into three styles — [Hogan Challenge sample](https://performanceprograms.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/Hogan-Challenge-Sample-Report.pdf); [Hogan HDS FAQ](https://www.hoganassessments.com/blog/faq-blog-series-the-hogan-development-survey/)
- **Big Five / Understand Myself**: 5 traits + 10 aspects (e.g. Extraversion = Enthusiasm + Assertiveness; Neuroticism = Withdrawal + Volatility), each a percentile vs a 10,000-person normative sample, with descriptive paragraphs per trait and aspect; third-party copies show wording like "less polite than 78 of 100 people" — [Understand Myself assessment](https://understandmyself.com/personality-assessment); [FAQ](https://understandmyself.com/faq); [Scribd copy, unofficial](https://www.scribd.com/document/629406134/Understand-Myself)
- **BetterUp Whole Person Assessment** -> "Whole Person Report" across 25 dimensions (mindsets, behaviours, outcomes; "Thriving" and "Inspiring"), re-measured at "Reflection Points" about every 4 months — [BetterUp support](https://support.betterup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049794271-The-Whole-Person-Assessment); [BetterUp Whole Person Model blog](https://www.betterup.com/blog/whole-person-model-to-build-inspiring-leaders-thriving-teams)
- **MBTI official**: separate reports by purpose: Step I Profile, Interpretive, Career, Stress Management, Decision-Making Style, Step II, Interpretive Report for Organizations; the plain Profile Report is "not tailored for focused development work" — [Myers-Briggs sample reports (older catalog)](https://shop.themyersbriggs.com/SampleReports/Reports.aspx); [Psychometrics MBTI profile report](https://psychometrics.com/assessments/mbti-profile-report)

### Inferences
- Common skeleton (6-10 sections): identity line -> core description -> strengths -> risk/blind spots -> relationships/communication -> work/value -> development -> (consumer only) upsell. The You board maps cleanly: who you are = identity anchor; gifts and knots = strengths paired with blind spots; what you struggle with = "under pressure" layer; this year = a BetterUp-style re-measured "now" layer.
- The first screen is a named identity plus one sentence, not a score.
- Hogan's three lenses (normal day / under pressure / what drives you) map well onto mind / struggles / soul's aim.

### Gaps
- Exact page-by-page headings of the Gallup 34 sample and Hogan HDS sample could not be extracted (PDFs unreadable here). Whether Gallup 34 has a dedicated "Questions" section is unconfirmed (reflection questions confirmed only for role reports).
- No official PrinciplesYou "challenges" heading; Hogan Potential Report exact headings not found.
- Enneagram Institute (RHETI), Truity, EnneaApp and DISC report section orders not confirmed from primary pages.

## 2. Framing weaknesses without fatalism

### Takeaway
Dominant devices: (a) weakness = a strength overused or under pressure (Hogan, Kaplan/Kaiser, Insights); (b) "blind spot" with an explicit "this may not happen" disclaimer (Gallup); (c) scale ends described neutrally with "may" and "tendency" (PrinciplesYou); (d) "lesser" instead of "weak" (Gallup "Managing your lesser strengths"); (e) healthy / average / unhealthy bands (Enneagram).

### Cited Findings
- Hogan: derailers are strengths in normal conditions that "under stress or pressure" become "crippling career obstacles" — [Hogan HDS FAQ](https://www.hoganassessments.com/blog/faq-blog-series-the-hogan-development-survey/). Insight HDS sample: elevated scores "can be strengths, but when overused can derail" — [Hogan Insight HDS sample](https://performanceprograms.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Hogan-Insight-HDS-Report-Sample.pdf) (404 at fetch; wording from search snippet)
- Hogan group report frames derailers as "risks of overdoing behaviors" — [Hogan Group Analytics sample](https://kl-solutions.azurewebsites.net/downloads/Hogan-Group-Analytics-sample-report.pdf)
- Gallup sections "Navigating blind spots" and "Managing your lesser strengths"; Gallup says blind spots are not guaranteed: "no promise that you will in fact trip" over them — [Gallup Top 5 report](https://gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/512510/cliftonstrengths-top-5-report.aspx); [Gallup on weaknesses](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/249404/insights-help-understand-people-weaknesses.aspx)
- Gallup "balconies and basements" metaphor (healthy vs unhealthy expression of the same theme) — title only confirmed — [Gallup article](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/251207/balconies-basements-strength-ever-problem.aspx)
- PrinciplesYou: "nothing inherently 'good' or 'bad' about scoring a particular way"; high Detailed "may have a tendency to lose the forest in the trees"; high Determined may read as "tunnel vision" — [PrinciplesYou results FAQ](https://support.principlesus.com/principlesyou-results-faq)
- 16Personalities: weakness as the cost of a virtue: may chase ideals so hard "they don't take adequate care of themselves"; "very sensitive to criticism of any kind" — [16Personalities INFJ](https://www.16personalities.com/infj-personality)
- Insights: blind-spot section explores "your less conscious side"; Opposite Type is "most difficult as well as your best ally" — [Colourworks guide](https://thecolourworks.com/getting-the-most-from-your-profile-2)
- Kaplan & Kaiser Leadership Versatility Index (360): each behaviour rated "too little", "the right amount" or "too much"; "too much strength can be a weakness"; the more senior and successful, the more a leader over-relies on skills that brought prior success — [Training Mag](https://trainingmag.com/?p=11950); [CBS News](https://cbsnews.com/news/stop-overdoing-your-strengths)
- Counter-view (Zenger Folkman, 2019): asking people to watch for a strength becoming too effective is "terribly confusing" — [Zenger Folkman 2019](https://data.zengerfolkman.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Can-Strengths-Be-Taken-Too-Far_WP-2019.pdf)
- Enneagram (Riso-Hudson): nine Levels of Development in three bands, Healthy (1-3), Average (4-6), Unhealthy (7-9); stress (disintegration) line 1->4->2->8->5->7->1 and 9->6->3->9, growth (integration) line the reverse — [Meaning Movement](https://www.themeaningmovement.com/enneagram-stress-and-growth/); [Sociotype levels](https://sociotype.com/enneagram/levels) (secondary; Institute page 404). Levels are specific to the Riso-Hudson lineage, not the wider tradition — [Sociotype lines](https://sociotype.com/enneagram/lines)

### Inferences
- For "gifts and knots": pair each gift with the knot it becomes under pressure or overuse. Avoid a free-standing "weaknesses" list.
- Use conditional verbs ("may", "tends to", "when pressed") and a Gallup-style "not a verdict" line; this also counters fatalism, which matters for an astrology product.
- "When well / when strained" bands give a toggle with no fixed verdict.

### Gaps
- Primary Enneagram Institute wording not retrieved. Hogan per-scale sub-headings not confirmed.

## 3. Turning traits into action

### Takeaway
Action comes as short immediate action items per strength (Gallup), do more / do less / keep doing (360 practice), too-little / right / too-much scales (LVI), growth vs stress direction (Enneagram), and periodic re-measurement with a coach (BetterUp). No product found with a formal 4-tier ladder.

### Cited Findings
- Gallup action items "you can implement immediately"; a Gallup debrief worksheet asks for two ways to use a theme "more intentionally tomorrow" — [Gallup product finder](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/253868/popular-cliftonstrengthsassessment-products.aspx); [Montana State debrief handout](https://fdn.montana.edu/aycss/montanas-own/CliftonStrengthsDebriefHandout.pdf)
- Kaplan-Kaiser/HBR: review top-rated behaviours and ask "is this too much of a good thing?"; ask coworkers what to do more, do less, continue — [Training Mag](https://trainingmag.com/?p=11950)
- Insights closes with "Suggestions for Development" — [Colourworks guide](https://thecolourworks.com/getting-the-most-from-your-profile-2)
- Hogan Summary ends with "tips for career development" — [Peter Berry LFS](https://peterberry.com.au/leadership-forecast-series/)
- BetterUp: report is "a starting point"; re-measured about every 4 months — [BetterUp support](https://support.betterup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049794271-The-Whole-Person-Assessment)
- PrinciplesYou reflection worksheet (unofficial): pick highest/lowest traits, write growth action plans — [Scribd worksheet](https://www.scribd.com/document/555029116/PrinciplesYou-Reflection-Exercise)
- Critique: LVI book "lacks a true action plan" — [Portland Book Review](https://portlandbookreview.com/fear-your-strengths-what-you-are-best-at-could-be-your-biggest-problem/)

### Inferences
- A 3-step card fits: "use it" (gift) / "watch it" (knot under pressure) / "try this week" (one concrete act). Do more / do less is the wording executives already know.
- A dated, revisited "this year" layer mirrors BetterUp's re-measurement cadence.

### Gaps
- 16Personalities, Truity and DISC action formats not confirmed.

## 4. Executive-grade vs consumer products

### Takeaway
Executive products (Hogan, LVI, BetterUp, PrinciplesYou) speak about reputation, how others see you and career risk; they are normed, scored and debriefed by a coach. Consumer products (16Personalities, Truity) speak about identity, love, friendship and parenting in warm prose and sell upgrades.

### Cited Findings
- Hogan contrasts what you see "in an interview" (HPI) with what you see "after prolonged exposure" (HDS) — [Hogan Summary sample (snippet)](https://performanceprograms.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/Hogan-Summary-Report-Sample.pdf)
- HDS behaviours emerge under stress, "damaging relationships, and derailing careers" — [Peter Berry LFS](https://peterberry.com.au/leadership-forecast-series/)
- 360 evidence (421 upper-level managers): least effective overrate themselves, most effective underrate — [Training Mag](https://trainingmag.com/?p=11950)
- PrinciplesYou: percentiles vs norm group, co-developed with Adam Grant, neutral tone — [PrinciplesYou FAQ](https://support.principlesus.com/principlesyou-results-faq)
- BetterUp outcome figures (e.g. 29% leadership improvement for low starters) are vendor claims; no independent evaluation found — [BetterUp Whole Person Model FAQs](https://www.betterup.com/whole-person-model-faqs)
- 16Personalities consumer profile includes Romantic Relationships, Friendships, Parenthood; paid report around $9 and career suite around €29 (prices vary by page) — [16Personalities INFJ](https://www.16personalities.com/infj-personality); [Career suite](https://www.16personalities.com/academy/logician)
- MBTI splits purpose-specific reports rather than one long one — [Myers-Briggs sample reports](https://shop.themyersbriggs.com/SampleReports/Reports.aspx)

### Inferences
- For executives and high earners: lead with consequence (how it shows at work, what it costs under pressure), keep it dense and specific, drop love/parenting filler unless asked, and end each block with one clear action.

### Gaps
- No survey or interview data found on what executives say they value in such reports; no HBS case on Hogan or BetterUp retrieved.

## 5. Segmentation by career stage, age and gender

### Takeaway
Segmentation is by report type and audience, not rewritten core text: Gallup Manager/Leader/Sales/Student reports, Insights optional chapters, MBTI College report, Hogan High Potential. No gender-adapted wording found; copy uses "you" or type-plural.

### Cited Findings
- Gallup role reports (manager, leader, sales, student) include reflection questions — [Gallup product finder](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/253868/popular-cliftonstrengthsassessment-products.aspx)
- Insights chapters assigned per person — [Insights support](https://support.insights.com/how-to-guides/understanding-and-assigning-chapters-for-insights-discovery-personal-profiles)
- MBTI Step I Interpretive Report, College version — [Psychometrics sample](https://www.psychometrics.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/JSample_Step_I_-_Interpretive_Report_College.pdf)
- Hogan High Potential Report — [Hogan HiPo overview](https://cdn.agilitycms.com/qti-management-services/hogan/Hogan%20-%20High%20Potential%20Report%20Overview.pdf)
- 16Personalities INFJ header art shows a bearded man; text uses type-plural "Advocates" — [16Personalities INFJ](https://www.16personalities.com/infj-personality)
- Truity reviewer: report "not practical for my stage in life" — [Truity reviews](https://www.truity.com/customer-reviews/enneagram-personality-test?p=15)

### Inferences
- Write in second person, no gendered pronouns; add a life-stage lens (building / leading / in transition) as context rather than separate copy.

### Gaps
- No product found that adapts copy by gender or age; no study of women 25-45 responses to such reports.

## 6. UI patterns in-app

### Takeaway
Ranked lists (Gallup 34, domain-coloured), percentile bars (Big Five, PrinciplesYou), wheels/maps (Insights wheel, PrinciplesYou "Archetype Archipelago"), type badge with illustrated character (16Personalities), variant toggle (Assertive vs Turbulent), re-measured dimension dashboard (BetterUp).

### Cited Findings
- PrinciplesYou "Archetype Archipelago": related groups shown as nearby islands — [PrinciplesYou FAQ](https://support.principlesus.com/principlesyou-results-faq)
- Gallup "Unique domain distribution" in the Top 5 report — [Gallup Top 5](https://gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/512510/cliftonstrengths-top-5-report.aspx)
- 16Personalities "INFJ-A vs. INFJ-T" variant link — [16Personalities INFJ](https://www.16personalities.com/infj-personality)
- Insights Wheel with Opposite Type — [Colourworks guide](https://thecolourworks.com/getting-the-most-from-your-profile-2)
- Truity free result: score graphic for each type plus a snippet; paywall complaints ("asked me to pay $30 for the results") — [Trustpilot Truity](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/truity.com?page=5)
- Praise: length "not too long, not too short"; users report feeling "seen" — [Truity reviews](https://www.truity.com/customer-reviews/enneagram-personality-test?p=27); [Trustpilot Truity](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/truity.com?page=10)

### Inferences
- Card per gift with its paired knot, a short ranked list, and one "when well / when strained" toggle suit the app's panel rule. Avoid percentile bars that imply false precision for chart-derived content.

### Gaps
- No formal UX teardown of these apps found; radar-chart praise/criticism unsourced.

## 7. What to cut; avoiding the Barnum / horoscope effect

### Takeaway
Critics say these reports feel accurate because statements are vague, positive and apply to everyone (Barnum/Forer effect), explicitly likening 16Personalities to horoscopes. Products that resist this rely on norms, behavioural specifics, others' view (360/reputation) and explicit "may not apply" caveats.

### Cited Findings
- Barnum critique; descriptions "all written in a positive way"; test called astrology "with a sciencey flair" — [Vice](https://vice.com/en/article/the-myers-briggs-personality-test-bullshit); [Lemmy thread](https://lemmy.4d2.org/post/132054/198655)
- Retest reliability: same person gets different types — [Neuroscience News](https://neurosciencenews.com/personality-tests-unreliable-29609/)
- Defender: fine "for self-exploration" with some research — [Vice](https://vice.com/en/article/the-myers-briggs-personality-test-bullshit)
- Gallup disclaims blind spots as non-diagnostic — [Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/249404/insights-help-understand-people-weaknesses.aspx)
- Hogan triangulates self-view vs reputation; PrinciplesYou explains "mixed patterns" across facets — [Hogan Summary sample](https://performanceprograms.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/Hogan-Summary-Report-Sample.pdf); [PrinciplesYou FAQ](https://support.principlesus.com/principlesyou-results-faq)

### Inferences
- Anti-Barnum rules for the You board: every statement names something checkable (a situation, a behaviour, a time window); include at least one kind but unflattering knot; use contrast ("unlike most people, you..."); confirm with the user before asserting. Cut generic love/friendship/parenting essays, long conclusions and upsell blocks.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data found on which sections users skip or ideal word counts per card.
