- **Validator caps** (`areaDoc.ts:48-52`): headline 22 words, 4 paragraphs, 140 words per paragraph (over the limit

- The reading's only "present" signals are:

### How the area board computes `self.activation` ("Present Tone Consensus")

The area board is code only: `selfService.ts:8-16` → `boardService.getAreaBoard` → `houseLayers.ts buildBoard`. The
web renders it in `AreaBoardView.tsx:453-493`. [fact]

- **The "7 clocks".** The five `BOARD_FAMILIES` (`houseLayers.ts:149-155`), plus `transit` and `annual`, which are
  added at `houseLayers.ts:877-878`. The five families are:
  - main (Vimshottari);
  - conditional;
  - rasi;
  - karaka;
  - short (Yogini and Tithi Ashtottari, which vote only when both agree).

  The web prints `{supVotes} supporting · {testVotes} testing of {families.length} clocks`. [fact]
- **Score of one period.** `gradeRow` (`houseLayers.ts:390-416`) calls `gradePlanet` (`:370-388`). Each of these adds
  ±1:
  - the D1 functional nature;
  - the D1 dignity;
  - the inner-varga dignity;
  - owning a primary house (+1) or a dusthana (−1);
  - the MD→AD charter cell: a 6/8/12 reversal (−1), or a star or sign link (+1).

  A total of 3 or more is graded strong, 2 moderate, 1 weak (`:388`).
- **Vote of one family.** `familyVotes` (`:424-445`) sums each system's score times its level weight (L1 = 1,
  L2 = 1.5, L3 = 2; `:156`). A sum of 0.5 or more votes support; −0.5 or less votes test. A family in `any` mode votes
  only when its systems do not disagree.
- **Transit vote.** `transitLayer` (`:448-466`) weighs what is in a primary house: Jupiter +1.5, Saturn −1.5, Rahu or
  Ketu −1, Sade Sati −1.5. `voteOf` (`:576`) turns a sum of 1 or more into support and −1 or less into test.
- **Annual vote.** `annualLayer` is summed the same way.
- **Consensus.** `votesSide` (`:754-772`) lets the conditional clock lead, or the main clock when there is no
  conditional vote; the secondary clocks cannot flip that side. `presentTone` (`:774-808`) then sets:
  - the **tone** (`demanding`, `easing`, `open`, `closing` or `steady`), from a month series (`:874`) that uses the
    activation families only;
  - the **confidence**: `confirmed` when at least max(3, 60 % of the families) agree and the main clock agrees;
    `likely` when 2 or more agree; otherwise `split`.
- **Core Strength** is `board.supports[0].text`. [fact]
- **Key Horizon** is `board.pressures[0].until`. That date comes from `releaseOf` (`houseLayers.ts:189-197`): the end
  of the afflicting planet's running period, Vimshottari first. Without one it is the window `board.ahead[0]` from
  `aheadWindows` (`:818` onwards). [fact]
- **Why we think so** opens the evidence list of those same rows (`AreaBoardView.tsx:491, 580`). [fact]
- **Shades.** `shadeResolver.ts resolveAreaShades` (`:825`) scores a fixed `SHADE_CATALOG` (`:78-540`, with 15 Self
  entries starting at `:383`). `scoreCandidate` (`:760-822`) matches the running drivers, the houses, the varga dignity
  and one person fact. The Self tie-break facts are `self.mind_shade` and `goal.self` (`:593-596`).

### Astrological derivation done in Node (each one flagged)

1. **Rasi-period lord lookup.** `houseLayers.ts:394-401`: for a sign (rasi) dasha period, Node finds the period's lord
   by looking up the D1 house card with the same sign. The co-lords of Scorpio and Aquarius are ignored. CLAUDE.md
   forbids even a lookup. [fact]
2. **Karaka join.** `houseLayers.ts:166-174` `resolvePlanet`: when a chara karaka has no `planet` field, Node joins it
   to the planet card with the same sign, house and dispositor. A heuristic, not an engine value. [fact]
3. **Transit house from sign.** `oracle.ts:325-351`: an ingress without `house_from_lagna` gets its house from a
   sign→house map that Node builds from the D1 cards (`signToHouse.get(ing.sign)`). [fact]
4. **"Over natal Moon".** `oracle.ts:352`: `[OVER NATAL MOON]` comes from Node comparing the transit sign with the
   natal Moon's sign. [fact]
5. **Activated houses.** `src/reading/digest/activation.ts:134-147` composes in Node which houses a period
   "activates". For a planet: the house it occupies, the houses it owns and the houses it aspects. For a sign: the
   sign's house and its lord's house. The inputs are engine fields; the rule is Node's. [fact]
6. **Scoring.** Everything in the tone and shade scoring above is interpretive arithmetic in Node over engine labels:
   `gradePlanet`, `YOGA_WEIGHT` (`:141-146`), the vote thresholds, the tone and the shade scores. Dignities, avasthas
   and taras are classified by regex (`houseLayers.ts:125-130, 539-540`). This is not astronomy, but these rules are
   decided in Node. [fact]
7. **Sensitive passage.** `isSensitive` (`service.ts:727-730`) and `purview.ts:233` hard-code one classical rule in
   Node (a Ketu sub-period, or Saturn over 8 and 12). [fact]
8. **Chapter agreement.** `panels/chapters.ts:20` computes a month distance: a confirming clock agrees when its
   boundary lies within N months (`SELF_CHAPTER_BOUNDARY_MONTHS`, `env.ts:855-857`). Date arithmetic, but a rule
   decided in Node. [fact]

**Not derived in Node:** the MD→AD house relation (6/8, 2/12) comes from the engine's charter
(`PlanetCard.dashaCharter.fruition`), and the aspects come from `aspectedGraha` and `aspectedBy`. [fact]

## 6. Dasha system selection

- **Where it is stored.** `kundlis.preferred_dasha` (`schema.prisma:125`). It is written only by `setPreferredClock`
  (`service.ts:733-740`), from `PUT /api/self/clock` (`selfReadingRoutes.ts:85-98`). A system with no stored level-1
  periods is refused with `unknown_system`. Null clears the choice. [fact]
- **The "Choose your dasha" list** is `clock.choices` (`clock.ts:34-40`), in this order:
  - the chosen system;
  - the applicable conditional systems;
  - `SELF_CONFIRM_SYSTEMS`;
  - every system with level-1 rows in `kundli_activation` (`service.ts:173-185`).

  So the list shows what the engine stored for that kundli, not a fixed list of 40. Web: `selfReadingApi.ts:243`
  `setSelfClock`, `SelfReading.tsx:212-216`. [fact]
- **Which clock is used** (`clock.ts:16-42`), first match wins:
  1. the preferred system, if it is stored;
  2. the first applicable conditional system in `SELF_CLOCK_CONDITIONAL_ORDER` (default at `env.ts:833`: dwisatpathi,
     ashtottari, chathuraaseethi_sama, dwadasottari, panchottari, sataatbika, shastihayani, shodasottari,
     shattrimsa_sama);
  3. Vimshottari.

  The confirming clocks are `SELF_CONFIRM_SYSTEMS` (default at `env.ts:834`: vimsottari, narayana, chara, yogini,
  karaka, tithi_ashtottari). [fact]
- **How the choice reaches the Oracle.** [fact]
  - The clock's label and reason are in the brief and in the dasha pack.
  - The spine and the running chain are computed for that system.
  - The clock is part of the panel store key (`service.ts:355`), the Oracle store key (`oracle.ts:765`) and the dasha
    pack's horizon key.
- **Does a switch rebuild the board? Not directly.** The PUT only writes the column. What follows (the code path is
  fact; the timing is inference):
  1. The next `GET /api/self/reading` recomputes the code panels inside the request, because their key changed.
  2. It finds no Oracle output for the new key, reports `pending`, and calls `queueSelfReadingBuild`
     (`selfReadingRoutes.ts:58-67`).
  3. The build marker is per kundli, board and day (`buildEvent.ts:92-104`). A `ready` marker does not block, so a new
     background build runs.
  4. If a build for the old clock is still `building`, the poll gets `already_building`, and the new build is queued
     only after the old one ends.
  5. If today's build `failed` in the last 30 minutes, the page is served with `oracle.status = failed` for up to 30
     minutes.
- **Quota.** `runSelfReadingBuild` charges `chargeBoard(userId, 'self', asOf)` only when the Oracle ran fresh and ok
  (`buildEvent.ts:153-156`). The charge key is `board:self:<YYYY-MM-DD>` (`src/billing/questionGate.ts:46, 211-218`).
  So **a board costs one question per day, however many times the clock is switched.** [fact]
  - Each switch costs a full Oracle build (the replay measured 59–72 s and about $0.05) and debits nothing after the
    day's first build.
  - This path has no per-user daily spend cap. `getSelfReadingMaxSpendUsd()` ($1.00 by default, `env.ts:892-896`)
    applies to one build only (`let spentUsd = 0` inside the build). `CostGuard`
    (`src/reading/oracle/costGuard.ts`) is used only by the chat path.

## 7. Build and cache path

- **Page request** (`selfReadingRoutes.ts:44-74`): `getSelfReading(..., { oracleMode: 'cached_only' })`, raced
  against 10 s. [fact]
  - No digest yet → 202 `preparing/chart`, and the digest is queued (`service.ts:283-287`).
  - Over 10 s → 202 `preparing/reading`.
  - No stored Oracle output → the code panels are served with `oracle.status = 'pending'`, and one build is queued.
- **Code panels** run inside the request whenever their store key misses. [fact]
  - Each panel is a `withTechniqueOutput` row in `technique_outputs`, keyed by digest version, clock, TP year or sky
    where relevant, and `SELF_READING_VERSION` (`service.ts:350-363`; the version is `s2.2026-11-08d`, at `:81`).
  - Then come the purview, the diagnosis, the cards and the code audit (`:434-500`).
- **Background build** (`buildEvent.ts`). [fact]
  - Marker: one `area_builds` row (area `self.reading`, today) that goes `building` → `ready` or `failed`.
  - On Lambda the function invokes itself (`InvocationType: 'Event'`, `:134-139`). Off Lambda the build runs detached
    (`:194`).
  - A build still `building` after 10 minutes is run again (`:25`). A `failed` build starts a 30-minute cool-down
    (`:27`).
  - The event needs `KUNDLI_DIGEST_EVENT_TOKEN` (`:36-42, 180-184`).
- **Model stages** (`oracle.ts:795-1335`), in order: [fact]
  1. Five family readers (`:801-995`). Their findings are cached in `reading_findings_cache` under
     `self:<kundli>:<family>:<packHash>:<PROMPT_VERSION>` (`:817`).
  2. The lineman (`:998-1160`), cached by a hash of its input.
  3. The Oracle (`:1161-1335`). Its output is stored in `technique_outputs` as technique `self.reading.oracle` and is
     valid for the running period. The key (`oracle.ts:765`) holds the digest version, board, scope, clock, TP year,
     sky flag, **answers hash**, code version, prompt version and output shape.
  4. Gemini prefix caching (`ensurePromptCache`) is active only with `READING_EXPLICIT_CACHE=1`
     (`src/reading/llm/promptCache.ts:3`).
- **Dossier.** A `reading_dossiers` row is written when anything was built fresh (`service.ts:633-666`). [fact]
- **Sections stream.** `GET /api/dashboard/sections-stream` (`src/telemetry/pageTiming.ts:666-740`) streams NDJSON for
  `profile`, `chart_status`, `biodata`, `content` and `timeline` only. **It does not carry the Self board.** [fact]
- **Build times** (from `docs/reading-engine/65-replay-2026-11-09-self-19b210cd.md:3-18` and doc 65 §9b). [fact from docs]
  - Whole run: 71.7 s wall with the family readers cached; 59 s for a cold build; $0.0547 for the run.
  - Lineman: 3.4 s on 126,105 prompt tokens (98,322 cached).
  - Oracle: 44.6 s on 40,665 prompt tokens (12,246 cached), 5,908 output tokens.
  - Earlier page-open builds (doc 64 §1) failed after 164–300 s on Gemini timeouts.
  - Doc 64's target is 10–15 s and is not reached. The lineman still gets the full packs as its prefix (126 k tokens),
    although docs 64 and 65 say it should get findings and claims only.
- **Stale build.** Any change to the digest version, clock, TP year, sky presence, a confirm answer, the code version or
  the prompt version changes the key. The page then reports `pending` and a rebuild is queued. Natal panels hold until
  the digest changes; dasha panels until the running sub-period ends; annual panels until the Mudda year ends
  (`service.ts:317-326`). [fact]
- **Failed build.** [fact; the effect over the validity window is inference]
  - An Oracle failure, or "no area document", is not stored (`skipStore: true`, `oracle.ts:1260`).
  - The service marks every panel `areaStatus: 'failed'` and keeps the code lines (`service.ts:607-610`).
  - The marker goes to `failed`, and no question is charged.
  - **`budget_exceeded` is stored** (no `skipStore`, `oracle.ts:980-995`), so it would be served for the whole
    validity window.
