## 1. Chara karakas, Atmakaraka, Karakamsa / Swamsa, Ishta Devata

### Takeaway
The school uses an 8-planet chara karaka scheme. Rahu is included, and his degrees are measured backwards from the end of his sign. The Atmakaraka's navamsa sign (Karakamsa) is read as "the seat of the real self". The Ishta devata is taken from the strongest planet in, owning or aspecting the 12th from the AK in D9. Rath's own page adds AK-by-house and AK-by-planet rules, and ties the AK to Narayana dasa timing.

### Cited Findings
- **8 karakas, Rahu reversed:** Take Sun through Saturn plus Rahu. Measure each planet's advancement within its sign; for Rahu, measure from the end of the sign (30° minus his degrees). Rank in descending order: AK (self), AmK (advisors and ministers), BK (siblings), MK (mother), PiK (father), PK (children; also subordinates and followers), GK/JK (rivals, literally paternal cousins), DK (spouse). PiK can also show a boss. Ties are broken by minutes, then seconds. If two planets are at exactly the same longitude, they share the karakatva, the next karaka role is left empty, and its sthira karaka is used instead. — [Rao-IA §8.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Rahu as AK:** Rao gives an example where Rahu is AK. He says Rahu then represents the self for raja yogas, sustenance, achievements and spiritual evolution, and can show a saint at one end or a revolutionary or outcast at the other. — [Rao-IA §8.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Rath's position on 7 versus 8 karakas:** Rath presented "Ātmakāraka: the final proof of two carakāraka schemes" at SJC conferences (USA 2003, India 2004). A critic's summary, seen only via search, reports that Rath always uses 8 karakas for living beings and 7 for mundane charts. Opponents read Parasara as allowing Rahu only when two planets share a degree. The K.N. Rao lineage is reported to use 7. — [SJC India 2004 deck listing](https://es.scribd.com/doc/21215811/SJC-India-2004) [Search-summary only; argument not read]
- **Karakamsa:** This is the navamsa (or other varga) sign holding the AK. It is treated as the Karakamsa lagna, "the seat of the real self". The AK's sign or navamsa shows the soul's desire, and the AK's planetary nature shows its spiritual development. — [Rath: Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **Rath's AK rules:**
  - A malefic AK indicates a more spiritually advanced soul than a benefic AK.
  - The AK brings suffering in its own Vimshottari dasa, especially when malefic.
  - Great achievements come in the **Narayana dasa of the AK's sign or of signs aspecting it**.
  - A retrograde AK shows a deep-rooted desire behind the birth.
  - A combust AK gives spiritual insight.
  - Per-planet lessons: Sun, humility; Moon, compassion; Mars, non-violence; Mercury, truthful speech; Jupiter, respect for guru and children; Venus, clean character; Saturn, not causing sorrow; Rahu, staying guileless although often deceived.

  — [Rath: Atmakaraka & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **AK placement counted to the navamsa lagna (Rath, citing Jaimini):** Count from the AK to the navamsa lagna and check that house's karaka in both D1 and D9. Rath says D1 results apply only to exceptional figures (his example is Krishna); D9 results apply to everyone else. His one-line results:
  - 1st: noble birth; a humble-born native can rise to high rank if the D9 lagna lord joins the AK.
  - 2nd: saintly nature (renunciation with strong Saturn, austerity with strong Venus).
  - 3rd: wealth and powerful friends.
  - 4th: karma yogi.
  - 5th: dharmic.
  - 6th: illness. Remedies: Saturn or Satyanarayana worship, fasting at the full moon, speaking the truth.
  - 7th: joys; a good marriage with strong Venus.
  - 8th: many troubles; same remedies as the 6th.
  - 9th: piety and fortune.
  - 10th: clean heart and good home.
  - 11th: bravery; raja yoga with strong Mars.
  - 12th: great wealth and Lakshmi's blessing.

  — [Rath](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
- **12th from Karakamsa:** It shows the soul's liberation. Ketu there is conducive to moksha. Propitiating the deity of the strongest planet in the 12th from Karakamsa in D9 moves the soul toward moksha. The remedies chapter states it as the strongest planet occupying, owning or aspecting the 12th from AK in D9. — [Rao-IA §7.3.6, §34.5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Commonly circulated Ishta rule:** Take the planet in the 12th from Karakamsa, the strongest if there are several, or the 12th lord if the house is empty. One variant sends the reader to D24 when the house is empty; treat it with caution. — [Search-summary only; secondary sites, e.g. medium.com Jaimini Ishta article](https://medium.com/jaimini-astrology-know-your-ishta-devta-spirituali/find-the-ishta-devta-with-jaimini-astrology-be5b4dd51967)
- **Open question on the student side:** A Visti Larsen course page records a student asking what happens when the 12th from Karakamsa holds the AK itself together with another planet. The answer was not visible. — [Rama Academy lecture page](https://rama-academy.teachable.com/courses/jyotish-library/lectures/58478144) [Search-summary only]
- **Planet-to-deity mapping used for remedies:**
  - Sun: Shiva, Rama.
  - Moon: Gauri, Lalita, Saraswati, Krishna.
  - Mars: Hanuman, Rudra, Kartikeya, Narasimha.
  - Mercury: Vishnu, Narayana, Buddha.
  - Jupiter: Hayagriva, Vishnu, Parameshvara, Dattatreya, any guru.
  - Venus: Lakshmi, Parvati.
  - Saturn: Vishnu, Brahma.
  - Rahu: Durga, Narasimha.
  - Ketu: Ganesha.

  Refine the deity by the planets influencing it. For example, Saturn or Mercury influence points to a Vaishnava form, Mars to a fierce form, and Mars with Rahu to Subrahmanya. — [Rao-IA §34.5, Table 78](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Graha lagnas:** For any house, the karaka planet can also serve as a lagna. Rao gives the 3rd from Mars, 4th from Moon, 9th from Sun, 5th from Jupiter, 7th from Venus, and 12th from Saturn. When the karaka is stronger than the lagna, the house counted from the karaka may matter more. — [Rao-IA §7.3.9](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- For an engine: compute 8 karakas with Rahu reversed, and Karakamsa in D9. Read the 12th from Karakamsa for moksha and Ishta. Flag the AK's Narayana dasa sign, and signs aspecting it, as peak periods (Rath's own rule).
- "Swamsa" is used in the wider literature for the AK's navamsa sign. It was not distinguished from Karakamsa in any source read here.

### Gaps
- None of the sources read gives Rath's own written ruling on 7 versus 8 karakas. His deck was not read.
- Swamsa versus Karakamsa: whether Swamsa means the AK's sign in D9 or the D9 sign carried into D1 was not settled in any retrieved source.
- How Rath handles an empty 12th or a tie (D9 versus D1, occupant versus aspect) was not found in his own words.

## 2. Arudha padas: computation, exceptions, AL / A10 / UL / A7 / graha arudhas

### Takeaway
Bhava arudha: count from the house to its lord, then count the same number of signs onward from the lord. If the result is the 1st or 7th from the house, move to the 10th from that result. Scorpio and Aquarius use the stronger co-lord, chosen by a fixed tie-break ladder. The AL shows status and perception, and the 11th and 12th from AL show gains and losses. A10 (rajya pada) shows the workplace, especially in D10. The UL (A12) shows marriage, and the 2nd, 7th and 8th from UL show its strain or end. A7 shows associates. Graha arudhas show how the native perceives things.

### Cited Findings
- **Bhava arudha algorithm (applies in every varga):**
  1. Find the lord's sign.
  2. Count signs from the house to the lord, always counting forward.
  3. Count the same number of signs onward from the lord.
  4. **Exception:** if the result falls in the 1st or 7th from the house, take the 10th sign from the result.

  — [Rao-IA §9.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Effective exception:** If the result falls in the house itself, the arudha goes to the 10th from the house. If it falls in the 7th, the arudha goes to the 4th from the house (the 10th from the 7th). This follows directly from the step 4 rule above.
- **Dispute:** Some secondary writers present only the general rule (a "2n−1" formula) alongside a separate "special rule" set. Not all schools apply the 1st/7th exception. — [Ratra article (secondary)](https://saptarishisshop.com/?p=34695) [Search-summary only]
- **Co-lords of Scorpio (Mars/Ketu) and Aquarius (Saturn/Rahu):**
  - If one co-lord is in the sign, use the other.
  - Otherwise, apply these rules in order:
    1. More planets conjoined.
    2. More of Jupiter, Mercury and the dispositor conjoining or aspecting, using rasi drishti.
    3. Exalted.
    4. Placed in a sign of higher natural strength (dual > fixed > movable).
    5. For dasa length: the lord giving the longer dasa. For arudhas: the more advanced planet, with nodes measured from the end of the sign.

  — [Rao-IA §15.5.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Arudha names:**
  - A1: Arudha / Pada lagna.
  - A2: Dhana pada.
  - A3: Bhratri / Vikrama pada.
  - A4: Matri / Sukha / Vahana pada.
  - A5: Mantra / Putra / Buddhi pada.
  - A6: Roga / Satru pada.
  - A7: Dara pada.
  - A8: Mrityu / Randhra pada.
  - A9: Bhagya / Pitri / Dharma pada.
  - A10: Karma / **Rajya** pada.
  - A11: Labha pada.
  - A12: **Upapada** / Gauna pada / Moksha pada.

  — [Rao-IA §9.2 Table 18](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **AL:** The lagna is the true self; the AL is the maya of self, meaning perception and status. The 11th and 12th from AL show gains and expenditure (attributed to Parasara and Jaimini). Natural malefics in the 3rd or 6th from AL make one bold and materially successful. Natural benefics there make one gentle and saintly. — [Rao-IA §9.3](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Three-layer reading rule:**
  - Planets from the lagna show reality.
  - Planets from the AL show the world's perception and material situation.
  - Graha arudhas counted from the lagna show how the native perceives and feels.

  — [Rao-IA §9.6](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **A10 in D10:** It shows the workplace or work environment. Mars there suggests dynamic or engineering firms; Jupiter suggests a university, court or temple. The 10th from the lagna in D10 shows true conduct and career; the 10th from AL in D10 shows career status. — [Rao-IA §9.3–9.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **"A10 is rajya pada; one becomes famous when this arudha is triggered"** (class note). "Houses are intangible, arudha padas are tangible" (class note). — [SJC-Boston notes](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/classes/book2-for-CD.pdf)
- **UL (A12):**
  - Planets in the UL show the kind of marriage and spouse.
  - The 8th from UL shows the marriage's longevity.
  - The 2nd and 7th from UL show its end.
  - Mars, Saturn or the nodes there can bring trouble or divorce.
  - The nature of the spouse and the length of the marriage are judged separately.

  — [Rao-IA §9.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **A7:** It shows the kind of people one associates with. In D10 it shows professional associates; in D24 college peers; in D20 religious colleagues. — [Rao-IA §9.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf). A class example uses A7 to show the tangible form of a business. — [SJC-Boston notes](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/classes/book2-for-CD.pdf)
- **Other padas by varga:**
  - A4 in D16: actual vehicles. A4 in D24: the school or university.
  - A3 in D10/D24: books written.
  - A5 in D10: trappings of power and awards. A5 in D24: exam results.
  - A9 in D24: degrees.
  - Rath's own D10 is used as the example: A3 in Virgo, with argala from Mercury, Venus and Jupiter, explains his books in Mercury dasa.

  — [Rao-IA §9.4, §16.6.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Graha arudhas:**
  - Count from the planet's sign to its stronger owned sign, then the same number onward. The same 1st/7th exception applies.
  - The stronger owned sign is chosen with the stronger-rasi ladder; rule 4 (lord in a sign of different odd/even parity) always resolves the tie.
  - If Ketu is 7th from Rahu, Ketu's arudha is placed with Rahu's. If they are conjoined, their arudhas are in opposite signs.

  — [Rao-IA §9.5, §15.5.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Rath (Shula dasha article):** Death can occur in the AL sign (or the relevant relative's arudha) and in signs aspecting the 3rd from the AL. The 3rd from AL is the place of death. — [Rath: Shula Dasha](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/dasa/shula-dasha/)

### Inferences
- Engine order for arudhas:
  1. Resolve the co-lords of Scorpio and Aquarius.
  2. Compute A1–A12 in D1 and in each target varga.
  3. Compute graha arudhas.
  4. Read placements three ways: from the lagna (reality), from the AL (perception), and graha arudhas from the lagna (the native's own perception).

### Gaps
- Rath's own text (*Crux*, *Upadesa Sutras*) on the arudha exceptions was not retrieved. The rule is sourced to his student.
- Visti Larsen's writings on the AL (sibling and AL techniques are mentioned in forums) were not retrieved.

## 3. Narayana dasa and other dashas: when to use which

### Takeaway
Narayana dasa (also called Padakrama dasa) is the school's main phalita rasi dasa. Rath's own summary says it starts from the stronger of the lagna and the 7th (the "satya peethas"). The full rules (sign movement, direction, Saturn and Ketu exceptions, lengths, antardasas, and how to read the dasa sign as a lagna) are written out by Rao. Other dashas have separate jobs:
- Lagna Kendradi rasi dasa: material success.
- Sudasa: wealth and power, counted from Sree Lagna.
- Drig dasa: spiritual growth.
- Shoola and Niryana Shoola dasas: death and health.
- Kalachakra: Parasara's "most respected" dasa.
- Vimshottari: general use, with variant starting stars.

### Cited Findings
- **Rath on the start:** The dasa begins from the stronger of the Lagna or the 7th, the satya peethas (seats of truth). Rath also calls it Padakrama dasa. His book *Nārāyaṇa Daśā* (Sagar, 1998) is the primary text. — [Rath: Narayana Dasa teaser on srath.com](https://srath.com/page/63/)
- **Choosing the stronger sign:** Apply these rules in order and stop at the first that decides:
  1. More planets in the sign.
  2. More of Jupiter, Mercury and the sign's lord occupying or aspecting it, by rasi drishti.
  3. Contains an exalted planet.
  4. Its lord is in a sign of the opposite odd/even parity.
  5. Natural strength (dual > fixed > movable). This never decides Narayana dasa, because a sign and its 7th are always the same type.
  6. The sign whose lord is more advanced in its sign (nodes measured from the end of the sign).

  Rao warns that the ladder is adapted by purpose. Ayur dashas weigh the luminaries' aspects instead of the lord's. AK-based dashas treat the AK's sign as strongest and also weigh the lord's kendra or panaphara placement from the AK. — [Rao-IA §15.5.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Progression (Rao):**
  - Movable seed: consecutive signs (Brahma).
  - Fixed seed: every 6th sign (Shiva).
  - Dual seed: trines from the seed, then trines from its 10th, 7th and 4th (Vishnu).
  - Direction: forward if the 9th from the seed is odd-footed (Ar, Ta, Ge, Li, Sc, Sg); backward if even-footed (Cn, Le, Vi, Cp, Aq, Pi).
  - **Saturn in the seed:** a plain forward zodiacal sequence.
  - **Ketu in the seed:** the direction reverses.

  — [Rao-IA §18.2.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Dasa length:**
  - Count from the dasa sign to its lord: forward for odd-footed signs, backward for even-footed. Subtract 1 to get the years.
  - Lord in its own sign: 12 years.
  - Lord exalted: add 1 year. Lord debilitated: subtract 1 year.
  - Scorpio and Aquarius use the stronger lord.
  - Second cycle: each sign gets 12 minus its first-cycle years.

  — [Rao-IA §18.2.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Antardasas:**
  - Twelve equal parts (n years gives n months each).
  - They start from the sign holding the lord of the stronger of the dasa sign and its 7th.
  - Direction: forward if that starting sign is odd, backward if even. This uses plain odd/even, not footedness.
  - The Saturn and Ketu exceptions apply here as well.
  - An alternative reading splits each dasa into thirds: the sign rules the first, its lord the second, and occupants and aspecting planets the third.

  — [Rao-IA §18.3–18.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Interpretation:** Treat the dasa sign as the lagna (or its 7th, if dashas started from the 7th). The sign holding its lord is the "paaka rasi". The strength of the dasa-lagna lord is the key factor. Parasara's rules, as Rao gives them:
  - Malefics in the 3rd or 6th: success. Benefics there: failure.
  - Benefics in trines or the 8th: happiness. Malefics there: obstruction.
  - Any planet in the 11th: gains.
  - Rahu in the 8th or 12th: constant fear.
  - Dasa-lagna lord, or a kendra or trikona lord, exalted or in own sign: excellent.
  - A debilitated dusthana lord: good.
  - Malefics in the 4th: discomfort.
  - Benefics in the 2nd or 5th: name and favour from authority.
  - An afflicted 7th from the dasa lagna or the paaka rasi: marital trouble.

  — [Rao-IA §18.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Natal-reference reading of Narayana dasa:**
  - The dasa of the rajya pada (A10) gives career success.
  - The dasa of the UL can bring marriage; the 2nd or 7th from UL, marital trouble.
  - The dasa of the GL gives power. Antardasas aspected by the GL bring promotions.
  - Antardasas aspecting the UL bring marriage.
  - Padas aspected by the antardasa sign shape its results. Rao's worked example: an antardasa aspecting A3 brought weapons for India in 1998.

  — [Rao-IA §18.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Varga Narayana dasa:**
  - The seed of D-n is the nth house; subtract multiples of 12 when n > 12 (D24 → 12th, D16 → 4th).
  - Take that house's lord, find the sign it occupies in D-n, treat that sign as the lagna, and run the rasi rules.
  - Use D10 for career, D4 for residence and foreign stay, D9 for marriage, D7 for children, D24 for learning, D12 for parents.
  - Rao warns that "dasa sign as lagna" reading has no basis for vargas; it applies only to the rasi chart.

  — [Rao-IA §18.5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Ashtakavarga with Narayana dasa:** Notes attributed to Rath say SAV and BAV can be used alongside this dasa. — [Scribd Narayana dasa notes](https://www.scribd.com/doc/151715883/NarayanaDasa-Resources) [Search-summary only]
- **Lagna Kendradi rasi dasa:** Start from the stronger of the lagna and the 7th. Direction is forward for an odd seed sign and backward for an even one, with the Saturn and Ketu rules. Order: kendras, then panapharas, then apoklimas. Lengths follow Narayana dasa. Rao frames it as Lakshmi's (trine-based) movement and says it shows material prosperity. — [Rao-IA §19](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Sudasa (Sree Lagna Kendradi):**
  - Start from Sree Lagna's sign. Direction by odd/even. Order kendras → panapharas → apoklimas. Lengths as in Narayana dasa.
  - The first dasa's balance at birth = (30° − SL's degrees in its sign) / 30°.
  - Dasas of the HL, its 7th, signs aspecting HL, or signs linked to HL by lord exchange or aspect bring wealth.
  - The same links to the GL bring power.
  - Upachayas from AL, especially the 11th, raise status. The 8th and 12th from AL bring setbacks.
  - Sree Lagna = lagna + (fraction of Moon's nakshatra traversed × 360°).

  — [Rao-IA §5.7, §20](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Drig dasa:**
  - Start from the 9th: the 9th and the 3 signs it aspects, then the 10th and its aspected signs, then the 11th and its aspected signs.
  - Each group's direction is set by that house's footedness. Lengths follow Narayana dasa.
  - Use: spiritual vision. The AL dasa can bring renunciation if parivraja yogas exist; signs aspecting the AL bring outward spiritual activity; the lagna and 7th bring inner awakening.

  — [Rao-IA §21](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Shoola dasa (Rath's own page):**
  - An ayur dasa for death and health of the self and relatives, "amongst the most accurate".
  - For the self: start from the stronger of the lagna and 7th. For relatives: the stronger of 3/9 (father, younger siblings), 4/10 (mother), 5/11 (children, elder siblings), 6/12 (servants, pets).
  - Always regular and zodiacal.
  - 9-year dashas and 9-month antardashas for humans (gestation = antardasha). For other charts, full span ÷ 12 per dasha and ÷ 144 per antardasha.
  - Rudra = the stronger of the 2nd and 8th lords, or the weaker one if it is more afflicted. The Rudra's sign is the Shoola; its trines are the Trishula.
  - Death comes in the Trishula, a Rudra yoga (Moon aspecting the 2nd or 8th natural signs), the AL, or signs aspecting the 3rd from AL. The antardasha should be a dusthana from the dasa sign.
  - Remedy: Mrityunjaya mantra and Shiva worship on Mondays.

  — [Rath: Shula Dasha](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/dasa/shula-dasha/)
- **Naming difference between Rath and Rao:**
  - Rao calls the 2nd/8th-based, 7-8-9-year dasa "Niryana Shoola dasa". It starts from the stronger of the 2nd and 8th, runs forward for an odd sign and backward for an even one, and gives movable, fixed and dual signs 7, 8 and 9 years.
  - Rao notes that his guru Rath uses the name "Shoola dasa" for a different dasa, the lagna/7th 9-year one.
  - Rao also adds his own rule: the AL or its trines can give death, as can the 3rd or 8th from the AL if malefics or marakas occupy or aspect them.

  — [Rao-IA §22–23, footnote 59](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Vimshottari:**
  - It is a general phalita dasa that can also be used as an ayur dasa. Each planet delivers what its positions in the relevant vargas promise. Examples: the 5th lord in D7 gives a child; the 7th lord in D9 gives marriage; a planet exalted in the GL in D10 gives power.
  - Variants start from the 4th, 5th or 8th star from the Moon (kshema, utpanna, adhana). Prefer the utpanna (5th) start when the sign of the 5th star is stronger than the Moon's sign. The 4th and 8th starts are for longevity.
  - A lagna-based Vimshottari is used only when the lagna is much stronger than the Moon.

  — [Rao-IA §16.4–16.5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Rath's "Tripod of Life":**
  - Judge the mahadasa from the Sun, the antardasa from the Moon, and the pratyantardasa from the lagna.
  - Planets in Ravi (solar) yogas give results in their mahadasa. Planets in Chandra yogas give results in antardasas. Other yogas, including raja yogas, give results mainly in pratyantardasas.

  — [Rao-IA §16.5.3](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Year length:** Rao says most scholars, Rath included, use solar years for nakshatra dashas. Rao himself uses savana (360-day) years. — [Rao-IA §16.2 "Controversy"](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Moola dasa (Kendradi graha dasa):** Planets are taken by kendra, panaphara and apoklima from the stronger of the lagna and the Moon. It is said to beat Vimshottari when 4 planets are in kendras. Rao says it is beyond his book's scope. — [Rao-IA §19.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Kalachakra:**
  - Called "most respectable" by Parasara.
  - Nakshatras are split into savya and apasavya groups.
  - Deha and jeeva rasis (first and ninth dasas, swapped for apasavya births) show body and spirit; transits over them matter.
  - A dasa gives the results of the houses its sign holds in each varga.
  - Signs with 30 or more SAV bindus in D10 give the best career phases; in D24, the best learning phases.

  — [Rao-IA §24](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Conditional nakshatra dashas:** In annual charts, Rao picks "the best nakshatra dasa" for each chart and varga, by his "Unified Nakshatra Dasa Approach". Examples: Shodasottari from the D10 Moon, Ashtottari from the D7 lagna, Chaturaseeti sama from the D10 Moon. Under Ashtottari, Ketu has no period, so Rahu gives Ketu's results. — [Rao-TP2014](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_tp.pdf)

### Inferences
- Synthesis of dasha routing, not a sequence stated by any source:
  - Narayana dasa (D1) for life phases, with the AK sign, A10, UL and GL as triggers.
  - Varga Narayana dasa for each domain.
  - Sudasa for money and power.
  - Vimshottari (or a conditional dasha chosen by strength) for planet-level timing, read through the vargas and the Tripod rule.
  - Shoola for health and longevity.
  - Drig for spiritual matters.
- The Ketu-reversal and Saturn-forward exceptions also govern antardasas and Lagna Kendradi. An engine should implement them once and reuse them.

### Gaps
- Rath's own book *Narayana Dasa* was not retrieved. The sign-results text ("sign dasha results") and any differences between Rath's 1998 rules and Rao's are unverified.
- Rath's specific conditions for conditional nakshatra dashas (Ashtottari, Shodasottari, Dwadasottari and others) were not found in his own words.
- Rath's Kalachakra calculation preferences were not found.

## 4. Tithi Pravesha annual chart versus Tajaka varshaphala

### Takeaway
SJC uses the Tithi Pravesha (TP) chart as its annual chart:
- **Rath's definition:** the Sun in its natal sign and the Moon at the exact natal Sun–Moon angle, cast for the birthplace.
- **Poddar:** the weekday lord at the TP moment is the year lord, and the hora lord sets the year's focus.
- **Rao 2014:** calls the hora lord "the lord of the year".

The TP dasha is Tithi Ashtottari. Any varga of the year chart can be read (D10 for career, D9 for marriage), and the natal chart always overrides the year chart. Tajaka uses a different, multi-candidate year lord.

### Cited Findings
- **Rath's definition:**
  - The TP is "the annual horoscopy of Jyotish" and the "truest astrological birthday".
  - The Sun is in its natal sign (not the exact degree). The Moon is at the exact natal Sun–Moon angle, so the same tithi and the same fraction of it are left.
  - Cast for the birthplace's coordinates, wherever the native lives.
  - Marriage is prohibited in the solar month of birth.
  - Tajaka (the solar return) is noted as a separate system, accepted by Nilakantha and B.V. Raman.

  — [Rath: Tithi Pravesha](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/tithi-pravesa/tithi-pravesha/)
- **Rath on mundane TP:** He reads India's 2008 TP with dasa and pratyantara periods (Ketu maraka antardasa; Moon pratyantara as trouble from neighbours and water). — [Rath: India Tithi Pravesh 2008](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/mundane/india-tithi-pravesh-2008/) [Search-summary snippet only]
- **Poddar (SJC student; credits Rath with making TP public):**
  1. **Year lord = lord of the weekday at the TP moment**, counted sunrise to sunrise, so a TP before sunrise belongs to the previous day. A functional benefic for the natal lagna that is well placed gives a good year. Its natal yogas are activated.
  2. **TP lagna versus natal lagna:** in a kendra, happiness; in a trikona, prosperity; in a dusthana or badhaka house, obstacles. The TP lagna lord shows success or failure.
  3. **Hora lord** at the TP moment sets the year's main focus through its ownership, placement and yogas. Poddar calls it the most important of the three. Vargas show the specifics.
  - Read the TP like a rasi chart, always with the natal chart, which has overriding influence.
  - The same factors apply to monthly and daily pravesha charts.

  — [Poddar, Sanskriti Magazine](https://www.sanskritimagazine.com/principles-of-tithi-pravesh-chakra-in-vedic-astrology/)
- **Rao 2014:**
  - The hora lord at the TP moment "is the lord of the year". The houses it owns and occupies in each varga matter most during the year.
  - Judge the TP like the natal chart, effective for one year. All vargas can be used.
  - Indications must be strong in the matching varga as well as in D1. Examples: a strong 5th in D1 is checked in D7 (child), D10 (promotion), D24 (academics) and D20 (mantra success).

  — [Rao-TP2014](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_tp.pdf)
- **Rao's 2014 departures (not Rath's method):**
  - Soli-lunar months are defined by the tropical sign of the Sun–Moon conjunction.
  - The Pushya-paksha ayanamsa is used for vargas.
  - He reports this fixed corner cases that the sidereal-month TP got wrong (worked marriage and layoff examples using D9 and D10).

  — [Rao-TP2014](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/pp_tp.pdf)
- **Class-note usage:**
  - "Hora Lord (Ruler of year)" is named first in TP readings, then the TP rasi and D10.
  - Tithi Ashtottari dasa periods are used inside the year (India 2005: Moon–Venus antardasa in Sep–Nov). A personal 1979 TP example also uses a Tithi Ashtottari Mars period.
  - Daily TP charts (each new tithi) are said to have no traditional basis: use them with caution.

  — [SJC-Boston notes](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/classes/book2-for-CD.pdf)
- **"The Dasha applicable to the Tithi Pravesha Chakra is the Tithi Ashtottari Dasha"** (Rath/Rao Jyotish Digest 2002 material). — [Scribd TP analysis](https://www.scribd.com/document/348629674/Tithi-Pravesha-Chart-Analysis) [Search-summary only]
- **Conflict:** One site says the tithi lord is the year lord. — [dekhopanchang](https://dekhopanchang.com/en/learn/tithi-pravesha) [Search-summary only; conflicts with Poddar and Rao]
- **Tajaka contrast (Rao-IA):**
  - The Tajaka year lord is chosen from 5 candidates: the lord of the Sun's sign (day birth) or Moon's sign (night birth), the natal lagna lord, the Muntha lord, the annual lagna lord, and the tri-rasi lord.
  - Shortlist those aspecting the annual lagna, then pick the highest pancha-vargiya bala.
  - Tajaka also uses Muntha, Tajaka aspects, Harsha bala, sahams and the 16 Tajaka yogas.
  - Sahams: a good yoga between the lagna lord and the lord of an important saham's sign brings that matter during the year. Natal sahams also work with transits, for example Saturn or Rahu over the paradesa saham for going abroad.

  — [Rao-IA §28](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- Engine sketch, a synthesis that keeps the disputed year-lord point explicit:
  1. Cast the TP chart and its vargas.
  2. Compute both the weekday lord and the hora lord, and record which convention is used.
  3. Compare the TP lagna with the natal lagna (kendra, trikona or dusthana).
  4. Read the topic varga of the TP (D10 for career, D9 for marriage).
  5. Time within the year with Tithi Ashtottari.
  6. Cap every conclusion by the natal promise.

### Gaps
- The **Tithi Ashtottari calculation** (starting lord from the birth tithi, sequence, period lengths, compression to a year) was not found in any retrieved source.
- Rath's own statement on weekday lord versus hora lord as year lord was not found. The two student accounts differ.
- Rath's 2002 Jyotish Digest papers were not retrieved.

## 5. Divisional charts, argala, rasi drishti, special lagnas, sahams

### Takeaway
Each varga is read for its own domain, and a D1 promise must be confirmed in the matching varga. The arudhas, special lagnas and Narayana dasa are all recomputed inside each varga. Argala (2nd, 4th, 11th, plus the 5th as secondary) is the "decisive intervention" on a house, blocked from the 12th, 10th, 3rd and 9th. Hora Lagna shows wealth, Ghati Lagna power and fame, and Sree Lagna prosperity (it drives Sudasa).

### Cited Findings
- **Varga domains:**
  - D1: physical existence.
  - D2: wealth.
  - D3: siblings.
  - D4: residence, property, fortune.
  - D5: fame and power.
  - D6: health troubles.
  - D7: children.
  - D8: sudden troubles, litigation.
  - D9: marriage, spouse, dharma, inner self, basic skills.
  - D10: career and achievements in society.
  - D11: death and destruction.
  - D12: parents.
  - D16: vehicles and comforts.
  - D20: religious and spiritual life.
  - D24: learning.
  - D27: strengths and weaknesses.
  - D30: evils, punishment, disease.
  - D40: auspicious and inauspicious events.
  - D45: all matters.
  - D60: past-life karma, all matters.

  Planes: D1–D12 physical; D16–D24 mental; D27–D30 subconscious; D40 and above karmic. — [Rao-IA §6.3–6.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Varga method:** Pick the varga for the matter, then look for links between the relevant houses, karakas and lords in it. Examples:
  - Foreign residence: D4, 9th/12th plus Rahu.
  - Promotion: D10, with links to the GL, and the AL or its 5th and 10th. The AL lord in the 10th from AL aspecting the GL gives a promotion in its period.

  — [Rao-IA §6.5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **The SJC varga workshop** (Hyderabad 2002, edited by Rath, book *Varga Chakra*) had chapters on divisional dignities, Hora, D3, D12, D60, D24 (including foreign education), D9, D16, D7, D4, Shatamsa, D30, D20, arudhas and special lagnas in vargas, and transits from the varga Moon and lagna. — [Rath: Varga Chakra](https://srath.com/misc/varga-chakra/)
- **D9 / Navamsa deity amsas (class notes):**
  - A class example reads deities of amsas, e.g. "Anantamsa", "Isana's amsa", "Yamamsa" for Jupiter as unemotional and dutiful.
  - The vimshopaka-style dignities "Devalokamsa" (own, exalted or moolatrikona in 7 of 10 vargas), "Parijatamsa" and "Simhasanamsa" are used for spiritual or dharmic status.
  - Class rule: Ketu in the 12th or 1st from the AK in D9, free of malefic aspect, shows nearness to moksha.

  — [SJC-Boston notes](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/classes/book2-for-CD.pdf)
- **D10 deities (unverified scheme):** The ten Dikpalas are Indra, Agni, Yama, Rakshasa, Varuna, Vayu, Kubera, Ishana, Brahma and Ananta. They run forward in odd signs and reverse in even signs. The page cites no source and is not attributed to Rath. — [astrologyayurveda.com](https://astrologyayurveda.com/blog/10-ruling-deities-of-the-dasaṁsa-d10)
- **Argala:**
  - Planets or houses in the 2nd, 4th and 11th from a house or planet cause primary argala; the 5th causes secondary argala. Benefics give shubha-argala and malefics papa-argala.
  - Virodhargala comes from the 12th, 10th, 3rd and 9th respectively.
  - The side with more planets wins; if equal, the stronger side wins.
  - Several malefics in the 3rd cause argala instead of obstruction.
  - When Ketu occupies the sign, count argala anti-zodiacally.
  - Argala from the 2nd is the basic ingredient that sustains a matter.

  — [Rao-IA §10.5–10.7](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Rasi drishti versus graha drishti:** Rasi drishti is the sign-based influence on "neighbours" and is the same for all planets in a sign. Graha drishti is a planet-specific, more concrete influence. Rasi drishti is mandatory in the strength ladders and Jaimini rules. — [Rao-IA §10.4, §15.5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Special lagnas:**
  - **Hora Lagna (HL):** the Sun's longitude at sunrise plus 0.5° per minute since sunrise. Shows wealth.
  - **Ghati Lagna (GL):** the Sun's longitude at sunrise plus 1.25° per minute. Shows power, authority and fame. It is very sensitive to birth time (1.25° per minute).
  - **Bhava Lagna:** 1° per 4 minutes. Defined but unused.
  - **Sree Lagna:** the lagna plus the Moon's traversed fraction of its nakshatra × 360°.
  - **Paaka lagna:** the sign of the lagna lord. Shows the physical self. Example: the 5th from AL shows competition, the 5th from lagna scholarship, the 5th from paaka lagna memory.
  - Sunrise is taken at the first ray of the Sun.

  — [Rao-IA §5, §7.3.5–7.3.8](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Sahams:** Rao presents them under Tajaka (computation plus use with the annual lagna lord and with natal transits). — [Rao-IA §28.8](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- An engine should compute, per varga: the lagna, arudhas, special lagnas, argala, and Narayana dasa seeded from the varga. It should require confirmation in the varga before stating a D1 promise. Rao states this rule explicitly for TP charts, and his varga method implies it natally.

### Gaps
- Rath's own D10 deity scheme, D60 deity use and D24 rules were not retrieved. The *Varga Chakra* book is listed but its text was not available.
- Rath's position on sahams outside Tajaka was not found.

## 6. Career method (10th, D10, A10, Amatyakaraka, GL, artha trikona)

### Takeaway
No Rath text laying out a step-by-step career method was retrieved. From student material, the working method is:
- Read the 10th from the lagna, Moon and AL.
- Read D10 for true work and conduct.
- Read A10 in D10 for the workplace and A10 as the fame trigger.
- Read the GL for power and promotions, and the HL for money.
- The AmK is the career significator (the class notes use the 6th from AmK in D9 for the Palana devata).
- Time with D10 Narayana dasa, rasi Narayana dasa of A10 or GL, Sudasa, and the D10 of the year's TP.

### Cited Findings
- **10th from three references:** A planet in the 10th from the lagna gives professional developments. From the Moon, mental activity in the profession. From the AL, developments in professional status. — [Rao-IA §7.3.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **D10:** The 10th in D10 is true conduct and career; the 10th from AL in D10 is status. A10 in D10 is the workplace. A7 in D10 is professional associates. A5 in D10 is trappings of power and awards. A3 in D10 is writing output. — [Rao-IA §9.3–9.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **GL for promotions:**
  - Planets or signs giving promotion are usually tied to the GL.
  - A planet exalted in the GL in D10 gives power in its Vimshottari dasa.
  - Narayana dasa antardasas aspected by the GL bring promotions.
  - Sudasa dasas linked to the GL bring authority.

  — [Rao-IA §6.5, §16.5.1, §18.4, §20.3](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Narayana dasa of A10:** The dasa of the rajya pada gives career success. Rao's example: Bill Gates' Capricorn dasa held A10, with an exalted dasa-lagna lord in the 10th. — [Rao-IA §18.4](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **D10 Narayana dasa:** Seed = the 10th lord of the rasi chart, placed in its D10 sign and treated as the lagna. — [Rao-IA §18.5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Class-note D10 reading:**
  - "See 2, 6, 10 for career" (the artha trikona) in D10.
  - The AL shows material status.
  - Malefics give results in reverse order; benefics in quarter-order: the house occupied, then the house owned, then yogas, then own house and avastha.
  - A9 together with A10 gives a material raja yoga.

  — [SJC-Boston notes](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/classes/book2-for-CD.pdf)
- **Kalachakra:** Signs with 30 or more SAV bindus in D10 give the best career phases. — [Rao-IA §24.3.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **AmK and career:** An SJC BAVA 2017 workshop was titled around "Karma, the 10th House, Saturn, Dashamsha and Amatyakaraka". This confirms that the school treats these as one cluster; the content was not retrieved. — [srath.com BAVA 2017 listing](https://srath.com/announcements/bava-2017/) [Search-result snippet only]
- **Remedy for career:** Wear the gemstone of the D10 yogakaraka. Fast on the weekday of the rajya-pada lord. — [Rao-IA §34.2–34.3](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)

### Inferences
- Plausible engine order (synthesis):
  1. Natal 10th from the lagna, Moon and AL.
  2. D10 lagna, 10th, and the 2/6/10 houses.
  3. A10 and A7 in D1 and D10.
  4. GL and HL links.
  5. AmK in D1 and D9.
  6. Timing: rasi Narayana dasa (A10/GL signs, antardasas aspected by the GL), D10 Narayana dasa, Sudasa for money and power, and the Vimshottari lord's D10 placement.
  7. That year's TP D10.

## 7. Remedies (mantra, deity selection, gemstones, fasting, daana)

### Takeaway
The written rules retrieved are Rao's, in the integrated tradition. Remedies come in four tiers: common-sense precaution, gemstones (functional benefics only), good deeds and charity, and mantra or propitiation of planets and their deities. Deities are picked by planet, refined by the planets influencing it, and tied to the relevant varga. The 12th from AK in D9 gives the moksha (Ishta) deity, and the lagna lord's deity in a varga helps that area of life. Rath's own pages prescribe specific deity remedies, for example for the AK in the 6th or 8th and for Shoola dasha. His book *Vedic Remedies in Astrology* was not available.

### Cited Findings
- **Rath's book:** *Vedic Remedies in Astrology* (Sagar, 2000) is described as gathering the remedial measures in sacred literature and showing how to read the chart to prescribe them. — [Exotic India listing](https://www.exoticindia.com/book/details/vedic-remedies-in-astrology-idj596) [Search-summary only]
- **Four ways to pacify planets:** gemstone, good deeds, planetary mantras and stotras, and deity propitiation. Common-sense precaution comes first. — [Rao-IA §34.1](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Gemstones:**
  - Only for functional benefics. Use the dasa lord or a planet in a good yoga. Prefer a weak or badly placed functional benefic; Rao's example is Venus as yogakaraka in the 8th.
  - Avoid stones of functional malefics, and of marakas during maraka dashas.
  - For a particular area of life, strengthen the yogakaraka of that varga (D10 for career).
  - Combine two stones only when the planets are friends, functional benefics, and forming a yoga.
  - First wear the stone on the planet's weekday, while that planet is strong.
  - Metal: gold for Sun, Moon and Jupiter; copper for Mars; silver or platinum for Mercury and Venus; iron or silver for Saturn; silver for the nodes.

  — [Rao-IA §34.2](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Good deeds and daana by planet:**
  - Temples for the Sun. Music for the Moon. Exercise for Mars. Scholars for Mercury.
  - Priests and cows for Jupiter. Poetry for Venus. Manual labour for Saturn.
  - Research or pilgrimage for Rahu. Meditation for Ketu.
  - Grains: wheat (Sun), rice (Moon), toor dal (Mars), moong (Mercury), chickpeas (Jupiter), a whitish grain (Venus), sesame (Saturn), black gram (Rahu).

  — [Rao-IA §34.3](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Fasting:** Fast on the weekday of the UL lord to ease marriage troubles, and of the rajya-pada lord to ease career troubles. — [Rao-IA §34.3](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Deity selection:**
  - The deity of the lagna lord in a varga helps that varga's area of life.
  - The deity of the strongest planet occupying, owning or aspecting the 12th from AK in D9 leads toward liberation.
  - Refine the deity by the planets combining with it. Rao's example: Rahu with Mars, aspected by Saturn and Mercury, gives Ugra Narasimha.

  — [Rao-IA §34.5](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf)
- **Rath's specific prescriptions:**
  - AK in the 6th or 8th: worship Saturn or Satyanarayana, fast at the full moon, speak truth. — [Rath: AK & Karakamsa](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/scholar/atmakaraka-karakamsa/)
  - Health and longevity: worship Shiva on Mondays with the Mrityunjaya mantra. — [Rath: Shula Dasha](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/dasa/shula-dasha/)
- **Palana devata:** It is read from the 6th sign from the AmK in D9, as the deity who sustains one in the world. — [learningastroscience blog](https://learningastroscience.blogspot.com/2015/08/amatyakaraka.html) [Search-summary only]
  - **Conflicting conventions:** one source counts the 6th *planet* from the AmK rather than the 6th sign ([sutramritam](https://sutramritam.blogspot.com/2008/12/krishna-mishra-navamsa-karakamsa-not.html), search-summary only). Another uses the 9th from the D9 lagna ([PanchangBodh](https://panchangbodh.com/jaimini-astrology), search-summary only).
  - A class-note example locates "6th from AmK in D9" and reads the 12th from the AK for Ishta, "a form of Vishnu avatara for moksha". This is one chart example, not a general rule. — [SJC-Boston notes](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/classes/book2-for-CD.pdf)
- **Dharma devata:** Sources disagree on whether to count the 9th from the AK or the 9th from Karakamsa in D9. One secondhand summary says Rath ties life direction to the D9 deity of the 9th lord. — [Scribd "devta" guide](https://www.scribd.com/document/135075156/devta); [rattibha thread](https://en.rattibha.com/thread/1628950793347801088) [Both search-summary only]

### Inferences
- Engine sketch for remedies (synthesis):
  1. Identify the problem area and its varga.
  2. Pick the planet: the varga lagna lord, the yogakaraka, or the afflicted significator.
  3. Choose the remedy by planet nature. Gemstones only for functional benefics. Mantra, daana and deity worship for malefics.
  4. Map the deity from the planet and refine it by the planets influencing it.
  5. Add the Ishta (12th from AK in D9) as the overarching deity.
  6. Optionally add the Palana and Dharma devatas, flagged as disputed.

### Gaps
- The text of Rath's *Vedic Remedies in Astrology* was not retrieved. His exact Ishta / Palana / Dharma / Guru devata definitions, mantra selection rules (for example by the nakshatra or sign of the deity planet), and Navagraha mantra counts are unverified.
- No authoritative, Rath-attributed source settled the Palana devata or Dharma devata rule.
- Freedom Cole's remedies writings (shrifreedom.org) could not be retrieved.
