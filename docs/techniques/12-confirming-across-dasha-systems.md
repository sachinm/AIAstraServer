# 12 · Every dasha system that applies, the current and the next maha dasha, and how the systems confirm one another

Written 2026-11-21; **rev 2 on 2026-11-22** after Sachin asked for the rule to be checked against the research school
by school (Parashara, Jaimini, Rath and the others). What rev 2 changes: the rule is restated as steps in order, not a
count of four voices (§4); the year chart places and no longer raises a status; the "half a voice" is gone; who leads
the star voice is set out as Sachin's decision with both sides (§4); a school table (§1b) and the reading order by
school (§1c) are added; one citation is fixed. A technique rule for every dashboard and sub-dashboard; no code is
changed by this document.
Sachin's instruction: every dashboard technique reads **all the dasha systems that apply to the person**, for **the
current maha dasha and the upcoming next one**; and the research (`docs/deep-research/`, `docs/research/`) is used to
work out **how the systems confirm one another and how many are needed**.

Source tags: **Rath** (his own text) · **Student** (Rao, Poddar, PVR) · **Text** (a classical verse) · **Modern**
(named modern practice, known here from secondary sources only) · **Inferred** (the research writer's reading, stated
by no source) · **Ours** (a house rule we chose; no source). File numbers refer to `docs/deep-research/sanjay-rath/`
unless named.

## 4. The rule (how they confirm, and how many)

**Rev 2: steps in order, not a count.** Rev 1 counted up to four voices. The research reads Rath's method as "a
hierarchy, not a vote" (Inferred, `05` §8), and the birth chart's dasha outranks the year chart, so the year cannot
lift a status the way a vote would. The steps:

1. **The promise** in the birth chart: from the lagna, from the Moon, and in the subject's divisional chart. Not
   promised: never timed. [Text; every school]
2. **The pair.** Vimshottari and the sign dasha both pointing to the matter's significators in the periods running:
   confirmed. [Rath, *Crux* p. 3]
3. **The purpose dasha** strengthens, for its own subject only. [Rath]
4. **The year chart places** a confirmed matter in a year. [Student; Modern]
5. **Transit** as the trigger. [doc 09]

| Status | When | Wording to the person | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not promised | the birth chart does not hold the matter | never timed | Text |
| **Confirmed** | Vimshottari **and** the sign dasha both point to the matter in the periods running | stated, with dates | Rath's pair |
| Confirmed, placed in the year | the above, and the year chart points the same way | "this year" | Ours, on Poddar's order |
| Leaning | one of the pair **with** the lens's purpose dasha (the Work, money and purpose lenses only); or the pair agrees while the conditional dasha that applies disagrees with Vimshottari | "the signs lean toward"; both readings named | Ours |
| Open | one of the pair alone. The year chart, or any number of other systems agreeing, does not lift it | what would bring it, asked | Ours |
| Contested | Vimshottari and the sign dasha point opposite ways | both sides, no date | Rath (no real conflict: say both) |

- **How many: two, the pair.** No source gives a number of systems; two dashas agreeing is what Rath, K.N. Rao and
  the BVB school each practise, with different partners (§1b). Nothing beyond the pair raises a status.
- **Everything else is placement or support.** The reading may say "the second star dasha (Yogini) agrees" and "of the
  12 other systems that apply, 9 point the same way". Neither sentence changes the status. More systems agreeing by
  number alone raises the chance of a false agreement (`reports/Vedic dasha systems…` L37; `reports/Sanjay Rath
  methods and precedence.md` L141).
- **The "half a voice" of rev 1 is gone.** A disagreement inside the star family (Vimshottari against the conditional
  dasha that applies) caps the status at leaning and both are named.
- **Who leads the star voice: Sachin's decision (open point H of doc 11).** The research does not settle it:

  | Side | Says | Tag |
  |---|---|---|
  | The conditional dasha leads (today's code: the person's stored choice, else the conditional dasha that applies, else Vimshottari) | "Use Ashtottari only when the conditions are met, otherwise Vimshottari" | Rath |
  | Vimshottari always leads; the conditional dasha is its confirming second clock | BPHS calls Vimshottari chief and says a conditional dasha "should be considered", never "replaces". Common practice in the BVB and K.N. Rao schools. PVR allows an override only when the dasha's controlling planet is strong, which the engine does not compute | Text; Modern; Student |

  The research files' own lines "never the lead clock" and "do not let it silently replace Vimshottari" are product
  recommendations by the research writer, not a school's rule. **Built: today's behaviour, unchanged**, as the setting
  `READING_STAR_LEAD=clock` (default) or `vimshottari`. Either way both are always read, and the person's stored
  choice still wins. On the test chart `clock` means Dwisaptati-sama leads.
- **At what level the rule works today.** Narayana is verified at maha dasha level only. So "confirmed" today means:
  the star voice at maha dasha **and** sub-period level, the sign voice at maha dasha level. Sub-period confirmation by
  a sign dasha waits for the engine check of Narayana's sub-periods (doc 07 E1–E2).

## 5. The current and the next maha dasha

For **each system that applies**, every dashboard technique reads:

| Part | What is read | Source of the rule |
|---|---|---|
| The running maha dasha | its lord or sign judged from the birth chart (a star dasha by its lord: owner, seat, dignity, navamsa, links; a sign dasha by the sign, its occupants and its lord); what it brings for the dashboard's subject; how far through it the person is | doc 09 §4; Text |
| Its sub-periods | all of them, with levels 3 and 4 under the running one | doc 11 §4.4 |
| Its closing stretch | the last sub-period, flagged as "closing": dashas tend to turn adverse there; the planet-by-planet notes of BPHS 47 apply | Rath's dictum; Text. Low weight |
| **The next maha dasha** | its lord or sign judged the same way from the birth chart; its start date; its first sub-period; **what changes** from the running one for the dashboard's subject | Text (BPHS 47.5–6: the lord at birth) |
| The previous maha dasha | one line, as today | — |

- **The chapter turn (Ours; no school mentions it).** When the star voice and the sign voice both change maha dasha
  within 12 months of each other, the reading names it as a change of chapter and reads the next maha dasha at full
  weight. The 12 months is a product number: "N is not given by any text found" (`dasha-sandhi-and-chidra.md` §4).
  On the test chart the pair and the purpose dasha all turn inside ten months: Narayana 2027-10-15, Dwisaptati-sama
  2028-02-16, Vimshottari 2028-03-16, Sudasa 2028-08-03.
- **Not available yet:** the chart for the moment a dasha starts (the second half of BPHS 47.5–6, and the Moon at the
  start, Saravali 41). The engine does not cast it. Until it does, the next maha dasha is judged from the birth chart
  only and the reading says so. Node computes nothing here.
- **The junction** is worded as "a transition" only. No width, no extra weight (no classical basis).
- On the test chart 32 systems have a next maha dasha stored; 10 of them start within two years.
